Why Start Your China Tour from Shanghai? Convenient arrival: Over 280 air carriers travel to Shanghai from the rest of the world. Shanghai is also accessible via international cruises. Gateway city: From Shanghai, travelers can take flights or high-speed train journeys to connect to major cities in China. Future of China: Visit this metropolis to witness the development of China. How to Plan a China Tour from Shanghai Depending on your schedule and interests, you can spend 2–4 days in Shanghai. Usually, 1 day is enough to cover the highlights, such as the Bund, Yu Garden, and the Shanghai Tower. More days can be spent at the French Concession, the Jade Buddha Temple, and Hong Kong Disneyland, among other attractions. If time permits, taking an extension tour to Suzhou, Hangzhou, or Huangshan for an extra 3–5 days is highly recommended.
