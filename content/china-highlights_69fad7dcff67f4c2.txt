Weather: Wuhan is known for rainy and hot weather in July unlike other cities in central northern China with dry-heat weather. It is the hottest month of the year. The average nightly low temperature is a comfortable 25 °C (77 °F), and the average daily high temperature is 33 °C (91 °F). The humidity at 80%, plus the heat, makes it sweltering many days. Especially in the broad inner city concrete and asphalt expanses, the city swelters in the heat and temperatures may rise above 40 °C (104 °C). This gives the city the reputation of being one of China's "Three Furnace Cities." It is the second most rainy month. There are heavy thunderstorms sometimes, but it is often sunny. It is cloudy about 26 percent of the time. The monthly precipitation totals 191 mm, and it rains in about 11 days of the month. Pollution: The Air Quality Index is good at 74. Things to Do in Wuhan in July Visit Yellow Crane Tower Park Yellow Crane Tower: For foreign tourists, this is Wuhan's second most popular park after East Lake Park. The tower and park area is on a scenic hilll near the Yangtze River. The tower is recently constructed, fifty meters high, and provides good views of the Yangtze River on each of the floors. There are interesting exhibits, art, and history displays on each flight. See the East Lake Lotus Festival East Lake Park: A million visitors a year enjoy East Lake Park that has the biggest lake of any city in China and many extensive gardens. The Lotus Festival is in full swing in July. The dreamy lotus blossoms emit a dreamy scent as they doze in pads in red, white and blue shades. Frogs provide a sonorous melancholy melody.
