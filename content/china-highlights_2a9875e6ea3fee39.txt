Chinese Language and Culture Immersion Tour This itinerary brings together the most classic elements of China, such as the Forbidden City, the Great Wall, the Terracotta Warriors, and giant pandas, ensuring that your first trip to China is filled with unforgettable memories. Additionally, we have carefully designed interactive experience programs for children learning the Chinese language and culture, immersing them in local communities so they can gain a deeper understanding of Chinese culture through hands-on participation. Day Highlights 1-5 Beijing Immerse yourself in Beijing's cultural roots, from the Great Wall to traditional Confucian and Taoist sites, and even experience tai chi with a master 6-8 Xi'an Unveil the ancient wonders of Xi'an, including the Terracotta Warriors, and enjoy a bike ride on the historic City Wall 9-10 Chengdu Experience Chengdu's famous street food, visit the Dujiangyan Panda Valley and marvel at the Leshan Giant Buddha 11-13 Shanghai Discover Shanghai's rapid rise, enjoy a day at Disneyland and explore the city's modern and historic highlights During this trip, you will: Enjoy a memorable family vacation • An educational trip covering many of China's popular icons • 100% tailor-made for your family's needs and desires Improve your children's Mandarin proficiency • Mandarin- & English-speaking guides • Practice Mandarin with friendly locals Gain great insights into China today • Chinese-culture immersion activities • Experience China's development first-hand Beijing-Xi'an-Chengdu-Shanghai Tour Map Discover the Mythical Animals in the Forbidden City Explore the Forbidden City in a way that's specially designed for families with kids. Worship Confucius and Visit the Highest Educational Institution in Old China Understand how Confucius's principles shaped Chinese tradition and beliefs and learn about the traditional education system of China. Walk on the Great Wall and Slide Down From It Visit a less-crowded Great Wall section at Mutianyu and try an exciting toboggan ride. Make Your Own Terracotta Warriors in A Local People's Cave Dwelling Visit a traditional cave dwelling, learn how to make Terracotta Warriors from a local artisan in a traditional way, and get to know local people's life in countryside. Create the Unique Calligraphy Scrolls Have a calligraphy class in the Intangible Cultural Heritage Museum, frame your own calligraphy into a scroll and take it home. A Morning Date with Active Pandas It is best to go early in the morning to see the pandas feeding at around 9:30am, which is their most active time of the day. Be a Half-day Local Residents at a Model Community Visit the local market and community exhibition hall. Then enjoy a simple and authentic lunch at the community canteen. Warm Your Stomach with Amazing Chinese Food Enjoy a local food hunt in the Muslim Quarter and have an authentic Sichuan hotpot meal in a restaurant loved by locals. The sample itinerary below is specially designed for 5th-grade children, who are in a Mandarin Immersion Program at school. We can, however, customize the trip to suit your interests, preferences, budget and children's Chinese-language proficiency. Day 1Beijing | Arrival Welcome to China! Your personal guide will meet you at the airport and escort you to your hotel for check-in. If you are worried about jet lag, we can have a late start on Day 2 or simply leave it as a free day. Feel free to let us know your preference. Day 2Beijing | Roots of Chinese Culture Let's start the tour at the Temple of Confucius, to begin to understand Chinese traditions and beliefs. Then we'll head to the Imperial Academy, to learn about China's traditional education system and listen to Dacheng Rites Music. Then visit the unique Lama Temple. Enjoy the famous Peking roast duck for lunch, where an a-la-carte meal will be arranged. Kids can talk to the waitress in mandarin and order food from a Chinese menu. Walk into a traditional courtyard and visit a hutong family. Discover the traditional lifestyle and let your kids be like Chinese children living in a hutong as they experience how they study and play, and taste the snacks they love. Day 3Beijing | The Great Wall Discover the Summer Palace and its beautiful architecture and vast gardens in the morning. Lunch will be arranged in a hidden retreat-restaurant at the foot of the Great Wall. The restaurant used to be a glazed-tile factory. Now it offers organic dishes and an opportunity to explore rural China. Drive to China's amazing icon, the Great Wall. We recommend the Mutianyu section. There are five gigantic Chinese characters on one hillside by the wall. Find out what they mean and why they are there from your knowledgeable guide. To help the kids have more fun, a chairlift going up and toboggan sliding down are included. Day 4Beijing | Eco Tour of City Highlights Explore the highlights of Beijing city using public transport. Kids will learn how to read Chinese signs along the way and gain deeper insights into local people's lives. First visit Tian'anmen Square, and then the Forbidden City. You will explore some sites that interest kids more. The guide will give you detailed information about the history, culture, and architecture of the Forbidden City, bringing the stories alive for your kids. If you would rather not use public transport, we can arrange a private vehicle instead. Day 5Beijing to Xi'an | Tai Chi Experience Head to the Temple of Heaven, a bustling place where locals participate in all kinds of activities, such as kite-flying and water-calligraphy. Join them and practice daily conversation Then it will be time for a tai chi class with a renowned local tai chi master. Race towards Xi'an and enjoy lunch on a high-speed train, a good way to experience the technological development of China. Day 6Xi'an | The Terracotta Warriors The Terracotta Warriors are one of the greatest man-made wonders. We will introduce you to a different Terracotta Museum, one that can't be googled. We'll visit a local cave dwelling and you can make a little warrior of your own, with your local host's help. After lunch, we visit the ancient City Wall of Xi'an and enjoy a bike ride on it. Day 7Xi'an | The Culture of Ancient Capital The Xian Museum show you the development of thirteen ancient capitals in the past dynasties. Then learn the traditional architecture at the Small Wild Goose Pagoda. You will use the pattern-making method to create a model of the pagoda, which is a forming process widely used in casting and the production of crafts. Visit an energetic local family. Learn how to make dumplings and enjoy a family meal with them. Have a calligraphy class in the Intangible Cultural Heritage Museum. Practice under the guidance of your teacher. And take home a scroll you wrote as a souvenir. Day 8Xi'an to Chengdu | Local Park and Street Food Catch the morning high-speed train to Chengdu, a city renowned mainly for two things – its pandas and its food. Spend your afternoon in People's Park. The park is a lovely place for a leisurely walk or to observe locals engaging in various activities like tai chi or traditional music performances. Have a cup of tea in tea house is a traditional custom for Chengdu people. Tasting local street food at Jinli Street. Try some famous food like bean jelly in chili source, sweet potato noodle and wonton in Sichuan style. If you are brave, you can try rabbit head in chili source. Day 9Chengdu | Leshan Giant Buddha Drive for 2 hours and arrive in Leshan by noon. After a short break and lunch, your journey to the Giant Buddha begins. Climb up the Buddha, then walk down the narrow plank all the way to its feet, to discover you are only as tall as one of its toes. A cruise on the river nearby is also included, so you can have a different panoramic view of the Buddha Day 10 Chengdu to Shanghai | The Giant Panda The Dujiangyan Panda Valley, nestled among mountains and waters, provides fresh air and a serene atmosphere with fewer visitors. Here, you can observe giant pandas up close, while red pandas roam freely—if you're lucky, one might even pass by you during your visit. Your lunch is an authentic Sichuan hot pot, known for its bold flavors, particularly the numbing heat from Sichuan peppercorns. Please advise if you can't handle spicy food. After lunch, you visit a Chinese painting studio, where a professional painter guides you in creating your own panda artwork to take home. After the tour, your private guide and driver will accompany you to the airport and you will fly to Shanghai. Day 11Shanghai | New China Follow your guide to discover the secret of Shanghai's rise to prominence. This morning, we'll explore the modern residential areas and local life in Cao Yang New Neighborhood. Visit local markets to sample fresh produce and snacks while practicing your language skills. Afterward, we'll stop by the community exhibition hall and enjoy a simple, authentic lunch at the community canteen. Experience a public ferry and then ascend the tallest building in Shanghai — Shanghai Tower. Last stop is the Bund, where you will explore diverse architectural styles and enjoy breathtaking views of the modern skyline. Day 12Shanghai | Disneyland It has been a long journey for the whole family. Why not keep a nice surprise for the end, as a reward for the kids? Let's create some memorable family moments at Shanghai Disneyland. A private vehicle will escort you directly to the Disneyland entrance. Enjoy fun experiences the whole day, and fireworks in the evening. Day 13Shanghai | Departure Pack the gains from Chinese immersion along with some other unforgettable memories. Your private car, driver, and guide will be ready and waiting at your hotel lobby to get you to the airport on time. Have a nice journey home! Day 1Beijing | Arrival Welcome to China!
