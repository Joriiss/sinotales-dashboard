Title: How to Travel from Changsha to Zhangjiajie Easily？

Source: China Highlights

Changsha is the closest big city to Zhangjiajie and the capital of Hunan Province. Visiting the two cities is a great way to explore Hunan. How do you get to Zhangjiajie from Changsha? It is really easy with just 320 kilometers (or 200 miles) between them and plenty of transportation options. We recommend traveling by train as it is the most efficient option. Taking the train in China is an experience in itself and offers a great way to enjoy the scenic Chinese countryside views and make new friends. There are no direct flights from Changsha to Zhangjiajie. If you prefer taking the bus, we also have listed the options for those below. Flying is an expensive hassle for the short distance. How to get to Zhangjiajie from Changsha by train The bullet train is available from Changsha to Zhangjiajie, taking approximately 2-3 hours. It departs from either Changsha Railway Station or Changsha South Railway Station and arrives at Zhangjiajie West Railway Station. Multiple trains operate throughout the morning, afternoon, and evening. You can use the China Highlights train ticket service to instantly check ticket prices per seat category and available times for the day you plan to travel. It departs daily at 14:00. The driving time is approximately 4 hours. The arrival station is Zhangjiajie Central Bus Station. Tickets for this route are around 100 RMB. You can buy the ticket at the bus station. To secure a seat, it is suggested that you buy it at least one day in advance. • 6 Days Zhangjiajie Tour from Shanghai • 9 Days Minority Discovery in Zhangjiajie and Guizhou Zhangjiajie Travel Tips Zhangjiajie National Forest Park The best time to go to Zhangjiajie is when there are no public holidays in China, as it gets really busy, and when Zhangjiajie's weather is best. Chinese public holidays and school summer holidays (July and August) will lead to more expensive accommodation and flights (and sold out flights) We recommend you get to the airport at least 2½ hours before departure, and 3 during peak times. There can be really long check-in and security lines during holidays and weekends. We recommend getting to the train station at least 1 hour being departure, 1½ hours during peak times or to be safe, 2 hours if you still need to collect your ticket.
