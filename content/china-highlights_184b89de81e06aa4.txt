In Zhangjiajie, you'll discover the real-life inspiration for the floating Hallelujah Mountains from "Avatar." Yet, there's even more to discover — experience a Via Ferrata and visit the 72 Wonder Tower, where you could see the world's tallest stilt house, explore Hunan's top food street, and enjoy the breathtaking night view. With enhanced flight and high-speed rail connections, your adventure can easily extend to nearby fancy towns of Furong and Fenghuang or the bustling cities of Chongqing, Chengdu, Yunnan, Guizhou, Beijing, and Xi'an.
