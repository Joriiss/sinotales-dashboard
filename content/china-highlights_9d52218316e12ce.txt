The Map of the Sui Dynasty shows the land under the rule of the Sui Dynasty and the location of the Grand Canal. In 577, the Northern Zhou conquered the Northern Qi and reunified the North China. The Northern Zhou, known as the reign of Yuwen family of the Xianbei ethnic group, continued for 24 years with five emperors over three generations.
