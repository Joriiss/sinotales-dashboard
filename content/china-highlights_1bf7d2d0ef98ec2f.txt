Guilin's picturesque karst landscape and idyllic countryside views attract millions of travelers every year. But how to make the most of your days in Guilin? Keep reading to find your ideal Guilin adventure! • How Many Days to Spend in Guilin? • 4 Days in Guilin: A Classic Highlights Tour • 5 Days in Guilin: Enjoy Vibrant Activities • 6 Days in Guilin: A Vacation for Relaxation How Many Days to Spend in Guilin? To have a relatively complete Guilin trip, we suggest spending at least 4 days in Guilin, including the arrival and departure days. For a shorter stay in Guilin, you could opt for the following day trip plans to fill your itinerary, or you may want more detailed ideas about How to Plan a Day Trip in Guilin. - Guilin city tour: 1 day for the Elephant Trunk Hill, Reed Flute Cave, and Daxu Ancient Town. Best for snapping the landmarks. - Longji Rice Terraces: 1 day for hiking between the terraces and visiting a local's house. Best for hikers and nature lovers. - Yangshuo Countryside: 1 day for the Li River Cruise and cycling to explore the landscape. Recommended for enjoying natural views leisurely with a little sweat. If you want to soak it all up and fully enjoy the vibrant outdoor activities and the rural vibe, we'd suggest taking it slow and aim for around 5-6 days to enjoy a leisurely stay. • Best for families with children for fun: 5-Day Dynamic Guilin and Yangshuo Tour • Aim for relaxation and slow pace: 6-Day Guilin Relaxation Tour If you have 7 or more days to spend and are looking for a trip combining other cities, there are some popular choices from our clients, such as Hong Kong, Zhangjiajie, Chengdu, and Guizhou. • Hong Kong: 2–3 days to enjoy Hong Kong's theme parks and landmarks. • Zhangjiajie: 3+ days to explore its stunning and surreal landscape. • Chengdu: 2+ days to adore the giant pandas and savor spicy hot pot. • Guizhou: 3+ days to explore diverse minority cultures. Please feel free to reach out to us, and we'd be happy to help create a tour just for you. 4 Days in Guilin: A Classic Highlights Tour Take a Li River Cruise to View the Karst Landscape, Guilin For a trip to Guilin, Guilin's key areas are Guilin city, Yangshuo, and Longsheng. With a 4-day itinerary, it would be a little difficult to conclude all at once, especially when you must consider the arriving and departing times. Since the peaceful countryside and mountains (especially in Yangshuo/Longsheng) are more rewarding than the city, we suggest skipping Guilin city or adding an extra day for a relaxed pace. However, it is possible if you don't mind a rather tight itinerary to include all 3 in 4 days, but it would be tiring and not recommended for most travelers. • Day 1: Guilin arrival. • Day 2: Take a day trip to Longsheng (hiking on terraced fields and experiencing local life). • Day 3: Guilin to Yangshuo (take a Li River Cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo). • Day 4: Travel around Yangshuo, then back to Guilin and departure (drive through the villages and the paddy fields, then take a ferry ride to visit a local town). If your flight arrives early on day 1, feel free to explore the city. You could savor some local street food at Zhengyang Pedestrian Street, and enjoy the night view of the Sun and Moon Pagodas. Embark early for a day trip to the stunning Longji Terraced Fields on your day 2. If you are not fit for walking, you could opt for an electric buggy to appreciate the landscape and get to the top easily. However, the best views come from walking. The hiking route from Ping'an Village to Ancient Zhuang Village is highly recommended, as it is less difficult and scenic. On day 3, you could take a 4-hour Li River Cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo County. This is a must-do as it is the best way to admire the karst landscape without rushing. Typically, we would book you a 4-star cruise with comprehensive facilities and a buffet lunch included. Day 4 would be your departure day, and we don't want to take you on an overly touristy experience. Instead, we suggest exploring the real rural life by hopping on a local farmer's taxi to a farmers market, then driving through the villages where you could see water buffaloes working in the field. Read on to get more details on our 4-Day Guilin Scenic and Ethnic Discovery. 5 Days in Guilin: Enjoy Vibrant Activities Enjoy the Panoramic View from the Top of the Xianggong Mountain, Yangshuo With 5 days in Guilin, you could enjoy a comfortable pace that perfectly balances city sights and countryside adventures. This itinerary is crafted for summer vacations, making it especially ideal for families traveling with children. • Day 1: Guilin arrival. • Day 2: Guilin day tour (explore the Li River Source Grand Canyon in Xing'an County). • Days 3–4: Yangshuo (take a bamboo raft on Li River, climb the Xianggong Mountain, venture around by an ATV, explore an underground cave, and SUP on the Yulong River). • Day 5: Back to Guilin and departure. Guilin and Yangshuo offer the ultimate cool summer escape, with refreshing water activities and shaded countryside adventures ideal for beating the heat. Before coming to Guilin, you may have heard that the Li River is the mother river of the Guilin people. Today, we would like to introduce the source of the Li River - Lijiangyuan Grand Canyon. You could swim in the clear water, enjoy the serene landscapes, and experience the Li River's purity firsthand. Spending 2 days in Yangshuo would be appropriate since it has so much to offer. Younger children enjoy gentle water fun like SUP(stand-up paddleboarding) and bamboo rafting, while older kids tackle rock climbing or ATV adventures.
