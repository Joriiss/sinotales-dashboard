Title: How to Travel from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie?
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/zhangjiajie/from-shanghai.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

The distance from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie measures 1,300 kilometers (or 808 miles). There are both flights and trains going from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie. Clearly the best option for speed and comfort is to take a flight. Traveling from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie by Air There are 3 to 4 direct flights daily, departing either in the early morning around 7 a.m., late evening around 7 p.m., or at night around 8:30 p.m. The flight from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie takes about 2 and a half hours. Flights all depart from Pudong International Airport (T1 or T2). Shanghai Pudong Airport is one hour's drive from the city center. Arrive at the airport 2 hours prior to your departure time. There can be really long check-in and security lines during holidays and weekends. Flights arrive at Zhangjiajie Hehua Airport, which is only 4 km (2 miles) from the city center. However, we don't suggest you stay in Zhangjiajie city unless you want to visit Tianmen Mountain first, as the city is 40 minutes' drive from the Zhangjiajie National Forest Park. We recommend you transfer straight to Wullingyuan Town next to the National Park. There is no direct transportation from the airport to Wulingyuan Town. It departs from Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station at 12:41 and arrives at Zhangjiajie West Railway Station at 21:55, with the journey lasting approximately 9 hours. Upon disembarking, you will likely reach your hotel after 10 pm. We recommend staying in Wullingyuan Town near the Forest Park, a 40-minute drive from Zhangjiajie train station. Alternatively, you may opt to stay in Zhangjiajie City, which is only 10-15 minutes from the train station but quite distant from the National Forest Park, making park visits less convenient, especially if you wish to arrive early to avoid crowds.
