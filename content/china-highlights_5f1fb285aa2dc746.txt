Title: 9-Day Yunnan Highlights with Spring Blossoms Tour

Seeking Yunnan's Floral Paradise Springtime in Yunnan is a nature lover's paradise, boasting mild temperatures, blooming flowers, and fewer crowds. It's the ideal time to explore the scenic landscapes, experience local culture, or relax and enjoy the breathtaking views. This 9-day Yunnan highlights tour takes you to iconic attractions and immerses you in the vibrant cultures of local minorities. Unique experiences include visiting breathtaking flower fields, personally experiencing the fun of making traditional flower cakes, and striking poses in Tibetan Finery. Take advantage of our free itinerary planning service to easily plan your Yunnan trip. Suggested 9-Day Spring Tour Day Highlights 1-2 Kunming Stone Forest with Tram, Yiliang Cherry Blossom Valley 3-4 Lijiang Dayan Town, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, Puji Temple, Lashihai 5-6 Shangri-La Tiger Leaping Gorge, Songzanlin Lamasery, Dress in Tibetan Finery, Dukezong Town 7 Dali Dali Town, Xizhou Village, Flower Cake Making, Erhai 8-9 Kunming Daguan Pavilion, Jinmabiji Archway, Nanping Street Revel in Yunnan's Spring Colors Admire the graceful cherry blossoms, the elegant crabapple blooms, the delicate peach blossoms, and the golden fields of canola in different travel destinations. Experience the Snow Mountain Soar to Glacier Park (4,680 meters) and explore the mesmerizing Blue Moon Valley, surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Strike a Pose in Tibetan Finery Dress in traditional Tibetan attire, immerse yourself in local culture, explore breathtaking scenery, and capture unforgettable moments. Hands-on Flower Cake Baking Learn to make flower cakes in a scenic garden by Erhai Lake, then enjoy your homemade treats with flower tea in the tranquil surroundings. Marvel at the Tiger Leaping Gorge Check out the Jinsha River's raw power in Tiger Leaping Gorge, carving through a narrow valley around a central rock formation beneath towering mountains. Discover Ancient Towns Experience the contrasting beauty of the ancient towns of Dayan, Dukezong, and Dali, each with its unique character and appeal. Explore the Stone Forest Wander through the Stone Forest with a tram and feel as if you've stepped onto another planet, with its otherworldly landscape of towering limestone spires. DAY 1 Kunming Arrival Arrive in Kunming and be greeted by our professional guide and driver, who will escort you to your hotel in a comfortable vehicle. DAY 2 Kunming | Discover the Stone Forest Breakfast & Lunch Explore the Stone Forest, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and be amazed by its breathtaking landscape of towering limestone spires resembling a magical stone forest(Tram included). Lose yourself in the beauty of Cherry Blossom Valley on Yiliang Mountain. This expansive valley, home to over 60 cherry tree varieties, offers a stunning display of blooms. DAY 3 Kunming to Lijiang | Dayan Town Awaits Breakfast & Lunch Transfer to Kunming Railway Station for a bullet train to Lijiang, where a private guide and driver will greet you. Admire the breathtaking crabapple blossoms at Puji Temple. These 200-year-old trees bloom in late spring, creating a picturesque scene. Discover Lijiang Ancient Town (Dayan Town), a UNESCO site inhabited by the Naxi minority. Stroll cobblestone streets lined with traditional houses adorned with China Roses blooming in the town. DAY 4Lijiang | Explore Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Breakfast & Lunch Explore Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, the holy mountain of the Naxi people. Take a cable car to Glacier Park at 4,680 meters, where you'll find stunning scenery of snow-covered peaks. Experience the Blue Moon Valley, a stunning Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Valley featuring an incredibly blue river winding its way through. Wander through the enchanting peach blossom fields of Lashihai, covering over 1100 acres. The trees are adorned with a dazzling array of flowers, resembling a plate overflowing with color. Discover the tranquility of Black Dragon Pool Park, a hidden gem in Lijiang. Stroll through the verdant gardens, marvel at the ancient structures, and find tranquility as you contemplate the majestic reflection of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. DAY 5 Lijiang to Shangri-La | Tiger Leaping Gorge Breakfast & Lunch Enjoy a seamless and private transfer from Lijiang to Shangri-La. Experience the awe-inspiring beauty of Tiger Leaping Gorge, one of the deepest and most spectacular canyons. The mighty Jinsha River rushes through this narrow valley, carving a path around a central rock formation. Following lunch, we'll ascend to Shangri-La, reaching an altitude of 3200 meters. Take some time to rest and adjust to the higher elevation at your hotel. DAY 6 Shangri-La to Dali | Unveil the Hidden Gems Breakfast & Lunch Dress in traditional Tibetan attire with hair-dressing, immerse yourself in local culture, feel the spirituality and capture unforgettable moments. Discover the grandeur of Songzanlin Lamasery, Yunnan's largest Tibetan Buddhist monastery. Explore this architectural masterpiece, filled with Buddhist statues and cultural treasures, providing insight into Tibetan spirituality. Wander through Dukezong Old Town, a vibrant Tibetan community, and immerse yourself in its rich culture. Visit Guishan Park and Moonlight Square, home to the world's largest spinning prayer wheel. Proceed to Shangri-La Railway Station for a bullet train journey to Dali, where your private guide and driver will welcome you. DAY 7 Dali | Experience Authentic Dali Breakfast & Lunch Explore Dali Ancient Town's ancient temples, vibrant markets, and charming neighborhoods to immerse yourself in its rich history and culture. Pause at the charming square and capture the iconic Three Pagodas, a breathtaking sight against the backdrop of the Cangshan Mountains. Experience Bai culture in Xizhou Village by visiting local homes and exploring vast canola flower fields spanning around 500 acres, offering a truly enchanting experience with their sweet fragrance and gentle sway. Learn the art of crafting traditional flower cakes at a local farm nestled near the picturesque Erhai Lake. Savor your handmade, unique cakes with a cup of aromatic flower tea. DAY 8 Dali to Kunming | Discover the Magic of Spring City Breakfast & Lunch Relax and enjoy a scenic bullet train journey to Kunming, where your private guide and driver will ensure a hassle-free arrival. Discover a kaleidoscope of colors at Daguan Pavilion, overlooking the serene Dianchi Lake. Combine history and modernity as you stroll through the ancient Jinmabiji Archway and the bustling Nanping Street. DAY 9 Kunming Departure Breakfast Enjoy some free time to explore Kunming before your private car takes you to the airport for your onward journey.
