Title: We're Nominated for a Virtuoso Travel Award | WildChina Blog

We are thrilled to announce that luxury travel agents from around the world have nominated us for the Destination & Experiences Partner of the Year Award through Virtuoso. For those that don’t know the name, Virtuoso is a leading consortium of the world’s top luxury travel agents. This prestigious award recognizes a company that epitomizes excellence and shines in all areas of the travel planning process and guest experience, and we’ve been selected as one of the top 5 finalists!We’re humbled by the support of Virtuoso’s luxury travel agents and it has been a true pleasure helping their clients to Discover China Differently. With every champagne brunch on the Great Wall or cooking lesson in an ethnic minority home in Yunnan, we’re committed to providing the best experiences that China has to offer and it’s thrilling to be honored for our excellence. In the lead up to Virtuoso Travel Week, a weeklong conference where tour operators and luxury travel agents will descend on Las Vegas to form lasting partnerships, Virtuoso Member Advisors are invited to vote for the most qualified prospect in 16 different categories. Votes must be cast before June 23rd 2017 and we can’t wait to see the result. — Discover why we were nominated as Virtuoso’s Destinations and Experiences Partner of the Year by planning your trip with one of our award-winning travel advisors today.
