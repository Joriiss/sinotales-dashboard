Weather: Jiuzhaigou's April weather is mild, with daytime temperatures mostly around 18–20°C (64–68°F). At the beginning of the month, there may be a few days when it drops to about 12°C (54°F). Mornings, evenings, and rainy times are cooler, at about 6–12°C (43–54°F). April may look rainy on the forecast, but actual rainfall is low (about 6 mm/0.2 in), mostly light showers. And Jiuzhaigou is in the highland area, so the weather changes quickly. Sunshine and showers can easily take turns within a single day, so you don't need to worry too much. Clothing tips: On sunny days, T-shirts, long skirts, trousers, light tops, shawls, or a thin jacket work well. In the evening or when it rains, bring a warm jacket for the chill. Is April a Good Time to Visit Jiuzhaigou? April is Jiuzhaigou's dry season, so some lakes and waterfalls may look less full, and cloudy weather can make the colors a bit duller. Therefore, for scenery, it's indeed not the best time. But this doesn't mean that April is a bad time to go. You'll still find unique landscapes and enjoy more affordable travel costs. In early April, you can still catch the pleasant peach blossoms. The forests also begin to turn green again, much verdant than in winter, and will give you a fresh and warm spring feel. Although some lakes have less water, there are still many lakes that remain crystal and full. Even on rainy days, they don't always turn gray and still look enchanting. While entrance tickets have returned to peak-season rates, hotels and transport are still relatively affordable. Crowds are also lighter, averaging about 10,000–18,000 visitors a day (20,000–40,000 in summer), making your overall experience more quiet and comfortable. When and Where to Catch the Peach Blossoms The peak bloom is usually from March 20 to April 5. From April 5–10, the blossoms gradually start to fade, and after the 10th, they are mostly gone. The peach blossoms are mainly concentrated in Shuzheng Valley, the lower part of Jiuzhaigou's Y-shaped visiting route. In April, the snow-capped mountains are still visible, so you can witness a stunning scene of snow, peach blossoms, and blue lakes in one frame. More specifically, you'll see most peach blossoms along the boardwalk near Tiger Lake, Shuzheng Lakes, Sparkling Lake, and Reed Lake. If you go to Reed Lake, you'll be surrounded by pink peach blossoms both by the water and on the mountains opposite. With colorful prayer flags fluttering on the hillside, you'll find the scene even more breathtaking than any photo can capture. Shuzheng Lakes Peach Blossom Visiting Routes In early April (before 10th), take the lakeside boardwalk to get up close to the peach blossoms at their best. Skip the viewing platform. At this time, the trees aren't very green yet, so the view from above looks bare and less appealing than the blossoms themselves. Peach flowers along the lakeside By late April, there are no peach flowers, but the valley trees are mostly green. During this time, I recommend you to take the upper viewing platform route, which lets you look down on the entire Shuzheng Lakes area and enjoy the lush greenery intertwined with the lakes. What to See if I Miss the Peach Blossom Season? If you come after the peach blossom season, don't worry. Jiuzhaigou's gradually greening forests are beautiful too. You can focus on visiting Arrow Bamboo Lake, Panda Lake, Five-Flower Lake, Mirror Lake, Rhinoceros Lake, Tiger Lake, and Shuzheng Lakes, all with plenty of water and beautiful scenery to enjoy. Five-Flower Lake, I captured. Which Lakes are Less Beautiful during the Dry Season? Long Lake is beautiful when it starts to thaw in early April. However, if you visit after the 20th, the water level gradually drops, and the view isn't as impressive as in early April. While lakes like Five-Colored Pool, Sparkling Lake, and Pearl Shoal have lower water levels during this season. Most waterfalls in Jiuzhaigou are also less spectacular at this time, such as the Arrow Bamboo Lake Waterfall, Pearl Shoal Waterfall, Shuzheng Waterfall, and Nuorilang Waterfall. The section from Primeval Forest and Grass Lake to Swan Lake reopens in April, but about half of the forest, grass, and water scenery is still dry. However, the snow-capped mountains are spectacular. If you love snowy peaks, it's still worth a visit. You can skip the left visiting route of Jiuzhaigou, which only includes Long Lake and Five-Colored Pool. The scenery along this route isn't very impressive in this season, too, so if you're short on time, you might consider skipping it. However, you can head to the nearby Huanglong, which is higher in elevation and colder, so snowfall is still possible even in late April. However, snow in Huanglong also melts quickly at this time, so you'll only see snowy scenery on the day it actually snows, and it depends a lot on your luck. You can keep an eye on the weather forecast. Snowfall is more likely when there's a sharp drop in temperature, so you can try your luck to see the snow at that time. April is also the dry season in Huanglong. Except for the Five-Color Pond at the top, most other spots have little to no water. Hiking down won't give you much scenery and can be tiring, so it's best to take the cable car and sightseeing bus for the round trip, and just enjoying the Five-Color Pond is enough.
