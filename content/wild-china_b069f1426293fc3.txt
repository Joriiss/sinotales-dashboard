Title: WildChina's Jia Liming to speak at Mekong Tourism Forum 2010
URL: https://wildchina.com/2010/05/speak-at-mekong-tourism-forum-2010/
Source: Wild China
Date: 2010-05-04T16:05:34+08:00
Tags: WildChina Experts, wild China, WildChina, WildChina Travel, jia liming, Travel in China, sustainable tourism China
================================================================================

WildChina’s Jia Liming, Director of Operations and Business Development, will give a talk on innovative tourism at the Mekong Tourism Forum 2010, held at Siem Reap, Cambodia’s Angkor Century Resort and Spa from May 7 to 8, 2010. Focusing on WildChina as a case study, Jia’s lecture is part of the panel discussion “Pioneering Mekong Tourism Products,” on which regional experts discuss innovative tourism experiences in Asia. Jia speaks between 9:15 and 10:45 am on Saturday, May 8, 2010. ———- Photo credit: ScottAsia More details on Jia’s talk and the event can be found on the Mekong Tourism Forum 2010 website.
