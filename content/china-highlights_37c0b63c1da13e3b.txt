Title: Gansu Tianshui Top Attractions

Tianshui has numerous historic sites, and picturesque landscapes of green mountains and clean rivers, which are rare in Gansu Province. Tianshui is the only way to pass along the ancient Silk Road, which leads to precious historic sites to the city, such as Mount Maiji Grottoes, Fuxi Temple, and Daxiangshan Grottoes. Wushan Water Curtain Caves is the best destination for appreciating natural landscapes. If you intend to travel to Tianshui or Gansu, have us tailor-make your own Tianshui tour by contacting us. Most Popular Tianshui Attractions Mount Maiji Grottoes Maiji Mountain is 150 meters tall, and it was named for its shape - like that of a pile of wheat. According to historical records, the Maiji Grottoes were excavated during the Hou Qin Dynasty of the Sixteen K..
