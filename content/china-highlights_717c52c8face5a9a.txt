Hangzhou, capital of Zhejiang Province and Song Dynasty capital, is known as 'Heaven on Earth', with its stunning natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. It was described by Marco Polo as "the finest and most splendid city in the world". Hangzhou is the political, economic, cultural, educational, transportation, and financial center of Zhejiang Province. The 2022 Asian Games was held in Hangzhou. Where Hangzhou Is Located Hangzhou is on the east coast of China, south of the Yangtze River Delta and about 170 kilometers (105 miles) south of Shanghai. By bullet train, Hangzhou is about 1 hour from Shanghai and 1½ hours from Suzhou. The Features of Hangzhou • Hangzhou is one of the Seven Ancient Capital Cities, with a history of about 2,500 years. Take a cruise to explore the World Heritage Site - the Grand Canal. • Hangzhou boasts enchanting natural scenery. West Lake, the symbol of Hangzhou, is considered to be one of the most beautiful sights in China. • Hangzhou offers a variety of traditional cultures, such as tea, traditional Chinese medicine, and Chinese silk. • Hangzhou has a highly developed transportation network. It is almost the only city in China following 'pedestrian first' laws. Check our 4-Day Shanghai, Hangzhou, and Nanxun Water Town Culture Tour for inspiration. Hangzhou Is a Leisurely City Hangzhou is a special city. It is developing at a high speed, while the lifestyle in Hangzhou is leisurely. Compared to Shanghai, the pace of life there is slow. People enjoy a leisurely life in Hangzhou. When traveling in Hangzhou, you can have a stroll by West Lake, take a boat cruise, feed the goldfish in the lake, and enjoy the beautiful scenery. Then have a delicious afternoon tea by the lake and chat with your guide, learning about the story and history of Hangzhou. If you want to feel the classical charm of Hangzhou, you can pay a visit to some water towns. There are old buildings, boats, rivers, ancient bridges, and wells. Everything there is peaceful and beautiful. If you want to know more about the tea culture of Hangzhou, you can go to Meijiawu Tea Plantation for an idyllic tour. Pick tea leaves with a local tea farmer, watch a Chinese tea ceremony, and learn about Chinese tea culture. Hangzhou Versus Shanghai Shanghai is 1 hour away from Hangzhou. Hangzhou is a leisurely city with beautiful scenery while Shanghai is an international city with a faster pace of life. Both Hangzhou and Shanghai have convenient transportation. If your itinerary is flexible, Hangzhou is worth a one-day tour to appreciate its beautiful scenery, wander around West Lake, and visit a Longjing tea plantation. Shanghai is a modern city in which you can see many skyscrapers and historical buildings. The most popular and convenient way to get from Hangzhou to Shanghai is traveling by bullet train. Enjoy a relaxing day in Hangzhou, take a bullet train to Shanghai, and explore the modern city there. Water Towns Near Hangzhou Wuzhen and Nanxun are the two most popular water towns near Hangzhou. Wuzhen is one of the top six water towns in China. Taking a gondola along the ancient canal should not be missed and the nighttime scenery in Wuzhen is impressive. Nanxun is not as famous as Wuzhen. The Grand Canal - World Heritage Site The Grand Canal was dug out in 486 BC and has a history of more than 2,500 years. It runs from Beijing in the north to Hangzhou in the south, stretching for 1,747 kilometers. It played a great role in the economic and cultural development, and with exchanges between the north and south of China. It was a great project created by the ancient Chinese and is now one of the symbols of Chinese culture. Hangzhou's unique Dragon Well tea (Longjing Tea) plays an important role among the varieties of Chinese green tea. It is produced mostly by hand, and is honored as China's Famous Tea due to its high quality. Hangzhou is regarded as the cradle of the Chinese silk culture and production. With a history of over 2,500 years, Hangzhou silk is renowned for its gentle texture, gorgeous colors, and wide varieties. In spring, West Lake is quite beautiful with peach blossoms and weeping willow catkins. It is very hot in summer and cold in winter. Hangzhou is always crowded with Chinese people at the weekends and Chinese public holidays, such as Labor Day (May 1st to 7th) and National Day (October 1st to 7th).
