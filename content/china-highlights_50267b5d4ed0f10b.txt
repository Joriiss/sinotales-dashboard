Visa-Free Cities under the 30-Day Visa-Free Transit Policy Residents from multiple European countries (such as Italy, France, and Spain) and Latin Latin America countries (Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Uruguay), as well as citizens of Singapore, New Zealand, Australia, and a few other designated countries, are now eligible for a 30-day visa-free entry to all of China's cities.
