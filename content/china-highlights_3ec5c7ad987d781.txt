The Huanghuacheng Great Wall, 75 km (47 mi) north of Central Beijing, is the only watery Great Wall in Beijing, combining mountains, lakes, and ancient Great Wall of legendary solidity. It is the latest section to become popular in hiking and camping for its seep and out of repair paths and the perfect blend of lake scenery. • Chinese name: 黄花城 Huánghuāchéng /hwung-hwaa-chnng/ 'Yellow Flower City' • Location: Jiuduhe Town, Huairou District of Beijing City; 1½ hours' drive from Beijing • Restoration: Most parts have been restored, with a few wild parts. • Length: 13 km (8 mi) • Activities: Hiking, camping, and boating • Opening hours: 8 am–5 pm (weekdays), 7:30 am–5:30 pm (weekends) • Features: a wild original section, a restored section mainly made of big stone slabs, three sections immersed in water Huanghuacheng's History: When and Why Built It? The construction of the Huanghuacheng Great Wall lasted 188 years, from 1404 to 1592. It was built to protect emperors - Alive and Dead! To enhance the defense of the northern border, the emperors of the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644) built an outer Great Wall and an inner Great Wall. The Huanghuacheng section is part of the inner Great Wall that connects the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall in the east, via Jiankou, and Juyong Pass in the west. Also to protect the mausoleum: After Emperor Yongle began his reign in the capital Beijing, he built a mausoleum at Tianshou Hill, south of Huanghua Town. Therefore, Huanghuacheng's Great Wall is not only to defend Beijing but also a wall to protect the mausoleum (which later became the Ming Tombs). It is "the essence of the Ming Dynasty Great Wall". Huanghuacheng section is an uncrowded section, less traveled but with more-varied scenery/activities. You can take a boat on the lake to appreciate the Great Wall on the surrounding mountains or hike around the lake and across the dam instead of climbing the mountains. Or camp on the Wall to appreciate the beautiful view of the sunrise and sunset. Beautiful Lakeside Scenery The Great Wall at Huanghuacheng is surrounded by mountains with verdant vegetation, lots of flowers, and other seasonal beauty. During summer the countryside around Huanghuacheng's Great Wall is swathed in yellow, as masses of wildflowers come into bloom. The Huanghuacheng wall is divided by reservoirs, and some parts are even immersed in the water, which is a very unique characteristic, distinguishing it from other sections of the Great Wall. Original Great Wall, Steep Climbs, Great Views This section's wild wall is pristine and hasn't been repaired like the Badaling Section. Its stone rebuilt section is not as magnificent as the restored Mutianyu Section, but it retains its original composition from when it was built. Accommodation and Restaurants at Huanghuacheng Since no hotels and restaurants are allowed to be built in the catchment, the nearest restaurants are small local ones in the valley bottom, in villages just outside the tourist area. The nearest hotels are in Changping or half the way from Huairou. Recommended Great Wall Tours: • 2-Day Great Wall Mutianyu-Simatai Day-Night Tour • 1-Day Jiankou to Mutianyu Great Wall Hiking Tour The Story of Huanghuacheng - "Metal Soup Great Wall" Chinese: Jintang Changcheng (金汤长城 /jin-taang chaang-chnng/ 'metal soup Great Wall', meaning firm and solid Great Wall). A general named Cai Kai, who led the construction of the Huanghuacheng Great Wall, was beheaded after the completion of his task because the emperor mistakenly believed other ministers' lies that his job was sloppy. But when a survey report, stating that the walls were solid, steep, and exemplified the finest workmanship, reached the emperor, the emperor realized that he had misjudged General Cai Kai. The emperor ordered a tomb and memorial stele to be erected in honor of the loyal general. He also had the characters 金汤 inscribed on the face of a huge rock below the wall.
