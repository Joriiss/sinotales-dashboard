Harbin and Yabuli Family Relaxation There are many reasons why families, especially those with children, should visit Harbin with us: watch a beluga whale show and explore a thrilling tiger park, marvel at stunning snow and ice sculptures, enjoy a variety of winter fun activities, warm up with a unique ice cream experience (can you believe that?), ski at one of China's top ski resorts, and unwind on a relaxing winter holiday at Club Med Yabuli. Our customers who have visited Harbin tell us that they've never been so cold, yet they've never been so amazed. The temperatures around -25°C (-13°F) can be quite challenging, but the experiences at the Harbin Ice and Snow Festival are absolutely worth it! As experts in tailor-made travel, we are ready to create wonderful winter memories for you and your family. Indulge in Winter Fun in a World of Ice and Snow Walk into the colorful wonderland of Harbin Ice and Snow World and the gorgeous sculpture spectacle of Sun Island, and enjoy many kinds of snow entertainment, such as thrilling slides and ice bikes. Say Hello to Adorable Arctic Friends Don't miss the "Heart of the Ocean" underwater show at Harbin Polarland, a stunning performance by beluga whales and their trainers, perfected over many years. Walk Back in Time to Harbin's Beginnings Take a historical walk on Central Street, appreciate the ancient buildings with Western facades, hear the stories of old Harbin, and taste the lovely local desserts. Explore Snow-covered Harbin's Forbidden City Visit the Harbin Confucian Temple, a magnificent Qing Dynasty complex built to royal standards. Here, people come to pray to Confucius, China's "First Teacher," for academic success and school admission. Have a Close Encounter with the Endangered Siberian Tigers Ride through Siberian Tiger Park in a bus protected by a security cage to see the purebred Siberian tigers roaming freely up close. You can even purchase meat or live poultry at extra cost to feed them. Enjoy an All-Inclusive Relaxing Winter Holiday at Club Med Yabuli Club Med Yabuli offers gourmet full board, a very comfortable stay, and a wide range of sports and leisure activities. Skiing and Snowboarding Freely with Professional Guidance Enjoy direct access to the thrilling ski champion slopes with professional guidance from ski instructors trained by a French ski school. The daily free skiing/snowboarding lessons are also suitable for kids and children. Excellent Children's Care, Clubs, and Services Club Med Yabuli has three children's clubs catering to children from 2 to 17 years old. The kids' restaurant takes care of kids from 2 to 10 years old. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a special winter tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Harbin Arrival Your private guide is waiting at arrivals. A comfortable private car (non-smoking) and an experienced driver will transfer you to the hotel. DAY 2 Harbin | Harbin Polarland and History Welcome to Harbin Polarland! Here, you'll find beluga whales, polar bears, seals, and even penguins. Don't miss the famous beluga whale show, which has been recognized as one of the "Top 50 Historical Moments in the World." For a moment of quiet reflection, visit the Harbin Confucian Temple, a place dedicated to China's "First Teacher", Confucius. Many people come here to pray for good luck on their exams and academic success. In winter, the red walls against the white snow create a truly beautiful scene. Explore Jile Temple, one of the most famous Buddhist temples in Northeast China, with its impressive halls and peaceful gardens. Take pictures at the castle-like St. Sophia Cathedral, then take a stroll down Central Street. This historic street is the perfect place to try local treats like red sausage and MaDieer ice cream while you browse the shops. Finally, be sure to see the incredible Ice Lantern Festival at Zhaolin Park. Every winter, artists create hundreds of dazzling ice sculptures that light up the night. DAY 3 Harbin | Full-Day Harbin Ice and Snow Festival Tour In the morning, head to Siberian Tiger Park to experience Siberian tigers up close. Let your guide know if you would like to pay the extra cost to feed them. In the afternoon, enter the fairytale kingdom of ice and snow at Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival's two major venues: Sun Island and Ice and Snow World. Walk through the white snow world of Sun Island to see thousands of snow sculptures made by artists from around China and the world. Enter the Ice and Snow World at around 3pm to see both the daytime and night views. Have fun with snow wonderland activities and immerse yourself in the colorful world of huge ice creations. DAY 4 Harbin to Yabuli | Private Vehicle to Club Med Yabuli Pick-up from your Harbin hotel for a 2.5-hour private drive to Club Med Yabuli. After check-in, begin your relaxing winter holiday. Parties are held every night at the resort, or you can enjoy some quiet time at other bars. In our all-inclusive package, you can enjoy: Childcare and children's club facilities Winter sports: ski pass and skiing/snowboarding lessons Indoor leisure activities: Table tennis, Dance lessons, Indoor pool, Lounge， festive evening entertainments, Karaoke, Card games, Mahmah-Jjong... Bars and restaurants: Full board throughout the stay, including wine, beer, and or soft drinks with lunch and dinner DAY 5 Yabuli Club Med Skiing Vacation Your skiing vacation is all according to your arrangements. All meals are offered with generous buffets, or you can order à la carte. Attend skiing classes and polish your skiing skills on some of the best ski trails in China. Besides skiing, the resort offers various choices for your leisure time. There are indoor swimming pools, sauna rooms, jacuzzies, gyms, dancing classes, yoga rooms, and more. Try some totally new experiences such as the flying trapeze. In the evening, treat yourself to a soothing spa (at extra cost). DAY 6 Yabuli Club Med Skiing Vacation Continue to freely choose how you spend your time amongst the huge range of leisure options. You could have one more day of skiing/snowboarding or enjoy the indoor sports and activities. DAY 7 Yabuli to Harbin | Private Airport Transfer Check out from Club Med.
