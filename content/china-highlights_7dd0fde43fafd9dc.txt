Built during the Tang and Song dynasties, the Dazu Grottoes are the largest grotto complex in southern China. Just 100 kilometers from downtown Chongqing, it's about a 2-hour drive - perfect for a day trip! What makes the Dazu carvings so special? They blend Buddhist, Taoist, and Confucian ideas, showing how the local people embraced different cultures during the Tang and Song periods. Unlike other grottoes that feel all about religion, these carvings have a warm, personal touch. You'll see heartwarming sculptures of parents raising their kids and vivid depictions of life's ups and downs. In addition, the Dazu Grottoes are featured in the game "Black Myth: Wukong!" Many gamers are flocking here to see the stunning images and spots brought to life in the game. Whether you're a history buff, an art lover, or a gamer, the Dazu Grottoes promise an unforgettable experience. This article will give you all the info you need - transport options, ticket prices, routes, and tips - to make your visit smooth and exciting! If you have limited time and can only choose one to visit, Baoding Mountain is undoubtedly the recommended option. • Baoding Mountain (2-3 hours): This is the core area of the Dazu Grottoes. Rock carvings at Baoding Mountain • Beishan Mountain (1-1.5 hours): Known for its small and exquisite carvings, Beishan attracts fewer visitors. Is Dazu Grottoes Suitable for Wheelchair Visitors? Dazu Grottoes are not fully wheelchair accessible. With proper planning and necessary assistance, wheelchair visitors can still appreciate the unique charm of this cultural treasure. If your group includes wheelchair users, I recommend choosing Baoding Mountain. Some areas here have flat pathways and ramps, and the route from the Baoding Mountain parking lot to the shuttle bus transfer point is accessible. The shuttle bus can take you directly to the entrance of the Baoding Mountain. However, to see all the carvings within the site, you will need to navigate around 100 steps. Baoding Mountain has flat land The visitor center and main entrance area are equipped with accessible pathways, including accessible restrooms and parking. If possible, consider bringing a lightweight all-terrain wheelchair to handle more complex terrains. Many other areas, especially Beishan, have steep slopes, steps, and uneven paths, making it difficult for wheelchair users to navigate. The Best Time to Visit Dazu Grottoes Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the best times to visit. • Spring: The temperatures are moderate, flowers are in bloom, and the air is fresh, making it suitable for outdoor activities. Spring is also the rainy season in Chongqing, so it's advisable to bring rain gear and wear shoes with good grip. • Autumn: With clear skies and mild sunshine, it is an ideal season for photography and hiking. The lighting in autumn is great for photos, especially in the afternoon when the sunlight casts a warm glow. Autumn light makes the colors more clear Summer (July to August) and the National Day Golden Week (October 1-7) are peak periods, with a high volume of visitors. It is advisable to avoid these times for a better experience. • Summer: There are many students and family tourists, leading to crowded conditions, and the heat makes it an extremely undesirable time to visit. • National Day Golden Week: Visitor numbers peak, and the scenic area may become overcrowded. Although the Dazu Grottoes generally see fewer crowds compared to other tourist spots in Chongqing, such as Wulong, it is still wise to follow these tips for a more enjoyable visit during peak seasons: • Visit on weekdays to avoid larger crowds. • Arrive before 10 AM to avoid peak tourist times. • Consider visiting in reverse order, starting with Beishan and then heading to Baoding Mountain to avoid the mainstream route. • Book tickets online in advance to avoid waiting in line on-site.
