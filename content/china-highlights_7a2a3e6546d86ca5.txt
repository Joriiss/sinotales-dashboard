Other Trains Between Hong Kong and Xi'an Besides high-speed trains, you can also choose overnight sleeper trains for a transfer. This will save you a hotel bill and save the time of the day for sightseeing. • Guangzhou to Xi'an: overnight sleeper trains from Guangzhou to Xi'an take 22-30 hours. They depart from Guangzhou Railway Station/Guangzhou North, which is not the same station for high-speed trains, and arrive at the centrally located Xi'an Railway Station. This option includes a transfer from Guangzhou South to Guangzhou Station by Metro Line 2 or to Guangzhou North by Metro Line 2, and then change to Metro Line 3 and finally take Metro Line 9. • Changsha to Xi'an: overnight trains from Changsha to Xi'an take 14-20 hours. They also depart from/arrive at the centrally located stations of Changsha and Xi'an. You need to transfer from Changsha South to Changsha Station to take an overnight train. High-Speed Train Vs Flight To travel such a long distance between Hong Kong and Xi'an, taking a flight is the fastest and most convenient way. The direct flight from Hong Kong to Xi'an and vice versa takes only 3 hours. Airfare is US$200-480. Traveling from Hong Kong to Xi'an and vice versa by high-speed train cost US$150-170 for second class. High-speed train between Hong Kong and Guangzhou/Changsha + overnight train between Guangzhou/Changsha and Xi'an costs US$130-140 for second class and soft sleeper. • High-speed trains to and from Hong Kong • Xi'an-Beijing high-speed train • Xi'an-Pingyao train
