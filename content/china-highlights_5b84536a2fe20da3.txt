Title: 6-Day Chengdu and Xiling Snow Mountain Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chengdutour/cd-5/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

This 6-day itinerary is perfect for families looking to spend their winter holidays skiing on snow-covered mountains and unwinding in soothing hot springs. Venture to Xiling Snow Mountain, a premier ski destination best visited from mid-December to early March, when the snow conditions are optimal. Here, you'll engage in a variety of snow activities, ski down picturesque slopes, and marvel at the captivating snowy landscape, all in temperatures comfortably above -6 degrees Celsius. After two days on the slopes, you can relax in outdoor hot springs. Additionally, you'll get the chance to meet adorable pandas and savor the warmth of a Sichuan hot pot in the city. As an expert in customized trips, we are thrilled to help you plan your winter journey in Chengdu. Kick-Off Your Two-Day Skiing or Snowboarding Adventure Dive into the exhilarating thrill of skiing amidst the stunning snowy landscapes at Xiling Snow Mountain Ski Resort. As the most renowned ski resort in southern China, it promises fun for all, from beginners to seasoned pros. Engage in Exciting Snow Activities with Your Family Participate in a variety of snow activities with your family and friends. From heart-racing snowmobiling to kid-friendly activities, there's something for everyone to enjoy. Luxuriate in the Hot Baths at a Premier Hot Spring Resort After a day on the slopes, indulge in the outdoor hot springs at one of the top local hot spring hotels. Enjoy a good soak and let the warm waters soothe your tired muscles. Warm Up with a Steaming Hot Pot in Winter Challenge your taste buds with a spicy hot pot or savour a clear soup with a fresh flavor derived from meat bones at a popular local Sichuan hot pot restaurant. Say Hello to Adorable Pandas Visit the panda base in the less-crowded winter season. During this time, all the pandas are in their outdoor enclosures all day, playing, eating bamboo, and sleeping. Take a Stroll in Kuanzhai Alley Experience the charm of ancient alleys, sample a variety of local snacks, and pick up some gifts in souvenir stores. Day 1Chengdu Arrival Welcome to Winter Wonderland Chengdu! Your personal guide and driver will greet you at the airport and accompany you directly to your hotel in Chengdu. Day 2Chengdu to Xiling Snow Mountain | One Day Ski Experience Depending on the weather, embark on a 2-3 hour journey from your hotel in Chengdu to Xiling Snow Mountain. Once there, a cable car and shuttle bus will whisk you up to the ski resort situated at an altitude of 2,200 meters, offering spectacular views of the snow-covered landscape. Check in at the hotel located near the ski area and get ready to start your skiing adventure! Known as the 'Oriental Alps,' Xiling Snow Mountain boasts the largest natural ski resort in South China. The resort caters to all skill levels with its primary and intermediate ski runs, ensuring a fun-filled experience for everyone. If skiing isn't your preference, the resort also offers many exciting snow activities, such as snowmobiling, snow rafting, and dog sledding. Note: As each guest's preferences differ, you'll pay directly at the resort for your chosen skiing or snow activities. Day 3 Xiling Snow Mountain | More Skiing and Soothing Hot Springs Dedicate another day to mastering the ski slopes, indulging in entertainment facilities, and basking in the mesmerizing snowscape of the mountain. In the afternoon, take the shuttle bus and cable car back to the resort entrance. From there, your private car will whisk you away to the Hot Spring Resort nestled at the foot of the mountain. Tonight, you'll be staying at one of the premier hot spring hotels, renowned for its therapeutic outdoor natural sulfur springs. Feel free to soak in the hot springs, providing the perfect remedy for your tired muscles after two days of skiing. Day 4 Xiling Snow Mountain to Chengdu | Anren Ancient Town Start your day with a relaxing hot spring dip before a one-hour drive to Anren Ancient Town. This 1380-year-old town blends history and tranquility. Enjoy the sight of 27 Chinese-Western fusion mansions from the Republic of China era. Don't miss the photo-worthy indigo-dyed ribbons hanging above the streets and local dishes like fried tofu, sesame oil duck, and sweet fermented rice. After your exploration, we will transport you back to your hotel in the city. Day 5 Chengdu | Panda and Hot Pot Start your day with a visit to the Chengdu Panda Base. In winter, all the pandas will stay in the outdoor enclosures, allowing you to observe them without the barrier of indoor glass walls. For lunch, indulge in the ancient Sichuan tradition of hot pot—a culinary delight no one can resist in the chilly winter months! Choose spicy or clear broth, then savour ingredients like beef slices, tofu, and wide rice noodles. Adventurous eaters might even try special items like duck feet and omasum. After your meal, take a stroll through the historic Kuanzhai Alley. Pause for tea in a traditional teahouse or a coffee in a charming café. The area's handicraft shops and souvenir stores are well worth exploring. Day 6 Chengdu Departure After breakfast, your guide and driver will ensure you reach Tianfu Airport safely.
