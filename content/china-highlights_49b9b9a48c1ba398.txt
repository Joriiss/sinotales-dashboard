Try the Drone Delivery Service on the Badaling Great Wall The Great Wall is a must-visit for travelers in Beijing. As a local, I recommend trying the drone delivery service at the Badaling section. Imagine exploring the Great Wall without the hassle of carrying heavy loads. Thanks to Meituan's drone delivery, a 50-minute walk is now reduced to a quick 5-minute wait. Simply scan the QR codes for Meituan's WeChat at the South Eight Tower and South Nine Tower, place your order, and in 5 to 15 minutes, your items will be delivered right to the South Nine Tower. Enjoy your visit without the extra weight! 2. Experience Driverless Taxis at Shougang Park Blast furnace in Shougang Park Shougang Park, located 20 kilometers west of Tiananmen Square, is a blend of high-tech and cultural tourism in modern Beijing, and driverless cars are highly recommended. Baidu Apollo Park offers driverless taxis. Scan the Robotgo QR code at the station, enter your destination, and when the car arrives, input the last four digits of your phone number. The rear door will automatically open for two passengers. Experience the convenience and innovation of this unique ride! If you want to have this interesting experience, feel free to contact us. Shougang Park was originally the Capital Steel Plant of Beijing. To protect the environment and prepare for the 2022 Winter Olympics, the plant ceased operations in 2010. The site has been transformed into an industrial heritage museum and a hub for sports, culture, and technology, featuring venues for winter sports, a hotel, an e-sports VR center, and more. So Real Science Fiction Park in Shougang Park 3. Dine at SPACELAB in Hopson One SPACELAB offers a dining adventure with food delivered by flying saucer-shaped robots. Kids will love the spectacle, and adults will appreciate the creativity. 4. Visit "China's Silicon Valley" to Experience Cutting-edge Technology Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park Beijing's Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park, known as "China's Silicon Valley," offers a unique opportunity to witness China's technological revolution. Whether you're interested in software development, AI, biotechnology, or design, there's something for everyone. • Zhongguancun Software Park: Home to numerous tech giants and startups, offering a glimpse into the future of technology. You can take guided tours of renowned company campuses like Baidu and Lenovo, attend tech seminars, or participate in hackathons and innovation competitions. • Zhongguancun Frontier Technology Innovation Center: Showcases breakthroughs in AI, biotechnology, and more. Engage with interactive exhibits, attend workshops led by industry experts, or try programming a robot through hands-on activities. • Z-Park (Zhongguancun Venture Park): An incubator for startups and a hub for entrepreneurial activity. • Science and Technology Innovation Outcomes Exhibition Hall: Displays the tangible results of scientific research and technological innovation. 5. Visiting Huawei's Smart Home Experience Center It offers a glimpse into the future of living. You'll discover how centralized control systems manage all your devices seamlessly. The intelligent lighting solutions allow easy adjustments to brightness and color, enhancing your home's ambiance. Advanced security features, including smart locks and cameras, work together to keep your home safe. Connected appliances like air conditioners and refrigerators can be controlled remotely, showcasing the convenience of automation. Environmental controls, such as automated curtains and air purifiers, ensure a comfortable living space. Interacting with Huawei's voice assistant highlights the simplicity of managing your smart home with voice commands, making this an engaging and futuristic experience. 6. Visit Yizhuang Economic and Technological Development Zone Many high-tech companies in Yizhuang offer public tours. Participate in scientific experiments and explore how technology has transformed daily life. 8.
