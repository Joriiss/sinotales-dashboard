Nature Beauty of China This 14-day tour is an idea choice for nature lovers and adventure seekers. It begins in vibrant Hong Kong, then moves on to the picturesque landscapes of Guilin with Li River cruise and terrace field hiking. Explore the surreal beauty of Zhangjiajie's National Forest Park and Tianmen Mountain, and immerse yourself in the ancient history of Xi'an and Beijing. The itinerary below is only one possibility. Tips to tailor-make your trip are offered as inspiration. Send us details of your preferences and our travel experts will customize this trip to suit your interests, tastes, and budget. Discover the Art of Crafting Bamboo-tube Rice You will visit a local minority family and and learn how to make bamboo-tube rice as well as cook it the local way. Chat with the family and walk around their traditional wooden house. Cycling the Rural Landscapes of Yangshuo Explore the winding paths and hidden trails. The stunning karst mountains, lush rice paddies, and picturesque villages are along the way. Witness the Incredible Floating Pillars at Avatar Mountain Walking among these floating pillars amidst lush greenery and stunning cliffs feels like stepping into another world. Enjoy Tianmen Mountain From a Different Angle There is a beautiful reservoir at the foot of Tianmen Mountain, enjoy the natural Tianmen cave and its reflection on the surface. Unveil the Secrets of the Terracotta Warriors With Making Clay Warriors Your guide will explain the secrets hidden behind the well-known museum to you and then accompany you to a local cave dwelling where a craftsman makes warriors in the traditional way. Bike on the City Wall Our experienced tour guides will lead you to appreciate the most essential parts, with vivid and interesting explanations that help you understand the history and culture behind the Terracotta Warriors. Hike the Most Picturesque Section of the Great Wall Mutianyu section is known for its stunning views, surrounded by lush greenery and rolling hills. The wall itself has been well preserved, offering a mix of original bricks and renovated sections. Learn About Chinese Emperors by Discovering the Hidden Hall Deviate from the conventional tourist path at the Forbidden City. Engage in absorbing narratives of iconic figures that remain untold by standard guides. Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful private China tour for you. Day 1Arrive in Hong Kong After arrival at Hong Kong airport, your guide will meet you to check-in at your hotel and have some time on your own to relax or explore near your hotel. Day 2Hong Kong | Free and Leisture Breakfast The public transportation system in Hong Kong is very well-developed, and English is widely spoken. It is easy to discover the city on your own. Hong Kong is a vibrant city with a lot to offer, from its bustling markets and lively neighborhoods to its beautiful hiking trails and scenic views. Day 3Hong Kong to Guilin | Bullet Train Breakfast Take a direct Bullet train from Hong Kong to Guilin. It is advisable to rise early to allow sufficient time for customs procedures at the train station. Day 4 Guilin | Terrace Field and Make Bamboo-tube Rice Breakfast & Lunch Longji is about a two-hour drive from Guilin. Today, we head to Ping'an Zhuang Village. During the hike, you may see farmers tending their crops or maintaining the earthen structures. Your guide points out many of the terraces' features, from the complicated irrigation system to the engineering that went into creating them more than 600 years ago. Down from the field, you will visit a local minority family and learn how to make bamboo-tube rice and cook it the local way. Chat with the family, walk around their traditional wooden house, and learn about mountain ethnic people's lives. Day 5Guilin to Yangshuo | Li River Cruise Breakfast & Lunch Take a morning cruise along the Li River from Guilin to Yangshuo. Enjoy breathtaking limestone karst landscapes, lush greenery, and picturesque countryside during the cruise. Spend your afternoon at the West Street. West Street is a vibrant and bustling pedestrian street, known for its lively atmosphere, diverse dining options, shops, and entertainment venues. Day 6Yangshuo to Zhangjiajie | Countryside Biking Breakfast & Lunch Bike around the countryside in the morning. Yangshuo's countryside is known for its idyllic scenery, and oddly-shaped hills thrusting to the sky. The scenery is breathtakingly beautiful, with karst peaks, rice fields, clear waters, and green bamboo. Transfer to Guilin to get the train to Zhangjiajie. Day 7Zhangjiajie | National Forest Park (Avatar Mountain) Breakfast & Lunch Spend a full day in the National Forest Park today. Take a cable up from the gate to Tianzi Mountain directly. Tianzi Mountain is famous for its tall quartz sandstone pillars, dense forests, diverse flora and fauna, picturesque landscapes, a mesmerizing cloud sea, and iconic sites such as Yubifeng and Tianzige. Then go to Yuanjiajie by shuttle bus. This area is famous and popular for its Hallelujah Mountain, named after Avatar's floating rocks. Pictures of "First Bridge in the World" from the "Viewing Bridge Platform" (Wangqiaotai) are a bonus. Take the Bailong Sightseeing Elevator to get down. Day 8Zhangjiajie | Grand Canyon Glass Bridge and Baofeng Lake Breakfast & Lunch Challenge Yourself on the Glass Bridge at Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon. It is the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge, which is 300 meters above ground level. Explore the various hiking trails in the canyon to witness the stunning landscapes and unique rock formations up close. You'll enjoy a 30-minute ride in a wooden boat at Baofeng Lake. It is a leisurely experience as you cruise on the clear water, view hills of various shapes, and enjoy listening to the local minority people singing their traditional songs. Day 9Zhangjiajie to Xi'an | Tianmen Mountain Breakfast Tianmen Mountain is regarded as the 'soul' of Zhangjiajie and is its highest mountain. The cable car ride takes 30 minutes from the city center to the mountaintop and it is the longest cableway in the world. Tianmen Cave (meaning 'Heaven's Gate') is a 30-meter-high hole that was formed naturally in the mountain and looks like a doorway. It is well-known for being glided through by world-class stuntmen in wingsuits. To reach the hole, you can climb for about half an hour up the 'Stairway to Heaven'. After the trip, we will transfer you to the airport to catch your flight to Xi'an. Day 10Xi'an | The Terracotta Warriors and City Wall Breakfast & Lunch Start your day by visiting the extraordinary Terracotta Army, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most significant archaeological discoveries in the world. Your guide would explain how and why the warriors and horses were created. After this, your family would learn to make your clay warriors with expert local artisans at a local cave dwelling. Explore Xi'an's well-preserved city wall, which offers panoramic views of the city and is perfect for a leisurely bike ride. Day 11Xi'an to Beijing | Small Wild Goose Pagoda and Xi'an Museum Breakfast & Lunch The Small Wild Goose Pagoda, a stunning multi-eave, brick structure with fifteen stories, features black stone-framed doors on its north and south sides. The intricately carved arhats and grass designs on the lintel showcase the exquisite artistry of the early Tang Dynasty. Don't miss the opportunity to ring the bell for blessings and good luck. Xi'an Museum is just near the pagoda. This museum collectively reflects the long history of Xi'an city, integrating with the small Wild Goose Pagoda, the national key protection culture unit, the ancient building groups of Janfu Temple, and the modern culture relic exhibition hall. Experience the speed of China by taking a high-speed train from Xi'an to Beijing, which can reach speeds of up to 350 km/h. Day 12Beijing | Forbidden City and Summer Palace Breakfast & Lunch Explore the enigmatic Forbidden City after strolling through Tian'anmen Square. Forbidden City is the largest imperial palace in the world. Get off the ordinary sightseeing route in the Forbidden City. Listen to the interesting stories of famous personalities that are not told by typical tour guides. Visit Summer Palace this afternoon. Stroll through the beautiful gardens, admire the traditional architecture, walk along the Long Corridor, and ride a dragon boat at Kunming Lake. Day 13Beijing | Great Wall and Temple of Heaven Breakfast & Lunch The Temple of Heaven served as a place where emperors would worship the God of Heaven and pray for good harvests. You can also observe locals engaging in various activities in the park. After lunch, visit the Mutianyu Section of the Great Wall. The Mutianyu section of the Great Wall is a masterpiece of restoration. Return to Beijing after the visit. Day 14Beijing | Departure Breakfast Time to say Goodbye. Your Beijing tour guide will see you off at the airport. Thank you for exploring China with us. Hope to see you again!
