Between Hong Kong to Macau, the direct transportation means could be helicopter transport, ship transport and jetfoil transport. It is really convenient and swift for travelers' traveling between these two cities, with the straight-line distance of about 60 kilometers (37.3 miles). Helicopter Transport / Shy Shuttle: the Swiftest but the Most Expensive Choice Helicopter transport is the most swift and convenient mean for transportation between Hong Kong and Macau. The length of the time for a journey is only 15 minutes. The helicopter transport is operated by East Asia Airlines Limited and Helicopters Hong Kong Limited. Service time: The Helicopter departs every 30 minutes from 09:00 to 23:00. There are additional services on designated holidays. Passengers should arrive at the passenger lounge at least 15 minutes before the departure time. Departure and arrival places: the aprons at Macau Maritime Ferry Terminal in Macau (澳门外港客运码头) and Hong Kong-Macau Ferry Terminal in Shun Tak Center, Hong Kong (香港信德中心港澳客运码头) Tel: (853)2872 7288 (Macau) and (852)2108 9898 (Hong Kong): Passenger can book a ticket via these telephone numbers. Approximate ticket fare: (Prices are in HKD / MOP per person) (Prices are in RMB per person) Total Price Total Price Hong Kong to Macau 2,900 Hong Kong to Macau 2,700 Macau to Hong Kong 2,900 Macau to Hong Kong 2,700 Peak Days Surcharge 200 Peak Days Surcharge 200 • Peak day surcharge is needed in Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Hong Kong/Macau public Holidays and designated holidays. • Passengers can book the helicopter tickets as early as 28 days in advance. Ship Transport: the Most Popular and the Most Economical Choice The length of time for a journey by ship between Hong Kong and Macau is about 50 minutes, which is much longer than that taking a helicopter. However, the ticket fare is much cheaper than taking a helicopter. Service time and departure interval (both in Hong Kong and Macau): The ship departs every 15 minutes from 07:00 to 00:00, and then departs at: 00:30, 01:00, 01:30, 02:30, 04:00, 04:45 and 06:00. Departure and arrival places in Hong Kong Hong Kong Macau Ferry Terminal 港澳码头 Service time: 24 hours (daily) It is in Shun Tak Center (信德中心). For passengers in Hong Kong International Airport: 1) take the airport express (departing every 10 minutes from 05:50 to 00:48) to Hong Kong Station (香港站), and then take a taxi to the terminal, and the whole journey coasts about HKD 120. 2) Take bus A11 (departing every 15 minutes from 05:50 to 00:48) to the terminal, which costs about HKD 18. 3) Take a taxi from the airport to the terminal, which costs about 375 HK$. China Ferry Terminal (中国客运码头) Service time: 07:00–22:30 (daily) It is located in Tsim Sha Tsui, Kowloon (九龙尖沙咀).
