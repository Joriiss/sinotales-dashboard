Development of the Economic Center in China During this 8-day educational trip, students will not only experience the charm of China's developed cities but also have the opportunity to immerse themselves in local communities and experience the lives of everyday people. With the guidance of knowledgeable tour guides, students will discover why Shanghai and Hangzhou have become economic centers of China, and it may inspire their career planning. Suggested 8-Day China Educational Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Shanghai New Shanghai: Modern Neighborhood and Architecture 4 Suzhou Traditional garden and water town 5-7 Hangzhou The development of Hangzhou and Song culture 10 Hangzhou departure Ascend the Shanghai Tower for a Panoramic View of The City Skyline As one ascends its heights, panoramic views of the dynamic city unfold, revealing a captivating blend of tradition and progress. Cruise on the Huangpu River Experience serene views of traditional Chinese architecture and modern skyscrapers reflecting off the tranquil waters as you glide along, creating a dazzling light show. Make Pastries or Wrap Wontons in a Modern Community Cao Yang New Neighborhood is known for its modern infrastructure, residential complexes, and various amenities. We will learn from local residents how to make pastries or wrap wontons in the community activity room. Learn to Make Bonsai at a Traditional Garden A skillful master who has 10 years of experience will show you how to make a bonsai at a Traditional Garden. Picking tea Leaves and Learn to Make a Cup of Tea You will acquire knowledge on West Lake Dragon Well tea, including tea picking, the process of stir-frying tea, and making a cup of tea. Travel Back to the Song Dynasty Wear the Traditional Hanfu, and enjoy the traditional Chinese music, dances, and performances from the Song Dynasty period (960-1279 AD) at Song city. Explore the History of Hangzhou Through the Jinghang Grand Canal Cruise The guide will tell you how the canal made Hangzhou a vital trade center. Thanks to the canal, the city flourished through the exchange of goods, cultural ideas, and technologies. Paint Your Own Oiled-paper Umbrella During your visit to the Workmanship Demonstration Pavilion, you'll create and paint your very own oiled-paper umbrella, which you can then proudly take home as a keepsake! Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful school trip to China for you. Day 1: Arrival in Shanghai Welcome to China! Your guide, who is experienced in leading student groups, will meet you in the arrivals hall at the airport. Your group will be transferred to your hotel on our comfortable bus. Day 2: Shanghai | City Highlights Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Start your day with a visit to the Shanghai Municipal History Museum, where you can explore the rich tapestry of the city's past through engaging exhibits and artifacts. After immersing yourself in history, head to the Shanghai Tower, the tallest building in China, to witness breathtaking panoramic views of the city skyline from its observation deck. Then take a leisurely stroll along the Bund. Afternoon: As the sun begins to set, embark on a relaxing Pujiang Cruise, where you can enjoy the shimmering lights of the city as they reflect on the water. Finally, end your day in the charming Former French Concession Precinct, where you can wander through tree-lined streets, admire the unique blend of Eastern and Western architecture. Day 3: Shanghai | Modern Community and Science and Technology Museum Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Visit the vibrant Cao Yang New Neighborhood, famous for its modern architecture and lively atmosphere. Explore the community exhibition hall, stroll through China's first high-line park, and learn to make pastries or wrap wontons with local residents. Enjoy an authentic lunch at the community canteen. Afternoon: Explore the fascinating exhibits at the Shanghai Science and Technology Museum, where you can engage with interactive displays and learn about the latest advancements in science and technology. You will have the chance to shop at the Science Museum's Underground Shopping Center, featuring various tech products like smart devices and robots. Students are encouraged to choose meaningful items for their team and practice their Chinese skills while bargaining. Day 4: Shanghai | Suzhou Traditional Garden and Water Town Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Arrive in Suzhou after a two-hour drive. Explore the Lingering Garden, an elaborate succession of small gardens, renowned for its exquisite design and beautiful landscapes. You will learn to make a bonsai from a skillful master who has 10 years of experience. Afternoon: Take the opportunity to visit the charming Tongli Watertown, which has a history of over one thousand years. You will see picturesque canals, ancient architecture, and a rich cultural heritage. Enjoy a leisurely stroll through the serene streets, savoring the local cuisine and immersing yourself in the history of this captivating area. Day 5: Shanghai to Hangzhou| West Lake and Lingyin Temple Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: The driver will take your group to the train station for the bullet train to Hangzhou, escorting you all the way to the boarding gate. Take a cruise on the West Lake and listen to interesting stories behind the sights, and discover the magic of this place for yourself! Afternoon: Discover the intricate carvings and sacred relics that adorn the halls of this revered Lingyin temple, experiencing a sense of tranquility and reverence. Continue your exploration to Feilai Feng(Flying Peak), a striking limestone formation that rises dramatically from the landscape. Strolling along the Qinghefang Pedestrian Street in the late afternoon. Browse through eclectic shops, taste savory local delicacies, and immerse yourself in the historical allure of this bustling marketplace. Day 6: Hangzhou | Tea Culture and Song City Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Picking tea leaves at Meijiawu tea garden, the place of origin of the best tea in China — West Lake Dragon Well tea. After tea-picking, the tea farmer will show you the process of stir-frying tea. Then enjoy the tea made by yourselves. Afternoon: The Song City is the largest theme park of Song culture all over the country. The streets, bridges, and shops here are all built in the style of the Song Dynasty. Put on a hanfu and embark on a journey through time. Day 7:Hangzhou | Development of Hangzhou Breakfast and Lunch Morning: The Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal plays an important role in the development of Hangzhou. Students will first enjoy a leisurely cruise to get a general idea of the Grand Canal. They will then visit a cluster of museums to gain a deeper understanding of Hangzhou's historical development and culture. The cluster of museums includes the Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal Museum, the China Knives, Scissors, and Swords Museum, the China Fan Museum, the China Umbrella Museum, the Hangzhou Arts and Crafts Museum, and the Workmanship Demonstration Pavilion. You will have a chance to paint your own oil-paper umbrella at the Workmanship Demonstration Pavilion. Afternoon: In the late afternoon, venture to the Xiaohe Street Historic Block. This quaint neighborhood brings the past to life with its picturesque architecture, winding alleyways, and traditional storefronts. Day 8: Hangzhou Departure Breakfast After a buffet breakfast at your hotel, your guide and driver will make sure you get to the airport on time and without any stress. Say goodbye to China and take some awesome memories home. More China tours Hotel Selection Selected hotels with good locations.
