Title: Top Inner Mongolia Tours 2025/2026: Horse Riding, Naadam, Yurt

Source: China Highlights

The best times to visit Inner Mongolia is between June and Early October, when the grasslands are lush and the weather is pleasantly cool. Naadam Fairs are a major highlight, offering authentic competitions and a spectacular grassland experience. Starting in July, they will be held in various locations in Inner Mongolia. The top three events are in: Xilinhot City's Fengqing Garden (July 13) Baotou City's Bailing Temple (July 19) Gegentala Grassland (early August) If you miss the festival ceremonies, you can still enjoy horseback riding and archery performances on the grasslands at other times. Moreover, there is much more for your exploration: unique volcanoes, endless deserts, ethnic culture, the legacy of Genghis Khan and his Mongol Empire...
