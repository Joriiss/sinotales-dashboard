The award-winning Mandarin Oriental Hotel Group represents the peak of luxury accommodation worldwide. They are famous for their legendary service, celebrity fans and a philosophy for luxury that is steeped in the values of the Orient. So it’s safe to say that when it comes to their properties in Mainland China and Hong Kong – they deliver excellence. Recently, we had the honor of becoming part of the Mandarin Oriental Fan Club, a select group of Mandarin Orientals preferred partners worldwide. This is great news for you because there is now a whole slew of guest benefits that WildChina clients are eligible for. Mandarin Oriental have properties in Shanghai, Guangzhou and Hong Kong. The Guangzhou location offers 263 sumptuous rooms. The Shanghai hotel is located right in the heart of Pudong’s central financial district along the banks of the Huangpu River. And not to be outdone on their home turf, Hong Kong offers three different 5-star hotels sprinkled throughout the best neighborhoods in the city: the flagship Mandarin Oriental, the Landmark, and the Excelsior. Mandarin Oriental Hong Kong Here are just a few of the benefits you can now enjoy by booking your Mandarin Oriental stay through WildChina. Stroll into one of the Mandarin Oriental hotels and be treated by a one category room upgrade (subject to availability at the time of check-in). Be greeted by personalized welcome amenities and wake up in the morning to a complimentary full American breakfast for two on each day of your stay. Upload the wonderful photos from your trip and never miss a beat with the complimentary high speed internet access and you’ll even be given priority waitlist clearance. With all these extra benefits, a stay at the Mandarin Oriental is the perfect complement to your trip to China. Mandarin Oriental Shanghai Contact us to see what other benefits the Fan Club has to offer and consider the Mandarin Oriental on your next Hong Kong; Pearl of the Orient or Shanghai Highlife WildChina Journey.
