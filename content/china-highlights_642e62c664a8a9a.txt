Cantonese Restaurants Jinyue Pavilion Restaurant It is a typical Cantonese style restaurant, mainly serving seafood, Cantonese dishes and tea snacks. Guests can feel that the restaurant has poured a lot of thoughtful ideas on making each kind of food when having dinner there. Servers are nice with timely and considerate services. The restaurant is quite near seaside, guests can have a walk along the seaside after dinner. Recommended dishes are: steamed creamy custard bun (流沙包), crispy durian cake (榴莲酥), shrimp dumplings (虾饺), pineapple bun (菠萝包), edible bird's nest tart (燕窝蛋挞), chicken claws in abalone sauce (鲍汁凤爪), seafood, young pigeon (乳鸽), steamed bun stuffed with barbecued roast pork (叉烧包), stir-fried rice (炒饭) and steamed spare ribs (蒸排骨). • Chinese name: 金悦轩 Jin Yue Xuan /jin youair sshywen/ • Average price per person: 100 yuan • Opening hours: The morning tea begins at 9am. • Address: Floor 1–3, Block B, Rihua Commercial Plaza, 265 Gongbei Qinglü South Road, XiangzhouDistrict (香洲区拱北情侣南路265号日华商业广场B区1-3楼) • Tel: 0756-8133133 • Transportation: Take bus 99 and get off at Rihua Garden (日华花园). Huangchao May Flower Grand Restaurant It is a typical Cantonese restaurant, mainly serving Cantonese dishes.
