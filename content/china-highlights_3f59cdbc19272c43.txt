Find out how to get to the Terracotta Army Museum from downtown Xi'an, the airport, and the railway stations (buses, taxis, private transport, and tours). The Terracotta Army, also known as Emperor Qin Shi Huang's Mausoleum Site Museum, is located in Xi'an.The 2,000-year-old army of clay statues includes life-size soldiers, archers, horses, weapons, and utensils. You can reach the Terracotta Army by taking a bus, driving yourself, or booking a private tour with us to enjoy a hassle-free visit.
