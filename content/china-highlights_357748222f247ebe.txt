Chinese New Year, the Spring Festival, is the top festival in China. It is a time for expressing good wishes and hopes, and celebrating. Special songs are sung to suit the mood of the festival. Here we have collected four popular Chinese New Year songs for you to listen to online, and even learn — pinyin and translations provided.
