Badaling Great Wall is the closest section of the Great Wall to Beijing, about 60 km (40 mi) northwest of central Beijing. It is easily accessible and offers easy walking conditions. It has been visited by over 500 state leaders and VIPs, including Presidents Nixon and Reagan, Prime Minister Thatcher, and Queen Elizabeth II. How to Visit the Badaling Great Wall The Badaling Fortress divides the Badaling Great Wall into two sections: • North Wall: The part of the Great Wall to the north of the fortress. It has 12 enemy towers and spans approximately 2.7 kilometers. The North Wall is quite steep, with the highest point being the North 8th Tower. North 8th Tower is the best spot to overlook the Great Wall. For visitors with good physical condition, you can choose to climb from the North 1st Tower to the North 12th Tower, which takes about 2.5 hours for a round trip. • South Wall: The part of the Great Wall to the south of the fortress. It has 7 enemy towers and spans about 1 kilometer. A round trip from the South 1st Tower to the South 7th Tower takes about 1.5 hours. Compared to the North Wall, the South Wall has a smaller slope, making it easier to climb and is suitable for the elderly and children. If time allows, you can first climb the South Wall and then go to the North Wall, which takes about 4 hours in total. Sunrise of Badaling Great Wall Best Times to Visit the Badaling Great Wall The best times to visit the Badaling Great Wall are from April to May in the spring and from September to October in the fall. These two periods offer comfortable temperatures. • Spring is a time of warmth and blooming flowers, with everything coming back to life - perfect for enjoying the scenery. • In the fall, the weather is cool and clear, and the forests are beautifully colored, making it the best time to appreciate the magnificent views of the Great Wall. More about the best times to visit the Great Wall To avoid the crowds as much as possible, it's suggested to avoid the Chinese summer holiday period (mid-July to the end of August), when Badaling sees its highest visitor numbers, reaching 50,000 to 60,000 visitors daily. Is the Badaling Great Wall Wheelchair Friendly? Yes. To accommodate wheelchair users, the Badaling Great Wall has an accessible ramp nearly 180 meters long, extending from Wangjing Square near the North Wall to the East Platform. In addition, there is a wheelchair elevator that can help visitors with mobility difficulties to get onto the wall. Furthermore, visitors using wheelchairs can choose to take a cable car directly to the North 7th Tower to overlook the magnificent view of the Great Wall. Day Trip to Badaling from Beijing A visit to Badaling generally takes up half a day. We suggest you also visit other famous attractions nearby, such as the Ming Tombs. You can visit the Ming Tombs in the morning and Badaling in the afternoon, as there are generally fewer visitors in the Badaling scenic area in the afternoon compared to the morning. Visit the Ming Tombs. It is 36 kilometers (a 30-minute drive) from Badaling. The Ming Tombs is the burial site of 13 Ming Dynasty emperors and is the most intact imperial burial site in the world with the most emperors buried. It was listed on the World Heritage List in 2003. There are many specialty banquets near the Ming Tombs scenic area, such as the spring pancake banquet in Kangling Village. Spring pancakes wrap a variety of fillings with thin dough, symbolizing wishes for a good harvest and hope. The preparation is exquisite, usually paired with fresh seasonal vegetables and meat, and you can roll it into your preferred flavor. In addition, there are many farmhouses near the Ming Tombs. The main modes of transportation from downtown Beijing to Badaling are by bus and high-speed rail. The bus tends to be crowded, and there can be long queues during peak hours. The high-speed rail is more convenient and suitable for visitors with tight schedules. High-speed rail: You can take the high-speed rail from Beijing North Station directly to Badaling Great Wall Station. The whole journey takes about 0.5-1 hour, and the fare is 25 yuan for a second-class seat. Bus: Departing from Deshengmen, you can take bus No. 877 directly to Badaling Great Wall Station.
