Title: Shanghai Hangzhou Transportation, Shanghai to Hangzhou

================================================================================

The distance between Shanghai and Hangzhou is about 170 kilometers ( miles). Travelers intending to travel between these cities can take a train or a bus/coach. Train Transport: the First Choice Every day, there are 134 pairs of trains running between Shanghai and Hangzhou, including 35 pairs of D trains, 53 G trains and 46 common train (see Shanghai to Hangzhou High-speed trains). The time for taking a G train and D train traveling between these cities is respectively about 50 minutes and 80 munities, and that for taking a common train (such as K train and T train) is longer as 2 hours or 3 hours. Hence, it is very convenient and swift for a traveler to travel between these two cities by train (see High-Speed Rail in China). The railway stations for catching a train are Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station, Shanghai South Railway Station, Hangzhou Railway Station and Hangzhou South Railway Station. You can clink the following URL to get the essential information for these railway stations: Shanghai train transport and Hangzhou transportation. China Highlights offers the service of searching and booking a China train on China Trains. The length of the highway between Shanghai and Hangzhou is about 180 kilometer (choosing different depart station might bring a little discrepancy), and the time for bus/coach travel is about 2 hours and 15 munities. Every day, there are numerous buses running between Shanghai and Hangzhou, and the approximate ticket price is 65 yuan. During Chinese New Year Festival period, the departure interval for buses running between the two cities can be 10 minutes. From Shanghai to Hangzhou Passengers in Shanghai can catch a bus to Hangzhou in several bus/coach stations. Every day, there are more than a hundred buses departing to Hangzhou. Below is the list of bus stations in which passengers can get a bus to Hangzhou. Among these bus stations, Shanghai Jiading Bus Station and Shanghai Pujiang Long-Distance Bus Station only operate 2 journeys to Hangzhou a day, comparing with the rest bus stations' dozens of journeys.
