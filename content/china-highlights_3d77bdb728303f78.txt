See below a list of most recommended places to eat in Changsha. Good Vogue Bayi Road Branch Rarely can such refinement be seen in Changsha. The light in the chartered room is comfortable and even the restrooms there are pretty clean. Hunan cuisine and Cantonese cuisine prevail there. On the whole, the flavors of the dishes are not too spicy and can be accepted easily by foreign tourists.
