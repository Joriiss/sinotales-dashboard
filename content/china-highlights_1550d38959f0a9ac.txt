Title: 8-Day Chengdu,Jiuzhaigou and Chongqing Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chengdu-jiuzhaigou-chongqing.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

From Nature's Palette to Urban Rhythm Embark on an 8-day journey spanning Chengdu, Jiuzhaigou, and Chongqing, a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural immersion, and modern urban exploration. In Chengdu, experience the city's laid-back lifestyle, meet enchanting pandas, and witness the magic of Sichuan opera. Marvel at the stunning landscapes of Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong, where our expert guides meticulously plan the best routes to ensure you capture every breathtaking highlight. End your journey in Chongqing, a city bursting with energy. Discover the city's unique vibe at the illuminated Hongya Cave, capture the unique sight of the Liziba light rail, and explore the lively Jiefangbei. Balance modern vibes with a visit to Ciqikou ancient town and other historic streets, where you can step back in time and experience the city's rich history. As a premier custom travel agency, we're ready to tailor this journey to your preferences. Marvel at the Fairyland-like Scenery of Jiuzhaigou Navigate through valleys, waterfalls, and forests. In the gentle breeze of Jiuzhaigou, slowly perceive the poetic transformation of water. Admire the World-Class Travertine Pool Scenery of Huanglong Ride a cable car uphill to witness shimmering multicolored pools, then hike down through the forest, serenaded by flowing water, and bask in nature's radiant display. Meet the Adorable Giant Pandas Visit the Panda Base in downtown Chengdu, the largest and most panda-populated among all bases, and feast your eyes on these adorable creatures. Enjoy Sichuan Opera and Explore Behind the Scenes Experience the enchanting Sichuan opera, puppetry, and shadow play at Shufengyayun Opera House, complete with an exclusive backstage pass to meet the master performers. Sip a Cup of Tea and Immerse in Chengdu's Leisurely Pace Come to an old teahouse in the park, bask under the warm sun, and engage in unhurried conversations. Immerse in Chengdu's Laid-Back Lifestyle. Marvel at the Nighttime Visual Feast of Hongyadong Explore Chongqing's iconic landmark and immerse yourself in the radiant Cyberpunk ambiance of Hongyadong, a nighttime visual feast. Gasp at the Madness of Chongqing's Transportation and Building Wonders Snap photos of a light rail through a building, ride the sky bus, explore a mall's hanging forest, and often find yourself bewildered about which floor you're on. Stroll Through Chongqing's Old Streets and Ancient Towns While experiencing the traditional lifestyle of old Chongqing, seek out unique coffee shops and a variety of interesting small shops. Dive into the Ultimate Culinary Expedition Savor local delicacies like sweetwater noodles, ice jelly, and sticky rice balls, and dare to challenge your taste buds with spicy hotpot, rabbit head, and pork brain. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. Day 1 Chengdu Arrival Upon landing, your personal guide and driver will greet you and escort you to your Chengdu hotel. Spend the rest of the day resting or exploring the city. Day 2 Chengdu | The City Highlights Kickstart your day with an engaging visit to the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding. Here, you'll have the opportunity to see adorable panda cubs, sub-adult pandas, and majestic adult pandas, all easily accessible via shuttle buses or a leisurely walk. Embark on a culinary journey at a bustling local food street for lunch. Savor authentic Sichuan dishes and snacks, then unwind with a refreshing beer or coffee in the quaint Kuanzhai Alley. Spend the afternoon meandering through historic lanes and browsing unique souvenir shops. Visit the People's Park and enjoy a leisurely cup of tea, immersing yourself in the laid-back pace of Chengdu life. Beyond its beauty, the park is a hub for local activities, from square dancing to matchmaking. Conclude your delightful day with a mesmerizing Sichuan opera performance at the revered Shufeng Yayun Opera House, a favorite among international visitors. Be captivated by the enchanting face-changing show, puppetry, and shadow plays, sure to charm both young and old alike. In an exclusive arrangement for China Highlights' customers, you'll gain backstage access before the show, providing a unique opportunity to interact with the renowned performers! Day 3 Chengdu to Jiuzhaigou | Huanglong National Scenic Spot Commence your journey with a high-speed train ride from Chengdu to Huanglongjiuzhai Station, the stepping stone to the awe-inspiring Huanglong National Scenic Spot. Due to the high altitude, we'll whisk you to the summit via cable car and shuttle, where you'll be dazzled by the gem-like Five Color Pools. Descend at leisure through forest trails, with our experienced guides always on hand to ensure your safety and comfort. After your visit, a 2-hour drive will take you to your hotel near Jiuzhaigou. Day 4 Jiuzhaigou | Zechawa Valley and Rize Valley, and Shuzheng Valley If memories could be colored, they would surely be painted with the mesmerizing blues of Jiuzhaigou. Its soul is reflected in the ever-changing waters, shifting hues with every angle. Here, you'll encounter a spectrum of blues, from Prussian to Tiffany, and even turquoise green. Explore the three main valleys of Jiuzhaigou in a day, featuring highlights like Long Lake, Five Flower Lake, and Shuzheng Lakes. No filters or pro cameras needed, your smartphone captures stunning photos everywhere. Our seasoned guides will customize your journey, choosing optimal routes influenced by weather conditions, crowd sizes, timing, and personal interests. Day 5 Jiuzhaigou to Chongqing | High-speed Train After breakfast, drive for 2 hours to Huanglong Jiuzhaigou Station. Then, a 2.5-hour high-speed train to Chengdu, followed by a 2-hour bullet train to Chongqing. Times may vary with train schedules. Day 6 Chongqing | Historic Lanes and Nighttime Radiance Discover the unique blend of old and new in Xiahaoli and Longmenhao, some of Chongqing's best-preserved old streets. Wander along flagstone paths, past stilted houses and quaint courtyards, to historical foreign firm sites. Trendy boutiques, teahouses, and cafes surprise you at every corner, providing a vibrant blend of the city's past and present. Next, ride on the 'air bus' across the Yangtze River for about 4.5 minutes, offering stunning views of the river and city traffic. Proceed to Jiefangbei, a war monument turned shopping haven in the city centre. Here, luxury meets local charm, making it perfect for retail therapy and savoring Chongqing's culinary delights. Arrive early at Hongya Cave to seize the lighting-up moment. Its glowing, stacked stilt houses transport you into a movie scene. Afterward, explore the eleven distinct levels for an unparalleled experience. Day 7 Chongqing ｜Ancient Charms and Urban Wonders Embark on a journey to Ciqikou Ancient Town. Beyond its lively main thoroughfare, delve into serene backstreets to fully embrace the allure of this thousand-year-old settlement, originally established during the Song Dynasty. In autumn, golden ginkgo leaves contrast with Baolun Temple's crimson walls. Discover a Qing Dynasty school and an ancient dock, enriching the town's historical aura. Meander through the Mountain City Alley, where old hill town charm meets nature. Nearby, shops brim with nostalgic allure. Then, visit Shiba Ti, a revitalized hotspot, perfect for your social media updates. Witness the magic at Liziba Station as a light rail zips through a building. Feel the thrill at Kui Xing Building, standing on two different floors at once, in an 8D urban marvel. Conclude your day at the Ring Mall, where a vast indoor garden and a futuristic floating forest design create a verdant, captivating urban oasis. Day 8 Chongqing Departure After breakfast, you'll be whisked off to Chongqing's airport, drawing a close to this memorable journey.
