This 11-day China top cities tour covers the most popular four destinations and five World Heritage sites in China. It makes an ideal tour for first-time visitors who would like to discover China's history, culture, modernization and landscape. The itinerary below is only one possibility. Tips to tailor-make your trip are offered as inspiration. Tell us your preferences and our travel experts will customize this trip to suit your interests, tastes, and budget. Suggested 11-Day Itinerary Day Highlights 1-4 Beijing Experience rich history: the Great Wall, Forbidden City, Temple of Heaven, Summer Palace and Tian'anmen Square 5-6 Xi'an Uncover the Terracotta Warriors, bike along the ancient City Wall, explore the lively Muslim Quarter and Xi'an History Museum 7-9 Guilin & Yangshuo Admire the stunning Longji Rice Terraces, enjoy a scenic Li River cruise, and relax in the charming countryside of Yangshuo 10-11 Shanghai Explore modern Shanghai: the iconic Bund, panoramic views from Shanghai Tower, and a personalized city experience Beijing-Xi'an-Guilin-Shanghai Tour Map Unveil the Lives of China's Royal Family in their Hidden Halls Get off the ordinary sightseeing route in the Forbidden City. Listen to the interesting stories of the famous personalities that are not told by typical tour guides. Witness the Great Wall and Enjoy the Dreamy Night Views Walk on the spectacular Great Wall at Mutianyu. See both a restored section and a wild section of the Wall. See the Simatai Wall lit up along a mountain ridge at night. Understand Beijing by Tasting Its Traditional Food Enjoy an authentic Beijing roast duck dinner in a time-honored brand restaurant. Unearth Secrets of the Terracotta Army and its "Hidden Collectibles" Marvel at the 2,000-year-old clay army that once guarded Emperor Qin's tomb. Join a guided tour to see and touch the artifacts from the same era, including the first unearthed terracotta figure. Immerse yourself in the role of an archaeologist by participating in a conservation workshop. Enjoy a Cruise in the Painting Gallery of the God Relax on a top-class guided Li River cruise, enjoying spectacular landscapes. Find the exact sight on the note of RMB20 Feast Your Eyes on the Best Scenery of the Rice Terraces. The Longji Rice Terraces are known widely for the awe-inspiring view. Standing on top of such a huge man-made wonder is itself an extraordinary experience. Visit a Minority Family and Explore Their Centuries-Old Lifestyle. Enjoy lunch with a Yao family who invite you into their home. Learn how to stuff and cook rice inside a bamboo tube, sip the freshly-extracted oil tea. Discover the Rural China by Exploring the Less Touristy Villages To experience the daily life of the local farmers will let you see another side of China. Discover the Hidden Stories of the Bund and Ascend Shanghai Tower Travel back in time to the golden age of the Bund in the 1920s. You will walk a route that seldom tourist will find by themselves, see the most original bricks, and hear some interesting stories that you'll never find in history books. Enjoy a Half-Day Customized Icing-on-the-Cake Experience. Whether you want to relax, experience fashion, appreciate art, explore folk customs, or shop; from having fun in a fancy bar to exploring an art center, trying nail beauty, testing your popularity in the marriage market, or just buying some lovely gifts, we will make it happen. To ensure your arrival is stress-free, your private tour guide will meet you in the arrivals hall and transfer you to your hotel in a private car. Your luggage and hotel check-in will be taken care of. Hotel options: Novotel Beijing Peace Crowne Plaza Beijing Chaoyang U-Town Day 2The Temple of Heaven and the Forbidden City Breakfast & Lunch Start your journey at the Temple of Heaven, a dignified complex of religious buildings, symbolizing the relationship between Earth and Heaven. There you will see local people doing morning exercise in their unique ways. Lunch is served in a popular local restaurant with authentic local food. Your guide will help you to order the dishes you like. Walk through Tian'anmen Square to explore the mysterious Forbidden City. Being the largest imperial palace in the world. Get off the ordinary sightseeing route in the Forbidden City. Listen to the interesting stories of the famous personalities that are not told by typical tour guides. Tips to Tailor-Make Your Tour Do you want to discover more about the life of Beijingers? Take a rickshaw tour through the hutongs, make dumplings in a local family's home, or experience a local market — let us know what you would like to do on the form to the right. The Temple of Heaven Day 3Day and Night Tour of the Great Wall Breakfast & Lunch After breakfast, travel to the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall. Listen to the stories about this world wonder. Your guide will show you the most recommended section to have a walk. Head to Simatai Great Wall in the afternoon. Spend the afternoon wandering around the Guibei Water Town, which is at the foot of the Great Wall. You can also enjoy a soak in a hot spring if you want to. When it gets dark, your guide will accompany you to take the cable car up to the Simatai section to enjoy the stunning night views, which is a different experience from the day version. It's the only section that's open and lit up at night. Tips to Tailor-Make Your Tour Would you like a more romantic experience? Go there between April and October to enjoy a nice dinner at a special restaurant, from where you can see the Simatai section of the Great Wall lit up all along the mountain ridges at night. Hotel options: Somerset Xindicheng Xi'an Wyndham Grand Xi'an South The Summer Palace Day 5The Terracotta Warriors Breakfast & Lunch Enter the Terracotta Warriors Museum via tram, and witness three captivating pits that display the concealed power of Emperor Qin Shi Huang's extensive military force. Your guide will provide insights into their fabrication and safeguarding, emphasizing the precise workmanship, lasting strength, and the former colorful brilliance of these historical sentinels. Get up close with the first restored Warriors, learn the restoration story, handle Qin Dynasty relics in the Conservation Laboratory. In the afternoon, visit the ancient city wall that has been guarding the inner city for 600 years. Walking along the wall provides a good view of the city. You can also rent a bike to cycle around the wall. Fly to Guilin in the afternoon. Hotel options: Lijiang Waterfall Hotel Shangri-La Hotel Guilin The Big Goose Pagoda Day 7Longji Terraced Rice Fields and Ethnic Minority Culture Breakfast & Lunch Begin your day by exploring the Longji Terraced Rice Fields. Built into the hillsides, the terraces are beautiful when the sun reflects on the water of the paddy fields, and the fields glow when the harvest is ripe. The Zhuang minority have lived there for generations in wooden houses, and the people eat their own unique food, such as bamboo rice and oil tea. Sit around a bonfire in a traditional kitchen, make bamboo rice with the host. Tips to Tailor-Make Your Tour Are you very active and love hiking?
