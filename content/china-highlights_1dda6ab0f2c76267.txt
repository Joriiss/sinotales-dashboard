Title: 10-Day Old and New China Educational Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/beijing-xian-shanghai-educational.htm

================================================================================

Experience Ancient and New China Embark on an extraordinary journey through China's rich national tapestry, exploring the iconic cities of Beijing, Xi'an, and Shanghai. This immersive experience is designed to inspire young minds with experiential learning, cultural immersion, and a deep understanding of China's past and present. From the grandeur of the Forbidden City and the Terracotta Army to the vibrant energy of Shanghai's skyline and residential communities, this tour offers a captivating blend of history, culture, and modernity. Students would not only witness China's iconic landmarks but also gain a deeper appreciation for the country's rapid transformation and its enduring traditions. Suggested 10-Day China Educational Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Beijing Great Wall, Imperial architectures and New Beijing 4-6 Xi'an Terracotta Warriors and Tang dynasty culture 7-9 Shanghai City skyline, maglev train, and a new residential community visit 10 Shanghai departure Walk on the Great Wall and Slide Down from It Visit a less-crowded Great Wall section at Mutianyu and try an exciting toboggan ride. Understand the Daily Studies of the Princes Visit the sites selected for children instead of the regular ones for adult tourists. Find out how hard princes had to study to be emperors! Unravel the Mysteries of Traditional Chinese Construction Get hands-on experience with the secrets of ancient Chinese wooden architecture. Explore the intricate world of mortise and tenon joints. Practice a Chinese Morning Excercise in the Temple of Heaven Feel the relaxing lifestyle of the local people and learn doing Tai Chi at the Temple of Heaven where the emperors prayed for good harvests. Make Your Own Terracotta Warriors Not only visit the Terracotta Warriors Horses Museum to see the life-sized Terracotta Army, but olso make a mini one by yourself. Use Teamwork to Experience Making Fire by Drilling Wood Visit the estimated-6,000-year-old Banpo cultural site. Learn the survival skills of primitive people and experience their entertainment. Unveiling the Black Technology of China's Logistics Visit JD Logistics' 'Asia No. 1' warehouse, one of the top 25 global supply chains. Witness how China utilizes high technology to ensure efficient and accurate logistics operations across 9.6 million square kilometers of land. Immerse Yourself in a Chinese Community Visit China's first workers' community that's now renovated and preserved for posterity. Learn how to make Chinese pastries at the community center. Perhaps play a basketball game with the local youths. Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful school trip to China for you. Day 1: Arrival in Beijing Welcome to China! Your guide, who is experienced in leading student groups, will meet you in the arrivals hall at the airport. Your group will be transferred to your hotel on our comfortable bus. Day 2: Beijing | The Great Palace Displays Traditional Chinese Construction Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Start your adventure in Beijing's heart, Tian'anmen Square — a massive space so big it's a favorite for many school trips at once! It's like stepping into a giant postcard. Next, walk through the grand entrance to the Forbidden City, a place where emperors once ruled. You would see famous palaces but also sneak a peek at secret spots! Imagine exploring the princes' personal library, checking out cool army tools at the Archery Pavilion, and even seeing the amazing royal collection of clocks and watches! Afternoon: Join in with an interesting Chinese architecture technique lesson in a hutong. Explore a great invention of imperial China — the mortise and tenon joint. Uncover the secrets behind why the Forbidden City could stand firm for hundreds of years — with no nails! Day 3: The Temple of Heaven and the Great Wall Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Get ready for an epic morning! Head to the Temple of Heaven, where ancient emperors connected with heaven through annual rituals. But this isn't your average history lesson! Watch as locals do their traditional hobbies — from graceful tai chi moves to mind-blowing calligraphy. Your own tai chi master would teach you the basics so you could join in with the fun. Afternoon: After soaking up the morning vibes, you are off to the Great Wall! Zip up to the top in a cable car and explore this medieval wonder. Snap some epic pics and enjoy some free time to wander around. And the best part? A thrilling toboggan ride back down! Evening: End the day with a mouthwatering Peking duck dinner at a local restaurant. Get ready to taste a time-honored local favorite! Day 4: Beijing to Xi'an | The Leading-edge Tech of New China Breakfast and Lunch Morning: Visit The Beijing Asia No. 1 warehouse which employs advanced technologies such as the goods-to-person system, cross-belt sorting system, and AGV (Automated Guided Vehicle) systems. Here, you can not only see the Tianlang and Dilang robots but also learn more about JD's sustainable development initiative, the "Green Flow Plan." Afternoon: Take a bullet train from Beijing to Xi'an in the afternoon. Day 5: Xi'an | The Secrets of the Terracotta Army Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Prepare to be amazed as you travel to the incredible Terracotta Army. This astonishing collection of life-sized clay soldiers and horses was created as a guard for Emperor Qin Shi Huang, a ruler obsessed with eternal life. Imagine thousands of warriors, horses, and chariots frozen in time, a testament to his power and ambition. Afternoon: Did you know these warriors were once painted in vibrant colors? Although the hues have faded, their incredible craftsmanship remains. Get ready to unleash your inner artist and create your own terracotta warrior. It's an experience you wouldn't forget! Pay a visit to the starting point of the Silk Road: Big Wild Goose Pagoda. End the day with an amazing traditional dumpling dinner. Day 6: Xi'an | Be an Ancient Chinese Citizen for a Day Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Visit Banpo Museum, which was built on the remains of an estimated-6,000-year-old Neolithic community and also a matrilineal society discovered in 1953. Visit the excavated artifacts exhibition hall to learn about the clothing, food, housing, and transportation of people during the Banpo period. Work together and try your hands at making a fire by drilling wood as the Banpo people did. Learn how to make a clay instrument and try to play it. Afternoon: Head back to the downtown area of the city in the afternoon. Dress up in traditional Chinese Hanfu clothes. Walk along the streets with pseudoclassic buildings. Feel like you're traveling back in time to the Tang Dynasty (more than 1,000 years ago). Day 7:Xi'an to Shanghai | A Morning Bike Ride on the Ancient City Wall Breakfast and Lunch Morning: Enjoy a biking experience on the ancient city wall in Xi'an. This aged and renovated defense project surrounded Xi'an during its days as a medieval China capital for several centuries. Afternoon: Fly to Shanghai in the afternoon. Day 8:Shanghai | The Most Modern City in China Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Do more than just take one or two selfies on the Bund. Travel back in time to the 1920s era of the Bund during a walking tour. Take the Bund Sightseeing Tunnel to the other side of the river, enjoying the colorful light displays, music, and visual effects during the journey. Ascend the tallest building in China, Shanghai Tower, where you would not only enjoy a panoramic view of Shanghai, but also see the future of China. Afternoon: Visit Aurora Museum, a hidden gem amid Shanghai's bustling cityscape. Keep discovering under your guide's inspirational commentary. Enjoy the beauty of the pottery, jade artifacts, porcelain, and Buddhist statues. Appreciate the Chinese craftsmanship and artistic expression. Day 9:Shanghai | Old Neighborhood, New Life Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Experience the rich history and culture of a 70-year-old community. Interact with local residents, learn about the community's transformation, and participate in traditional food preparation, such as making pastries or wrapping wontons. Enjoy a taste of authentic local cuisine at the community canteen. Afternoon: Enjoy some free time in the afternoon to conclude your China tour and maybe buy some souvenirs to share with your families back home.
