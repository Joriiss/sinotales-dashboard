Title: 3-Day Wuyuan Spring Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/wuyuan/tours/spring-tour.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Step into the Spring of Wuyuan Wuyuan, an ancient county of Jiangxi Province not far from Huangshan, is often called "the most beautiful countryside in China" because of its incredible Chinese-calligraphy-style views in both spring and autumn. Its Huangling Village even earned the honor of "Sino-Foreign Most Beautiful Shooting Location" at the 74th Venice International Film Festival in 2017. Dozens of movies and TV series have been filmed there… Don't miss this super highlight of spring! Our 3-day tour of Wuyuan in spring covers the most attractive sites and is totally private and flexible, which means the best use of your time during the hustle and bustle of the popular spring season. Put your mind at ease with our one-to-one tour service, and enjoy a colorful worry-free holiday experience with us! Walk into the epitome of spring and lose yourself in the seas of rapeseed flowers. All the local farmers plant rapeseed in their fields to enrich the soil, and it has become a symbol of spring. In Wuyuan, you can see endless flowery seas in spring. Make the most of the scenery! Have a walk between the sea of flowers and prepare enough memory space for eye-catching photos. You can be the star of the moment… and a Moments star. Visit the local villages your way and engage with them undisturbed Discover the poetic villages in both day and night! We can't control the enthusiasm of travelers, but we do carefully manage our itineraries to minimize the negative impact of the crowds. You can even enjoy the lovely "blue hours" and discover the local village at night when most travelers have gone home. Catch the smell of a refreshing tea plantation and learn some tea culture What does the first batch of spring tea look and smell like? Let's view the largest tea plantation of the area during our visit, and discover why the Chinese people so love tea and have traded it for centuries. You would appreciate this trip's insights if you ever chat with your pals about tea. Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Wuyuan Arrival | Pick-up and Sightseeing in the Afternoon Your personal guide and driver will meet you at the railway station in Wuyuan. You will be transferred to Xiaoqi (a very famous village, for its tea culture, architecture, and another interesting point — it is the hometown of former Chinese leader Mr. Jiang) and Jiangwan Village for sightseeing in the afternoon. DAY 2 Trip to Huangling and Overnight Village Stay Praised as "the most beautiful countryside in China", Huangling attracts visitors from all over the world. The quaint and elegant village has preserved and maintained its ancient Hui-style architecture and offers an authentic Chinese countryside life experience. We have a full day tour there, so you can enjoy the unique views that can only be found at certain times of day… and night. DAY 3 Wuyuan Departure | Until Next Time, Wuyuan! Say goodbye to Huangling after a good rest. Then head to the Sixi — a village that famous for its history, carvings, and bridges. After that, see the tea plantation in Jinzhu Village, where you can witness and experience the first batch of spring tea. We'll finish the tour by driving you to Wuyuan Railway Station for your bullet train back home.
