Title: 3-Day China's Silicon Valley-Shenzhen Highlights Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/guangzhou/tours/shenzhen-highlights.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Past Roots, Future's Edge Discover Shenzhen's remarkable transformation, a city where the profound history of reform and opening up stands as a testament to China's four decades of rapid development. This tour provides a unique opportunity to witness its cutting-edge technology up close, from a driverless taxi ride to a drone-delivered drink, offering an unforgettable journey into a city that continues to innovate and shape the future. This itinerary is designed to be fully customizable to your needs and interests. Our team is on hand to provide you with personalized support. Experience China's future, where the "Eye of Shenzhen" and the vibrant bay skyline showcase innovation and urban splendor. Witness Future Innovation Take a driverless taxi and get a drone delivery to experience how innovation is redefining our world. Recall Shenzhen's Rise Story Discover how Shenzhen rose from a humble fishing village to a global technology hub by exploring the sites of its economic miracle. Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1Shenzhen Arrival Welcome to Shenzhen, Silicon Valley of China! Your journey begins with a warm greeting from your knowledgeable tour guide at the airport or train station. Enjoy a seamless transfer to the city hotel. Day 2Shenzhen | The Story of Spring Breakfast & Lunch Meet your guide and driver to begin your one-day tour exploring Shenzhen's Reform and Opening-Up. Explore the Yumin Village Museum, where old photos and artifacts tell the story of Shenzhen's first "ten-thousand-yuan village," highlighting Deng Xiaoping's pivotal 1984 visit. Ascend Lianhua Mountain Park to see the Deng Xiaoping statue, a symbol of his role in establishing Shenzhen as a Special Economic Zone. From the summit, you can enjoy a panoramic view of the Futian CBD. Discover the Shenzhen Reform and Opening-Up Exhibition Hall to explore the city's transformation over four key periods. Witness the Eye of Shenzhen at Gangxia North Subway Station before taking the subway to the famous Huaqiangbei Electronics Market. You'll have time to explore its endless selection and join a fun challenge to "Find the most beautiful phone case for 2 bucks". Day 3 Shenzhen Departure | High-Tech for Life Breakfast & Lunch A comfortable drive from your hotel to Nashan awaits, during which you'll gain fascinating insights into the region from your guide. Step into the DJI Flagship Store to learn about the company's history and its role in the future of the 'low-altitude economy.' Enjoy a drone operation demonstration, where you'll witness a drone flying and discover how features like obstacle avoidance and automatic tracking make capturing stunning video effortless. Catch a PonyPilot Taxi at Shenzhen Bay Park Metro Station and head to Shenzhen Talent Park. It's a futuristic, driverless ride, offering the unique experience of having just a large screen in front of you instead of a driver. Explore Shenzhen Talent Park and enjoy stunning views of the city's modern skyline, including the iconic 'Spring Bamboo Shoot' building. Experience the latest technology by ordering your takeout for Drone Delivery, with options from KFC, Subway, Chagee Tea, Naixue drinks, and more. Relax and enjoy a comfortable transfer to the airport or train station. Day 1Shenzhen Arrival Welcome to Shenzhen, Silicon Valley of China!
