Enjoy Family Time in Disneyland This private tour is specially designed for families. Discovering the city's rich history, modern architecture, and classic water town. Enjoying a day of fun at Shanghai Disneyland. This tour offers a perfect balance for families. Children can enjoy the thrills of the amusement park and learn about the city's history, while parents can relax on a leisurely city tour. We're happy to tailor your tour to your specific interests. Just share your ideas, and we'll create a personalized itinerary. Explore the Enchanting World of Disneyland. Experience thrilling adventures on attractions like the Enchanted Jungle Cruise and the Roaring Rapids. Uncover Hidden Histories behind the Historic Buildings. Follow your insider guide through the Bund. Uncover hidden artifacts from its golden age. Ascend the World's Second Tallest Building for Breathtaking Panoramic Views. Shanghai Tower is an iconic landmark in Shanghai, China, boasting several impressive records. It stands at a towering 632 meters (2,073 feet) and houses the world's fastest elevators. Step Back in Time to Discover Stunning Architecture. Wander through Zhujiajiao Water Town, a fan-shaped village with centuries of history. Admire Ming and Qing Dynasty architecture along the lake. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1Shanghai Arrival Your private guide will be waiting for you in the airport/train station arrivals area. A comfortable vehicle (non-smoking) with an experienced driver will transfer you directly to your hotel. Your guide will help you to check in. Day 2City Exploration Breakfast & Lunch Your family will explore the city to see old and new Shanghai, and experience real Shanghai life. Start your day to have a relaxing morning in Zhujiajiao Water Town. Follow your guide to walk across little bridges, explore a traditional Chinese garden, and sit in a rowing boat to enjoy the Venice of Shanghai. In the afternoon, follow your guide along the Bund and go into historical buildings, touch the old items, and learn about Shanghai's progression through history. Then ascend to the world's second tallest building of Shanghai Tower. The elevators whisk visitors up to the observation deck at a speed of 73.8 kilometers per hour (46 mph), which is the fastest elevator in the world - your children will love this experience. Day 3Shanghai Disneyland Breakfast This day is for family fun! We know people don't like time limitations in Shanghai Disneyland. To guarantee the best experience, we haven't arranged a private transfer on this day. You can have fun in Shanghai Disneyland from the morning until the evening. Your personal tour guide will help you to reserve a taxi in advance. If you prefer, you can also try the metro or a bus as public transportation is very convenient. Day 4Shanghai Departure Breakfast & Lunch Before departing Shanghai, you'll have a half-day to uncover some of the city's hidden treasures. Simply share your interests with your guide, whether it's shopping or a hands-on handicraft experience, and he/she will lead you to unexpected delights. After lunch, your private guide and driver will take you to the airport or train station for your departure.
