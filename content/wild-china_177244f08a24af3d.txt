Title: Mei Zhang is Wendy Perrin's Trusted Travel Expert for China
URL: https://wildchina.com/2018/01/wendy-perrin-wow-list-2018/
Source: Wild China
Date: 2018-01-26T19:56:13+08:00
Tags: In the News
================================================================================

We’re excited to announce that WildChina Founder and CEO, Mei Zhang has been named to Wendy Perrin’s WOW List of Trusted Travel Experts for 2018. Since 2014 Wendy Perrin, who was formerly the Director of Consumer News and Digital Community at Condé Nast Traveler, has vetted travel planners around the world to select only the best for her WOW List. Wendy Perrin clients enjoying a WOW experience in Beijing We’re so proud that Mei Zhang has been chosen as the single Trusted Travel Expert for China. When talking about Mei, Wendy writes that “sharing her homeland’s hidden gems has been Mei Zhang’s lifelong passion. No matter the occasion—family trip or 50th birthday celebration—Mei can filter the seemingly endless options to craft an experience that’s just right. Her guides and drivers are the best in the business, opening the doors to authentic Chinese culture and steering travelers away from the crowds, the expected, and all things touristy.” We’re looking forward to welcoming many more Wendy Perrin travelers to China this year and highly recommend checking out the rest of the WOW List if you’re planning a trip to another destination. Follow #WOWWeek on Social Media To celebrate the announcement of the 2018 WOW List, Wendy Perrin is hosting her first #WOWWeek. Trusted destination specialists from around the world (including us!) will be sharing juicy insider tips every day until January 28th. Join the conversation on Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram, and be inspired by WOW Week.
