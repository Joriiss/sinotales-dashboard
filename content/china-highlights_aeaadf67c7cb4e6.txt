Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge is the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge spanning over two mountains in Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon. It is a must-see attraction if you want to experience something exciting in China. But do you know that it is not as easy as to visit the other attractions in Zhangjiajie due to its a little bit complicated ticket booking, and inconvenient transportation? Want to have a hassle-free trip to the Glass Bridge in Zhangjiajie? Continue to read. Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Facts • Length: 430 meters (1,410 feet) • Width: 6 meters (20 feet) • Height: 300 meters (984 feet) • Highlights: Walk on transparent glasses and enjoy a panoramic view of the stunning Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon. There is a platform for making a 300 meters (984 ft) bungee-jump. • Opening Hours: April - November: 7:30am-5:30pm, December - March: 8am to 4pm Bungee Jumping at Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Bungee jumping at Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge opens to visitors on December 15, 2020. It is the highest bungee platform in the world, which has a drop of 300 meters (984 feet). The bungee platform (allowing 30 people maximum) is in the middle of the glass bridge. The bungee jumping activity is operated by the company Bungy China. It's brother company Bungy Japan owns six platforms in Japan. Bungy China's parent company is one of the biggest bungy companies in the world. AJ Hackett, the father of bungee jumping, commented that the bungee jump at Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge is the most exciting, the most beautiful, and the highest bungee jump in the world. Come to Zhangjiajie to enjoy bungee jumping and the glass bridge: bag this record-breaking bungee while it's still the highest! A bungee jump costs around CN¥3,000 (≈US$420). The entering time is set when booking the ticket. Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Ticket C is only used in the afternoon. If you want to visit the bridge in the morning, you need to buy the Ticket B even though you don't want to see the canyon. You need to book a ticket in advance from the official website or through a travel agency, as no tickets are sold on the spot except for discounted tickets for kids, students, and senior people. The official website only accepts bookings made with a Chinese identity card number. Being a foreign visitor, you'd better book through a travel agency. Our best-value, customizable, 1-Day Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Tour includes tickets and access to the glass bridge. How to Tour Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge 1. Visitors can only take mobiles and small-sized bags on the bridge. There is a luggage storage area before the check-in entrance where you can leave your belongings for a fee. 2. If you plan to visit both the glass bridge and the canyon, we recommend you to visit the glass bridge first to avoid the detour. 3. The tour trail is one-way. The entrance is on the mountain and the exit is at the bottom. To retrieve your baggage, you have to return back to the entrance after your tour. To save time and energy, you are recommended to go there with just your wallet, passport, smartphone (or snapshot camera), and a small handbag. 4. The glass bridge is very popular with visitors from home and abroad. It is crowded most of the time, especially during national holidays. We recommend you start your day early and try to arrive before large bus tour groups. 5. Check the weather in advance as the glass bridge is likely to close in severe weather. 6. Do not attempt it if you have heart problems, high blood pressure, or a fear of heights. How to Get to Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge is 60km from the downtown of Zhangjiajie City. Zhangjiajie is easily accessible by airplane or train from the main cities of China, such as Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Xi'an, Changsha, Shenzhen. Check the details on Zhangjiajie transportation. It takes about 1 hour by car from either downtown Zhangjiajie or the Wulingyuan Scenic Area to the Glass Bridge. Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge vs Tianmen Mountain Glass Skywalks Tianmen Mountain Glass SkywalK There are three glass skywalks in Tianmen Mountain Scenic Area, which is about 85 kilometers away from Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge. Different from the Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge hanging in the air, Tianmen Mountain Glass Skywalks are built along the edges of the mountains' summits, clinging to the vertical cliffs at a height of over 1,400 meters (4,600 feet). Similar to the glass bridge, the glass skywalks will give you a thrilling feeling, like you are walking in the sky. If, unfortunately, you do not get a ticket for the glass bridge, you can try the glass skywalks. • Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is China's first national forest park, featuring Zhangjiajie's classic rocky tower scenery. • Zhangjiajie's Bailong Elevator: Do You Dare Ride It?
