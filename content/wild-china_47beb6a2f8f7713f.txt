Title: How to Get the Latest Updates on Travel Requirements in China | WildChina

As travel restrictions are continually changing due to COVID-19, it’s important to make sure you have the most up-to-date information on Travel Requirements in China. Luckily, there’s now an app for that (and chances are, you might already have it). Simply follow the step-by-step guide below to learn how to use the app. Step 1: Head to the Tencent travel service mini program (腾讯出行服务) by opening WeChat and scanning the QR code below. Step 2: Select your current location (left) and where you’re looking to travel (right): Step 3: If you’d like to translate the content to English, you can take a screenshot, send the image to yourself on File Transfer, long press the image and click “translate image text”: Step 4: To use this app in the future, simply open WeChat and swipe your screen down to locate the mini app in the dashboard.
