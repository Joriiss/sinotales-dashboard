Title: 6-Day Chongqing, Wulong and Wujiang Gallery Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chongqingtour/cq-3/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Exploring Trending Hotspots and Natural Splendors Immerse yourself in a week-long journey that perfectly combines urban exploration and natural wonder. This itinerary is crafted for those who wish to delve into the heart of the city, exploring iconic sites like the Hongya Cave and the Kuixing Building. Yet the journey continues beyond the city, leading you into the awe-inspiring natural beauty of the Three Natural Bridges and Fairy Mountain Forest Park in the Wulong area. Enhance your journey with distinctive experiences, like savoring a hotpot meal in a bomb shelter, and embarking on the mesmerizing Wujiang Gallery cruise. We are more than happy to customize your trip according to your preferences, whether you want to change the attractions or extend your journey to other destinations in China. Uncover the Magic of Chongqing's Urban Landscape Start a day-long journey through Chongqing's renowned destinations, including Kuixing Building, Liziba Station, and Hongya Cave. Immerse in the Karst Scenery of Wulong District Marvel at the three naturally formed stone bridges towering over two hundred meters high and the waterfall cascading down from a hundred-meter cliff. Stroll the Expansive Meadows of Fairy Mountain Forest Park Encounter grazing cows and horses, soak in the serene scenery, and enjoy a delightful train ride. Embark on the Wujiang Gallery Cruise: A Journey in Art On sunny days, the water turns a translucent, soothing green – a sight to behold. On cloudy days, a mystical fog shrouds the river, adding a layer of mystery and allure. Dive into the Rich History of Old Chongqing Journey through Gongtan Ancient Town and Chongqing's old streets, touch the age-old architecture, and follow the cobblestone paths of the ancients. Experience the Craziest Hotpot Dining in a Bomb Shelter Explore Chongqing's largest underground hotpot restaurant and savor the steaming hotpot in a naturally cool environment. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights DAY 1: Chongqing Arrival Upon your arrival, your guide and driver will warmly welcome you at the airport or train station entrance and escort you to your hotel in Chongqing. DAY 2: Chongqing | All-In-One City Highlights Start your day in Shancheng Alley, a place imbued with the distinctive character of Chongqing, where traditional residences meet a riverside walkway with stunning Jialing River views, punctuated by Western-style buildings. Experience authentic hotpot dining in a former bomb shelter! The shelters naturally provide a cool retreat, offering a stark contrast to the city's typically hot and humid climate. Proceed to Goose Neck Park, where traditional Chinese gardens and local leisure activities captivate. Capture the unique sight of a light rail crossing a building at Liziba Station and marvel at the enigmatic Kuixing Building, where the first and 22nd floors coexist. Venture into the heart of the city at Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street, a hub for shopping and gastronomy. Conclude your day at Hongya Cave, a dazzling 11-tiered stilt house complex. Illuminated at night, it offers stunning views of the city skyline, Qiansimen Bridge, and the two rivers. DAY 3: Chongqing to Wulong | Hiking in Wulong Karst Formation Embark on a three-hour journey to the captivating Wulong region in the morning, ready to immerse yourself in the natural splendors of Wulong National Park. Stand in awe of the Three Natural Bridges, a mesmerizing karst formation that has graced the silver screen in Transformers 4. This awe-inspiring trek spans 3 kilometers, with grand natural bridges and sky holes punctuating your journey, and a sprinkling of stairs to challenge your steps. In Longshuixia Gap, experience the thrill of navigating through a naturally formed, deep, and narrow canyon. Feel the spray of a majestic waterfall that cascades from a cliff a hundred meters high, a spectacle so close you can almost walk through it. DAY 4: Wulong to Pengshui | Fairy Mountain and Furong Cave Immerse yourself in the tranquility of Fairy Mountain National Forest Park, often likened to 'Eastern Switzerland' due to its vast grasslands and lush forests. A delightful mini-train ride is available, offering a respite from walking. Marvel at the majesty of Furong Cave, a million-year-old limestone marvel filled with rare, radiant stalactites illuminated by vibrant lights. After your exploration, a one-hour transfer will escort you to your hotel in the Pengshui area. DAY 5: Pengshui to Chongqing | Wujiang Gallery and Ancient Town Savor a one-hour antique-style boat cruise along the most picturesque stretch of the Wujiang River, known as Wujiang Gallery. In the sun's glow, the river winds like a shimmering emerald ribbon around mountain bases, framed by unique canyon peaks for a stunning landscape. After disembarking, a one-hour drive will transport you to Gongtan Ancient Town, a historical gem with over 1,700 years of history. Here, traditional cliffside courtyards and stilt houses are preserved, with ancient cobblestone paths and tiered steps adding to the charm of these old-world lanes. After visiting, a 3-hour drive will return you to Chongqing. DAY 6: Chongqing Departure Relish a private transfer to the airport or railway station, ready to journey to your next destination or return home.
