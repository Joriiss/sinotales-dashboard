Title: Guangzhou’s April Weather: 5-Day Forecast, Travel Advice

Source: China Highlights

It continues to warm up, and the monsoon season begins. The average low and high temperatures in April are respectively 19 °C (66 °F) and 25 °C (77 °F). Most people will find the weather uncomfortably damp with 201 mm of total rainfall and high humidity at 84%. It is the most humid time of year. However, it is warm enough to enjoy the local parks on sunny days. Clothing: A long-sleeve shirt is good for the day, and you might need a sweater or light water-resistant jacket for early morning and evening. Bring an umbrella. Guangzhou Average Daily Temperatures Based on Historical Data Things to Do and Events in April Attend the Canton Fair The China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair for short) happens twice a year, and the spring fair falls in April. It is the largest trade fair in China with the widest range of goods and the highest business turnover. Stroll in Yuexiu Park Yuexiu Park is a popular site for local people. In spring, visitors go there for short hikes and to admire the flowers. If you want to get closer to the local people, visit the park in the morning or at night and join in with the locals as they do their exercises. Don't forget to see the statue of five goats, which is the symbol of the city. Experience the European Aesthetics in Shamian Island Shamian Island used to be the concession of Britain and France, and the island still preserves architectural structures from that time. In spring, the blooming flowers decorate the island so that it becomes a fantastic floral world. On the island, you may walk around, find a characteristic bar or restaurant to relax in, or pray in a historical church or cathedral. Visit Chen Clan Ancestral Hall Chen Clan Ancestral Hall is one of the top favorite sites for foreign tourists in Guangzhou. The museum compound features Qing Dynasty era buildings that were built in the late 1800s. Enjoy the gardens, the architecture, and the decor of the many buildings. You can watch craftsmen create artwork on the grounds. April is a Shoulder Travel Season Guangzhou experiences a middle level of travel in April except during the Canton Fair days from the 12th to the end of the month. Flights and target hotel rooms are more difficult to get during those days.
