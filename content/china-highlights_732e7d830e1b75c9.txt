Title: One day Private Great Wall Hiking Trip at Jinshanling
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/beijingtour/bj-32/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Hike the Picturesque Great Wall at Jinshanling The Jinshanling section of the Great Wall is half repaired. On this trip, you will see both a repaired section of wall and the original wall in ruins. There will be no crowds around you, so you can get the most out of China's legendary landmark as you tread the Wall's centuries-old stone steps or walk through a ruined ancient watchtower. This one-day Jinshanling Great Wall hiking tour is private. Your own personal tour guide will hike with you all the way. Your guide will respond to your requests with flexible arrangements to make the most of your time. Walk on a Delicate Mixture of the Restored and Wild Great Wall The wild section gives you a sense of what it would have been like in its original glory. While the repaired section will transport you back in time to the heyday of imperial China. Take Great Photos of the Most Photogenic Section of the Great Wall The Great Wall at Jinshanling has the most beautiful natural scenery. You will get a wider variety of shots than you can at any other section of the Great Wall. Many professional Great Wall photos are taken at this section. An Expert Private Guide Will Lead the Tour Your guide will hike all the way with you. He/she will help you take photos, tell you the best location for photos, and give you information about the wall. It could be different due to the traffic on the day. 08am - 10:30amMeet your guide and drive to the Great Wall at Jinshanling Your private guide will meet you in your hotel lobby at 8:00 am. If you would like to start from the airport/train station, we will be happy to arrange a pick-up there. A comfortable vehicle with will be ready for your private Jinshanling Great Wall hiking tour. It takes about 2½ hours to travel to the Jinshanling section of the Great Wall from Beijing. 10:30am -2:00pmHike the Jinshanling Great Wall (3½ hours, 6 kilometers) Mount the wall via an unrepaired watchtower called Zhuangduokou. From the first tower at Zhuangduokou onward, you will see the repaired ancient watchtowers and sections of wall during your hike in the first 2 hours. Stop to take in the amazing view from one of the highest points you will reach during the day. See the Great Wall stretching out to the horizon… Encounter the wild part of the wall and see many broken watchtowers of various styles in the last 1½ hours of your hike on the Wall. Arriving at East Five-Eye Tower, you will then descend from the wall. It is possible to hike in the reverse direction. 1:30pm - 2:30pmHave lunch at a local restaurant There is a local restaurant at the base of the entrance to Jinshanling, offering traditional local dishes. Your guide will help you to order the dishes you like. 2:30pm – 5:00pmReturn to your hotel in Beijing Going back to your hotel in Beijing normally takes about 2½ hours.
