Title: Hong Kong Island Half day Tour

Hong Kong Island is the heart of Hong Kong; it's home to the most high-profile tourist sites, historical sites, mountain ranges, and shopping areas. Experience the stunning panoramic city skyline views from Victoria Peak Explore the special fishermen's living area - Aberdeen Enjoy the gentle waves and soft sand in Repulse Bay 9:00amHotel pick up In the morning, your private English-speaking guide will pick you up from your hotel at 9:00am. The starting time is flexible for you to decide. Your guide will call you at the hotel one day in advance or leave a message to double-check the collection time with you. 9:30amVictoria Peaks Visit Victoria Peak, which is also the highest peak of Hong Kong. It features the 112-old-year Peak Tram and it's the best place to overlook the city. Good to know: One way Peak Tram (8 minutes) is included. Your guide will purchase the ticket for you in advance to save your time, but during peak season, you will still need to queue for about 8 to 20 minutes to take the tram. Your experienced guide will adjust the itinerary flexibly to minimize the waiting time. 11:00am Repulse Bay The next spot is the "Golden Beach" in Hong Kong, Repulse Bay. Located at the southern part of the peak, Repulse Bay is the most representative bay of Hong Kong. Shaped as a crescent, the bay stretches a long way; the waves are gentle and the sand is soft. It is now one of Hong Kong's most exclusive residential areas. Good to know: If you are not interested in Repulse Bay, we can arrange Stanley Market for you instead. Once a fishing village, Stanley Market is now a great destination for purchasing various goods, including Chinese-style works of art, silk fabric, antiques, and king-size clothes. 12:00pm Aberdeen Our last stop is a special one; we have planned an experience for you in the fishermen's living area — Aberdeen. It has become a world-famous travel spot for its fishing village customs and seafood. A sampan ride for 15 minutes is included for you to have a close-up look at this unusual way of life. 13:30pmBack to Hotel At the end of the tour, you will be escorted back to your hotel.
