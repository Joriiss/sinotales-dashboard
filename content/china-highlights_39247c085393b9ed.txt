Weather: July and August are the hottest months of the year in Guangzhou. It is scorching hot and rainy with the highest temperature at noon often hitting above 35 °C (95 °F). The average temperature in summer is 29 °C (84 °F), and at night the low temperature is still about 26 °C (79 °F). It rains most of the days of the month, and the humidity is 82%. Clothing: Bring summer clothes such as shorts, T-shirts and skirts. Wear sun protection such as hats, sunglasses and sun cream. Try drinking the local herbal tea. It's very popular in Guangzhou and is believed to be able to relieve heat and dampness inside the body. We recommended bringing an umbrella for the afternoon thunderstorms and occasional tropical storms. See the current weather and forecast>> Depending on the weather, you might want to go to a museum or visit a local outdoor attraction. The Sun Yat Sen Museum is a small museum in a park with pleasant surroundings. It is a place to learn about China's history and about the life of the "Father of Modern China." It is a short walk from Yuexiu Park. Relax in Yuexiu Park: Walk around and enjoy the summer weather in Guangzhou's biggest city park. It is more than 200 acres in area, and it features hills, many gardens, and three ponds. It is designed to be both beautiful and interesting. You can exercise there and watch the locals do morning exercises, play instruments, practice martial arts and walk around. Subway Line 2 goes there. July Trade Fairs: The trade fairs in July include the Fastener Trade Show, the Building Decoration Fair, the International Packing Box Show, and others. Shoulder Travel Month July isn't a high travel month for tourism and travel. Hotel rooms and train tickets and flight tickets are usually available.
