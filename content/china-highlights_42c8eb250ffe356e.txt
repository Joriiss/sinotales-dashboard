Serving as neighboring cities, Beijing and Tianjin are well-connected by 230 pairs of high-speed trains, offering a transportation option comparable to buses. The fastest train takes only 21 minutes to finish the 122 kilometers trip. These high-speed trains operate between Beijing South and Tianjin railway stations, some make a stop at either Tianjin West or Tianjin South railway stations. A few of these trains continue their journey to Tanggu and Binhai stations in the coastal area. There are two main types of high-speed trains on this line, G trains, and C trains, with similar speeds and a small price difference. The cost for a second-class seat ranges from US$ 4 - 11, differing based on the specific route and train type.
