Title: Beijing Tour for Family with Kids

Chinese Cultural Immersion with a Twist This tour is designed for families who want to avoid the hassle of planning sightseeing in a super metropolis, bridging cultural gaps and balancing diverse interests. During this 5-day tour, your family will visit historical sites in an interesting way and enjoy cultural activities together to create unique moments perfect for Instagram stories and family legacy albums. Let us design a Beijing experience that's as unique as your family. Contact us today to start crafting your story. Discover the Mythical Animals in the Forbidden City Explore the Forbidden City in a way that's specially designed for families with kids. Walk on the Great Wall and Slide Down from It Visit a less-crowded Great Wall section at Mutianyu and try an exciting toboggan ride. Practice a Family Morning Excercise in the Temple of Heaven Feel the relaxing lifestyle of the local people and learn doing Tai Chi at the Temple of Heaven where the emperors prayed for good harvests. Dress in Traditional Han Clothing for a Memorable Photo Enjoy an immersive sightseeing experience at the historical sites. Step back in time to ancient China. Say Hi to the Chinese Celebrity Panda Known for its Escape Antics Visit the Beijing Zoo, one of the top places to see pandas outside of the Chengdu Panda Base. The pandas here, especially those with the "Meng" name, are beloved by Chinese netizens. Cheer for Fearless Monks as They Battle Imaginary Foes Immerse your family in the ancient art of Chinese martial arts with a captivating live performance by the legendary Shaolin Monks. Experience the Trendy Culture and Creativity of China's Young Generation Collect trendy toys and join interactive games at Pop Mart City Park, and enjoy the surprise of blind boxes. Explore the 798 Art District with repurposed industrial buildings, and feel the charm of contemporary art. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. Day 1 Arrival in Beijing Your private guide will meet you in the arrivals hall at the airport or at the train station's exit. You will be taken in a private, professionally chauffeured car directly to you hotel. Your guide will help you to check in. Day 2 The Forbidden City and Hutong Start your journey in Beijing with Tian'anmen Square (the 'Gate of Heavenly Peace' square). As the world's largest public square, it is an essential attraction for school kids' field trips in Beijing. Walk northward from the square to enter the renowned Forbidden City. Besides the main palaces on the axis, you will also explore some places that are not on the touristy route and interest kids more. These include the library of the princes, the Archery Pavilion (the tools of the army gallery), and the Hall of Clocks and Watches. Conclude your Forbidden City exploration with a bird's eyes view of the Forbidden City on the top of Jingshan Hill. In the afternoon, explore the hutongs - the narrow alleys between rows of quadrangle dwellings. Pay a visit to a local family who live in a traditional and original quadrangle dwelling. There, you can try your hand at Chinese calligraphy or paper cutting. During the tour today you can participate in a Hanfu photo safari, dressing up in ancient costumes against historic backdrops and taking unique family pictures Day 3 The Temple of Heaven and the Great Wall Visit the Temple of Heaven, the royal altar where emperors worshiped heaven during the Ming and Qing dynasties. Go across the local people doing their morning exercises, including tai chi, taking their singing caged birds for some fresh air, writing in calligraphy on the paving slabs (with long brush and water), and playing keep-it-up with kick shuttlecocks. A tai chi master will be ready to teach your group. Join the locals and enjoy some interesting morning exercises as a family. Move on to the Great Wall at Mutianyu, which is about a 1½-hour drive from the Temple of Heaven. Take a cable car up and your personal guide will walk with you on the wall to give you some information and to help you if you want to take some photos. You will have some free time to walk around. Then take a toboggan ride down the Great Wall. Drive back to downtown Beijing and enjoy the famous Peking roast duck dish for dinner in a time-honored brand restaurant. Day 4 Discover the Summer Palace and Beijing Zoo Your Beijing discovery tour will continue with a visit to the Summer Palace and the Beijing Zoo. The Summer Palace was used as a summer retreat for the imperial court, providing a nice place to escape from the summer heat. You can climb the hill or take a stroll in the charming decorated walkways before your cruise in a dragon-shaped boat on Kunming Lake. In the afternoon, engage young explorers at Beijing Zoo, home to giant pandas and rare species. Conclude the day with a Kung Fu performance, showcasing discipline and grace in a family-friendly show. Day 5 The Trendy Culture and Creativity and Departure Dive into creativity at 798 Art District, where street art and avant-garde installations spark imagination. Then, step into a fantasy world with giant character sculptures, vibrant murals, and limited-edition merchandise in Pop Mart City Park. Snap photos with life-sized Molly or explore the Dimoo Dream Garden, a floral paradise. Say goodbye to Beijing and you will be transferred to the airport or train station according to your schedule.
