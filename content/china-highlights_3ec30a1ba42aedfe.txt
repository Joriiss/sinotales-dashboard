Title: 3-Day Wuyishan Refreshing Tour with Tea Experience
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/xiamen/tours/wuyishan-refreshing.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Relax in the Breezes of Wuyishan Located in the borderland of Fujian and Jiangxi provinces, UNESCO World Heritage Site Wuyi Mountain has glorious Danxia landforms and rich cultural gems, making it an ideal destination for a refreshing holiday. Come with us and have a relaxing mountain vacation! Get prepared for a close encounter with natural magnificence, taste world-famous local tea, and revitalize yourself with the fresh mountain air and a hot-spring bath! Walk the mountain paths, gaze up the "Sky Cleft", refresh your mind and body Unload the luggage, check in at your hotel, and enter Wuyishan National Park. Breath the fresh air, wander in the mountains, and embrace this natural gift to urban people. Meet a brand-new version of yourself! Take a bamboo raft to float down the picturesque Nine Twists Stream A walking tour — not relaxing enough? Here is another more relaxing way of sightseeing — a bamboo raft on crystal-clear Jiuquxi ("Nine Twists Stream"). Spring and autumn are the ideal times, when it is not too cold or hot. Phones and cameras at the ready (but carefully)! Appreciate celebrated tea plantations, infuse in tea culture, and sip the renowned local tea Wuyishan is a Mecca for tea travelers: it is the birthplace of black tea and also famous for its unique rock tea. Follow us into Da Hong Pao ("Big Red Robe" — the king of Oolong tea) Scenic Area and Yanzike ("Swallows' Nest" — famous for its rock tea) Tea Plantation. Enter the rich world of tea culture! Unwind in peace and partake in an open-air hot spring bath at your hotel after the day's exploration Feeling tired after a day in the mountains? No worries! We've chosen excellent and quiet accommodation with private open-air hot-spring pools for you to relax in. Spring are autumn are most suitable for hot spring bathing! Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Wuyishan Arrival | Pick-up and Sightseeing in the Afternoon Your personal guide and driver will meet you at the railway station in Wuyishan. You will be transferred to the South Gate of Wuyishan Scenic Area for sightseeing in the afternoon. Beautiful mountain paths and Yixiantian ("One Line Sky" or "A Thread of Sky") are recommended. (Yixiantian is not for travelers who suffer from claustrophobia, we could skip that if needed) DAY 2 Wuyishan-Tea-Theme Day Trip Being a sacred place for tea lovers, Wuyishan has become world famous for decades. Day 2 is a full day devoted to visiting two famous local tea attractions, so that you can have a better understanding of the local tea culture and taste the most authentic flavors of Wuyishan tea, which cannot be experienced any other way. DAY 3 Bamboo Rafting and Departure | Fun and Farewell, Wuyishan! Put on a lifejacket and embark on bamboo rafting on Jiuquxi — the most picturesque stream in Wuyishan. Don't worry about having to stand up: you'll be seated on a laid-back rattan chair attached to the raft, from which you can leisurely enjoy the rich natural surroundings. We'll finish the tour by driving you to Wuyishan North Railway Station for your bullet train back home.
