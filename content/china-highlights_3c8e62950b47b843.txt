Title: 8-Day China Family Winter Tour (Beijing–Harbin)

Enjoy China's Top Snow Activities in Beijing and Harbin This trip starts in the capital. In Beijing, explore China's rich history and culture from the Forbidden City to the Great Wall. You will then head to Harbin to begin your ice and snow extravaganza. There, plentiful exciting ice activities will give your family an unforgettable and spectacular winter vacation. This private tour is fully customizable. Feel free to ask us to modify the destinations, activities, or length of the tour according to your preferences. Our travel advisors are ready to personalize your family vacation based on your requests. Suggested 8-Day Family Tour Day Highlights 1-4 Beijing Imperial and Historical Sites include Forbidden City, Great Wall and Summer Palace 5-8 Harbin The Ice and Snow World, Polaland Park, and Siberian Tiger Park Explore Harbin's Stunning Ice Sculpture Park — Ice and Snow World This wintry family-fun paradise has large man-made skiing areas, large ice slides, a children's playground, Christmas square, maze, large inflated buildings, and much more. Travel Through the Arctic and Antarctic At Harbin Polarland, you can enjoy breathtaking human-whale interactions, watch polar bears swim up close, and observe the communal life of Antarctic penguins. Feed the Siberian Tiger on a Safari bus Taking a bus with wire-mesh to see the tigers roam freely in the fields, challenge your courage to feed meat to them. Enter the Fairy-tale World of Europe The Volga Manor is a Russian-style cultural theme park. The beautiful architecture and gardens make it feel as if one has stepped into the fairy tale world of Europe. Experience the Daily Life of Beijing Children Visit a family in a hutong (imperial-era neighborhood) and experience the traditional lifestyle of Chinese children. Your kids could learn Chinese calligraphy, play with a Chinese yo-yo, and taste sugar painting. Walk on a Child-Friendly Section of the Great Wall The Mutianyu section of the Great Wall is ranked top on TripAdvisor due to its picturesque scenery and beautifully-restored architecture. We arrange a round-trip cable car to maximize your time on the wall. Discover the Secrets of Chinese Emperors at the Forbidden City The Forbidden City was home to 24 emperors. We will show you their hidden halls and relate their most interesting stories. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful China family tour for you. Day 1Arrive in Beijing | Airport Transfer Upon your arrival in Beijing, your private tour guide and driver will greet you with warm smiles. They will escort you to your hotel, allowing you to rest after your long flight. Day 2Beijing | The Great Wall and the Temple of Heaven Breakfast & Lunch After a 1½-hour drive from downtown Beijing, you will reach Mutianyu. This is the most child-friendly section of the Great Wall, with a pristine 2½-kilometer (1½-mile) section that was fully restored in 1986. Use the round-way cable car to save time and energy. At the Wall, your guide will give an introduction and tips for the best experience. Then you will have about 2 hours free time to explore. Later, head to the Temple of Heaven, a sacred site and architectural masterpiece. Many locals go to the Temple of Heaven to engage in traditional activities such as tai chi, calligraphy, painting, and other cultural practices. Day 3Beijing to Harbin | The Life of Royalty and Commoners Breakfast & Lunch Begin your day at iconic Tian'anmen Square. Then, continue north to visit the majestic Forbidden City to explore ancient Chinese imperial architecture. Your knowledgeable tour guide will explain its historical with stories of the emperors and help you uncover the secrets of its royal architecture. Visit a hutong family to discover the traditional lifestyle of local people, and let your kids experience how Chinese children study and play. Taste the snacks they love. Day 4Beijing to Harbin | The Summer Palace and a Bullet Train Breakfast The Summer Palace is the best-preserved and largest imperial garden area in China. Stroll through its lush gardens, admire the stunning architecture, and get a glimpse into China's imperial history at the Long Corridor and the Marble Boat. Take a bullet train to Harbin after lunch. Day 5Harbin | The Ice and Snow World and Sun Island Breakfast & Lunch Visit Harbin Sun Island Scenic Area, and enjoy the charming ice or snow sculptures and architecture. Take memorable photos with the giant snowman and the sunset at 'Diamond Sea', an ice harvesting site on the Songhua River. Spend your evening in the Ice and Snow World. Welcome to a family paradise where boredom isn't an option! The Eastern Scenic Area offers ice and snow activities, including large man-made ski slopes, ice slides, a children's playground, a Christmas square, a maze, inflatable structures, and more for endless entertainment. Day 6Harbin | Harbin Polarland and Zhaolin Park Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Harbin Polarland is another must-visit park for a family with young kids. As the world's first polar-themed park, it offers a unique experience with attractions and exhibits featuring polar animals like penguins, polar bears, and arctic wolves. At lunchtime, join an old Harbin food tour in the Laodaowai area, where Harbin originated. You'll sample six kinds of traditional Harbin food while exploring the vibrant streets filled with local life. As night falls, Zhaolin Park becomes iridescent and lively when the ice lanterns light up. Enjoy an evening time in a magical winter wonderland. Day 7Harbin | Siberian Tigers and Volga Manor Breakfast & Lunch Discover Harbin Siberian Tiger Park for a thrilling experience. We have arranged a mini bus safari for you, allowing you to get up close to the tigers and even feed them. Experience a slice of Russian heritage at Volga Manor. The manor replicates traditional Russian architecture, including a wooden church and bridges, set amidst a beautiful landscape. You can enjoy activities like horse-drawn carriage rides, ice skating, and exploring various exhibitions. Day 8Harbin | Departure Breakfast Our guide will escort you to the airport.
