As home to 26 officially recognized ethnic minority groups, Yunnan is renowned for its picturesque landscapes and diverse ethnic cultures. So where should you start your trip in Yunnan? How many days are needed to explore Yunnan's top attractions? This article will help you plan a great itinerary in Yunnan. • How Many Days to Spend in Yunnan • 5–6 Days in Yunnan • 7–8 Days in Yunnan • 9 Days in Yunnan • 10+ days in Yunnan How Many Days to Spend in Yunnan The following are popular tourist destinations in Yunnan with suggested travel durations: • Kunming (best gateway city): 1–2 days • Dali (ancient towns and Erhai): 2–4 days • Lijiang (snow mountains and old towns): 2–3 days • Shangri-La (plateau scenery and Tibetan culture): 2–4 days Therefore, for a first-timer to Yunnan, it's recommended to spend at least 7 days there to cover the highlights, including Kunming, Dali, Lijiang, and Shangri-La's essences. If you want to look for more destinations, here are some suggestions for you: • Xishuangbanna: 2–3 days (for tropical scenery) • Yuanyang: 1–2 days (for rice terraces) • Tengchong: 2–3 days (for volcanic landforms and hot springs) • Jianshui: 1–2 days (for ancient architecture) • Nujiang: 2–3 days (for canyons and ancient villages) Ultimately, the ideal duration of your stay in Yunnan would depend on your interests, budget, and schedule. With 5 or 6 days in Yunnan, we recommend visiting two or three cities. Option 1: Recommended for First-Timers If you are visiting Yunnan for the first time and want to see as much as possible, we recommend visiting Kunming, Dali, and Lijiang: • Day 1: Kunming arrival • Day 2: Visit the Stone Forest and transfer to Dali • Day 3: Dali sightseeing (Dali Old Town, Erhai, and Bai villages) • Day 4: Transfer to Lijiang and visit Lijiang Ancient Town • Day 5: Lijiang sightseeing (Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and a Naxi culture experience) • Day 6: Departure This 6-day itinerary could let you visit three of the four most popular cities in Yunnan (if you want to include Shangri-La, you could refer to the suggestions below for 7–8 days). This itinerary is well-paced without feeling rushed. You wouldn't miss out on the most classic attractions. You would take a leisurely bike ride alongside Erhai to enjoy the wind and flowery meadows. Take a cable car ride to Jade Dragon Snow Mountain to see the snow and meadows. Also, take a stroll in the ancient towns to explore markets and shops and taste some local food. At the same time, you would also immerse yourself in some cultural experiences to help you get to know Yunnan better, such as trying Bai tie-dyeing, learning some Naxi characters, and enjoying a piece of old Naxi music by visiting a shaman's house. Option 2: Recommended for Hiking Lovers If you want a slower pace or a deeper exploration, then we recommend visiting two cities, such as Lijiang and Shangri-La. • Day 1: Lijiang arrival • Day 2: One-day hiking tour of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain • Day 3: Transfer to Shangri-La with a stop at Tiger Leaping Gorge and a 2-hour hike across an alpine meadow • Days 4–5: Shangri-La exploration (Songzanlin Monastery, Napa Lake, and a Nixi Tibetan village) • Day 6: Departure In this itinerary, you would have the chance to see awesome landscapes on a hiking trip and learn about old and mysterious cultures. In Lijiang, besides the ancient towns, your guide would arrange a hike of about 12.5 km (8 miles) through the mountainside bushes, where you would wade across a little stream and enjoy exclusive views of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain that only hikers could see. On the way to Shangri-La, you would journey through Tiger Leaping Gorge and discover the best-kept secret of Haba Snow Mountain, where you would take a 2-hour hike across an alpine meadow, which offers exclusive views of Haba Snow Mountain and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. If you are interested in ethnic life, then visit a Nixi Tibetan village and a Tibetan family's house in Shangri-La. Yunnan is an ideal destination for school trips, especially during the summer, as it offers a cool respite from the heat along with numerous opportunities for outdoor adventures and cultural experiences. Children could cycle along the shores of Er Lake while enjoying the distant view of Cang Mountain and hike in Shaxi Ancient Town to learn about its historical architecture and the history of the Tea Horse Road. A hike through Tiger Leaping Gorge, one of the world's deepest canyons, offers the chance to witness the roaring river and feel the power of nature. Additionally, cultural experiences should be included to immerse the children in local life, such as trying the Bai ethnic group's tie-dyeing process, visiting the former residence of American explorer Joseph Rock to understand his connection to Yunnan, and experiencing a Tibetan blessing ceremony in Shangri-La, where they could also create a thangka painting as a keepsake. Often overlooked as merely a transportation hub, most itineraries in Kunming only allow for a quick visit to the Stone Forest before heading to other cities. Kunming, known as the Spring City, has much more to offer, especially during its blooming season from March to April. • Days 1–2: Kunming (the Stone Forest and cherry blossoms on Yiliang Mountain) • Day 3: Kunming to Lijiang (admire crabapple blossoms at Puji Temple) • Day 4: Lijiang (Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and peach blossom fields at Lashihai) • Day 5: Lijiang to Shangri-La (Tiger Leaping Gorge) • Day 6: Shangri-La sightseeing and then to Dali • Day 7: Dali (Dali Old Town, Xizhou Town, and make flower cakes) • Day 8: Dali to Kunming for sightseeing (Daguan Pavilion and Nanping Pedestrian Street) • Day 9: Departure The highlight of this itinerary is the opportunity to experience the vibrant colors of Yunnan, making it particularly suitable for nature lovers. The best time to undertake this journey is during spring (March to April). In addition to the must-see landmarks, the itinerary includes several flower-viewing spots that are perfect for the season. In spring, Kunming is at its most beautiful, with the city awash in blue jacaranda blooms. Yiliang Mountain is covered in cherry blossoms. Daguan Park features a stunning display of cherry, peach, and magnolia blossoms, creating a kaleidoscope of colors from the top of Daguan Pavilion. The ancient towns of Lijiang and Dali are also more vibrant, with flowers blooming along their streets. Don't miss the enchanting peach blossom fields near Lashi Lake and the vast canola flower fields in Xizhou, Dali. The following are popular destinations to combine with Yunnan. If you could adapt well to altitude sickness and are interested in Tibetan culture, Tibet is undoubtedly the best choice. For those who particularly enjoy scenic views and road trips, we recommend Chengdu, driving through Xinduqiao and Daocheng to reach Shangri-La, and then continuing your journey in Yunnan. If you are concerned about acclimatizing to high altitudes but still love natural landscapes, Zhangjiajie would be a great choice. For those who prefer urban cultural experiences and culinary delights, consider visiting Chongqing or Chengdu. For a trip combined with Yunnan, we suggest 12 to 14 days with 6 to 7 days in Yunnan and 6 to 7 days in another destination.
