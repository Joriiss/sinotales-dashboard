Title: 5-Day Shanghai and Hangzhou High Tech Tour

Trip to China's High-Tech Frontier Journey into the future with our exclusive 5-day tour, a deep dive into China's groundbreaking technological landscape. This isn't just a sightseeing trip; it's a hands-on exploration of the innovations shaping tomorrow. This tour is perfect for tech enthusiasts and children who are curious about the cutting edge of human innovation. To add more destinations to your tour and explore how technology influences daily life in China, don't hesitate to tell your personal trip advisor. Dive into the Future Playground Where Tech Giants Spark Imagination and Innovation Come Alive. Get hands-on with Hangzhou's tech giants—like Alibaba and Unitree Robotics—and plug straight into the digital age at the Wensan Future Technology Center. Discover the Secret to Shanghai Tower's Stability — A Massive Damper Balancing the Tallest Landmark. Get up close to this futuristic marvel and discover how Shanghai's tallest landmark stands strong above the clouds. Strap in at Full Sense VR Park for a Wildly Fun VR Ride. Get ready to laugh, play, and explore at the VR park that transforms your favorite Chinese legends into thrilling adventures you can live! Ride a Self-Driving Bus and See How Technology Is Steering China into the Future. Feel the thrill of a self-driving ride where the streets glide by and the future unfolds right outside your window. Savor the Flavors at a Stylish Local Market Bursting with Vibrant Culinary Delights. Unlike traditional markets, this one is modern, clean, and buzzing with energy, showcasing the city's vibrant urban spirit. Jump into the World of Imagination and Joy at Shanghai Legoland. Visiting Shanghai Legoland will be a vibrant and thrilling experience, with colorful Lego creations, interactive rides, and imaginative attractions that delight both kids and adults alike. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1 Shanghai Arrival Welcome to Shanghai! To ensure your arrival is stress-free, your private tour guide will meet you in the arrivals hall and transfer you to your hotel in a private car. Your luggage and hotel check-in will be taken care of. Day 2 Shanghai | City Exploration Breakfast & Lunch Begin your journey at China's tallest building, the Shanghai Tower. Take the world's fastest elevators, which will whisk you to the top in just one minute. From the observation deck, you'll be treated to a stunning 360-degree view of Shanghai. Fewer people know that near the top of Shanghai Tower hangs a massive golden damper weighing over 1,000 tons. This giant tuned mass damper helps the tower resist swaying from strong winds and earthquakes. Continue your journey up to the 125th floor, where you can see this engineering marvel up close. Walking around the Bund, the historic waterfront promenade, you will admire the row of "Museum of Buildings" featuring 19th to 20th century. Ready for a thrill? Step into Shanghai's VR theme park, where virtual reality drops you right into the action! One moment you're battling through scenes from hit Chinese TV shows, the next you're diving into epic historical adventures. It's not just watching — it's living the story. Afterwards, swing by Shanghai's newest landmark — the eye-catching Louis Vuitton ship-shaped store — before heading to Nanjing Road. As one of the city's busiest pedestrian streets, it's alive with neon lights, local snacks, street performers, and the non-stop buzz of shoppers and sightseers. Day 3 Shanghai | Legoland Fun Breakfast The world's largest LEGOLAND opened on July 5, 2025, built with more than 85 million LEGO bricks. The park features record-breaking attractions, including the tallest LEGO minifigure ever at 26 meters high, the world's first ride weaving through towering LEGO minifigures, and the debut of the LEGO Monkie Kid live show. Step into a universe where creativity, culture, thrills, and storytelling come alive. Build boats, battle alongside NINJAGO heroes, or cruise through LEGO-inspired water towns—every corner offers something magical to discover. If Legoland doesn't suit your taste, Disneyland makes an excellent alternative, offering a full day of fun for both kids and adults. You can change it after you send the booking. Day 4 Shanghai to Hangzhou | Future Forward Breakfast & Lunch It's very convenient to travel from Shanghai to Hangzhou, taking a 1-hour high speed train. You will begin your visit at the iconic West Lake, enjoying a leisurely ride on a pedal boat. It's the perfect opportunity to capture stunning photos of the lake's bridges, pagodas, and surrounding hills. Then, head to Wen'er Market and experience a living gallery of Hangzhou's vibrant lifestyle. Stroll past colorful stalls filled with fresh produce, taste good local food, and soak in the city's authentic, energetic spirit. Get ready to take a ride on a driverless bus. Watch the wheel turn on its own as the vehicle glides seamlessly through the streets. Continues to the Alibaba Creative Ecological Park, once the headquarters of the global internet giant. Uncovering the story of how Alibaba and Hangzhou grew together, fueling the spirit of innovation that defines the city. Step into the Wensan Future Technology Center, where tomorrow comes alive. Explore breakthroughs from Hangzhou's tech giants—Alibaba, Unitree Robotics, Deepseek, BrainCo, and more—through dazzling displays and hands-on experiences. It's a high-energy playground of innovation, imagination, and the digital age. Day 5 Hangzhou Departure Breakfast As your journey draws to an end, your dedicated guide and driver will escort you to Hangzhou Airport. Alternatively, you may choose to depart from Shanghai, depending on your flight schedule. We look forward to welcoming you again for another unforgettable adventure in China.
