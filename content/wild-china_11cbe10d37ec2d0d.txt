At WildChina, we pride ourselves in providing the highest levels of luxury travel and adventure design, making the real China accessible and delivering life-changing experiences to people from all over the world. So, we are beyond pleased to share that we’ve been nominated for Travel + Leisure’s 2019 World’s Best Awards in the Tour Operators and Safari Outfitters category. We know China intimately, from its kaleidoscope of cultures and people to all the stories they have to share, and we thrive on punching through the tourist bubble and bringing our valued clients along for the ride. It is this passion for experiencing China differently that has earned us the opportunity to compete for the ultimate title of World’s Best Tour Operator. The winner is decided on by voters, like you! If you believe that your WildChina journey is one of the best tours you’ve ever been on, or that we are one of the best adventure services currently in existence, then we would love for you to take a few minutes to help us earn this honor. RELATED READING: WildChina Journey Named a Nat Geo Tour of a Lifetime Vote for WildChina in just a few easy steps: Go to www.tlworldsbest.com/vote Use your email address and a password to register. (No spam, only voting!) The survey will ask you where you’ve traveled and which categories you’d like to review. To skip right to voting for WildChina just: Click on ASIA, then hit NEXT. Click on CHINA, then NEXT. Click on TOUR OPERATORS AND SAFARI OUTFITTERS, then NEXT. Way down near the bottom, click on “WILDCHINA” and hit NEXT. Now you can begin telling the world how you feel about your experiences with WildChina. Be sure to mention what you loved most about your wild experiences in the Middle Kingdom. RELATED READING: WildChina Nominated for Virtuoso Sustainable Tourism Leadership Award Voting is open until March 4th, 2019, and the winners will be announced in the August 2019 issue of Travel + Leisure. Once you’ve submitted your review, you’ll also be entered into Travel + Leisure’s giveaway* – an opportunity to win a trip for two valued at USD$10,000 and planned by a Travel + Leisure A-List Agent. We are honored to be able to share China with you, and forever grateful for you helping us to continue to share this beautiful and diverse country with the world. — *Please be aware that the giveaway is only open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia. Individuals living outside these areas can still vote and rate Travel + Leisure’s 2019 World’s Best, but you won’t be entered into the giveaway and, therefore, will not be eligible to win.
