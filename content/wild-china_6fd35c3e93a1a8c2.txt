What’s the next big thing in Asia adventure travel? At the PATA Adventure Travel and Responsible Tourism Conference, WildChina CEO, Mei Zhang joined fellow industry leaders on the podcast, TalkTravelAsia to discuss changes in Asia travel and what their picks are for the best multi-destination Asia adventure. Adventure travel can mean different things to different people. Some see it as a chance to get outdoors and test their physical endurance, and others find the same level of adventure when immersing themselves in unfamiliar cultures. TalkTravelAsia’s guests point out that a common theme for everyone is getting ourselves a bit beyond our comfort-zones. While Asia has always been known as a destination rich with opportunities for cultural exploration, Mei points out that it is only in the last few years being recognized as a place full of outdoor exploration opportunities as well. For those looking for an adventure by any definition, Asia offers experiences like nowhere else in the world. Head to TalkTravelAsia.com to hear the whole discussion including each expert’s suggestions for your next big Asia adventure. Inspired to set out on your own adventure?
