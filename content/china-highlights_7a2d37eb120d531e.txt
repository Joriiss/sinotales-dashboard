Changsha is regarded as "a city of entertainment" in China, due to a series of famous entertainment programmes held by Hunan Television and the rich and colorful nightlife. Watching the folk performances, pubs, cafés, singing halls and dance halls and so on are all available in Changsha. Watching Performances in Theatres Many Changsha people take theater as their first choice for the rich and wonderful performances. Qindao Theatre and Hongtaiyang Theater are the most famous two in Changsha.
