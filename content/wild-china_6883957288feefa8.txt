We’re thrilled to announce that WildChina has received the 2018 TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence. This award is given to businesses that “consistently achieve outstanding traveler reviews on TripAdvisor,” and we’re so thankful for the 88 five-star reviews we have received so far. We couldn’t have done this without our wonderful WildChina clients whose consistent feedback has allowed us to continue to be a top-rated China tour and activity provider.
