The main tourist destinations along China's Silk Road Itinerary 1. 14-Day China Silk Road Culture Tour from Xi'an to Kashgar This itinerary is designed for culture lovers who are interested in China's Silk Road. Almost all its highlights are included in this 14-day journey. From east to west, you will delve into the China Silk Road's historical culture and art, amazing natural landscapes, and exotic customs. • Days 1–3: Xi'an • Day 4: Zhangye • Days 5–7: Dunhuang and Jiayuguan Pass • Day 8: Turpan • Days 9–10: Urumqi • Days 11–14: Kashgar Week 1: Historical Sites and Marvelous Landscapes Your trip would start from Xi'an, and you would spend the first week on Xi'an, Zhangye, and Dunhuang. Deeply explore the culture of China's Silk Road, marveling at the precious cultural heritage and wonders of nature. Highlights includes the Terracotta Army, Mogao Caves, and Rainbow Mountains. Our guests in Zhangye Danxia National Park Week 2: Xinjiang Highlights and Exotic Charm The second and final week of your trip covers remote Xinjiang. Xinjiang was an important region on the ancient Silk Road. Explore its Silk Road cultural relics and experience its exotic charm in desert-bound Turpan, capital Urumqi, and frontier trading hub Kashgar, which is the westernmost city of China. In addition to visiting the classic highlights of the Silk Road, such as the Mogao Caves and Rainbow Mountains, you would explore the Tibetan Buddhist culture and stunning natural scenery of Gannan. • Days 1–2: Lanzhou, Gansu's capital on the Yellow River • Days 3–4: Xiahe, home to Labrang Tibetan Buddhist Monastery • Days 5–6: Langmusi, a picturesque Tibetan town in Gannan • Days 7–8: Zhangye Rainbow Mountains • Days 9–10: Dunhuang desert and Jiayuguan's fortified pass • Days 11–12: Turpan vineyards and ancient relics • Days 13–14: Urumqi, Xinjiang's capital Gannan Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture is famous for having a similar feel to Tibet, but it doesn't require a Travel Permit. Visit the biggest Tibetan Monastery in Gansu Province there, where you would have chance to chat with the Tibetan monks to learn more about Tibetan Buddhism. With this itinerary, you would experience the diverse communities and cultures of the Tibetan, Hui and Uyghur peoples. Moreover, there are grand prairies and amazing plateau landscapes in Gannan that still have cool weather in summer (from June to August), so Gannan is an ideal summer destination to escape the heat.
