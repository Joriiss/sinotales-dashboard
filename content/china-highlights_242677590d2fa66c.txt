Dunhuang has a very arid, temperate continental climate — it's in the Gobi Desert! The desert weather is usually dry, and temperature differences are big. Dunhuang has a warm and windy spring, a hot summer, a cool and mild autumn, and a freezing winter. The average temperature range for a (middle-season) day in Dunhuang is roughly 3–19°C (37–66°F). The coldest month is January with average daily low temperatures of around -15°C (5°F). Conversely, average daily highs are 35°C (95°F) in July. Sunshine in Dunhuang is strong and long in daily duration, making it easy for you to get sunburned. So use a parasol or clothing to cover your skin, or put on sunscreen, when you go out. Also, as Dunhuang has Muslim and Buddhist areas, modest clothing is advised. • Best Times to Visit Dunhuang • Dunhuang in Spring • Dunhuang in Summer • Dunhuang in Autumn • Dunhuang in Winter Best Times to Visit Dunhuang The best times to visit Dunhuang are from May to October. spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) are peak travel seasons, as the weather is neither too cold nor hot. You may feel cool and comfortable outside as you enjoy the spectacular natural scenery in Dunhuang. Dunhuang in Spring (March to May) Temperature averages: March 0–16°C (32–60°F), April 6–23°C (43–73°F), May 11–28°C (52–82°F) Good to know: Spring in Dunhuang is windy and virtually rainless. Occasionally there will be a sandstorm. March and April are less crowded than May. Both outdoor and indoor activities are suitable. Clothing: Bring a jacket/coat, shirts/sweaters/hoodies, and other clothing appropriate to the month's temperatures above, factoring in a possible wind chill of -10°C (14°F) or more. There are big temperature differences between morning and afternoon, afternoon and evening, so bring layers that can be stowed in a backpack and put on again as the day goes on. Dunhuang in Winter (December to February) Temperature averages: December -13–1°C (9–34°F), January -15–-1°C (5–30°F), February -10–5°C (14–41°F). Good to know: An uncrowded environment and favorable prices are the advantages when traveling in winter. Indoor heating is available, but you'll need to wrap up warm when going out for sightseeing. Snow is rare and light: less than 4 mm (0.2 in) a month. Clothing: winter boots, sweaters, overcoat/ down jacket, gloves, hats, and scarfs: basically, desert winter clothing with moisturizer for the dryness and shades for the glare. It sometimes snows in Dunhuang's winter. You may see startling snow-covered desert landscapes and there are plenty of interesting things to do indoors. • Go to the Mingsha Mountains (Singing Sand Dunes) and maybe see remarkable snow scenery. • Visit the Mogao Caves — deep grottoes can be surprisingly warm in winter. • Visit Dunhuang Research Academy and learn mural painting.
