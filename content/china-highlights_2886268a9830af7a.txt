Day 4Zhangjiajie | Baofeng Lake and Glass Bridge Breakfast & Lunch Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon boasts stunning mountains, winding streams, mysterious caves, towering cliffs, and lush forests. This scenic area is also home to the world's longest and highest glass bridge. For the adventurous, walking across the glass-bottomed bridge, suspended 300 meters above the ground, is an unforgettable experience. Baofeng Lake is in a basin created by the terrain in the middle of this mountainous area. Take a wooden boat ride for a leisurely experience as you float across the clear water, see the hills of various shapes, and enjoy entertainment by the local minority people as they sing their traditional songs. Day 5Zhangjiajie to Fenghuang | Tianmen Mountain Breakfast & Lunch Tianmen Mountain, the towering soul of Zhangjiajie, offers breathtaking views of ancient trees, dramatic karst formations, and winding roads carved into the mountainside. A 30-minute cable car ride, the world's longest, transports visitors from the city center to the mountaintop. Prepare for an adrenaline rush as you ascend the world's longest cable car, traverse the suspended glass walkway nearly a mile above the ground, and navigate the thrilling "99 Bends" mountain road. Tianmen Cave (literally meaning 'Heaven's Gate Hole') is a 30-meter-high hole formed naturally in the mountain like a doorway. It is well-known for being glided through by a world-class stuntman in a wingsuit. To reach the hole, you can climb for about half an hour up the 'Stairway to Heaven'. A scenic 3.5-hour drive from Zhangjiajie will take you to Fenghuang. With your knowledgeable guide by your side, the journey will be filled with interesting stories and insights, making the time fly by.
