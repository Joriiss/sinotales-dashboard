The Moon Hill Scenic Area is located about 9km from Yangshuo West Street. It takes about 30 minutes by bicycle or 15 minutes by car to reach the site. The site features a massive karst arch cave formation. When viewed from afar, the cave looks like a moon. Hence it got the name "Moon Hill." The climb involves over 800 stone steps from the base to the arch. The summit of the hill is at an elevation of around 380 meters. The ascent typically takes about 1 hour to complete. Be prepared for a challenging final stretch. The upper section features steep steps with about a 60-degree incline. Is Moon Hill Worth Climbing? Our customers are taking photos from the mountain base For most visitors staying just 2 nights in Yangshuo, Moon Hill isn't a must-see destination. Many visitors pass by and take photos from the grassy base area. However, if you have 3-4 days in Yangshuo, it's worth spending half a day to hike up and experience Moon Hill more intimately. The climb features three scenic viewpoints: • First Viewing Platform (~15 min climb) - Perfect for upward shots framing the "moon cave" against the mountain. • Half-Moon Platform (~30 min) - Offers views through the cave to distant peaks, especially photogenic with mist after rain. • Summit Platform (~1 hour) - 360° panoramic views, ideal for sunset shots of the arch framing the karst landscape. Beyond climbing the hill, the area offers other rewarding activities. You could enjoy hiking or cycling through the stunning karst scenery. Or explore countryside paths by bike in nearby Licun village. Recommended Activities Around Moon Hill Moon Hill Scenic Area is often combined with nearby attractions like the Yulong River and Ten-Mile Gallery for day trips. Here are some recommended activities: 1.Rock Climbing Near Moon Hill Rock Climbing Near Moon Hill Yangshuo boasts world-class karst cliffs, with unique limestone walls featuring natural holes and cracks. It is a rock climbing paradise. Moon Hill is one of the prime climbing areas. It offers well-established routes suitable for everyone from beginners to advanced climbers. Best Season for Climbing: Late September to November, when the weather is dry and cool. Safety & Guidance: Professional guides accompany all climbing sessions. Well-equipped protection stations allow for top-rope climbing on most routes, ensuring a safer and more enjoyable experience. Whether you're a first-timer or an experienced climber, Moon Hill's thrilling vertical challenges await! 2. Cycling or Hiking Around Moon Hill Cycling Around Moon Hill Cycling or hiking near Yangshuo's Moon Hill offers an immersive way to experience the stunning karst landscape. Cycling Route: The main cycling route is starting from Yangshuo West Street. The smooth asphalt road takes 30-45 minutes to reach Moon Hill. Along the way, you would pass through picturesque villages and attractions, such as: • Ten-Mile Gallery • Big Banyan Tree • Julongtan (Gathering Dragon Pool) Best times: Early morning or evening to avoid busy traffic. I still remembered my last trip to Moon Hill with my child. We cycled through Licun village and encountered farmers harvesting wampees (yellow-skinned fruits). With their permission, we joined the harvest. A local villager patiently taught my child how to pick the fruits properly. As we left, the kind farmer gifted us a bag of fruits - that sweetness became our most cherished travel memory. During peak season (Oct-Nov), Ten-Mile Gallery sees heavy traffic - always cycle on the right side and stay alert. Hiking Suggestions: As the main roads can be busy, opt for quieter village paths. Recommended route: Explore the countryside trails near Licun village at Moon Hill's base to observe rural life. 3. Explore Caves Known Only to Locals Just 3 kilometers from Moon Hill, near the rock climbing base, there are two hidden tunnel caves rarely visited by tourists. Accompanied by our guide, you'll traverse farmland and bushes, then ascend along a limestone-paved path. After a 15-minute hike, you'll reach the first cave. In the cave, you would discover the fascinating rock formations. Try to research how these magnificent peaks were formed. Feel free to explore the cave's mysteries. Passing through this cave brings you to a breathtaking overlook of the Yulong River. A short descent leads to a second, lower tunnel cave offering panoramic views of local villages and surrounding peaks. This makes for a perfect post-climbing mini-adventure, especially suitable for families with children (recommended ages 10+).
