Xinjiang province has been important in Chinese history as a Silk Road gateway to the West. It was the westernmost territory of several big empires. Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region is known for its Uyghur culture, beautiful natural scenery, juicy fruit and melons, and tasty Xinjiang cuisine.
