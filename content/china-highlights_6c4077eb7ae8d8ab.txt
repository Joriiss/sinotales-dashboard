Title: Guangzhou Opera House, Designed by Architect Zaha Hadid

================================================================================

Guangzhou Opera House was designed by Zaha Hadid, who was the first woman to win the Pritzker Architecture Prize. Located downstream of the Pearl River, the opera house looks like two pebbles washed and smoothed by the river. The irregular-shaped buildings and the towers of Guangzhou CBD look like a fantasy landscape. Adopting a futuristic design and state-of-the-art technology, the opera house is one of the three biggest theaters in China, along with the National Grand Theater in Beijing and Shanghai Grand Theater. In 2014, Guangzhou Opera House was rated as one of the 10 best opera houses around the world by USA Today. • Name: Guangzhou Opera House, 广州大剧院 • Area: 42,000 square meters • Popular activities: check out the bold design of the building, visit the facilities inside, enjoy an opera or concert performance • Suitable for: architecture lovers, opera lovers • Time needed: A short visit takes about 1 hour. Guangzhou Opera House looks like two pebbles - one large pebble and a smaller one. The larger pebble serves as the opera hall. Guangzhou Opera House The opera hall is an asymmetric structure with 1,687 seats in three stories. The streamlined walls and special pits in the hall provide an unrivalled acoustic effect in the auditorium for the entire audience. When you look above you from your seat, you'll see that the ceiling is as beautiful as a starry sky. The opera hall's stages consist of the main stage, the left and right wings, and the rear stage. The entire stage axis is 74 meters wide and 46 meters deep, with the proscenium being 18 meters wide and 12 meters high. The Experimental Theater The smaller pebble serves as an experimental theater. The Experimental Theater has 443 seats. The audience seating and even the stage are movable to adapt to the requirements of the performances. Guangzhou Opera House not only creates the world's top auditory feasts but also visual delicacies of modern art. A modern gallery is set in the non-performance area of the opera house with artwork that has been created by new prominent artists from all around the world.
