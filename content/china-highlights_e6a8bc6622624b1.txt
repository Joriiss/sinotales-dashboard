Title: Hong Kong Weather in April: Temperature, Clothing and Travel Tips

In April, the probability of precipitation increases with time. April weather in Hong Kong is mostly rainy or cloudy with some mist. The average low and high temperatures are 16 °C (61 °F) and 29°C (84°F). The temperature can get even higher, which makes it suitable for spring outings. Sunshine isn't very frequent in April. Ultraviolet intensity is low so you don't need to be as careful when going outside. What to Wear and Prepare You can wear summer clothes: The weather is getting much warmer and you may feel hot as the temperature may rise above 30°C (86°F). Some summer clothes, such as T-shirts, are enough for the daytime, although you should also prepare a light jacket for the evenings. Take or buy an umbrella: If you want to travel lightly, you can choose not to take your own umbrella and buy one in Hong Kong instead, if needed. Best Things to Do in Hong Kong in April 1. Sightsee on Victoria Peak The view from the Victoria Peak The cool spring weather is ideal for walking along the paths on Victoria Peak. For extended walks, several paths wind down among the country parks that surround the peak. You can walk down a path towards the city to the Hong Kong Zoological and Botanical Gardens. 2. Enjoy a Romantic Night in Victoria Harbor On a fine and comfortable evening in April, it is pleasurable to take your lover to Victoria Harbor and admire the nighttime scenery. With the spring breeze blowing across you, the night will be a romantic memory for you. At 8pm, A Symphony of Lights shines every night. Enjoy the spectacular performance! 3. Have Fun with Your Kids in Theme Parks On a fine spring day, nothing can be better than having a full day of happiness with your kids in Hong Kong Disneyland. This is the fifth Disneyland to be opened worldwide and it includes seven theme parks, among which the Grizzly Gulch and Mystic Point are unique. Ocean Park Hong Kong is another choice.
