Learn about China with Many Experiential Activities During this 10-day educational trip, students will dive deep into classic elements of China, exploring the Great Wall, the Forbidden City, the Terracotta Warriors, and the home of giant pandas. Through engaging activity arrangements, they will experientially journey into the local environments, immersing themselves in China's ancient history and diverse cultures. Suggested 10-Day China Educational Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Beijing Great Wall, Imperial architectures, and Tai Chi 4-6 Xi'an Terracotta Warriors and Tang dynasty culture 7-9 Chengdu Pandas and Local Chengduers' Life 10 Chengdu departure Take Lessons in Chinese Architecture Focusing on Old Buildings Learn mortise-and-tenon joinery in Beijing, which is applied in the buildings of the Forbidden City, and create a model of the Small Wild Goose Pagoda using a traditional craft method in Xi'an. Practice Tai Chi as Morning Exercise at the Temple of Heaven Locals can always be found doing morning exercise activities in the largest park in Beijing — Temple of Heaven Park. You will join them, and practice tai chi under the guidance of your teacher. Make Your Own Terracotta Warrior at a Cave Dwelling Are you curious about how the Terracotta Warriors were made? You will visit a traditional terracotta warrior workshop located in a cave dwelling and make a small terracotta warrior figure by yourselves. Discover the Revitalization of a Village at the Foot of the Great Wall Under the shadow of the Great Wall, a village has transformed from a simple rural area to an international cultural destination. Visit the art museum to experience the blend of tradition and modernity. Put on Hanfu and Travel Back to the Tang Dynasty Xi'an has numerous historical sites, making it the city with the highest compatibility with Hanfu. The fluttering sleeves of your Hanfu paired with medieval-style buildings simulate traveling back a thousand years through time. Experience the Eternal Charm of Tang Dynasty Court Dance and Music The Tang Imperial Banquet and Show is a wonderful performance inspired by court songs, dance, and music from the golden age of the Tang Dynasty. Combined with a delicious dinner, the show provides a glimpse into that prosperous period. Tell Your Story with Chinese Shadow Puppetry Chinese Shadow Puppetry is a traditional form of storytelling and entertainment. The teacher will show you how to operate the puppets from behind the curtain to tell a story. Get Close to Pandas and Paint a Traditional Chinese Painting of a Panda Visit Dujiangyan panda base in the morning, then create unique ink paintings of a panda yourselves under the guidance of a professional teacher. Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful school trip to China for you. Day 1: Arrival in Beijing Welcome to China! Your guide, who is experienced in leading student groups, will meet you in the arrivals hall at the airport. Your group will be transferred to your hotel on our comfortable bus. Day 2: Beijing | The Imperial Palace and Other Architectural Wonders Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Start your tour from Tian'anmen, a popular spot among students, immersed in the centuries-old allure of Chinese history. Next, make your way through the grand gateway into the Forbidden City, once home to powerful emperors. Among the iconic palaces, discover hidden treasures like the exclusive princes' library, intriguing military artifacts at the Archery Pavilion, and the splendid royal collection of timepieces. Afternoon: Discover the architectural wonder of the mortise and tenon joint, a groundbreaking innovation from imperial China that transformed construction methods, ensuring the Forbidden City's structural stability for centuries without the need for nails. Day 3: Beijing | The Great Wall and Village Visit Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Head to the less-crowded Mutianyu section of the Great Wall, a picturesque expanse where history is recreated through the fully-restored fortifications. A chairlift offers a comfortable ascent to stunning views from the wall's watchtowers. Have some fun with a thrilling toboggan ride down from the wall. Afternoon: Visit a village at the foot of the Great Wall. Have a glimpse of the lifestyle in rural China. and learn how it has evolved—from a once-backward village into a picturesque one that now serves as a model for rural revitalization. You will also visit an art museum that highlights the village's transformation through exhibits and contemporary artworks. Evening: End the day with a mouthwatering Peking duck dinner at a local restaurant. Get ready to taste a time-honored local favorite! Day 4: Beijing | Practice Tai Chi and Visit the Summer Palace Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Temple of Heaven Park not only contains an awesome array of worshipful buildings, but also a popular spot for locals to do their morning exercises. You'll likely find people practicing tai chi, qigong, dancing, and other forms of exercise there. Start your day with the serene practice of tai chi amidst the sacred surroundings of the Temple of Heaven, where each movement connects you with the essence of ancient harmony. Afternoon: Following a peaceful lunch, venture to the illustrious Summer Palace, where history and nature intertwine seamlessly. Explore the vast palace grounds, adorned with vibrant gardens and shimmering lakes, and immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of imperial splendor. Day 5: Beijing to Xi'an | Bullet Train and Muslim-Quarter Food Tasting Breakfast, Dinner Morning: Your Beijing driver will take your group to the station for your bullet train ride to Xi'an, escorting you all the way to the boarding gate. Afternoon: After putting down your luggage and resting briefly at your Xi'an hotel, your Xi'an guide and driver will continue to escort you to the Muslim Quarter to search for local delicacies. The Muslim Quarter offers a wide variety of delicious foods ranging from liangpi (cold noodles) to roujiamo ('Chinese hamburgers') and from paomo (crumbled bread in mutton soup) to preserved beef and lamb. Each type of snack is filled with rich northwestern flavors. In the heart of the Muslim Quarter, near the city center, lies one of the largest Islamic mosques in China known as the Great Mosque. It is a significant historical and architectural site, blending traditional Chinese and Islamic architectural styles. Day 6: Xi'an | The Terracotta Warriors and City Wall Biking Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Experience archeology come to life in the form of the Terracotta Army, stood in arrays in the underground pits where they were found. An interesting museum and film deepens the experience. Unravel the mysteries surrounding these figures with our knowledgeable guides. After visiting the Terracotta Warrior Museum, we will add to your understanding of this great wonder with practical experience. Your group will visit a countryside family workshop and create your own terracotta figurine. Afternoon: Take a bike ride along Xi'an City Wall, one of the oldest and most complete city defenses still standing. There are ramparts, gates, and watchtowers in abundance to explore. Day 7:Xi'an | A Day Traveling Back to the Tang Dynasty Breakfast and Lunch Morning: The first step of day 7's itinerary is to change into Hanfu, the traditional attire of the Han Chinese, to kick off a day of time travel. Start at the Xi'an Museum, hosting 130,000 artifacts from different historical periods. Adjacent is the Small Wild Goose Pagoda, showcasing a distinctive brick structure with a sloping roof made from blue bricks on a square base. You can participate in creating a model of the pagoda at this location. Afternoon: Visit a Chinese Shadow Puppetry studio, and try your hand at dyeing a carved puppet. The teacher will show you how to operate the puppets from behind the curtain. Evening: Finish your day with the Tang Imperial Banquet and Show. The show, set in a top-tier theatre restaurant, offers a captivating experience paired with a delectable dinner, showcasing the grandeur of the prosperous period of the Tang Dynasty. Day 8:Xi'an to Chengdu | Be a Chengduer for an Afternoon in a Local Park Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Embark on the bullet train to Chengdu in the morning, which takes 3–4 hours. Afternoon: Spend a relaxing afternoon at People's Park after lunch. Take a seat and sip a cup of tea in an old tea house. Feel the good vibes and join in with square dancing, a waltz, or a little Chinese salsa if you want to. Visit the marriage market and try an ear massage. Evening: Enjoy Chengdu's renowned Sichuan hotpot at dinner. Day 9:Chengdu | Panda Day Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: It's time to meet the pandas! Dujiangyan Panda Base is less crowded, allowing visitors to get closer to the pandas. Students can observe the pandas in a naturalistic environment, learn about their habitat, diet, and the challenges they face in the wild. Afternoon: Leaving the panda base, will you be reluctant to part with the adorable pandas? In the afternoon, we will take you to an art studio, where, under the guidance of a professional traditional Chinese painting teacher, you will paint your favorite panda and take this beautiful memory back home. Day 10: Chengdu Departure Breakfast After a buffet breakfast at your hotel, your guide and driver will make sure you get to the airport on time and without any stress. Say goodbye to China and take some awesome memories home. More China tours Hotel Selection Selected hotels with good locations.
