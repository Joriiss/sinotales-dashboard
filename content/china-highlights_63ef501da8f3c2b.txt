China International Aviation and Aerospace Exhibition Time: every two years in November Being approved by the central government of China, this air show is one of the most influential air shows in the Asian-Pacific Region, featuring real-size aircraft display, technical exchange, trade discussion, and flying performance. Zhuhai International Circuit Location: Jinding Town, Zhuhai The international auto racing is during every autumn, and other auto racing would be all the year round.
