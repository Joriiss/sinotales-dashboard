Title: 5-Day Harbin, Yabuli and  Snow Town Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/harbin/tours/harbin-yabuli-and-china-snow-town.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Experience a Winter Wonderland: Skiing, Rime, and Fun This tour conveniently connects Harbin, Yabuli, and Snow Town with short, scenic drives—each under two hours—so you can spend more time enjoying the magic. Feel the thrill of skiing at Yabuli's 5S resort before a fun-filled journey to fairytale Snow Town. The trip from Yabuli to Snow Town isn't just a ride; you'll travel along China's most beautiful winter road, with exhilarating snowmobile rides and stunning rime scenery along the way. Unwind in a hot spring to feel the contrast of ice and fire, and don't miss Harbin's must-see attractions, including the spectacular Ice and Snow World and the majestic Siberian Tiger Park. As your personal travel experts, we are ready to customize this unique winter trip just for you. Prepare to Be Amazed by Rime Spectacular Whether you're driving along China's most beautiful winter road, the Yaxue Highway, or strolling through the Rime and Snow Gallery, you'll be treated to a breathtaking spectacle of rime and snow. Explore the Charming Snow Mushroom Village Stroll along the lively Snow Charm Avenue and climb to the top of Bangchui Hill for a stunning panoramic view. From there, you can see the snow-covered farmhouses. Conquer the Slopes at Yabuli's 5S Ski Resort Carve through a stunning winter panorama at a prestigious 5S-rated ski resort, where the slopes wind through snow-covered mountains like elegant silver ribbons. Jump into the Ultimate Winter Playground Want to experience all the most popular snow activities? We've got you covered! Enjoy a thrilling dogsled ride, a snowmobile climb, or a blast on snow tubing and long ice slides. Relax in a Snowy Hot Spring Rejuvenate in the hot springs, which feature both a large indoor spa with a tropical garden feel and outdoor pools where you can soak in the warm water while admiring the snowy scenery. Step into a World of Ice and Snow Beyond the elegant ice sculptures, you can get your heart racing by swooshing down a giant ice slide or taking a spin on the Ferris wheel for an unforgettable winter adventure Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour to suit your interests. DAY 1 Harbin Arrival When you arrive at the airport, your private guide and driver will be there to take you to your hotel. DAY 2 Harbin | City Highlights A thrilling minibus safari awaits you at the Siberian Tiger Park, where you'll journey through the world of these magnificent creatures. Get ready for an up-close look at majestic tigers and maybe even catch a glimpse of some playful cubs. Next, we'll visit the stunning Harbin Pharmaceutical Sixth Factory Museum of Prints, a hidden gem often called the "Chinese Louvre." Prepare to be amazed by the spectacular blend of Classical, Baroque, and Rococo architectural styles that make this building a true work of art. Your tour continues at the iconic St. Sofia Church, a historical and architectural landmark of Harbin. Afterward, you'll explore Central Street, a bustling, open-air architectural museum. This vibrant street is not only a shopper's paradise but also a foodie's delight, offering a taste of the city's unique culture. As evening approaches, we'll enter a magical world at the Ice and Snow World. Prepare to be amazed by the incredible spectacle of towering ice sculptures and brilliant light shows, a perfect end to an unforgettable day. DAY 3 Harbin to Yabuli | Ski and Hot Spring After an hour-long high-speed train ride from Harbin, you'll arrive in Yabuli. Embark on a unique horse-drawn sleigh ride that goes beyond the typical tour. You'll journey through a "bandit's lair" and stop at a local farmhouse to savor authentic regional delicacies like sticky bean buns and waxy corn. Yabuli has hosted many international events, including the Asian Winter Games, so you'll be skiing at a truly renowned resort. With trails suitable for all levels, from beginners to advanced skiers, you can enjoy stunning mountain views while you glide through the snow. Tonight, you'll relax and unwind at one of Yabuli's best hot spring hotels. Enjoy a therapeutic soak in the warm water while surrounded by a beautiful, snowy landscape. DAY 4 Yabuli to Snow Town | Rime Landscape and Yaxue Highway Today, you'll travel along the Yaxue Highway, hailed as China's most beautiful winter road, on your way to the stunning China Snow Town. Your private car can stop at any time, giving you the perfect opportunity to capture photos of the breathtaking winter scenery. Take a thrilling snowmobile ride through a pristine forest to the summit of Mount Datudingzi, Heilongjiang province's highest peak at about 1,690 meters. Afterward, you'll explore the Rime and Snow Gallery, a real-life winter wonderland where trees are covered in stunning rime. You might even spot wildlife like roe deer. Attractions such as Bear Ridge and Weihu Hall add to the charm of this area. Next, you'll head to China Snow Town. Shaped by the wind, the heavy snowfall here creates adorable "snow mushroom" formations, making the village look like something out of a fairy tale. After your visit, you'll take a two-hour drive back to your hotel in Yabuli to relax and enjoy the hot springs. DAY 5 Yabuli to Harbin | Harbin Departure After breakfast, you'll take a high-speed train from Yabuli to Harbin, where a private car will pick you up and take you to the airport. When you arrive at the airport, your private guide and driver will be there to take you to your hotel.
