Hong Kong – Xiamen High-Speed Train Ticket Fares and Seat Classes Ticket Types First Class Second Class Adult ticket US$72 (CNY485) US$45 (CNY303) Child ticket US$36 (CNY245) US$23 (CNY153) Second Class Seat on Hong Kong to Xiamen High-Speed Train High-speed trains between Hong Kong and Xiamen have two classes of seats: first class and second class. Business class or superior class on these trains is not available these trains. 1. Second class has 5 chairs (3+2) in a row. It is the most common class on a high-speed train and ticket fare is the cheapest. Like the seat on an airplane, the seatback is adjustable. Each seat is equipped with a foldable tray table and a power socket. 2. First class has four chairs in a row (2+2). It is more spacious, quieter, and more comfortable than second class. The seatback can also be adjusted to a comfortable position. A power socket and foldable table are available to each passenger. On a high-speed train, there is usually only one carriage for first class, on the first or last carriage. Immigration Formalities at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station The co-location arrangement at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station provides great convenience to travelers taking high-speed trains between Hong Kong and the Chinese mainland. The arrangement allows them to complete entry and exit formalities on the same floor, saving both time and hassle. • To enter Hong Kong, simply get off your train at B4 and then proceed to B2 for exit formalities for China Mainland and entry formalities for Hong Kong. • To exit Hong Kong, it's recommended to purchase your train tickets before arriving at West Kowloon Station. Once you arrive, head to B3 for exit formalities for Hong Kong and entry formalities for China Mainland, and finally proceed to B4 to board on your train.
