What Is Unique to Buy in Guilin? Landscape-Inspired Cultural Creations Guilin's iconic mountains and rivers aren't just for postcards, but they've inspired beautiful art and crafts that let you bring a piece of this magical landscape home. These unique creations will spark cherished memories every time you see them. If traditional paintings or calligraphy feel beyond your budget, you'll find charming alternatives like fridge magnets, postcards, and canvas bags that capture Guilin's spirit perfectly. There are delightful shops for everyone on your gift list! For true Guilin flavor, explore these local-favorite spots bursting with regional charm and budget-friendly treasures. Remember that bargaining is part of the fun at smaller shops and street stalls, and always negotiate if prices aren't marked (and sometimes even when they are). A friendly "太贵啦！" (tài guì la!/"Too expensive!") often works wonders. If a vendor pressures you into paying for unsolicited "experiences" (like impromptu massages or trinket engraving): • Use body language or a translation software to politely but firmly decline. • Contact your guide immediately. For serious disputes: (Keep these numbers saved in your phone!) - Guilin Tourism Hotline: 0773-2800315 - Police: Dial 0773-110 Guilin's Top 2 Shopping Malls • Address: No.1 Hongling Road, Xiufeng District • Opening hours: 10 AM -10 PM Inspired by Guilin's karst landscapes and Northern Guangxi folk dwellings, Guilin MixC's architectural showcasing its beauty with unique designs different from MixCs in other regions. Limited by the consumption level of Guilin locals, Guilin MixC offers affordable luxury boutiques and mid- to high-end restaurants. It is also the home to Guilin's only POP MART store, which is perfect for shopping for trendy collectible souvenirs. Guilin High-Tech Wanda Plaza (高新万达广场) • Address: No.43 Chuanshan East Road, Qixing District • Opening hours: 10 AM - 10 PM Slightly different from Guilin MixC, Guilin High-tech Wanda Plaza focuses on brands at great value and affordable diners. After nightfall, the street outside the shopping mall transforms into a bustling night market that boasts budget-friendly choices, such as gadgets, snacks, and street games. The Walking Street: Zhengyang Pedestrian Street • Address: No.29 Zhengyang Road, Xiufeng District • Opening hours: 10 AM - 10 PM Nestled beside Guilin's Central Square(桂林中心广场), Zhengyang Pedestrian Street(正阳步行街) pulses with infectious energy, especially on weekend evenings and holidays when crowds thicken like moving walls. Stroll past boutique shops showcasing ethnic handicrafts and clothing, pause at milk tea stands for refreshments, and follow the aroma of sizzling street food to Shangshui Street(尚水街), a narrow culinary alley adored by locals. This hidden gem awaits your exploration, offering everything from steaming Guilin rice noodles to sweet osmanthus cakes. The Antique Commercial Street: East-West Lane • Address: No.18 Jiefang East Road, Xiufeng District • Opening hours: 24/7 Just across the street from Zhengyang Pedestrian Street, East-West Lane (东西巷) revitalizes Guilin's heart with newer renovations and a more diverse cluster of boutiques. Wander through Qing dynasty-inspired alleyways illuminated by silk lanterns, where live folk music bars spill melodies onto bluestones, creative workshops showcase minority embroidery, and steaming local snacks perfume the air. At the lane's end, ascend the freely accessible Xiaoyao Tower (逍遥楼) to witness a panoramic vista blending history with modernity, when twilight paints the Li River and Jiefang Bridge in gold, then lights up by neon lights after dark. The Underground Market: "Little Hong Kong" • Address: No.95 Little Hong Kong Underground Market, Xiufeng District • Opening hours: 10 AM - 9 PM Beneath Central Square lies "Little Hong Kong"(小香港), a labyrinthine underground bazaar, where winding corridors overflow with stalls hawking budget-friendly clothing, handbags, shoes, and trinkets. It's time to encourage your inner treasure hunter and start an adventure. Peer into crammed racks and glittering displays to unearth hidden gems, such as ethnic embroidered pouches or pearl-adorned hairpins at startlingly low prices. Just 200 meters north, the Cross Street Underground Passage offers a different experience, browsing snack carts and sundry shops selling steamed buns or bamboo trinkets while crossing beneath the bustling avenue. The Wholesale Market: Wa Yao Wholesale Market • Address: 15 South Huancheng Road 2nd Section, Xiangshan District • Opening hours: 9 AM - 7 PM As Guilin's largest wholesale hub, Wa Yao Wholesale Market(瓦窑批发市场) immerses you in a bustling sprawl of creativity and commerce. Navigate warehouse-like alleys stacked with minority silverwork, lacquerware tea sets, and hand-painted scrolls. Don't be afraid to ask for prices since all at wholesale rates. Beyond souvenirs, discover everyday bargains: from pajamas and ceramic rice bowls to fragrant osmanthus-scented candles. Whether sourcing bulk orders or hunting unique keepsakes, this is where locals and traders alike strike deals.
