Chengdu's nightlife strikes a perfect balance between relaxation and energy. It features the bustling atmosphere of hotpot restaurants and the chic vibes of neon lights. Both tourists and locals can find their own rhythm of nightlife here. Young people prefer dynamic venues like bars, nightclubs, and live houses, while middle-aged and older adults tend to enjoy tea houses, night markets, or park strolls. Students love exploring night markets, discovering food, and soothing their souls. As a local guide, here are my top recommendations for different types of nightlife, popular among locals too. The song perfectly embodies the local phrase "巴适得板" (meaning very comfortable). Yulin Street is more than just one bar or street. It's a collection of old neighborhoods. Its lively streets and vibrant energy thrive among old residential areas, where new and old coexist harmoniously. If time allows, arrive around 5 or 6 PM. You can take the subway to Fangcao Street Station, Exit D, and walk along Fangcao East Street. This street is lined with charming shops, including vintage stores, boutiques, and dessert shops, each with unique decor perfect for photos. Notable places include CARPARK Coffee and 咔嚓BB机. Then head to Yujie Lane (玉洁巷), home to quaint bookstores and bars. Visit "退步集旧书房" for a literary vibe. On weekends, the Creative Arts Park might host trendy market events, attracting many young people. Continue through Yulin Zhongheng Alley 玉林中横巷/Yulin Comprehensive Market 玉林综合菜市场 (bustling with snacks and daytime activity) to Yulin Street. Here, you'll find the famous "Yulin Skewers", "Wang Ma's Hand-Torn Rabbit", and "Lao Huang's Hand-Torn Rabbit", showcasing Chengdu's love for rabbit dishes. A few minutes further is Yulin Third Alley 玉林三巷, featuring bars with folk music, often with free performances on weekend nights. Cross Yulin Fourth Alley (Aiqing Street 爱清街) and Yulin East Road to reach Yulin West Road, the highlight of the night. This area is a hub for young people, with vibrant folk music bars, renowned hotpot restaurants (like Youth Hotpot), and a street adorned with colorful lights and decorations. It's perfect for a leisurely stroll to enjoy the lively atmosphere. Experience Chengdu's lifestyle with us: 1-Day Chengdu Lifestyle Tour. Second Recommendation: Jinli 锦里古街 • Location: No. 231, Wuhouci Street, Wuhou District, Chengdu • Duration: 1-2 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone Jinli is a blend of history, culture, natural scenery, and folk life, akin to Chengdu's version of the "Along the River During the Qingming Festival 清明上河图" painting. At night, the street is illuminated with bright lights, red lanterns, and colorful decorations, recreating the bustling life of the Shu Han Dynasty. Night view of Jinli, Chengdu Though not very long, Jinli encapsulates much of Chengdu's charm. Just 20 meters in, you'll find a shop called "Blowing Sugar Figurines 吹糖人", showcasing a traditional sugar art form. Some local artisans have inherited this craft, creating intricate designs in just three minutes. This art once attracted children with the sound of a gong. While now fixed in tourist spots, it remains a nostalgic symbol of old Chengdu. Kids can even participate by blowing the sugar (with guidance), creating edible and memorable keepsakes. About 10 meters further is a stall called "Sugar Painting 画糖人", where sugar is used as ink and a spoon as a brush to create vivid designs on a slate. The classic spinning game at the stall offers a "blind box" surprise, where children spin to select a design, and the artist improvises, adding an element of excitement and creativity. Continuing to the right at the fork, you'll find a small workshop for seal engraving. Here, a skilled artisan can design a personalized seal based on your name, with customizable stone options, resulting in a unique piece rich in Chinese cultural heritage. In the center of the fork is a store selling Shu Brocade and Shu Embroidery. Both are renowned intangible cultural heritages representing the pinnacle of Sichuan silk craftsmanship. Shu Brocade embodies Chengdu's philosophy of meticulous workmanship. Inside, you can appreciate the elegance of Shu Brocade and the finesse of Shu Embroidery. If you're looking for a gift, a scarf or a cheongsam would be a delightful choice. Jinli's intriguing shops offer much more to explore, inviting you to savor and experience them slowly. Top Night Views in Chengdu 1.Anshun Lang Bridge 安顺廊桥 • Location: Hejiang Pavilion, Jinjiang District, Chengdu • Duration: 1-2 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone Anshun Lang Bridge dates back to the Yuan Dynasty and was a crucial hub on the ancient Southern Silk Road. It was listed by the Italian traveler Marco Polo as one of the four most impressive bridges in China. At night, the bridge dazzles with neon lights, surrounded by illuminated bars and restaurants, casting a dreamy reflection on the river. It is a key landmark of Chengdu's nightlife. The best time to visit is at dusk to watch the sunset over the river and then enjoy the leisurely night scene. Hejiang Pavilion is located at the confluence of two rivers in Chengdu and is one of the best spots for photographing the bridge. Nearby, you'll find the city's only heart-shaped traffic light and heart-shaped graffiti on the crosswalk. It used to be a popular spot for locals to take wedding photos, symbolizing a beautiful wish for their marriage. As you walk along the river towards the bridge, you'll encounter many young people singing with a guitar and microphone, creating a romantic and pleasant atmosphere for passersby. Continuing on, you'll reach Chengdu's Lan Kwai Fong bar street. Crossing Anshun Lang Bridge leads to another bar street on the opposite bank, known for its vibrant music scene due to its proximity to the Sichuan Conservatory of Music, offering a relaxing and enjoyable stroll. Note: If you wish to enjoy drinks and experience the nightlife, be aware that prices here are not cheap, but the quality in Lan Kwai Fong is generally better. • Location: Binhe Road, Tianxianqiao, Jinjiang District, Chengdu • Duration: 1-2 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone Dongmen Wharf is a testament to Chengdu's historical waterfront and a symbol of modern cultural and tourism innovation. Whether you're tracing the millennia-old prosperity described by Chinese Poet Du Fu, or experiencing a "time travel" through neon lights, this spot satisfies both the traditional and international allure of Chengdu. If time allows, consider combining a night cruise of Jinjiang River with exploring nearby shopping areas to feel the city's unique rhythm of "fast fun and slow living". Using LED screens and projection technology, the riverbank recreates the bustling trade scenes of the Tang and Song dynasties, accompanied by ancient music and sound effects. The boat departs from Dongmen Wharf, passing through Shuijin Bridge, Lan Kwai Fong, Hejiang Pavilion, extending to Jiuyan Bridge and Chenghua Park 339. The entire journey is about 4 kilometers and takes 1 hour. The night tour of Jinjiang is a paid activity, with tickets ranging from 120-180 RMB per person (depending on the boat type and route), available from 18:00 to 22:00. The light show is best experienced at night. It's recommended to book in advance during holidays. Chengdu South Financial City 成都南面金融城 • Location: Chengdu Financial City • Duration: 1-3 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone Chengdu Financial City is the city's core business district, renowned for its dazzling lights, modern landmarks, and ecological landscapes. It's an excellent choice to experience the international urban style of Chengdu, blending the city's ancient charm with new vitality. Chengdu South Financial City Here are the must-see illuminated landmarks: Financial City Twin Towers 成都南面金融城: • Standing at 218 meters, the Twin Towers feature themed light shows on their LED facades, such as "Poetry on the Moon" for Mid-Autumn Festival and "New Year Celebrations" for Spring Festival. • These shows incorporate elements like poetry, pandas, and Jiaozi culture, creating an immersive light and shadow feast. • Best Viewing Time: Nighttime during holidays (e.g., 19:30-21:00) for optimal effects; basic lighting on regular days. • Viewing Spots: Jiaozi Park, Jiaozi Ring Pedestrian Bridge, Global Center Plaza. • The world's first circular landscape bridge inspired by "Jiaozi," it synchronizes with the Twin Towers' lights. The bridge's light bands and reflections create a "time tunnel" visual effect. China Merchants Financial Center 中国华商金融中心 • The red maple stone and glass curtain walls exhibit a unique texture under night lighting. • The top floor offers a panoramic view of Financial City. The neon signs of nearby culinary landmarks (like Michelin-starred restaurants) add a lively atmosphere. • The largest single building in the country, featuring a wave-shaped roof with gradient lighting at night. Jiaozi Pedestrian Bridge 交子人行桥 • An artistic bridge connecting the High-Tech Zone and Jinjiang District. It offers a futuristic feel when lit at night, with views of the Twin Towers. SKP - Tower of Life SKP-生机之塔 • Designed to resemble bamboo, with mist symbolizing the "water of life" descending from the sky. The tower is adorned with colorful gradient lights, creating a stunning night scene. Top Night Shows in Chengdu 1.Shu Feng Ya Yun 蜀风雅韵 • Location: Cultural Park, Qintai Road, Chengdu • Duration: 1.5 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone Shu Feng Ya Yun Sichuan Opera Performance Shu Feng Ya Yun offers a unique Sichuan Opera experience, blending traditional tea house culture with live performances. This style reflects the leisurely lifestyle of Chengdu residents, allowing audiences to enjoy tea and snacks while watching the show. The semi-open stage creates a relaxed atmosphere, encouraging audience participation rather than passive viewing. You can also experience local life activities such as trying on costumes, makeup, massages, and ear cleaning. The performances feature a variety of traditional acts, including face-changing, fire-spitting, lantern rolling, shadow puppetry, puppetry, excerpts from classic operas, erhu, and suona music. This showcases the traditional street culture of old Chengdu. The show lasts for 1.5 hours, with the number of performances varying by season. Seating is divided into VIP, A, and B sections, with prices and viewing experiences improving the closer you are to the stage. It's advisable to book tickets in advance. • Location: Sichuan Opera Theater, Jinjiang District, Chengdu • Duration: 1.5 Hours • Suitable for: Everyone This is the birthplace of Sichuan Opera face-changing, performed at the Sichuan Opera Theater. The actors are professionally trained, and the seating is tiered like a cinema, providing a theatrical experience. The show features renowned artists from the Sichuan art scene, presenting meticulously arranged classic performances that highlight all the unique skills of Sichuan Opera. The face-changing, fire-spitting, puppet face-changing and fire-spitting, costume changing, triple face-changing, and lantern rolling are particularly impressive and unforgettable. Additionally, shadow puppetry, acrobatics, and puppet performances are crowd favorites. The performance lasts 70 minutes, with the number of shows varying by season. Seating is divided into VIP, A, B, and C sections, with better viewing experiences and higher prices the closer you are to the stage. It's advisable to book tickets in advance.
