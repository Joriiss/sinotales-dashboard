Title: Guilin Weather in November - Best Time for Autumn Colours

Weather-wise, November remains quite pleasant in Guilin. Daytime temperatures generally range between 20-26°C, while nights stay above 10°C. Skies are mostly clear or partly cloudy. With only 3-4 rainy days throughout the month, there's no need to worry about rain disrupting your travel plans. Temperature variations between midday and early morning/evening can be significant. On sunny days, noon temperatures can occasionally rise above 30°C, so it's advisable to dress in layers to adapt to these changes comfortably. Is November a Good Time to Visit Guilin? A village in Guilin in November The answer is absolutely yes, especially after November 15th. Guilin offers some of the most pleasant conditions for travel. • The weather is comfortable. • Hotel prices drop by more than 30% compared to the peak season. • You can explore scenic areas in a more peaceful and relaxed atmosphere. For water activities or swimming in the Li River, November may not be the best time. The weather turns cooler, and water levels in many waterfalls and streams begin to recede, making them less suitable for playing in the water. By this time, the rice in the Longji Terraces has already been harvested, so you won't see the golden fields. However, the exposed layers of terraced ridges create a unique and striking landscape of their own. If you enjoy hiking or cycling, November is still an excellent time. You can trek or ride along the river or through the countryside around Yangshuo, taking in the distinctive late autumn scenery. November is also when osmanthus flowers bloom throughout Guilin. The city fills with their sweet fragrance. And it's a perfect opportunity to admire the blossoms and taste traditional osmanthus cakes. In addition, many ginkgo trees in Guilin turn golden this month, offering fantastic photo opportunities. Unique Local Activities in November In addition to classic Guilin experiences such as Li River cruises, cave explorations, hiking, and cycling, November offers some unique local events and activities worth trying. You can join the Persimmon Festival, visit popular local spots to admire golden ginkgo trees. Or even participate in the Guilin Marathon - a great way to enjoy the city's stunning landscape while running. 1. Cycling or Hiking Amidst the Scenery Yangshuo biking in November Pedal through karst peaks and rustic villages, where mountain shadows reflect on the river and bamboo rafts occasionally drift by. While hiking, gentle breezes brush past, and the mountainsides are painted in deep red and orange. It is a striking contrast to summer's lush green. Classic Hiking Routes: • Along the Li River: Trek from Quanjiazhou to Xingping to enjoy close-up views of iconic sights of the Li River, such as the 20 yuan backdrop. • By the Yulong River: This popular trail passes ancient bridges, bamboo groves, and villages, with crystal-clear water and picturesque peaks. You can also join local activities in villages like Xiatang Village, such as cooking classes or learning to make traditional oil tea. • Longji Terraces Hike: Walk from Ping'an Village to Ancient Zhuang Village (approx. 5 km), or explore the two main viewing platforms in Ping'an. Discover more hiking routes in Guilin. 2. Join the Persimmon Festival and Taste Persimmon Delicacies About 140 km from Guilin lies Gongcheng, a region famous for its persimmons. In November, when the persimmons ripen, you can take part in the Gongcheng Persimmon Festival. It typically runs from November through February. During this time, Gongcheng transforms into a golden paradise. The main event is usually held in the Hongyan Village Scenic Area. Here, the vast persimmon orchards heavy with fruit create a spectacular sight. The golden persimmons hang like lanterns from the branches. Walking through the fields, you can not only share the joy of the harvest but also observe locals drying persimmons in the traditional way. With very few non-local tourists, this is an authentic opportunity to experience rural life. Locals drying persimmons in the traditional way At the festival, you can immerse yourself in local culture and cuisine. Be sure to try freshly picked crisp and sweet persimmons, and chewy traditionally sun-dried persimmon cakes. Other delicacies like persimmon wine, osmanthus cakes, oil tea, cured meats, and stuffed dishes are also available. The festival also features lively celebrations with Yao song and dance performances, folk activities, and interactive experiences. You could fully appreciate the unique charm and warmth of Yao culture. 3. Admire the Golden Sea of Ginkgo Trees Ginkgo Trees in Haiyang village, Guilin In late November, the ginkgo leaves in Guilin turn completely from green to gold, reaching their most brilliant state of the year. The cool, dry weather and bright sunshine during this period create perfect conditions for photo. The suburban Haiyang village is a well-known viewing spot, located about a 1.5-hour drive from downtown Guilin. Here, you can stroll through ancient villages surrounded by golden leaves covering both the branches and the ground. This breathtaking scenery, set against white walls, black-tiled roofs, and karst mountains, forms a serene autumn painting. Ancient villages surrounded by golden leaves At local restaurants, you can also savor dishes prepared by villagers using fresh ginkgo nuts, such as ginkgo and duck soup or sweet ginkgo nut tea. The best time to visit is generally from mid-to-late November to early December. 4. Watch or Join a Scenic Marathon With its cool and pleasant weather, November is a great time to experience a marathon amidst Guilin's stunning landscape - a truly unique experience. The event is usually held on a weekend in mid-November (e.g., November 16, 2025). Race options include a full marathon (42 km), a half marathon (21 km), and a fun run (approx. 5 km). You can choose an event based on your fitness level and interest. Children aged 10 and above can participate in the fun run. Registration information is typically released 1-2 months in advance on official platforms such as "Running China" or local race WeChat accounts, where online sign-up is available. The route is mainly set in urban Guilin and crosses several bridges over the Li River. If you prefer not to run, you can still join local residents in cheering enthusiastically for the participants. After the race, don't forget to try local specialties like Guilin rice noodles and beer fish! What to Wear in Guilin in November With its cool days and significant temperature variations, dressing in layers is key to staying comfortable. For daytime outings, we recommend an "onion-style" layered approach. Start with a long-sleeved shirt or lightweight knit sweater, and add a windproof or casual jacket to shield against cooler breezes. Opt for long pants or jeans for comfort and ease of movement. As sunlight remains fairly strong, don't forget sunglasses and sunscreen. Temperatures drop noticeably in the early mornings and after dark. It's important to have an extra layer ready - a thin down jacket or heavier coat is ideal. If you plan to visit mountainous areas or the Li River banks, where it often feels cooler, a lightweight jacket is highly recommended.
