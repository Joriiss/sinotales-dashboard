Yunnan, an ideal holiday destination located in Southwest China, enjoys a perfect combination of amazing natural scenery, diverse ethnic customs, and a pleasant climate all year round. From the snow mountains to the tropical rainforests, from the highland lakes to the canyons, from the historic cities to the fascinating minority villages. The hot cities of Kunming, Dali, Lijiang and Shangri-La can't be missed for a thoroughly Yunnan travel. The province boasts abundant natural sceneries and cultural sites, among which the most popular ones are Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, Lijiang Old Town, Dali Ancient City, Tiger Leaping Gorge and Stone Forest. Travelling in Yunnan to enjoy the wonderful natural beauty and get an in-depth experience by reliving the history, immersing in the culture, and interacting with locals.
