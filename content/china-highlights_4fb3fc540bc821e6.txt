Trains exteriors are decorated in red and silver colours. The running speed on this route is 120 kph (74 mph). High-speed trains between Beijing West Station and Daxing Airport have three classes of seats: second class, first class and business class. Like other intercity high-speed trains, second class seats have 2+2 configuration. The aisle is widened to 1.01 meters to facilitate large suitcases. Luggage racks (45-50cm wide and 50-60cm high) are equipped above the seats on both sides of the carriage. These are for bags and normal-sized suitcases (24-inch or smaller). Large and heavy suitcases can be put in the large luggage racks beside the door and in the middle of the carriage. They are over 1 meters high (3.3 feet) and is enough for 30 inch suitcases. There are two types of washrooms on bullet trains on Beijing West Station - Daxing Airport intercity rail line: western-style toilets and squat toilets. They are available at the end of each carriage. A small washbasin and a mirror are equipped in each washroom.
