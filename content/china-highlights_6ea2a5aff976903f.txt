Thanks to its tropical border location, Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture (Banna for short) has become a popular tourist destination, known for its wonderful tropical rainforest life and ethnic folk customs. Scroll down to find maps of Xishuangbanna's location in China and in Yunnan Province, and a map of Xishuangbanna's tourist attractions. Xishuangbanna's Location on a China Map Xishuangbanna is a prefecture-level area in south China, bordering both Laos and Myanmar. Flights connect Xishuangbanna with about 46 domestic cities, including Beijing and Shanghai in around 4 hours and Southeast Asia cities in Laos, Thailand, and Cambodia. Xishuangbanna's Location on a Yunnan Map Xishuangbanna Prefecture lies in the southern tip of Yunnan Province with Jinghong as its city seat of government.
