Guilin, a masterpiece of God and an Earthly paradise, is a beautiful and picturesque small city in South China. Take a little boat on the famous Li River and enjoy the cool breeze and the beautiful scenery along the river bank. Hike on the Longji Terraced Rice Fields, where local minorities have been laboring on this land and have made it the now amazing scenery that attracts travelers from all over the world. Walk on the city streets where musicians perform and see the locals…This is Guilin, a place that looks like it belongs in paintings. You can expect personal attention from your guides and much more unique experience than most China tours. Let's see this fantastic city the way the locals do.
