Panda and China's Past & Present This 10-day family tour is designed for first-time visitors who love history and pandas. Beijing and Xian are rich in history, culture, and architectural wonders, they are must-visit destinations for travelers interested in exploring China's past and present. Chengdu is home to the famous Giant Panda. You will have the chance to see these adorable creatures up close and personal in their natural habitat. Here is a suggested itinerary. If your family wants to add any activities or change anything, just feel free to let your travel adviser know. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and needs. Suggested 10-Day Family Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Beijing Great Wall and Imperial architectures 4-5 Xian Terracotta Warriors and Tang dynasty culture 6-7 Chengdu Giant Pandas and Sanxingdui Museum 8-10 Shanghai Traditional Chinese Garden and Modern China Hop on a toboggan and zip down from the iconic Great Wall The toboggan ride offers a fun and slightly adventurous way to descend from the Wall, providing stunning views of the surrounding landscape as you weave down the hillside. Discover the secrets of the Forbidden City in an interesting way with your kids Besides the six main palaces on the axis, you will also explore some sites that interest kids more. You will play a game about the nine sons of the dragon and the auspicious beats in the Forbidden City. Hop on a rickshaw to ride through the Hutong and enjoy the traditional Beijing afternoon tea You can hop on a rickshaw to ride through the narrow alleys as well as visit a local family. Then enjoy a traditional afternoon tea in a Peking Opera-style teahouse. Journey through time and uncover a magnificent archaeological marvel at the Terracotta Warriors Marvel at the archaeological wonder of thousands of life-sized clay soldiers, chariots, and horses. then make a Terracotta Warriors by yourself at the family workshop in the countryside, Explore Xian's ancient beauty from atop the city wall on a bicycle Riding around the perimeter of the well-preserved wall, which dates back to the Ming Dynasty, offers a unique perspective of the city and its surroundings. Meet the cuddly pandas in Dujiangyan Panda base There are fewer tourists at the Dujiangyan Panda Base, allowing for a closer observation of the pandas. Capturing the incredible moments as they laze about or showcase their acrobatic skills. Savor Authentic Delights at Jinli Old Street Try some famous food like bean jelly in chili source, sweet potato noodle, and wonton in Sichuan style. Discover Ancient Mysteries at the Sanxingdui Museum Marvel at the monumental bronzes, intricate masks, jade ornaments, and other archaeological finds that shed light on the cultural sophistication and artistic achievements of this ancient civilization. Travel from the historic Bund area to Pudong's modern skyline by taking a trip through the Bund Sightseeing Tunnel Experience the mesmerizing transformation of the tunnel into a magical passage through time and space with its vibrant light displays, immersive sound effects, and captivating multimedia presentations. Day 1Arrive in Beijing | Airport Transfer Your private guide will be ready to meet and greet you upon arrival. A comfortable private car (non-smoking) with an experienced driver will transfer you directly to your hotel. Your guide will help you check in. Day 2Beijing | Highlights of Beijing City Breakfast & Lunch Start your day at Tian'anmen Square, the heart of Beijing and the largest city square in the world. Delight in a unique tour of the Forbidden City with your guide who brings its rich history, culture, and architecture to life for your children. Experience the mysteries of the Forbidden City in a captivating style alongside your kids. Enjoy a traditional Beijing lunch at a local Roast Duck restaurant. Visit Hutong this afternoon. Hop on a rickshaw to ride through the narrow alleys as well as visit a local family. At the end of the tour, enjoy a traditional Pecking operal style afternoon tea at Hutong. Day 3 Beijing | Great wall and Temple of Heaven Breakfast & Lunch Visit the Temple of Heaven in the morning, where emperors of old prayed for a good harvest, and where local people now do morning exercises. Lots of elderly local people meet there daily to sing, exercise, and do tai chi. Visit the Temple of Heaven, an ancient Chinese architectural masterpiece symbolizing the connection between Heaven and Earth. Local residents engage in recreational activities at the Temple of Heaven Park, including singing, dancing, playing chess, and practicing Tai Chi. Opt for the Mutianyu Great Wall section: less crowded than Badaling, it boasts numerous watchtowers that harmonize with the disappearing wall. Enjoy a cable car ride for a fresh arrival atop the wall, where you can spend unlimited time. Descend with exhilaration using the toboggan slide for added fun. Day 4Beijing to Xian | Summer Palace and Bullet Train Breakfast & Lunch Explore the imperial gardens and palaces at Summer Palace. Take a leisurely boat on Kunming Lake. After lunch, we would take you to the train station for your bullet train to Xi'an, escorting you all the way to the boarding gate. The maximum speed could reach 350 km/h (217 mph) Day 5Xian | The Terracotta Warriors and Muslim Quarter Breakfast & Lunch Explore the famous Terracotta Army, a UNESCO World Heritage site, showcasing thousands of life-sized terracotta soldiers and horses. Cycle on the ancient City Wall of Xian, offering panoramic views of the city. Stroll through the vibrant Muslim Quarter, exploring the bustling market and trying local street food. Day 6Xian to Chengdu | Big Wild Goose Pagoda and Anrenfang Heritage Museum Breakfast & Lunch The Small Wild Goose Pagoda has a beautiful tower shape and is a heritage of Buddhist architecture from the Tang Dynasty. It is a landmark building that signifies the introduction of Buddhism into the Central Plains and its integration into Han Chinese culture. During the visiting, you will have chance to ring the bell for blessings and good luck. The Anrenfang Heritage Museum was built on the site of the Anrenfang District during the Tang Dynasty. It features exhibits and artifacts that provide insights into the traditions, customs, and historical events that have shaped the local community. Take a high-speed train to Chengdu this afternoon. Day 7Chengdu | Giant Pandas and Sichuan Snacks Breakfast & Lunch Dujiangyan Panda Base, a much less crowded facility known for its conservation efforts and provision of natural habitat for giant pandas, visitors can observe pandas and learn more about conservation efforts. On Jinli Snack Street, you can have the real taste of local snacks, which are well-known and unique in China. Then go to Chunxi Road to take pictures with the big Panda Sculpture. Day 8 Chengdu to Shanghai | Local life and Sanxingdui Museum Breakfast & Lunch Take a seat and sip a cup of tea at a local park. You will see some vibrant scenes that may only happen in Chengdu: parents seeking partners for their unmarried children in a marriage market, locals enjoying traditional teahouse ear massages, and people playing mahjong everywhere in summer - even in the river. Witness the ancient Chinese civilization at Sanxingdui. Excavations at Sanxingdui have unearthed a variety of objects, including bronze masks, statues, jade artifacts, and ceremonial relics. Your personal driver and guide will escort you to the airport to catch your flight to Shanghai in the evening. Day 9Shanghai | Panoramic View Over the City and Bustling Market Street Breakfast Shanghai is a collision of new and old. Visit the new part of Shanghai in the morning. Reach the 118th-floor Observation Deck of the Shanghai Tower, the world's second-tallest building, for a panoramic view of the city. Take the Bund Sight-seeing Tunnel to other side of the river, and enjoy the colorful light displays, music, and visual effects during the journey. In the afternoon, travel back in time to the old part of Shanghai: the famous Yuyuan Garden that was built during the Ming Dynasty. A market street near Yuyuan Garden is an exciting experience where crowds of people buy local snacks, handicrafts, antiques, and souvenirs. Day 10 Departure Breakfast After a buffet breakfast at the hotel, your guide and driver will make sure you get to the airport on time and without any stress.
