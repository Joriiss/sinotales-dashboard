Time Duration G319 12:41 15:57 3h 16min G407 13:46 16:57 3h 11min G2963 15:43 18:55 3h 12min Transportation to Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station West Kowloon Station is the southern terminus of the Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong Express Rail Link (XRL). This station is in West Kowloon and adjacent to the metro stations of Kowloon and Austin. So it is now the major travel hub in the Kowloon area for both China travel and internal transportation. Hong Kong Mass Transit Railway (MTR) Through the footbridges on the first floor above the ground and underground pedestrian tunnels, a 2-3 minutes' walk can get you from West Kowloon Station to MTR Austin Station, and a 10-12 minutes' walk to MTR Kowloon Station, which allows fast transfer between the high-speed railway and subway. Through the MTR, you can easily transit from the West Kowloon Station to other regions of Hong Kong: • Tung Chung Line: from MTR Kowloon Station to Lantau Island and Hong Kong Island. • Airport Express: from MTR Kowloon Station to Hong Kong Airport and Hong Kong Island. • West Rail Line: from MTR Austin Station to Tsuen Wan and Hung Hom Railway Station; West Rail Line interacts with Tsuen Wan Line which can transfer you to Tsim Sha Tsui, Mong Kok, Central, Wan Chai, and Causeway Bay. For information about suggested touring routes using the MTR for transportation, see Touring Hong Kong by MTR Subway and Tram. Public Bus from/to Hong Kong West Kowloon There are 14 bus routes from West Kowloon Bus Terminus to various regions of Hong Kong, they are W1, W2, W3, 36X, 36B, 42A, 46, 60X, 63X, 69X, 81, 95, 268X and 271. Transportation to Yangshuo Railway Station Opened in 2016, Yangshuo Railway Station was built for high-speed trains to and from Yangshuo. It is actually far away from Yangshuo downtown. This station is located in Xingping Country, with a distance of 25 kilometers (16 miles) to the West Street. At present, high-speed trains from Yangshuo to Guilin, Hezhou, Guangzhou, Guiyang, and Chongqing depart from here. The public transportation from Yangshuo Railway Station to anywhere in the town, or to the city of Guilin is to take a coach. Coaches are available at the entrance of the station to West Street, Guilin Railway Station, and Guilin Liangjiang International Airport.
