Weather: In Jiuzhai Valley National Park, average daily low and high temperatures are 17°C (63°F) and 30°C (86°C) in August. Although it's the warmest month here, it's still cooler than many other parts of China. But you may still feel hot under the sun, 30-35°C (86-95 °F). While in the morning or in shaded areas, it's relatively cool around 20+°C (68+°F), and a bit cold on rainy days (15°C/59°F). Rainfall: August is the rainy season, with about 26 rainy days in Jiuzhaigou. Usually, the rain is a light shower, so it won't affect your viewing experience so much. But sometimes the heavy rain can cause mudslides, and you might get stuck in traffic for 1 to 3 hours. Clothing: You can wear short sleeves, lightweight breathable pants, sun-protective shirts, and a thin windbreaker to handle both warm and cool weather. For rainy days, bring a raincoat, umbrella, non-slip shoes, and a waterproof cover for your camera. Is August a Good Time to Visit Jiuzhaigou? If you consider travel costs, crowds, and weather, August might not be the best time to visit. But if you're looking for a cool place to escape the heat, Jiuzhaigou is definitely a good choice. The temperatures remain comfortable in August, cooler than downtown Chengdu (34°C/93°F). And the scenery in August is still quite nice. You can admire fuller and more vibrant lakes than during the dry season, lush and green forests, and more spectacular waterfalls. But you can use these tips to handle common summer travel issues. 1. Start Early at 6 pm or Enter Late at 9 pm If you can get up early, it's best to arrive at the park entrance by 6:30 am to start queuing. This way, you can be among the first to enter or cut your waiting time in half, and enjoy the park before the peak crowds arrive. We recommend staying near the park so you don't have to wake up too early. If you stay farther away, you might need to leave your hotel around 6 am. Jiuzhaigou opens at 7:30 am in August. If you leave at 7 am, you'll likely run into traffic on the way, which will cut into your visiting time. If you're travelling with elderly people or children and don't want to wake up early, you can enter the park after 9 am. Although your visiting time will be shorter by 1–2 hours and you may miss some sights, there will be fewer visitors. You'll only need to wait about 10 minutes to get on the bus, and it's easier to find a seat. 2. Bring Your Own Lunch Jiuzhaigou has only one dining area, the Nuorilang Center. From around 12 noon, it gets crowded with long queues for food, and you may have to wait a long time with no seats available. You can bring your own sandwiches, self-heating meal boxes, or snacks for a picnic. This also gives you more time to explore, as attractions are usually less crowded when most visitors are having lunch in the center. Prepare a simple meal while visiting Jiuzhaigou in August 3. Travel after August 20th Based on past years, visitor numbers in early August are usually around 30,000–40,000, and the wait for the shuttle bus is about 25 minutes. But after August 20, the number drops to around 10,000–30,000, so it's much less crowded, and ticket checks plus shuttle bus waits take only about 10 minutes. It's more likely to find a photo spot without large crowds after August 20th 4. Choose Flexible Train Station High-speed train tickets from Chengdu to Huanglong Jiuzhaigou Station (the nearest and most popular station) often sell out within seconds in early to mid-August. If you can't get one, you can book tickets to Huangshengguan or Songpan stations instead. They're also about 1 to 2.5 hours' drive from Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong.
