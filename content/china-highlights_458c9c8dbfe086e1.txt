Title: 6-Day Chengdu City and Nature Explorer Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chengdutour/cd-3/

Experience the Classic and Vibrant Chengdu This 6-day tour is ideal for those with ample time and a desire to explore Chengdu and its surrounding cities in depth. The curated route introduces you to iconic natural landmarks like Mount Emei and plunges you into historical and cultural marvels such as Dujiangyan and the Leshan Giant Buddha. It also invites you to savor the distinctive urban lifestyle of Chengdu, with excursions to the People's Park and a gastronomic experience waiting at Jinli Street. Additionally, we've meticulously included emergent hotspots for you to sense the vibrancy of Chengdu, featuring destinations like the Panda Valley, the Zhongshuge Bookstore, Dongjiao Memory and the Instagram-worthy panda sculpture. China Highlights is committed to crafting your dream private trip, enabling you to weave an intricate tapestry of treasured memories. Observe Giant and Red Pandas in the Panda Valley It is best to go early in the morning to see the pandas feeding when they are most active during the day. Explore the Trendy and Vibrant Dongjiao Memory Pose for pictures against industrial backdrops and brightly-colored boutiques, sip coffee at an exquisite cafe, and pick up unique designer souvenirs and apparel. Snap a Picture with the Internet-Famous Panda Sculpture Take photos with a cute giant panda sculpture in Yangtianwo Square and share memories with your family and friends. Step Into the Magical Bookstore of Hogwarts Zhongshuge Bookstore is renowned as the most beautiful in Chengdu and a paradise for bookworms and photography enthusiasts. Experience Chengdu's Laid-back Lifestyle Have a cup of Gaiwan tea at Chengdu's oldest tea house, chat with locals, or try the ear-picking service. Begin a Gastronomic Adventure in the Bustling Jinli Street Accompanied by your experienced guide, savor a variety of Sichuan snacks and explore artisanal handicraft shops. Witness the Largest Buddha Statue in the World A stroll allows you to inspect the Buddha's intricate details close-up, while a boat tour affords a comprehensive view, perfect for snapping a memorable picture with this giant sculpture. Enjoy the Spectacle of Mt. Emei Take a cable car to Golden Summit, the crowning glory of Emei Mountain. Immerse yourself in Buddhist culture and appreciate the splendid views of the sea of clouds, mountain ranges, and ancient temples. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. Day 1 Chengdu Arrival Your private guide will be waiting in the arrival hall of the airport with a nameplate and a welcoming smile. You will be transferred to the hotel by private car and driver, and the guide will help you check in and ensure everything is okay at the hotel. Day 2 Chengdu City Tour Explore Wenshu Monastery, a remarkably well-preserved Buddhist temple with over a thousand years of history. It's a peaceful place where you can truly immerse yourself in Buddhist culture. Then, visit People's Park and have a cup of tea. It's a fantastic place to observe the daily life of Chengdu people, from unique matchmaking events and calligraphy to lively square dancing. You might even catch a special folk or flower exhibition! If you're a true foodie, you can't miss the chance to sample a variety of tempting cuisine in Jinli Old Street. With the help of your local guide, embark on a gastronomic journey! Head to Dongjiao Memory, a buzzing street area famed for its distinctive industrial architecture, vibrant livehouses, trendy shops, and abundant coffee spots. It's a haven for young enthusiasts, who can be found effortlessly taking chic photos throughout the area. Admire the illuminated night view of Anshun Bridge, then return to your hotel or enjoy some free time at the nearby Jiuyanqiao Bar Street. Day 3 Dujiangyan Tour Take a 1.5-hour drive from the city to Dujiangyan Panda Valley to witness lively giant and red pandas. In the red panda release area, you can observe red pandas at close range! Known as Chengdu's most attractive bookstore, Zhongshuge Bookstore uses mirrored ceilings to create an expansive, Hogwarts-like haven ideal for photography enthusiasts. Though both are giant projects, the Great Wall no longer serves its original purpose and remains as historic ruins. In contrast, the Dujiangyan irrigation system, over 2,000 years since its inception, continues to protect against floods and irrigate the fertile Chengdu Plain. Visit this place to uncover the secrets of this magnificent project. Finally, take photos of the giant selfie panda sculpture in Yangtianwo Square and back to Chengdu. Day 4 Leshan Giant Buddha The Leshan Giant Buddha, a UNESCO World Natural and Cultural Heritage site, stands as the world's largest stone statue at an impressive 71 meters. This stunning work of art and faith has a rich history of over 1,200 years. We offer two viewing options to visit the Giant Buddha: a boat tour, which allows you to appreciate the full view of the Buddha and take photos, or a walking tour, offering a closer look at the intricate details of the sculpture. After the visit, you will be transported to a hotel in Mount Emei in preparation for the following day. Day 5 Mount Emei Mount Emei, another World Cultural and Natural Heritage site, offers stunning natural scenery and a plethora of temples that provide insight into Buddhist culture. Its Golden Summit is known for providing the opportunity to witness four natural wonders: sunrise, the sea of clouds, Buddha's light, and the holy light. Take a sightseeing bus and after a half-hour walk, take a cable car to Mount Emei's Golden Summit. As you hike, you'll see many naughty wild monkeys–keep an eye on your bags and pockets, as they might snatch your food or belongings! After the tour, retreat in your private vehicle to Chengdu City. Day 6 Chengdu Departure After breakfast, the driver and guide will escort you to the airport or high-speed rail station. China Highlights hopes to create a fabulous memory with you.
