Lost in the Beauty of Zhangjiajie The direct bullet train connecting Hong Kong and Zhangjiajie has been operating daily since 2024, making Zhangjiajie a popular travel destination from Hong Kong. This tour caters to those on a business trip or visiting family in Hong Kong, who are interested in nature and adventure. By joining the 6-day tour, you'll discover Zhangjiajie's top attractions, including the iconic floating Hallelujah Mountains, the breathtaking Tianmen Cave, and the exhilarating Glass Bridge. Benefit from our VIP access to skip the long queue. Suggested 6-Day Hong Kong & Zhangjiajie Tour Day Highlights 1-2 Hong Kong Free to explore the city on your own 3-6 Zhangjiajie Tianmen Mountain, National forest park, and grand canyon glass bridge Soak in the Beauty of Nature at 'Sky Field Garden' Visit 'Sky Field Garden' and be in awe of this compromise between nature and humans. Skip the Queues with VIP Pass The long lines of the Bailong sightseeing elevator and Tianmen Mountain can extend waiting times to 2 hours during peak hours. Opt for VIP access to skip the queues and optimize your time at the park. Feel Your Heart Beating Faster at Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge Walk across the transparent floor suspended over the awe-inspiring natural landscape of the Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon. Go on an Adventure at Tianmen Mountain One of the highlights of Tianmen Mountain is the Tianmen Cave, a naturally formed arch that is often referred to as the "Heaven's Gate." Tianmen Mountain is also known for its winding road with numerous hairpin turns, aptly named the "99 Bends." Ride a Wooden Boat in Baofeng Lake Experience the Peak Tram and get up to the Peak Tower. Enjoy a panoramic view of the city from Victoria Peak. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights The itinerary below can be one possibility and your interests will decide how this trip is to be designed and executed. Day 1 Arrive in Hong Kong After arrival at Hong Kong airport, you will be met by your guide to check-in at your hotel and have some time on your own to relax or explore near your hotel. Day 2 Hong Kong | Free and Leisure Enjoy a free day in Hong Kong to do some shopping or sightseeing. Hong Kong is a heaven for shoppers, high-end fashion, funky styles, and other choices are around every corner. Most of the famous attractions are close to the city center, such as Victoria Peak, Avenue of Stars, and Wong Tai Sin Temple. They are easy to access with the public transportation Day 3 Hong Kong to Zhangjiajie | Bullet Train Breakfast Today, it is advisable to rise early in order to allow sufficient time for customs procedures at the train station. The high-speed train takes around 6½ hours. You can also choose a flight instead, but the flight is not available every day. Transfer to the hotel located in the Wulingyuan area after arrival. Day 4 Zhangjiajie | Hallelujah Mountain with Sky Field Garden Breakfast & Lunch Spend a full day in the national forest park today. Take a cable up from the gate to Tianzi Mountain directly. Tianzi Mountain is famous for its tall quartz sandstone pillars, dense forests, diverse flora and fauna, picturesque landscapes, a mesmerizing cloud sea, and iconic sites such as Yubifeng and Tianzige. Your guide will accompany you to the Laowuchang to shoot the "Sky Field Garden". Then go to Yuanjiajie by shuttle bus. This area is famous and popular for its Hallelujah Mountain, named after Avatar's floating rocks. Pictures of"First Bridge in the World" from "Viewing Bridge Platform" (Wangqiaotai) are a bonus. Take the Bailong Sightseeing Elevator to get down. Day 5 Zhangjiajie | Grand Canyon Glass Bridge and Baofeng Lake Breakfast & Lunch Challenge Yourself on the Glass Bridge at Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon. It is the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge, which is 300 meters above ground level. Explore the various hiking trails in the canyon to witness the stunning landscapes and unique rock formations up close. You'll enjoy a 30-minute ride in a wooden boat at Baofeng lake. It is a leisurely experience as you cruise on the clear water, view hills of various shapes, and enjoy listening to the local minority people singing their traditional songs. Day 6 Zhangjiajie Departure | Tianmen Mountain Breakfast Tianmen Mountain is regarded as the 'soul' of Zhangjiajie and is its highest mountain. The cable car ride takes 30 minutes from the city center to the mountaintop and it is the longest cableway in the world. Tianmen Cave (meaning 'Heaven's Gate') is a 30-meter-high hole that was formed naturally in the mountain and looks like a doorway. It is well-known for being glided through by world-class stuntmen in wingsuits. To reach the hole, you can climb for about half an hour up the 'Stairway to Heaven'. After the trip, we will transfer you to the airport and wave goodbye.
