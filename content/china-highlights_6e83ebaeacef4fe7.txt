Title: Zhangjiajie Weather in October: Travel Tips

Source: China Highlights

Weather: The temperature falls gradually in October, and it is cool and dry. The trees have autumnal colors. If it isn't raining, the sky is clear and the air is crisp. The average high temperature is 23 °C (73 °F) and the average low is 14 °C (57 °F). There is about 81 mm of rain, and it rains about 11 days of the month. Clothing: A sweater or a light jacket is adequate along with a thick shirt and pants. Things to Do in Zhangjiajie in October Admire the View in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is known as "home of the Avatar mountains". October is the best season to enjoy the autumnal colors. You can see fall scenery all around while hiking in the park. You can see giant sandstone pinnacles rising skyward from the valley floor - some up to 200 meters tall with tufts of evergreen shrubs clinging to their narrow summits. Visit Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge has become a hot attraction since it opened. It is the world's longest and highest glass-bottomed bridge, with a length of 430 meters (1,410 feet) and a height of 300 meters (984 feet). Another highlight of the mountain is the world's longest cable car extending over 7 kilometers from downtown Zhangjiajie to the top of the mountain. Experience the Mid-Autumn Festival The Mid-Autumn Festival is the second biggest holiday in China. It's on the 15th day of the eighth month of the Chinese lunar calendar. It occasionally falls in early October near the October 1st National Day holiday and becomes part of the "golden week" holiday. You can not only taste various moon cakes but can also have a chance to see lantern exhibitions. Touring Zhangjiajie in October October is a peak travel season in Zhangjiajie. Pleasant weather, the autumnal scenery, and the National Day holiday attract a large number of tourists to Zhangjiajie. If the Mid-Autumn Festival is also in the month, the crowds are greater.
