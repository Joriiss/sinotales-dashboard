Guilin is reputed as being one of the world's most beautiful places with its karst landscapes, emerald rivers, idyllic countryside, majestic rice terraces, and ethnic minorities. You're in good hands with us, as China Highlights is based in Guilin and has been working to provide unique and personalized trips in Guilin for more than 25 years. We inspect the attractions/experiences nearly every month in order to be up to speed with the most suitable way to discover them and also find yet more activities that are unique to Guilin but have still not been promoted to most travelers.
