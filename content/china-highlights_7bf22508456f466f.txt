Title: 1-Day Panda-Themed Family Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chengdutour/cd-1a/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Spend a Panda-Filled Day This one-day panda-themed tour is perfect for families with kids - ideal for panda enthusiasts who are short on time but want an immersive experience. You will visit the Chengdu Panda Base and participate in our exclusive panda-themed Chinese ink painting workshop at a local art studio. Additionally, don't miss the Instagram-famous 'Here I Am' panda sculpture and a charming panda-themed afternoon tea experience. We can customize a comfortable itinerary tailored to your needs. Witness the Giant Pandas Visit the Panda Breeding and Research Center to understand the pandas' daily habits and capture their endearing moments. Create a Chinese Ink Painting About Pandas Under the guidance of a professional instructor, learn to wield the Chinese ink brush correctly and then craft your own depiction of pandas. Enjoy a Popular Panda-Themed Afternoon Tea Savor a delightful panda-themed afternoon tea experience while shopping for adorable panda merchandise, including panda plush mystery boxes. Take Photos with the Iconic Climbing Panda Sculpture Come to the bustling Chunxi Road shopping street and share pictures of the internet-famous panda sculpture on your social media. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Suggested Itinerary Here is a suggested itinerary and the timetable is only for reference. We can also flexibly adjust the itinerary based on your arrangement and the day's situation. 9:00 am Meet Your Guide In the morning, your guide will pick you up from your hotel in urban Chengdu. Your guide will call you one day in advance to your hotel or leave a message to double-check the pick-up time with you. 10:00 am Panda Breeding and Research Center The first stop is Panda Breeding and Research Center, home to nearly 50 giant pandas and red pandas. This beautifully laid-out park is about 10 kilometers (six miles) from downtown Chengdu, with excellent walkways to various open panda enclosures. The base is called a paradise for national treasures and features luxury panda villas nestled amid the woods. 2:00 pm Chinese Painting Experience About Panda After carefully observing pandas in the morning, head to a downtown art studio to create your ink painting of pandas. Guided by a traditional Chinese painting instructor, you'll learn to master the Chinese ink brush. You can not only create traditional Chinese ink paintings but also let your imagination run wild, crafting a unique panda based on your envision. 4:00 pm Panda-themed Afternoon Tea Visit Chunxi Road for an unforgettable panda-themed afternoon tea experience - where delectable treats meet Instagram-worthy presentation. While you're waiting, you can browse and purchase from a range of panda-themed souvenirs available on the premises. 4:30 pm Take Photos with the Panda Sculpture After that, you will be transferred to take photos with the 15-meter-tall climbing panda sculpture. Created by preeminent artist Lawrence Argent, this climbing wall panda is called "I Am Here!" It will be an interesting experience to capture the moment with this trendy landmark of Chengdu. 5:00 pm Wave Goodbye At the end of the trip, we will escort you to your hotel, the airport, the cruise port, or the train station.
