If you're seeking dry weather, winter scenery, and a less-crowded atmosphere, Yunnan in February might be just what you're looking for. Read on for more travel tips for visiting Yunnan in February. • Yunnan Weather in February • Top 5 Places to Visit • Crowds and Costs Yunnan Weather in February In February, the average daily temperature range in Yunnan is from 5°C to 20°C (41–68°F) and it's dry throughout. The north remains quite cold, the central region gradually warms up, and the south is still warm. Northern Yunnan (Lijiang and Shangri-La) The northern regions remain quite cold, especially Shangri-La, where temperatures could drop to freezing and occasional frosts may occur. Lijiang is relatively warmer, but still chilly at night. Daytime temperatures are usually between 10°C and 15°C (50–59°F), while nighttime temperatures could drop to about 0°C (32°F). Clothing suggestions: Take warm trousers, jacket, coat, and sweaters. Central Yunnan (Dali and Kunming) In the central regions, temperatures gradually warm up. Dali and Kunming are generally mild and comfortable in February. Daytime temperatures typically range from 10–17°C (50–63°F), with nighttime temperatures between 5°C and 10°C (41–50°F). Kunming experiences stable weather with ample sunshine, while Dali may be slightly more humid, but has more moderate temperatures overall. Clothing suggestions: Pack a lightweight jacket, some sweaters, or long-sleeved shirts for the evenings and T-shirts for the milder daytime. Southern Yunnan (Xishuangbanna) In the southern region, Xishuangbanna is warm and comfortable in February, with temperatures usually between 11°C and 28°C (52–82°F), making it ideal for outdoor activities. The humidity is higher, but precipitation is low, and the climate is very dry with plenty of sunshine. Here are the top five recommended places in Yunnan in February. In Lijiang, February mornings and evenings could be quite windy but once the sun comes out at noon, it becomes pleasantly warm. The scenery under the sunlight is even more beautiful and perfect for photography. Chinese New Year usually falls in February and if you visit Lijiang during this time, you could experience the festive atmosphere in Lijiang Old Town. On New Year's Eve, there is usually a fireworks show in Lijiang Old Town. In February, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain still offers magnificent snowy views. Since it's the low season, you could better appreciate the scenery of Blue Moon Valley, especially on its many clear, cloudless days. In February, Kunming enjoys abundant sunshine, making it perfect for a city walk. You could visit Dounan Flower Market to see a variety of flowers or enjoy the blossoms at Green Lake Park. A highly recommended activity is feeding seagulls at Haigeng Dam by Dianchi Lake. By the end of February, Luoping's rapeseed flowers enter their peak blooming period. A 2-to-3-hour drive from Kunming would take you to vast fields of golden flowers. If you're captivated by the beauty of the cloudless blue skies in Yunnan, then autumn and winter are the best times to visit. In Dali, the autumn and winter months are mostly sunny, and February is no exception. The sky is blue and so is the water of Er Lake. February is also the last month to see flocks of migratory seagulls. Visiting Dali during the low season allows you to better appreciate its unique charm. You could watch a sunset over Erhai Lake at Longkan Wharf, explore the history of the Tea Horse Road in Shaxi Ancient Town, and experience the nightlife in Dali Old Town in the evening. If you're a photography enthusiast or nature lover, you shouldn't miss the Yuanyang Rice Terraces in February. The terraces are at their most stunning during the irrigation period in February, when the reflections on the water create mesmerizing views. The beauty of the scenery depends on the sky — blue skies create blue terraces, while sunrises and sunsets paint them in vibrant colors. February is also the peak season for misty seas of clouds, making it the best time to witness the terraces shrouded in mist. In addition, you could also explore the largest preserved Hani ethnic village in China, where you would see traditional mushroom-shaped houses and learn about Hani culture. Tropical Botanical Garden Xishuangbanna is an ideal escape from the cold in February. The daytime climate in February is warm and sunny. There, you could immerse yourself in the unique culture of the Dai minority, experience the tropical rainforest, and be surrounded by lush greenery and abundant fruit. Explore the rainforest and various plants at Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden. Visit Wild Elephant Valley to catch a glimpse of Asian elephants. In the evening, take a stroll through Starlight Night Market, one of the largest night markets in Asia, and savor an authentic Dai-style barbecue. Chinese New Year sometimes falls in February and during this period, Yunnan becomes a popular vacation destination for many Chinese travelers. With many locals also out and about, this time tends to be crowded and attractions could be busy. Travel costs are usually higher compared to other times in February. During Chinese New Year, some shops and restaurants close, but many remain open, so the selection might be limited. Hotels and attractions operate as usual. Aside from the Chinese New Year holiday, the rest of February is the low season, with fewer crowds and more affordable prices. It's an excellent time for travelers seeking a quieter experience and better deals.
