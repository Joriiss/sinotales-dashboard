Winter touring and shopping in Chongqing Weather: It is cold and overcast. The average daily temperature ranges from 6 °C (43 °F) at the lowest to 11 °C (52 °F) at the highest. It sometimes freezes at night. It is usually cloudy and smoggy, and drizzle or light snow falls on 10 days of the month for a monthly total of 20 mm. This is much less precipitation than most of the rest of the year. Less rain is good for touring, sightseeing, and activities outside. Forecast: The weather is changeable, so stay abreast of the local weather forecast>> Air quality: Heavily polluted. On some days, smog is severe. Those with respiratory issues should avoid the city. Clothing: A coat, layers of clothes, and some rain gear will help you stay comfortable. Things to Do in Chongqing in January Shop and Sightsee at the Major Shopping Areas People's Liberation Monument in Jiefangbei Square Chongqing shopping: You can take advantage of your time in the city for shopping. The top luxury shopping area is in the Jiefangbei area shown in the picture. You'll find Maison Mode and many luxury shops near Jiefangbei Square. Experience the Aromatherapy of Ronghui Hot Springs The Ronghui Hot Springs is rated the best by foreign tourists. It is part of the luxurious Raddison Hotel in the center of the city. There are dozens of different pools with different scents. There are restaurants and healthful snacks. Chongqing is known around China for its hot spring spas. The Three Gorges Museum is a large, modern and interesting museum in which to spend a few hours. It is a fascinating place to learn about the region and its history. Displays teach about one of the biggest dams in the world, the Three Gorges Dam.
