Xiamen has intercity air transport, train transport, and coach transport. Transportation within Xiamen is also very convenient: we have information on taxis, city buses, ferries, and cycling. It's very convenient for you to travel to Xiamen and its main attractions (Gulangyu Island and Yongding's Earth Buildings). Xiamen Gaoqi International Airport (厦门高崎国际机场) is merely 10 kilometers (6 miles) from downtown. It has daily domestic flights to more than 40 large cities in China, such as Beijing, Shanghai, Hangzhou, Xi'an, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Guilin, and regional tourist destinations like Mount Putuo and Mount Wuyi. It also has direct international flights to Hong Kong, Macau, Bangkok, Singapore, Manila, Seoul, Amsterdam, and Osaka. By Taxi The driving time from the airport to the city center is less than 30 minutes. Xiamen Station - Ordinary Trains Every day more than 100 ordinary trains come and go from Xiamen Railway Station. Direct connections include north to Beijing, south to Guangzhou, and west as far as Xi'an, Chongqing, and Kunming. For regular trains search here. • Xiamen Station (厦门站): at the junction of Xiahe Road (厦禾路) and Hudong Road (湖东路). Take bus 16, 21, 28, or 37. Xiamen North Station - High-Speed Trains Shenzhen, Quanzhou, Fuzhou, Wenzhou, Ningbo, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Suzhou, and Shanghai, are on the High-Speed Railway (HSR) through Xiamen North Train Station. There are many high-speed trains between Xiamen North and Shenzhen North Station. From there you can take a high-speed train to Guangzhou, or a bus or subway train to Hong Kong. • Xiamen North Station (厦门北站): Houxi Town, Jimei District. Take bus 690, 790, 890, 901, 905, 907, 936, 950, 953, or 980. Xiamen Bus/Coach travel Direct buses depart to many domestic cities, as far north as Shanghai and Nanjing, and as far south as Guangzhou, but journeys are longer and pricier than for trains. Bus travel is best for regional attractions. For example, bus is the best way to Yongding (famous for Hakka Earthen Buildings), apart from private transport (see our tour services below). Public buses are 1 yuan in summer and 2 yuan in winter. There are also special-line minibuses. The names of the starting and ending stops are in the bus window. You can catch one en route. The fare depends on the distance you travel, at least 1 yuan, payable on boarding. These minibuses are usually used by locals. They're very convenient if you can read the signs or know which one to take, because they often stop if you wave for them, and they may stop when you call out to get off. Day Sightseeing Bus - Xiamen Island Circuit Distance: 50 km, 1 hour 30 minutes Major Stops: (Hubin Long-Distance Bus Station) – Jinyuan Mansion Square (金源大厦广场, the starting point) – Great Hall of The People (人民大会堂) – Tourist Ferry Terminal (旅游客运码头) – Heping Port (和平码头站) – Hulishan Battery (湖里山炮台) – Peal Bay (珍珠湾) – Taiwan Folk Village (台湾民俗村) – Haiyun Tai (海韵台) – Yefeng Village (椰风寨) – Convention Center (会展中心) – Zhongzhai Wan Bridge (中宅湾大桥) – Jinyuan Mansion Square Departs: 08:40–16:40 (every 40 minutes, except the 12:00, 13:00, and 14:00) Fare: 20 yuan usually, or 30 yuan on weekends and public holidays (children under 1.4 meters pay half) Good to know: Keep the ticket if you get off at a stop for sightseeing, and then you can get on the bus again (the same day). However, you cannot get on the bus twice at the same stop. Night Sightseeing Bus Distance: 60 km, 2 hours Major stops: Jinyuan Mansion Square – Hubin West Road – Hubin North Road – Lianyue Road – Xianyue Road – Jiahe Road – Lianban – Hubin South Road – Lujiang Avenue – Yanwu Bridge – Baicheng (furthest stop) – Lujiang Avenue – Xiahe Road – Shifu Avenue – Jinyuan Mansion Square Departs: 19:30 (Monday to Thursday); 19:30 and 20:30 (Friday); 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 (weekends) There are plenty of taxis, charging 8 yuan for the first 3 kilometers (about 1.9 miles), and 2 yuan for each additional kilometer. Bicycle rental shops are mostly along Huandao Road (环岛路), the quiet and beautiful road along the coast of Xiamen Island. The deposit is about 300 yuan, and cost varies, from about 10 yuan per hour.
