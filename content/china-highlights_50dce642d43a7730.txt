Vegetarianism has been popular in developed countries for many years. An increasing number of Chinese people have joined the trend in search of a healthy lifestyle. Vegetarians are less likely to suffer from chronic illnesses such as coronary heart disease, diabetes, and cancer. Let's eat more vegetables and fruits and live a healthy life together! Whether you're a resident of Shanghai or just touring the city, you will find numerous restaurants that cater for vegetarians and vegans. Typical entrées served in these eateries include soups, salads, noodles, sushi, burgers, desserts, and much more. The selection of food is astounding, giving vegetarians and vegans considerable choice. Shanghai offers a great selection of vegetarian restaurants. The variety of foods is immense given China's long Buddhist history. Those who believe vegetarians have limited choice will have to think again. Even if you are not yourself a vegetarian or vegan, why not give some of our recommendations a try? Here is our selection of restaurants to set you up in your exploration of what the city has to offer. 1.
