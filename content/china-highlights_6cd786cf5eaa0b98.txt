High-speed trains run between Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station and Guangzhou South Railway Station, which are far away from both cities' downtown. Normal-speed overnight trains run between Shanghai / Shanghai South Railway Station and Guangzhou/Guangzhou East Railway Station, which are located near city centers. The fastest bullet train takes less than 7 hours to travel between these two cities. Overnight trains (D-category trains) only operate on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and Mondays.
