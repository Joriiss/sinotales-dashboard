Title: How to Travel from Singapore to Zhangjiajie

To travel from Singapore to Zhangjiajie, it is best to take a plane. Currently there is no direct flight. You are likely better off flying directly to nearby Changsha and then taking a bullet train from there. How to Get to Zhangjiajie from Singapore by Airplane and Bullet Train There are direct flights to nearby Changsha from Singapore. It takes about 5 hours. The flights cost about 100-200 USD one way or 150-250 USD for a return trip, depending on the time of year. To travel from Changsha to Zhangjiajie, you can take a bullet train with a journey time of around 2-3 hours. The train departs from either Changsha Railway Station or Changsha South Railway Station and arrives at Zhangjiajie West Railway Station. It takes about 1 hour by car from Changsha airport to the railway station. Tickets for these train journeys cost approximately from 50 USD. The ride will take approximately 4 hours, depending on traffic, and we can take you directly to the front door of your hotel. Suggested reading: 7-Day Changsha, Zhangjiajie, Fenghuang Ancient Town Tour How to Get to Zhangjiajie from Singapore by Air with a Stopover If you don't want the hassle of transferring between a flight and a train, you can choose to take a flight to Zhangjiajie with a stopover in between. The fastest option takes 10-12 hours. The cost is approximately from 400 USD. The total journey time is nearly equal to the option of taking a flight plus a train. Zhangjiejie Travel Tips Zhangjiajie National Forest Park gets very crowded at peak times. The best time to go is when there are no public holidays in China, as it gets really busy in Zhangjiajie, and when the weather in Zhangjiajie is best between September and early November.
