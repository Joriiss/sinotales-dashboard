Beijing and Tianjin are neighboring cities with very convenient transportation between them. The nearest international cruise port to Beijing (Tianjin International Cruise Home Port) is also located in Tianjin. This article will introduce the main transportation options from Beijing to Tianjin. • 1. Beijing City to Tianjin City • 2. Beijing City to Tianjin Port • 3. Beijing Airports to Tianjin Port • 4. Tianjin Port to Beijing Great Wall 1. How to Get from Beijing City to Tianjin City The Porcelain House in Tianjin Beijing is very close to Tianjin, so there are no flights. The fastest and most convenient way is by high-speed train, which is also the most recommended option, taking only 30 minutes to arrive. For the departure station, choose Beijing South Station, as most trains to Tianjin depart from there. Regarding the arrival station, there are three train stations in Tianjin city. The first choice is Tianjin Station, located in the city center and close to major attractions. Alternatively, you could choose Tianjin West Station, which is about a 30-minute combination of subway and walking from the city center. There are many trains from Beijing to Tianjin every day. Except during holidays, you don't need to buy tickets in advance; purchasing one day ahead is usually sufficient. How to Get from Beijing City to Tianjin Port Tianjin International Cruise Home Port If you have a lot of luggage or are traveling with a group, the best option is to take a taxi or book a private transfer. The estimated taxi fare is around ¥900 ($126). (This is a reference price, and the actual cost may vary depending on traffic conditions.) The drive from Beijing to Tianjin Port takes approximately 2.5 hours. We recommend departing from Beijing 3.5 hours before your boarding time to ensure you don't miss the ship. For a more economical option, you could take a high-speed train from Beijing to Binhai Station, which takes about 1 hour. From Binhai Station, you could take a taxi to the port, which takes about 35 minutes and costs around ¥75 ($11). Binhai Station also has Bus 513 that goes directly to the port, taking about 1.5 hours. How to Get from Beijing Airports to Tianjin Port Similarly, if you want a convenient and time-saving option, the best choice is a taxi or private transfer. It takes about 2.5 hours from Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) to the port and 2 hours from Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX) to the port. The estimated taxi fare is around ¥700-800 ($98-112). For a more economical option, you could take the subway from the airport to Beijing South Station, then a high-speed train to Binhai Station, and finally a bus or taxi from Binhai Station to the port. The entire journey takes about 3-4.5 hours and costs approximately ¥100-190 ($14-27). 4. How to Get from Tianjin Port to Beijing Great Wall Our guestes visited the Mutianyu Great Wall There are many sections of the Great Wall. We usually recommend the Mutianyu section because it's less crowded and flatter, so it's great for seniors and kids. Mutianyu is about a 3.5-hour drive from Tianjin Port. A private transfer is the recommended option. Whether you want to visit Mutianyu or other sections like Jiankou or Simatai, it allows for a more convenient and stress-free journey. If you prefer to explore by yourself, you could take a taxi from the port to Binhai Station, then a high-speed train to Beijing South Station, and transfer to the subway to Shaoyaoju Station to catch the tourist bus to Mutianyu. The entire trip takes about 5 hours. Besides the time, you also need to be familiar with China's transportation system to make this journey.
