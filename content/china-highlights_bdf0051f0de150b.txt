Title: How to Travel from Shanghai to the Great Wall of China

Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

The Great Wall is about 1,300 kilometers (800 miles) away from downtown Shanghai. As swift and convenient transportation is available, the two places are not as far apart as they seem. If you are already in or plan to be in Shanghai, it is possible to visit one section of the Great Wall in Beijing within 1 or 2 days. For first-time visitors, the Great Wall at Mutianyu is recommended as this section of the Great Wall is well-preserved and not as crowded as other sections. See the top 10 sections of the Great Wall to visit. Just read on to see how to travel from Shanghai to the Great Wall and how to visit the Great Wall in 1 or 2 days. Step 1: Shanghai to Beijing There are over 50 direct flights and 40 high-speed trains from Shanghai to Beijing. Both types of transportation are convenient for overseas travelers. If you haven't experienced China's high-speed railway, you are recommended to have a try. There are over 50 flights from Shanghai to Beijing every day. Most of the flights depart from Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) to Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK), and about 14 flights depart from Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG). Taking a flight from Shanghai to Beijing takes about 2 hours 20 minutes. Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport Arriving in Beijing on a flight is the best choice as a flight is quicker than taking a train and the airport is closer to the Great Wall. There are 40 pairs of high-speed trains running from Shanghai to Beijing every day. Most of the trains depart from Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station to Beijing South Railway Station, and about four trains depart from Shanghai Railway Station. A high-speed train between Shanghai and Beijing operates at a speed of 350 kph and takes you to your destination in 5–6 hours. See how to take a train in China. Step 2: Railway Station / Airport to the Great Wall at Mutianyu Great Wall at Mutianyu There are no direct metro lines or buses from Beijing Capital International Airport and Beijing South Railway Station to the Great Wall at Mutianyu. You need to take the metro to downtown Beijing first and then transfer to at least two buses to get there. Traveling by metro and bus is not recommended as the transfers could be hard to manage if you are not familiar with Beijing, and it may take you 3 hours or more. A better choice is to take a taxi, which will take you to the Great Wall at Mutianyu in 1 hour from the airport or 2 hours from the railway station. Be aware that the language barrier could be a challenge. Check the following tour plan: Morning: Take a flight or train ride from Shanghai to Beijing. Noon: Have a meal near the Great Wall. Afternoon: Visit the Great Wall at Mutianyu. You may choose to take a cable car or chairlift up to the wall in 7 minutes, or hike for about 45 minutes. Then, enjoy hiking between the watchtowers and take some excellent photos on the wall.
