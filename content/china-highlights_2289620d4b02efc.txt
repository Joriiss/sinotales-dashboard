Xianggong Hill is a superb viewing platform for Guilin's karst landscape. You could capture the sunrise, sea of clouds, and the iconic S-bend of the Li River. It is located on the western bank of the Li River, about 60 kilometers (a 1.5-hour drive) from downtown Guilin. If you're coming from Yangshuo, it's even closer - just 26 kilometers, roughly a 50-minute drive. The fastest route is from Xingping Ancient Town: take a 5-minute boat ride followed by a 25-minute transfer to reach the scenic area. Through firsthand exploration, I'll share key details - best viewpoints, optimal visiting hours, transport tips, and itinerary advice. It will help you plan your trip and photograph the Li River at its finest. Three Platforms on Xianggong Hill Xianggong Hill Platforms With an elevation of about 350 meters, Xianggong Hill features a stone-step hiking trail. With some steep sections, Xianggong Hill offers a moderate climbing difficulty (last time, I brought a 10-year-old child who managed it without breaks). The hike to the summit takes 15-20 minutes. Along the way, there are three tiers of viewing platforms from platform 1-3. They all face the First Bend of the Li River but providing slightly different perspectives: • Platform 1 It offers the most expansive views. This makes it the prime spot for sunrise and sea of clouds. However, its small size gets crowded during peak season (May-October), so arrive early to secure a good position. • Platform 2 Ideal for capturing the Li River's S-bend + fishing boats in a more relaxed setting. Less crowded than Platform 1, perfect for non-sunrise hours. • Platform 3 Best for portrait + landscape combo shots, featuring layered karst peaks as a stunning backdrop. Great for avoiding crowds while still showcasing Guilin's iconic scenery. Best Time to Visit Xianggong Hill: When to Go for Stunning Shots? Xianggong Hill Sunrise The optimal time to visit and photograph Xianggong Hill is from September to November. During this autumn season, you could enjoy cool, comfortable weather with exceptionally clear visibility. It offers perfect conditions for capturing breathtaking sunrise shots over the Li River. The period from May to August tends to be hot and humid with frequent rainfall (especially during May and June). However, this season offers higher chances (about 50%) to witness spectacular sea of clouds, especially on early mornings right after rainy days. December to April often brings morning mist, making the Li River and peaks faintly visible - perfect for capturing misty riverscape photography. Best Time to Shoot Sunrise The Xianggong Hill scenic area opens at 5:00 AM (May-October) and 5:30 AM (November-April). For summer sunrise shots, arrive at the summit by 5:30 AM; in winter, aim for 6:00 AM to secure a good spot. The best shooting position is on the left side of the summit platform.
