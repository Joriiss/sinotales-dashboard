Weather: The temperature is comfortable. It ranges from an average low of 15 °C (59 °F) at night to a high of 23 °C (73 °F) during the day. But it turns rainy. Rain falls in half the days of the month for a total of 105 mm. It is usually partly cloudy or cloudy. See the flowers in the parks. Forecast: The weather is changeable, so stay abreast of the local weather forecast>> Clothing: We recommend that you wear a light jacket in the mornings and evenings. A long-sleeve shirt at noon is adequate. Have rain gear. Things to Do in Chongqing in April The Lesser Three Gorges: A fine time to visit is in spring when everything is green. Both the water and the landscape are green. The color contrasts with the muddy brown color of the Yangtze. The scenery is better than at the big Three Gorges. The green canyon walls tower above the river. Ciqikou Old Town: Spring is a good time to wander around and sightsee in the large old city center of Chongqing. There are less crowds and much less rain before the heat of summer. The old buildings in this area have been preserved. Tourists like the local spicy hot snacks and yoghurt and hunt for bargains in the shops. See the Three Hanging Bridges Wulong Karst: This famed natural park has large caves and three natural stone bridges. It is four hours from Chongqing. It is World Heritage Listed. April is a good time to go for hiking and touring before the heat and heavy rain of summer. Touring Chongqing in April Shoulder Travel Season Travel volume picks up as the weather warms. Flight tickets and hotel rooms are higher priced. Train tickets are readily available.
