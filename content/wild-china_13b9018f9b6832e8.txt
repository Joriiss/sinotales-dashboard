Title: Mei Zhang Named a Town and Country Travel Guru

Town and Country Magazine is known globally for its showcase of exceptional people and exclusive places. This year, WildChina founder Mei Zhang was named a Town and Country travel guru. The magazine selected her as one of the top thirty-four global leaders in the travel industry. In the words of Town and Country, these travel gurus’ day job is to design the best itineraries out there. RELATED READING: Yunnan Food According to Mei Zhang In the June/July 2018 print issue of Town & Country, Mei and the other gurus nominated vetted hotels in their guide to the best new hotels in the world. Mei’s suggestion? – The new Amanyangyun in Shanghai. RELATED READING: Introducing the Amanyangyun About the Amanyangyun, the article writes: “The design, as one would expect of an Aman, is stunning. Structures from the Ming and Qing dynasties have been transplanted and reassembled, brick by ancient brick, to create 37 suites and villas 45 minutes outside Shanghai. Wow Factor: The owner also moved thousands of trees—the relocated forest is something you have to see to believe.” Want to visit China with one of the world’s top travel guru and stay in the top new hotel in the region? Plan your trip with WildChina today and we’ll provide you with a private, local guide who’ll lead you through our insider experiences in Shanghai like a sidecar ride through the French Concession and Bund history expert. When you’re ready to tuck in for the night, we can arrange your accommodation in the Amanyangyun to complement your stay.
