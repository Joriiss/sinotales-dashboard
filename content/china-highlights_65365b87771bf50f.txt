China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select • China Train • Int’l Train from China The Guangzhou – Shenzhen – Hong Kong Express Rail Link, also called the Guangzhou – Shenzhen – Hong Kong High-Speed Railway, links Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hong Kong, and connect Hong Kong with the high-speed railway network in Chinese mainland. The journey time from Chinese mainland to Hong Kong and vice versa are greatly reduced. High-speed trains from Hong Kong to Guangzhou/Shenzhen depart from Hong Kong's newly-built West Kowloon Railway Station (Chinese: 香港西九龙站). They arrive at Futian, Shenzhen North, Guangzhou South railway stations as destination teminals. The shortest travel time for train journey from Hong Kong to Shenzhen North is only 19 minutes; from Hong Kong to Guangzhou South is only 47 minutes. • Distance: 142 kilometers (88 miles) • Terminals: Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station, Shenzhen North Railway Station, Guangzhou South Railway Station • Stops: Hong Kong West Kowloon (香港西九龙), Futian (福田), Shenzhen North (深圳北), Humen (虎门), Guangmingcheng (光明城), Qingsheng (庆盛), Guangzhou South (广州南) • Futian – Hong Kong shortest duration: 14 minutes • Shenzhen – Hong Kong shortest duration: 19 minutes • Guangzhou – Hong Kong shortest duration: 48 minutes Hong Kong to Shenzhen/Guangzhou High-Speed Train Schedules (Updated on March 14th, 2025) Hong Kong to Futian Train Number Departure Time Arrival Time Duration G5820 07:07 07:21 14 min G6582 07:25 07:39 14 min G5810 14:39 14:53 14 min G5832 19:00 19:14 14 min G5826 21:15 21:29 14 min Hong Kong to Shenzhen Train Number Departure Time Arrival Time Duration G5624 07:03 07:21 18 min G56582 08:25 08:49 24 min G6534 10:42 11:00 18 min G5620 13:38 13:56 18 min G6586 16:57 17:15 18 min G6538 17:30 17:48 18 min Hong Kong to Guangzhou Train Number Departure Time Arrival Time Duration G6582 08:25 09:29 1 h 4 min G6528 10:30 11:29 59 min G6530 13:25 14:34 1 h 9 min G6586 16:57 18:04 1 h 7 min G6526 17:13 18:22 46 min G6538 17:30 18:32 1 h 2 min *There are 54 pairs of bullet trains running on the Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong high-speed railway every day. Hong Kong Train Ticket Prices Guangzhou – Hong Kong Train • Business class: US$ 66 (452 CNY) • First class: US$ 48 (323 CNY) • Second class: US$ 32 (215 CNY) Shenzhen – Hong Kong Train • Business class: US$ 34 (226 CNY) • First class: US$ 18 (120 CNY) • Second class: US$ 12 (75 CNY) Children's Tickets for High-Speed Trains to/from Hong Kong According to China Railway's policy, children under 12 years of age should be accompanied by an adult. Children's tickets can be booked online. Fares are as below: 1. Children below 1.2 m (3.9 feet) in height can travel for free, but without their own seat or berth. A child ticket is needed if a seat or berth is required. One adult can bring only one child (under 1.2 m) for free. If there is only one adult and more than one child, the other children need to buy children's tickets. 2. Children 14 years of age and under, not taller than 1.5 m (4.9 feet) are eligible for children's tickets. The children's ticket price is half that of an adult ticket. 3. Children more than 14 years of age or more than 1.5 m (4.9 feet) tall need to buy adult tickets at the full fare. What Is the Train Like? 1. Guangzhou – Shenzhen – Hong Kong high-speed trains are called "Dongganhao Chinese: 动感号". 2. Each train has 8 carriages. The first and last carriages are first-class. The remaining 6 carriages are second-class. Two wheelchair seats are available in the No.7 carriage. 3. The whole train has access to Wi-Fi. 4. First-class has four seats (2+2) arranged across the carriage width. Second class has five seats (3+2) arranged across the carriage width. 5. Each seat is equipped with power sockets. Each row of seats can be rotated 180 degrees. 6. Each carriage has luggage racks for large luggage. And there are luggage racks above the seats for suitcases less than 24 inches wide. 7. A handicapped restroom is available at the connection between carriage No. 7 and carriage No. 8, and equipped with a diaper-changing table. How to Change Hong Kong Train Ticket • After the tickets are issued, China Railways only allows changes on train number, seat class and travel date. Departure and arrival stations and passenger information cannot be changed. • Changes should be made more than 3 hours before departure time. • If no paper tickets have been collected, China Highlights will handle all the changes for you online. • If paper tickets have been collected, you need to change the tickets at the train station yourself with the original ID documents. High-speed train from Guangzhou to Hong Kong Hong Kong Train Ticket Cancellation 1. Cancellations should be made more than 3 hours before departure time. 2. If no paper tickets have been collected, China Highlights will handle all the cancellations and refunds for you online. 3. If paper tickets have been collected, you need to cancel the tickets at the train station yourself with the original ID documents. • 5% of the ticket price if make cancellation more than 15 days before departure. • 30% of the ticket price if make cancellation 48 hours – 14 days before departure. • 50% of the ticket price if make cancellation less than 48 hours before departure. Please note: if your tickets are issued using Hong Kong Railway's system (e.g. tickets are issued on MTR's website), then we cannot cancel the tickets for you. Cancellations need to be made at the ticket counters in West Kowloon Station with passengers' original passports. Customs and Immigrations at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station For passengers who take high-speed trains from Hong Kong to Chinese mainland or vice versa, immigration inspections are needed at the Immigration/Customs Departure Hall on -3F or Arrival Hall on -2F at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station. All your luggage must also go through security checks and x-rayed. We advise that you arrive at the train station at least 2 hours before your departure time. You will also need to complete the 'Departure Card' and the 'Arrival Card' beforehand. If you fail to obtain and complete these cards, you would be sent back to collect and fill-in the forms, and queue again. The queues can be long and this will cause some inconvenience with the possibility of you missing your train. • Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station • Guangzhou to Hong Kong High-Speed Train • Beijing–Hong Kong Train • Shanghai–Hong Kong Train
