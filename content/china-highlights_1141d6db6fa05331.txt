The hottest months are July and August with average high temperatures around 30°C (86°F). The coldest month is January with an average temperature of -13°C (9°F). Winter is long and cold in Urumqi, and the snow is often heavy. Urumqi sees large temperature differences between day and night, so bring a sweater or jacket even you visit during the hottest month. Spring is short and cool, and usually ends in June. Temperatures can be as low as 10°C (50°F) and up to 30°C (86°F). About 1/3 of the meagre yearly rainfall comes in the spring. Jackets and jeans are recommended, and bring a sweater with you. Things to Do in Spring Late March to April is the apricot blossom season in Xinjiang. The best place to see apricot flowers is in Xinghuagou ('Apricot Blossom Valley') in Ili. In Urumqi's spring, visiting some of the popular cultural sights is recommended too: • See precious and amazing exhibits in Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region Museum. • Experience an Islamic-style bazaar. • Learn about the story of the Silk Road in Xinjiang Silk Road Museum. The duration of summer differs in the city and surrounding mountain and desert areas. Summer is only 62 days long in Urumqi city but goes for 90 days in the suburbs. Temperatures get to around 30°C (86°F) in the day, but fall to around 18°C (65°F) at night. Since Urumqi is the furthest large city from the sea on earth, the air is dry, and even during the hot summer you won't feel stickiness or humidity. Urumqi is a good place to avoid the heat during the summer in China. We recommend that you bring a T-shirt and long pants to Urumqi during the summer, and a jacket for the cool nights. Things to Do in Summer In summer, especially in July, the grasslands of Nanshan Scenic Area are at their best with thick green grass. Enjoy ethnic pastoral scenes. Heavenly Lake is also an ideal place to go in summer. Take a boat to cruise around Heavenly Lake in the Tianshan ('Heavenly Mountains') Range. See the magical colors reflected on the surface of the lake and enjoy beautiful surrounding scenery. Recommended tour: 8-Day Xinjiang Tour with Ili River Valley July to October is the harvest season for grapes. Fall in Urumqi starts from late August. The weather stabilizes in the autumn, and it rains on fewest days. The average temperature is around 13°C (55°F) by the end of fall. After late September, cold air reaches Urumqi from Siberia and the temperature decreases rapidly. During late October, the day-and-night temperature difference is even larger than in the summer. You may be able to wear a T-shirt at noon, but bring a coat for the evenings. Things to Do in Autumn In autumn, Heavenly Lake is also worth visiting to enjoy charming autumnal scenery. The calm water reflects Bogda Peak and the colorful trees along the lake. Autumn is also a harvest season for grapes in Xinjiang. Don't miss the chance to try the sweet and juicy grapes. A visit to Kanas would allow you to revel in the most beautiful autumn scenery in Xinjiang, but it is about 800 kilometers (500 miles) from Urumqi. If you are interested in a visit, contact us. The average high temperature in Urumqi during the winter is below freezing, and lows reach around -15°C (5°F). So winter's generally not recommended for tourism. Some snow falls, much more in the mountains, and it stays all winter, which makes the mountains south of Urumqi a good place to ski. Down-filled coats, and full Siberian winter clothes are recommended. Things to Do in Winter Skiing is a highly recommended thing to do in winter. Silk Road International Ski Resort near Urumqi is the best choice. It is one of the three biggest ski resorts in China. Indoor activities are also recommended such as visiting museums. Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region Museum, which exhibits some of the most precious mummies in the world as well as many other regional historic and prehistoric artifacts, is most recommended.
