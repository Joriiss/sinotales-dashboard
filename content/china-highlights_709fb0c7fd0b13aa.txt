Title: Zhangjiajie Weather in March: Travel Tips

Source: China Highlights

Weather: During March the temperature continues to rise but it is not very warm. The lowest temperature is 7 °C (45 °F), and the highest temperature is 16 °C (61 °F). It rains more than in the winter. There is about 114 mm of total precipitation, and it rains about half of the days of the month. Clothing: March marks the transition period from winter to spring, and temperature differences between day and night are large, so wear layers accordingly. Wear shoes with good traction to avoid slipping on wet paths or stairs. Things to Do in Zhangjiajie in March See the Scenery in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park In March the scenery in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is beautiful. You can see the green-topped high quartzite rock spires. These quartzite rock towers and bluffs inspired generations of painters. One of the rock pillars is an amazing 1,080 meters (3,544 feet) tall. The rock formations are almost unique in the world. The park was honored with World Heritage listing in 1990. You can see the beauty during a walk in early spring when there are fewer tourists and crowds than in the rest of the year. See Yellow Dragon Cave Yellow Dragon Cave is one of Asia's largest caverns. It extends more than ten kilometers, and it is so big there is a boat tour inside. Unless you want the adventure and wish to see the sights without the crowds, the boat tour is preferable. The cave is damp inside, and the walking paths are slick, so wear sneakers or boots with good traction.
