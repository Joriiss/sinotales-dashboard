Title: 3-Day Guangzhou Foodie & Shopping Adventure
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/guangzhou/tours/guangzhou-culinary-shopping-adventure.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

A Palate & Purchase Spree This 3-day adventure provides an exciting blend of flavors and fantastic finds. You'll savor Guangzhou's famous culinary delights, explore its bustling markets for a lively shopping spree, and discover the city's unique mix of old-world charm and modern development. This itinerary is designed to be fully customizable to your needs and interests. Our team is on hand to provide you with personalized support. Find incredible deals and one-of-a-kind items at the clothing and leather wholesale markets! Savor Traditional Cuisine Experience Canton's deep-rooted culinary heritage, a regional tradition refined over centuries, with every bite. Walk through a living history book, where remarkably preserved homes and buildings tell the stories of the past. Encounter Today's Guangzhou Experience modern Guangzhou by ascending the Canton Tower and cruising the Pearl River to admire the spectacular city scenery. Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1Guangzhou Arrival | Meet the Modern Guangzhou Welcome to Guangzhou! Your journey begins with a warm greeting from your knowledgeable tour guide at the Baiyun Airport. Enjoy a seamless transfer to and explore the city. Ascend the Canton Tower to the 108th-floor Star Observation Deck. This highest indoor viewpoint offers unparalleled, breathtaking 360-degree vistas of Guangzhou. Enjoy dinner (at your own expense) at Guangzhou Restaurant, famous for its authentic Cantonese cuisine, including classic dim sum, roast meats, and stir-fries. Glide along the Pearl River on a relaxed Night Cruise to experience Guangzhou's spectacular illuminated skyline, where modern skyscrapers, historic bridges, and iconic landmarks create a dazzling panorama. Day 2Guangzhou | Clothing & Leather Hunt Breakfast Meet your guide and driver for a one-day Clothing and Leather Hunt tour. Explore Baima Clothing Market, famous for its vast, rapidly updated styles. Your guide will help you navigate its zones to find everything from Japanese/Korean-inspired to sweet/retro looks, and affordable European/American fashion. Have lunch at Liuhua Pavilion (on your own expense), known for its Cantonese cuisine that combines classic methods with local ingredients. Shop at Zhonggang Leather Mall, a massive complex packed with hundreds of shops. It's a prime destination for buyers, offering an immense variety of trendy, mid-to-high-end leather goods along with competitive prices and excellent sourcing options. Day 3 Guangzhou Departure | Experience the Cultural Fabric Breakfast Appreciate the traditional Guangdong-style architecture and decoration at Chen Clan Ancestral Hall. It is especially known for its display of delicate craftsmanship and folk art, including historical figures, carvings and cast iron. Venture into Xiguan for a Guided Foodie Tour, where you'll walk along the Yongqingfang Qilou buildings and taste 4-5 authentic Cantonese specialties. Along the way, enjoy cultural insights into local life and architecture, and explore Bruce Lee's Ancestral Home to learn about his family history. A comfortable airport transfer awaits you for the flight home. Day 1Guangzhou Arrival | Meet the Modern Guangzhou Welcome to Guangzhou!
