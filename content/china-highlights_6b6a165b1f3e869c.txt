While Hong Kong and Zhangjiajie may seem like a world apart, most of the 850km (or 530 miles) is easily traveled by either train or airplane. How to Get to Zhangjiajie from Hong Kong by Airplane The direct flight from Hong Kong to Zhangjiajie is only available on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. It typically departs around 4 pm and takes about 2 hours. If you are traveling on other weekdays, it is recommended to consider taking a bullet train for efficiency. How to Get to Zhangjiajie from Hong Kong by Train There is a bullet train departing daily from Hong Kong to Zhangjiajie. It departs from Hong Kong West Kowloon Station at 08:30 and arrives at Zhangjiajie West Railway Station at 14:52. The journey takes about 6.5 hours. You can also go between April and June if you don't mind humidity and the occasional rain. Hong Kong's weather is a little different: September is likely to still be very hot in Hong Kong though, so October or November are better for visiting Hong Kong if you don't cope with heat well.
