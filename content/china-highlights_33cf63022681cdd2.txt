San De Long Halal Restaurant Adhering to the essence of traditional cuisine and shouldering the responsibility of upholding food culture, San De Long was established in 2005 and today holds the reputation as one of Xi'an's best Muslim restaurants. All dishes served at San De Long integrate elements of local Chinese cuisine, which helps the establishment appeal to a wider demographic and promotes the branding of ethnic food. This results in some truly unique and flavorful halal dishes which not only tastes good but also contributes to the continuous growth and development of the local halal food scene. • Chinese Name: 清真三德隆饭庄 Qingzhen San Delong Fanzhuang • Recommended dishes: Sizzling chicken with soybean sauce (铁板豉汁滑鸡), sweet and sour beef (糖醋里脊), and boiled beef (水煮牛肉). • Average price per person: ¥65 or about 9 USD • Address: 219 West Street, Lianhu District (莲湖区西大街219号) • Tel: 029-87288816 • Transportation: Bus 4, 15, 23, 31, 43, or 45 to Qiao zi Kou Stop (桥梓口).
