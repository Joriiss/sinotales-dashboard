Beshan Gāi is coming up quickly on our (and hopefully your) calendar. Over 60 carefully selected artisans, from Xinjiang to Yunnan, are already on their way to Beijing to celebrate artisanal culture and in the lead up to the event series, you have the chance to win some amazing prizes. There are prizes ranging from a WildChina weekend stay in Dali and Brunch for 2 at The Opposite House’s Sureño to 1000RMB to spend at Rosewood Beijing’s Bistrot B. The treasure hunt has already begun so read on to find out how to win! Here’s a reminder of how the treasure hunt works: 1. Follow us on WeChat 2. Check out the graphics at the bottom of this post and when a prize catches your eye just save the image and re-post it on your WeChat moments. 4. Send a snapshot to @BeshanWildChina and your name will automatically be entered into the prize draw. 5. Come to Beshan Gāi 2016 and search for your treasure. You can find the name of the winner on the table of the artisan who offered the prize. Please note: To collect your prize you must attend Beshan Gāi 2016 Here are the prizes up for grabs… Beshan Gāi @Rosewood Beijing (Friday 23 September, 12 – 8pm) Beshan Gāi @The Opposite House (Saturday 24 – Sunday 25 September, 11am – 6pm) BESHAN GAI 2016 With less than one week to go, Beshan Gāi 2016 is set to be an exciting celebration of artisanal China. Friday the 23rd from 12 – 8pm will see Beijing’s Central Business district transformed into a foodie’s paradise as China’s food focused artisans set up shop in Rosewood Beijing. On Saturday 24th and Sunday 25th between 11am and 6pm, The Opposite House in central Sanlitun will be the place to be as artisans and craftsmen specializing in everything from Tibetan handmade jewelry to Jingdezhen Ceramics exhibit and sell their handcrafted treasures. Beshan Gai 2016 is supporting Roundabout China, a charity that constantly inspires us with their efforts across the country. Roundabout will be conducting a clothing drive across the Beshan Gai event series so bring along your gently loved clothes and donate them to a good home. We look forward to seeing you there!
