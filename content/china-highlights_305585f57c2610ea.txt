Title: 7-Day Kunming, Dali, and Lijiang Tour: Yunnan in Winter
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/kunmingtour/7-day-kunming-dali-lijiang-tour.htm

A Memorable Winter Trip in Yunnan Travel to Yunnan this winter and experience a perfect blend of warm sunshine, stunning natural beauty, friendly locals, delicious cuisine, and 5-star accommodations at affordable prices. It's the ideal destination for a memorable Christmas and New Year holiday. Join this 7-day tour to Kunming, Dali, and Lijiang and discover must-see attractions, historical sites, and minority cultures. Highlights include visiting Instagrammable spots and experiencing a traditional Naxi costume change. Benefit from our free & personalized Yunnan itinerary planning service, expertly crafted to ensure a perfect trip for you. Suggested 7-Day Winter Tour Day Highlights 1-2 Kunming Stone Forest with Tram, Dianchi Lake 3 Dali Dali Town, Bai villages, Erhai and Tie-dye Making 4 Shaxi Shaxi Ancient Town 5-7 Lijiang Dayan Town, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, Black Dragon Pool, Yuhu Village Yunnan Winter Tour Map Witness the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Soar to Glacier Park (4,680 meters) and explore the mesmerizing Blue Moon Valley, surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Encounter the Black-headed Gulls Black-headed Gulls flock to Kunming's warm embrace each winter, finding refuge from colder climates. Experience the Naxi culture by indulging in a traditional hot pot, exploring a shaman's dwelling, adorning yourself in traditional garb, and delving into the ancient Naxi tongue. Learn the Bai Tie-Dye Learn the art of making a traditional Tie-dye in Zhoucheng Village and keep your handicraft as a souvenir. Soak up the Sun by Erhai Lake Let the watery serenity of Erhai Lake purify your soul, and have a peaceful moment as the sun descends behind the Cangshan mountains. Meet Shaxi and Meet Yourself Discover Shaxi's rich history by strolling its charming streets and listening to stories shared by the ancient walls, Sifang Street, and the historic stage. Get Better Deals on Luxury Hotels Take advantage of cost-effective rates and enjoy exclusive perks at five-star hotels during the winter season. DAY 1 Kunming Arrival Arrive in Kunming and be greeted by our professional guide and driver, who will escort you to your hotel in a comfortable vehicle. DAY 2 Kunming to Dali | Venture into the Stone Forest Breakfast & Lunch Explore the Stone Forest, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and be amazed by its breathtaking landscape of towering limestone spires resembling a magical stone forest. (Tram included). Bask in the warm sunshine and stroll along the shores of Dianchi Lake, where black-headed gulls glide through the winter sky. Transfer to Kunming Railway Station for a bullet train to Dali, where a private guide and driver will greet you and accompany you to the hotel. DAY 3 Dali | Discover Dali Highlights Breakfast & Lunch Explore Dali Ancient Town's ancient temples, vibrant markets, and charming neighbourhoods to experience its rich history and culture. Pause at the charming square and capture the iconic Three Pagodas, a breathtaking sight against the backdrop of the Cangshan Mountains. Enjoy a comfortable drive to Xizhou Village, a picturesque blend of ancient architecture and Bai ethnic heritage. Learn the art of traditional Tie-dye in Zhoucheng Village and take home your unique creation. Continue your journey to Shuanglang, a charming fishing village, and capture the stunning views of Xiaoputuo Island. Cap off your day at the Santorini-inspired Ideal Kingdom, where you can indulge in an optional afternoon tea beside Erhai. DAY 4 Dali to Shaxi | Unveil the History of Shaxi Breakfast & Lunch Experience the tranquility of Shaxi Ancient Town, a hidden gem on the old Tea-Horse Road and the base of the TV drama of Meet Yourself. Embark on a scenic walk through Shaxi Town. Stroll through Sideng Street, cross Yujin Bridge, and walk along the Heihui River. Discover the Librairie Avant-Garde in Beilong village, housed in a former crop processing factory. Reach Duanjiaden and marvel at a well-preserved 150-year-old Dengkueige Stage. Discover the Masiye Caravan Museum and learn about the horse caravans traversing the Tea Horse Road before returning to your hotel. DAY 5 Shaxi to Lijiang | Dayan Awaits Breakfast & Lunch Relax and enjoy a comfortable transfer to Lijiang with your private guide and driver. Discover the tranquility of Black Dragon Pool Park, a hidden gem in Lijiang. Stroll through the verdant gardens, marvel at the ancient structures, and find tranquility as you contemplate the majestic reflection of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. Delve into the vibrant Naxi culture by wandering through the enchanting Dayan Ancient Town and strolling along Sifang Street to absorb the town's captivating ambiance. DAY 6 Lijiang | Explore Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Breakfast & Lunch Discover Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, the holy mountain of the Naxi people. Take a cable car to Glacier Park at 4,680 meters, where you'll find stunning scenery of snow-covered peaks. Experience the Blue Moon Valley, a stunning valley featuring an incredibly blue river winding its way through (Tram excluded). Savor a traditional Naxi fire pit hot pot for lunch then venture to Yuhu Village, nestled at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. Step into the daily life of a Naxi family by trying on their vibrant traditional attire. Uncover the secrets of Dongba papermaking and learn to write the enigmatic Dongba characters. DAY 7 Lijiang Departure Breakfast Spend your free time exploring Lijiang until your private transfer takes you to the Airport or Train Station for your onward journey.
