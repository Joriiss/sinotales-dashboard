Highlights Itinerary Price FAQ You'll definitely be amazed by the huge contrast between the hustle and bustle of Hong Kong Island and the serene beauty of Lantau Island. Our well-designed two-day Hong Kong essence tour offers a full day tour to explore vibrant landmarks of Hong Kong Island such as Victoria Peak, Tai Kwun, Man Mo Temple and taste dim sum in a Michelin-awarded restaurant. The next day to take the Ngong Ping 360 cable car to get a full-view of Lantau Island. Discover the spiritual peace of Po Lin Monastery, admire the majesty of the Tian Tan Buddha, and stroll through the traditional fishing village of Tai O. Tour Highlights Skip the line at the Peak Tram up to see the skyline on Victoria Peak. Visit landmarks of Tai Kwun, Man Mo Temple with Michelin Dim Sum tasting. Enjoy a door-to-door private transfer service to Lantau Island. Take round-way Ngong Ping 360 cable car crystal cabin for panorama views and see the Big Buddha. Explore the original Tai O fishing village by cruise and have a chance to meet pink dolphins. Hassle-free tour with comfortable air-conditional vehicle and passionate tour guides.
