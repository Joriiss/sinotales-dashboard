Title: Where to Go at Night in Xi'an: Local Guide's Authentic Picks

Xi'an is a world-renowned ancient city. During the day, you can explore its 3,000-year-old history and culture through various historical sites. At night, Xi'an takes on a different charm. Xi'an's nightlife is rich and dynamic, featuring night markets where you can taste delicious food, magnificent performances, and streets lined with bars. Both your taste buds and your soul can be satisfied here. If you're visiting Xi'an for the first time, don't miss the delicious food in the Muslim Quarter and the fountain show at the Big Wild Goose Pagoda. These are also the places I love to take my out-of-town friends when they visit. Here are my top picks for different types of nightlife, as a local tour guide. Here, you can taste a variety of foods from different areas of Shaanxi province—northern, central, and southern. I particularly enjoy the Chinese hamburger here, which features a mixture of fatty and lean pork inside the bun. The steamed cold noodles are also worth trying; if you like spicy food, feel free to add as much local chili oil as you want. Dongxin Street Night Market • Location: Dongxin Street, New District, Xi'an (opposite the Hilton Hotel) • Ticket: Free • Recommended visiting time: 1.5 to 2 hours • Suitable for: Everyone I often come here with my friends for food adventures on weekends. I follow my foodie friends to discover some "hidden treasure" eateries. This night market comes alive after dark, offering unique food options in excellent varieties. The Zheng Family Buns (郑家包子 are remarkable, filled with cumin and fennel-seasoned beef, and are incredibly delicious. You should also try the Old Chen Hotchpotch (老陈家烩菜). This dish features a diverse range of ingredients, including dried tofu, green vegetables, black fungus, daylily flowers, beef chunks, coriander, and beef meatballs! The meatballs are delightfully bouncy and chewy, unlike typical dough balls. You can also find Mongolian and Sichuan-style hotpot restaurants here worth trying. Top Night Views in Xi'an 1. Bell and Drum Tower Square • Location: Very center of Xi'an city • Ticket: Free • Recommended visiting time: 1 hour The Bell and Drum Tower with lights on After strolling through the Muslim Quarter and tasting local snacks, take a walk in the nearby Bell and Drum Tower Square. Under the night sky, the ancient Bell and Drum Towers are ablaze with lights. The observation deck on the fifth floor of Kaiyuan Shopping Mall is an ideal spot to overlook the entire Bell Tower. 2. Everbright Tang City • Location: South Square of the Big Wild Goose Pagoda (near the Westin Hotel) • Ticket: Free • Recommended visiting time: 1 to 1.5 hours Everbright Tang City is best for taking Hanfu costume photos The night view of Everbright Tang City is truly captivating. The area is illuminated by countless lights, creating a magical atmosphere. The ancient buildings are outlined with soft yellow lights, evoking a sense of history and mystery. The bustling streets, adorned with neon signs from shops and restaurants, add a modern touch. You can often find "classic beauties" dressed in Tang or Han Dynasty costumes walking the streets, posing for photos in front of these ancient-style buildings. Feel free to take photos with them! If you want to experience this traditional cultural activity, many costume rental shops are available here, offering makeup and photography services. 3. Xi'an City Wall Park • Location: Near the South Gate of Xi'an city • Ticket: Free • Recommended visiting time: 1 to 1.5 hours Witness the brilliant night view of Xi'an City Wall You can taste over a dozen kinds of dumplings in various colors, shapes, and fillings. They are steamed, boiled, and pan-fried, coming in sweet, salty, and spicy flavors. The Xi'an City Wall Park stretches long, with one side adjacent to the ancient city wall and the other next to the moat. While walking at night, you can enjoy the evening breeze by the river. After dark, the lights on the wall illuminate another fabulous view. Occasionally, you might encounter locals dancing or children playing in groups. When you get tired, find a stone bench to rest. Though it may lack a bit of prosperity, it returns to the essence of life. In the archway of the South Gate in Xi'an, bands often perform open-air concerts, singing about their lives and love for the city. Passersby can take this opportunity to showcase their singing talents. Top Night Shows in Xi'an 1. Tang Dynasty Palace Dance and Music Show • Location: No. 75, North Chang'an Road, Beilin District, Xi'an • Ticket: CNY 298 (show only); CNY 400 (show and dumpling banquet) • Recommended visiting time: 1.5 to 2 hours • Opening time: 19:30-20:35 • Suitable for: Everyone, but not small children The dance and music show in Tang Dynasty Palace This is one of my favorite shows in Xi'an, as I admire the splendor of the Tang Dynasty. This performance has been running since 1987 and is approaching its 40th year, maintaining its popularity and attracting many foreign dignitaries and celebrities. Through live dance and the vocal and instrumental performances of the orchestra, it vividly portrays the openness, inclusiveness, confidence, and prosperity of the Tang Dynasty. If you're interested in ancient Chinese culture, you must watch this performance. Additionally, you can enjoy a dumpling banquet here, sampling a variety of dumplings in different colors, shapes, and fillings—steamed, boiled, and pan-fried, with sweet, salty, and spicy flavors. 2. Fountain Show at the Big Wild Goose Pagoda Square • Location: North Square of the Big Wild Goose Pagoda • Ticket: Free • Recommended visiting time: 30 minutes • Suitable for: Everyone Fountain Show at the Big Wild Goose Pagoda Square Whenever I have friends or relatives visiting Xi'an, I always take them to watch the fountain show. The fountain show at the Big Wild Goose Pagoda is the largest in Asia. Using high-tech sound, light, and film, it combines various fountain displays, including the Butterfly Lovers, Fire and Water Fog, and the 100-meter variable-frequency running fountain, creating beautiful and dynamic scenes. The water columns dance gracefully to the music, resembling elegant young girls. The fountain show occurs at 7 p.m. and 9 p.m. every night, each lasting about ten minutes. The best viewing spot is outside the railing on the north square of the Big Wild Goose Pagoda, directly facing the pagoda's axis. It's advisable to arrive 15 to 20 minutes early.
