Ideal for: first-time visitors to China who want the classic China experience • Days 1-3: Beijing • Days 4-5: Xian • Days 6-8: Shanghai The map of a classic 8-day China itinerary Beijing's airports have China's largest number of international flights, so it is an ideal first stop for a China tour. In Beijing, explore the Great Wall by walking on its centuries-old bricks. If you would like to have a more authentic experience of the Wall, take a hike along the unrestored section from Jinshanling to Simatai. Marvel at the lavish lifestyle of medieval emperors at the Forbidden City, and get a glimpse of local life by visiting traditional hutongs. For a special experience, we recommend that you visit the Great Wall at sunset when all the travelers have left. Enjoy having the Wall to yourself, and take some stunning pictures. In Xi'an, Discover the awe-inspiring Terracotta Army up close, and make your own terracotta warrior to take home as a memento with a local artisan's guidance. Stroll atop the City Wall of Xi'an, where the well-preserved architecture and grand wall extent transport you back in time. For an unusual-yet-memorable experience, take a leisurely bike ride on the medieval wall. In Shanghai, Elevate to the top of the Shanghai Tower — the tallest building in China, the second tallest in the world, and you would have an inspiring view of the city's impressive skyline and a glimpse into China's dynamic future. China 8-Day Itinerary with History, Culture, and Scenic Splendors This itinerary highlights China's historical charm, modernity, captivating natural scenery, and serene rural life. Ideal for: travelers who like history, beautiful scenery, and outdoor activities, and those looking for a more relaxed travel experience. • Days 1-3: Beijing • Days 4-5: Xian • Days 6-8: Guilin The map of a classic 8-day China itinerary with natural wonders In Beijing, Discover the best of Beijing in 3 days. Hike along China's imperial-era defense line — the Great Wall, marvel at the splendor of the ancient imperial palace complex at the Forbidden City (boasting 9,999 rooms), and enjoy a relaxing stroll at a tranquil royal garden — the Summer Palace, just as the more recent emperors did. Don't forget to visit one of the city's vibrant night markets to fully immerse yourself in the bustling local atmosphere. In Xi'an: A visit to the Terracotta Warriors in Xi'an is a must for any traveler to China. Marvel at these impressive millennia-old figures up close. To enhance the experience, you might like to make a clay warrior yourself and take it home. Don't miss out on this fascinating activity! Take a leisurely stroll on the City Wall of Xi'an, and witness the captivating blend of old and new within the cityscape. Remember to take lots of beautiful photos along the way! In Guilin: Have a few relaxing and enjoyable days in Guilin and Yangshuo. The city and the peaceful surrounding rural areas offer an escape from the hustle and bustle of modern life. Take a Li River cruise, and enjoy the breathtaking karst mountains, lush greenery, and rural scenery. Enjoy relaxing free time exploring the serene rivers and picturesque lakes within the city. Ideal for: Travelers who love giant pandas. • Days 1-3: Beijing • Days 4-5: Xian • Days 6-8: Chengdu The map of a classic 8-day China itinerary with giant pandas In Beijing, hike the Great Wall of China, delve into the ancient palace complex of the Forbidden City, and stroll through the hutongs to explore old Beijing. It's highly recommended to visit the Great Wall at sunset when most travelers have left, providing a great opportunity to capture awe-inspiring photos. In Chengdu: Chengdu is the hometown of giant pandas. The cute pandas are definitely the highlight of most Chengdu trips. Witness the adorable pandas as they munch on bamboo, climb trees, lounge on branches, or playfully roll on the ground. Their cuteness would surely melt your heart. Chengdu's cuisine is a must-try for any visitor to the city. Known as a gourmet's paradise, Chengdu offers an abundance of culinary delights. Stroll through Jinli Old Street and sample the local street food. It would be a truly unforgettable Chinese food experience.
