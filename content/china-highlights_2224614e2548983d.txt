Yangshuo West Street is renowned for its welcoming international atmosphere, vibrant tourism scene, and lively nightlife. As the natural starting point for your Yangshuo visit, it offers an ideal base to refresh upon arrival. From here, you can embark on explorations of the region's idyllic countryside landscapes, or choose to linger and immerse yourself in Yangshuo's slow-paced rhythm. What is Yangshuo West Street? Yangshuo West Street lies at the heart of town, spanning 517 meters in length and 8 meters in width. While its renovation lends a polished and contemporary feel, the street retains deep historical roots dating back over 1,400 years. Located just 30 minutes on foot from Longtoushan Pier, where your Li River cruise from Guilin concludes, West Street embodies a "global village" spirit. You might even hear local residents conversing in English, reflecting its international character. And it is best to visit on weekdays. West Street is exceptionally busy during holidays and weekends (such as National Day and Spring Festival). Weekday evenings remain vibrant but are less overwhelming. What to Do in Yangshuo West Street? Throughout West Street, you'll find numerous cafés perfect for relaxing afternoons. As evening arrives, the town transforms: lights illuminate the streets, energizing the nightlife. Enjoy a drink at a lively bar, explore the bustling night market, or simply admire the charming townscape after dark. Try Some Local Specialties West Street offers an extensive array of restaurants serving diverse cuisines. For reliable quality and transparent pricing, we recommend opting for established chain restaurants. While the culinary experience may be more consistent than adventurous, these venues ensure value and peace of mind. Recommended Local Chains: • Chunji Roast Goose(椿记烧鹅): A must-try for its signature crispy-skinned yet tender roast goose. • Laobiaoji(老表记): Famous for its authentic Guangxi dishes. • Liaoge Crucian Carp(廖哥土鲫鱼): Known for generous portions and affordable, flavorful fish specialties. Shop Around for Souvenirs When you come to a commercial street like West Street, the first thing you think of is definitely shopping and buying some souvenirs and gifts for yourself, your family, and friends. Be prepared to bargain if the price is not clearly stated, and politely decline unreasonable offers. As a popular scenic area, prices for food, souvenirs, and services on West Street are typically higher than in downtown Guilin. Here are some souvenir shops for cultural and creative stuff in West Street for reference, all with clearly marked prices: • Momicafe (猫的天空之城): No.101, Folded Green Road • Grey Ink (灰墨文创): No.21, Folded Green Road • Osmanthus (木樨堂): No. C116, 1st Floor, Area C, West Street • Fingertip Craft Workshop (指尖工艺坊): No.45, West Street Experience Vibrant Nightlife In addition to shopping at the night market for snacks and souvenirs, you can also walk into a local bar, have a craft beer, and enjoy a slow-paced evening of folk music. Remember to keep your belongings close in crowded spaces. • Mixology Homebar (酉居): No. 8, Fuqian Lane • Kaya Reggae Bar (KAYA雷鬼西街老店): 2nd Floor, No. 37, Xianqian Street, near the Shuangyue Bridge • Xiaoma's Sky Folk Bar (小马的天): No. 28, Xianqian Street, near the Shuangyue Bridge If strolling around isn't your thing, you can catch Yangshuo's nighttime shows, such as Impression of Sanjie Liu (which showcases the local people's labor history) and Guilin Eternal Love (which tells a legendary love story). Every Saturday evening between 6 pm and 8 pm, you can also go to the Golden Dragon Bridge to watch the Golden Dragon Bamboo Rafts cruising on the Yulong River. Dozens of bamboo rafts connected to form a golden dragon swimming freely on the Yulong River, creating a spectacular scene. Is It Recommended to Stay in West Street? Typically, our top recommendation is to stay in the Shili Gallery Area for its stunning rural scenery and tranquil rural vibe that suit for everyone. If you want to choose between West Street and Xingping, it depends more on your own needs. If you are more interested in vibrant nightlife and bustling streets, staying in West Street may suit you more. Yangshuo West Street offers convenient transportation, a wide selection of restaurants, and affordable hotels. Just a half-hour bike ride away, you'll find stunning rural scenery, making it an ideal spot for an efficient Yangshuo getaway. However, it may not be suitable for seniors to stay overnight. Daytime is ideal for relaxed family moments. While the bar scene becomes lively at night, the noise may make it less suitable for seniors seeking quieter venues. For your journey to Yangshuo, we typically recommend the 4-star Li River cruise to Yangshuo for around 4 hours. Upon disembarking, a 15-minute electric shuttle bus (approximately US$2 per person) would take you directly to West Street. Alternatively, for a faster option, we could offer a private transfer service that reaches West Street from Guilin in under 2 hours. The best way to experience West Street is on foot. Walking on its historic bluestone lanes, shopping around, or unwinding with a leisurely afternoon at one of its many cafés. To explore Yangshuo's countryside, we suggest renting a bicycle or electric scooter. This allows you to navigate narrow rural roads freely while avoiding traffic jams.
