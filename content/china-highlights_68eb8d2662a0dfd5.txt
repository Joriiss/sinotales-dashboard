Title: Anshun Transportation: Flights, Train, Bus to/from Anshun, China Highlights
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/anshun/transportation.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Huangguoshu Airport, which was opened in September 2004, is 6 km from the city center. There are regular flights to Beijing, Dalian, Qingdao (in Shandong Province) and Kunming (in Yunnan Province). Anshun is only 100 km away from the Guiyang Airport, where there are many regular flights to many other major cities in China. Anshun is connected with many major cities, such as Beijing, Shanghai, Chongqing, Guangzhou and Kunming, through railway. The tourists express "No. Huangguoshu" runs between Guiyang and Anshun every day. The railway station is located at the end of the Nanhua Road, Anshun. Tourists can take buses from Guiyang to Anshun, which start every 20 minutes, at the Guiyang Long Distance Bus Station, which is opposite to Guiyang Railway Station. The main long distance bus stations of Anshun are Anshun South Long Distance Bus Station and Anshun West Long Distance Bus Station. Anshun South Bus Station • Address: opposite to the Qiandong Mansion, Nanhua Road • Buses to Guiyang, Liuzhi, Xingyi, Zhijin County, Huangguoshu Waterfall Scenic Area and Dragon Palace Scenia Area are available. Anshun West Bus Station (also called Suburb Bus Station) • Address：Near the Police Building, Taxi Road There are buses to Puding, Getu River, Huajiang Canyon, Guiyang, Liuzhi Special Region, Xingyi, Zhijin County, Huangguoshu Waterfall Scenic Area and Dragon Palace Scenia Area. Tips: The last express bus from Anshun to Guiyang starts at 19:10 from Anshun. Some private buses run until 22:30, but the price is more expensive.
