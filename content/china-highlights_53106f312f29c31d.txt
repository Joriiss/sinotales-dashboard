Located in the south of Yunnan Province, Xishuangbanna borders Laos and Myanmar and is close to Thailand and Vietnam. Thanks to its tropical monsoon climate, it's a kingdom of wildlife, including many rare and endangered species. It's a great place for winter escapes in China! Immerse yourself in the customs, unique architectural style, food, costumes, and festivals of the Dai Minority. An encounter with wild elephants will top your visit beautifully.
