Hong Kong is a global shopping paradise, known for its duty-free policy and strategic location as an Asian transport hub. It offers Michelin-starred restaurants, authentic street food, and stunning nightscapes. If you're planning a China trip and Hong Kong fits your style, consider starting or ending your journey there. This will help you make the most of China's 240-hour visa-free policy. Most travelers can enter Hong Kong without a visa. If you end your trip there, the days in Hong Kong don't count towards the 240-hour limit. This allows you to explore mainland China for 10 days (240 hours) plus time in Hong Kong. Of course, starting in Hong Kong also allows you to benefit from this policy. Many mainland cities now offer high-speed train services to Hong Kong with travel times under 4 hours, such as Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Guilin, Changsha (near Zhangjiajie), and Xiamen. You can plan them as the last stop before Hong Kong or the first stop after visiting. Here are some popular China tours with Hong Kong we recommend.
