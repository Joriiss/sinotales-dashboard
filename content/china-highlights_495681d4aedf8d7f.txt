Title: Zhangjiajie Weather in July: Travel Tips
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/zhangjiajie/weather/july.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Weather: July is the hottest month in Zhangjiajie. The average temperature is 29 °C (84 °F). The average high temperature is 33 °C (91 °F), and the average low temperature is 24 °C (75 °F). Rainfall is high, but less than in June. There is about 155 mm of total precipitation each July, and it rains about 11 days of the month. Clothing: Summer clothing should keep you cool, but cover your skin if you plan on walking on sunny days. Mosquitoes and sunburn are things to avoid. A hat and sunglasses will help protect you from solar radiation. If you plan on hiking or walking up stairs, drink a lot of water and wear a hat on sunny days to prevent heatstroke, and make sure your shoes have good traction. Things to Do in Zhangjiajie in July Visit Zhangjiajie National Forest Park Covered with lush greenery and wild flowers, Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is very beautiful and cool during the summer months when its nature flourishes. Enjoy Rafting on Mengdong River Mengdong River Scenic Area is widely known as an ideal place for rafting. Because of the fantastic scenery and unforgettable experience, rafting on Mengdong River has gained an appellation (locally) of "number one rafting spot in the world". You can float down the river on rubber rafts. It has an adventurous feel, with torrents of water careening at sharp turns and off steep ramps. However, it is designed to be quite safe.
