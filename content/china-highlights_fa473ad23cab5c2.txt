Our map of the Eastern Han Dynasty (25-220 AD) shows the Han Dynasty's territory, including the Silk Road corridor in the northwest, during the latter Han Dynasty. Click the map to enlarge. The Han dynasty was historically divided into two major periods: the Western Han Dynasty, also known as the Former Han, and the Eastern Han, a.k.a. the Latter Han.
