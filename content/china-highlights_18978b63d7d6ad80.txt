Chengdu is one of the historic cities in China as well as the hometown of giant pandas. It has a long history of 2300 years, and enjoys the title of "the land of abundance". Nowadays, Chengdu has become a tourist city with rich culture. 1. Watch Giant Pandas and Join the Panda Keeper Program See pandas and join the volunteer program in Dujiangyan Panda Base Giant pandas are called living fossils by zoologists. Chengdu is the hometown of the giant pandas. Dujiangyan Panda Base offers panda-keeping programs where participants can help take care of the pandas. Or you can visit Panda Breeding and Research Center to see most pandas and cubs there. Recommended tours with pandas: 6-Day Chengdu City and Nature Explorer Tour 1-Day Dujiangyan Panda Volunteer Program Tour Recommended tours with pandas: • 6-Day Chengdu City and Nature Explorer Tour • 1-Day Dujiangyan Panda Volunteer Program Tour 2. Watch Magical Face Changing Sichuan Opera with Face-changing Sichuan opera is one kind of opera drama, and opera masks are the most important component element in Sichuan opera. Magical face changing is a visible stunt used to express the character's invisible mood in Sichuan Opera. Magical face changing should not be missed when enjoying Sichuan opera. 3. Visit the Leshan Giant Buddha Visit Leshan Giant Buddha by hiking The Leshan Giant Buddha is the largest stone seated Maitreya in the world at 71 meters (233 ft) high and 24 meters (79 ft) wide. As the local saying goes, "The Mountain is a Buddha, the Buddha is a Mountain". The Leshan Giant Buddha has been included on the World Heritage List. Recommended tour: 3-Day Giant Panda and Giant Buddha Tour • 3-Day Giant Panda and Giant Buddha Tour 4. Visit Chengdu's Historic Sites Chengdu is a city with a splendid history. There are many historic sites showing the long history and the rich culture of Chengdu, and the religious culture of Buddhism or Taoism, such as the Sanxingdui Museum, Jinsha Ruin Museum, Wuhou Shrine, Wenshu Monastery, Qingyang Palace and more. Sunrise at Mount Emei Mount Emei is one of the four famous Buddhist mountains of China. It is one of the most well-known summer destinations in China thanks to its scenery and cultural and religious attractions. Recommended tour: 5-Day Chengdu, Leshan and Mount. Emei Tour • 5-Day Chengdu, Leshan and Mount. Emei Tour 6. Relish Sichuan Cuisine Chuan cuisine is one of the most popular cuisines in all of China, and it is famous for its spicy-hot flavors, fragrant and various dishes. Dry-braising and dry-stewing are two special cooking methods particular to Sichuan cuisine. You can relish local dishes and snacks at Jinli Old Street or Kuanzhai Alleys. There are many great tourist destinations near Chengdu City. Visitors can take some onward tours after finishing a tour of Chengdu.
