Vegetarian food is common and readily available in Shenzhen, although vegetarianism is only practiced by a relatively small fraction of the population. An emphasis on fresh vegetables makes Chinese cuisine perfect for vegetarians. We at China Highlights have listed some of the best ones in tourist popular areas with average price, address, and transportation information. These are suitable for tourists and travellers because most are in Futian and Luohu where they would most likely go. Yunlaiju Vegetarian Restaurant Xianmi Lake Branch It mainly serves Yunnan-style vegetarian dishes. Many dishes include Yunnan natural mushrooms that taste spicy. The servers are considerate and gentle. After the meal, you might also be able to make tea, taste tea and evaluate tea with the boss who is quite proficient about tea culture.
