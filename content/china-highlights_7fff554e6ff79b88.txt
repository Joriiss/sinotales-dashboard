Currently, Zhangjiajie Railway Station is the only train station serving this city. It is located near the city center, about 40 kilometers (25 miles) away from the world-renowned Wulingyuan Scenic Area and 68 kilometers (42 miles) from Zhangjiajie National Forest Park. Zhangjiajie West Railway Station The high-speed train station, Zhangjiajie West Railway Station, is now under construction. It is expected to be open by the end of 2019. After completion, Zhangjiajie West Railway Station will be serving most high-speed rail lines to and from the city, including high-speed lines from Zhangjiajie to Changsha, Chengdu, Chongqing, Guiyang, Xi'an, Nanning, Changde and Hengyang.
