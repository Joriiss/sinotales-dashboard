Title: 7-Day Beijing and Xi'an Tour, the Best of China's Ancient Capitals

A Perfect Introduction to China's Ancient Capitals Designed for first-time visitors to China, this 7-day journey offers the ideal way to experience two of the country's most historically significant cities in just one week. Perfect for travelers who want to efficiently explore China's imperial past while enjoying authentic, immersive, exclusive experiences beyond the typical tourist trail. This itinerary can be customized to suit your interests, ensuring a memorable journey through China's rich history and culture. Suggested 7-Day Itinerary Day Highlights 1-5 Beijing Explore imperial history, savor local cuisine, and enjoy scenic gardens 6-7 Xi'an Marvel at the Terracotta Warriors, stroll the ancient city walls, and hanfu experience. Discover Beijing's Imperial Axis: A Grandeur Unmatched Visit Tian'anmen, the Forbidden City, and the Bell & Drum Tower, linked by Beijing's central axis—one of UNESCO's World Cultural Heritage Sites. Explore the symmetrical layout of the ancient capital, tracing the path of emperors past. Ride Toboggan Down From the Great Wall The Great Wall at Mutianyu is famous for its picturesque scenery and beautifully restored architecture. Riding a Toboggan down is a fun way to enjoy a superb view of the wall from a different angle. Unearth Secrets of the Terracotta Army and its "Hidden Collectibles" Marvel at the 2,000-year-old clay army that once guarded Emperor Qin's tomb. Join a guided tour to see and touch the artifacts from the same era, including the first unearthed terracotta figure. Immerse yourself in the role of an archaeologist by participating in a conservation workshop. Become a Chinese who lived 1300 Years Ago Transport yourself to the Tang Dynasty in a museum showcasing dresses, musical instruments, furniture, and calligraphy. Slip into an embroidered hanfu robe and stroll along the ancient city wall. Experience a Traditional Opera up Close at the Muslim Quarter Enjoy traditional Chinese opera at a teahouse-style theater located in the Muslim Quarter. Performances include Qin opera, shadow puppetry, Huayin Laoqiang (a traditional Shaanxi opera), Sichuan opera face-changing, and more. Savor Beijing's Soul in Hutong: From Street Eats to Imperial Delicacies Feast on street favorites like roast buns with minced pork and flatbread stuffed with beef, then unwind with a palace-inspired afternoon tea featuring delicate pastries and tea rituals—a harmonious blend of humble and refined Beijing. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Beijing Arrival Your private guide will be ready to meet and greet you at the arrival hall. A comfortable private car with an experienced driver will transfer you directly to your hotel.The rest of the day is your own to explore Beijing. DAY 2 Beijing | The Core of the Beijing's Imperial Axis After breakfast, Starting with Tian'anmen Square You will visit the famous sites on Beijing's central axis, including the Forbidden City. In the Forbidden City. Your guide will help you explore carefully-selected places in the Forbidden City. In addition to giving a background introduction on architecture, history, and art of the city, your guide will explain to you stories of the famous figures in the 2 dynasties and help you to trace elements of their lives here. Capture some photos of the Drum Tower and the Bell Tower, which told time in ancient days. Then, walk along a hutong near Shichahai Lake. Your guide will explain the traditional residential houses in Beijing. Taste some traditional snacks loved by locals and desserts that were once exclusive to the royal court. DAY 3 Beijing| The Temple of Heaven and the Great Wall at Mutianyu In the morning, your guide and driver will take you to the Temple of Heaven – a place where emperors used to pray for a good harvest. The Park is full of life. Immerse yourself in the lives of local people – now and centuries ago – through joining in local elderly people's morning activities. In the afternoon, hike the Great Wall at Mutianyu. Take the Chairlift to ascend the wall, saving time instead of spending about 40 minutes climbing the steps. On the Wall, you will have free time to walk around, after your guide does some introductions. Riding a toboggan down from the wall to end your visit. DAY 4 Beijing to Xi'an |The Summer Palace and Bullet train In the morning, visit the Summer Palace. It is where the emperors used to retreat from the summer heat. Your guide will show you the palace area and the lake area. The best way to do sightseeing at the Palace is by taking a dragon boat on the lake. On the way, you will have a chance to view all the beautiful palaces around the lake. After lunch, enjoy a high-speed train ride to Xi'an (4.5 hours -6 hours). The trains can reach speeds of up to 300 km/h.. DAY 5 Xi'an | Secrets of the Terracotta Army Enter the Terracotta Warriors Museum via tram, and witness three captivating pits that display the concealed power of Emperor Qin Shi Huang's extensive military force. Your guide will provide insights into their fabrication and safeguarding, emphasizing the precise workmanship, lasting strength, and the former colorful brilliance of these historical sentinels. Get up close with the first restored Warriors, learn the restoration story, handle Qin Dynasty relics in the Conservation Laboratory. Head back to the city and visit the Muslim Quarter for a glimpse into the lifestyle of Chinese Muslims. Enjoy a cup of tea and Chinese opera at a teahouse. DAY 6 Xi'an | A Taste of Tang Dynasty Travel back in time to China's most famous dynasty at the Ansrenfang Heritage Museum. Appreciate the exquisite elegance of life 1,300 years ago. Dress up like someone from the Tang Dynasty and take amazing portrait photos on Xi'an's Ancient City Wall. Visit the Little Wild Goose Pagoda, a Tang Dynasty Buddhist architectural gem, symbolizes the integration of Buddhism into Han Chinese culture. Ring the bell for blessings and luck for your family. DAY 7 Xi'an Departure Our guide will accompany you to the train station or to the airport, for your departure.
