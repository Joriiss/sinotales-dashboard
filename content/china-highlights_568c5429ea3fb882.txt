Title: Beijing Weather in July 2026: Things to Do & Weather Tips
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/beijing/weather/july.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

July is the hottest month in Beijing, with daytime temperatures typically ranging from 25°C to 33°C (77°F to 91.4°F), and a maximum temperature of 38°C (100.4°F) possible. The high humidity would make you feel quite sweltering. July also marks the rainy season in Beijing, with frequent heavy rainfall typically occurring between the 20th-30th. Traveling during this period might be impacted, and you may need to prepare another plan. Therefore, we suggest visiting from the 1st-19th, when rainfall is less frequent and has minimal impact on your travel plans. For attractions that require walking or have little shade, such as the Forbidden City, Temple of Heaven, and the Great Wall, we suggest scheduling your visit between 8-11 am. A Recommend Itinerary A trip to Beijing in July usually takes 4-5 days. You will need 2-3 days to visit the main attractions. If you want a deeper experience or plan to explore the surrounding areas, consider adding 1-3 more days. Here is a recommended 5-day itinerary: • DAY 1: Beijing Arrival • DAY 2: Visit the Forbidden City, Hutongs (a family visit and a walking tour) • DAY 3: Visit the Temple of Heaven, Afternoon Tea at Prince's Mansion, the Summer Palace • DAY 4: Visit Jinshanling Great Wall, Night Tour at Simatai Great Wall • DAY 5: Gubei Water Town and Beijing Departure Since July is hot, we suggest a more relaxed itinerary for comfort (such as resting in a resort in the afternoon), allowing you to be more flexible in dealing with the weather. There are many summer resorts around Beijing, such as Chengde (3-hour drive from Beijing), Shilin Gorge (1.5-hour drive from Beijing) and the Xilamuren Grassland (2.5-hour bullet train plus 2-hour drive from Beijing). Consider adding an additional 1-3 days. If you have interest, feel free to contact us. Our itineraries can be customized or adjusted. July is the peak tourist month in Beijing, making it the busiest time of the year. Attractions are usually crowded. Compared to June and August, July tends to be hotter and sees the highest influx of visitors. If you have flexibility, consider visiting in June for cooler weather and fewer crowds. If July is your only option, you could plan your explorations for early morning or late afternoon to avoid peak crowds and sweltering heat. Additionally, visiting during the first or second week of July would help you dodge the busiest times. Beijing is in high demand for travel in July, and the closer you book your flights and hotels to July, the higher the prices will be. To secure the best rates, we suggest you book your trip 1-2 months in advance.
