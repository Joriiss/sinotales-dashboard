Title: Zhangjiajie Weather in May: Travel Tips
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/zhangjiajie/weather/may.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Weather: May is early summer in Zhangjiajie. The average low temperature is about 18 °C (64 °F), and the average high temperature is 27 °C (81 °F). It is the start of the rainy season, with about 160 mm of rain predicted for May. It rains about half of the days of the month. It is often foggy or misty. Clothing: Generally a T-shirt and long trousers with a light jacket for the evenings is enough. Bring rain gear too and wear shoes with good traction. If it isn't rainy, the days are clear and sunny, so wear a hat and sunglasses. Things to Do in Zhangjiajie in May Enjoy the Scenery in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park Zhangjiajie National Forest Park in May is full of vigor, leaves are verdant and buds are tender. It is a beautiful time in the forest. When it rains, it creates an enchanting atmosphere with mountains surrounded by mist. You are likely to enjoy seas of clouds. Many take the world's longest cable car up the mountain. During the 30-minute ride, you will enjoy graceful ancient trees, karst hillocks, and a "road of 99 bends" carved in the mountainside. On the mountain, do not miss Tianmen Cave — 'Heaven's Door' — and the Glass Skywalk, hanging from the cliffs. Take a Boat Ride on Baofeng Lake If you would like to enjoy some leisurely time in Zhangjiajie, Baofeng Lake is a good place. It is in a basin in the middle of the mountainous area. The placid lake reflects a picture of verdant mountains— the two in perfect harmony. You can take a boat ride on the lake to relax and take in the peaceful atmosphere and the beauty of the mountains. Touring Zhangjiajie in May May marks the start of the peak travel season in Zhangjiajie. Labor Day holiday, May 1–3, is a hugely popular time to visit. The prices of hotels may as much as double. Train prices remain steady, but flight prices will be higher.
