How did the Rat Become the First Sign in Chinese Zodiac? Once upon a time, there was a great race organized by the generous Jade Emperor in China. All the animals in the kingdom were excited to participate and show off their skills. The Jade Emperor had an important decision to make - he needed to determine the order of the 12 animals in the Chinese zodiac. One little creature that was especially smart and quick was the Rat. The Rat knew it wasn't the fastest animal, so it needed to come up with a clever plan. It approached its friend, the strong and dependable Ox, and asked for some help. The Rat said to the Ox, "Dear friend, I have an idea. You are so strong, and I'm good at navigating. If you let me ride on your back, I can help guide you through the race." The kind-hearted Ox agreed, and they set off together. As they reached the river, all the other animals rushed in to swim across. But the Rat and the Ox had their secret plan. The Rat perched itself on the Ox's back and held on tightly. The Ox powered through the water, carrying the Rat safely across. Just as they reached the other side, the Rat sprang off the Ox's back and quickly scurried to the finish line. The Rat's speed and cleverness helped it to cross the line before anyone else. Everyone cheered for the Rat, and the Jade Emperor declared it the first winner of the race. From that day on, the Rat became the first animal in the Chinese zodiac.
