Title: Shangri-La Ecotourism Region formed
URL: https://wildchina.com/2010/10/shangri-la-ecotourism-region-formed/
Source: Wild China
Date: 2010-10-14T18:32:27+08:00
Tags: wild China, WildChina, WildChina Travel, yushu, Tibet
================================================================================

Monks in Qinghai Local governments of Tibet, Yunnan, Sichuan and Qinghai have recently signed a strategic cooperation agreement to create what is being called the Shangri-La Ecotourism Region. Officials from Tibet’s third-largest city, Changdu, reached the agreement with Yunnan’s Diqing, Ganzi in Sichuan and Yushu in Qinghai. All participating areas will remove barriers in areas including policy, transportation and services. Although details are still not very clear, we are hopeful that the agreement will bring ecotourism that is both sustainable and beneficial to local residents to this beautiful part of China. We especially hope it assists Yushu, which was struck by a deadly earthquake in April of this year, with its rebuilding efforts. To get a feel for Yushu, check out this video of its annual horse festival, which takes place in July.
