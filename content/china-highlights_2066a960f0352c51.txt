China's expansive and diverse terrain bestows upon it some of the world's most stunning natural spectacles. Here, you can witness almost every type of landscape imaginable. From the picturesque karst scenery in Guilin and Yangshuo to the awe-inspiring rock pillars of Zhangjiajie; from the multicolored lakes of Jiuzhaigou to the Rainbow Mountains of Zhangye, China's varied and breathtaking natural landscapes are as captivating as its rich cultural heritage. Through the meticulously curated nature and scenic tours by China Highlights, you can not only fulfill your wish list of visiting acclaimed destinations such as Zhangjiajie, Jiuzhaigou, Guilin, and Huangshan, but also venture off the beaten path to discover hidden gems in remote areas.
