Timeless Capital, Dramatic Landscapes This tour unveils the rich history of Xi'an and showcases the breathtaking landscapes of Yan'an. Witness the majestic Terracotta Warriors, explore the mighty Hukou Waterfall, discover the surreal beauty of the Wave Valley, and appreciate the serene depths of the Yucha Grand Canyon. Additionally, immerse yourself in the region's rich cultural tapestry and savor the authentic flavors of local cuisine. Need adjustments? We're here to help. Our team is incredibly supportive, and we'll ensure everything is perfect. Suggested 7-Day Xi'an and Yan'an Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Xi'an Terracotta Warriors, Qin Relics Handling, Hands-on Warrior Restoration, Ancient City Wall, Muslim Quarter, Huaqing Palace 4-6 Yan'an Hukou Waterfall, Wave Valley, Yucha Giant Canyon 7 Xi'an Departure Xi'an, Wave Valley & Yucha Grand Canyon Tour Map Capture Your Ultimate Photo in Yucha Grand Canyon Discover the magic of gullies, where sunlight sculpts unique rock formations with dramatic lighting and intricate patterns, creating perfect photo opportunities. Marvel at Hukou Waterfall Witness the Hukou Waterfall, where the Yellow River showcases its power as it rushes through a narrow passage, creating a booming sound and a misty spectacle. Meander through the Wave Valley Walk the trail through the red valley, witnessing the vivid red sandstone, its fluid, wave-like textures shaped by eons of erosion. Deepen Your Connection with Qin Dynasty Relics View the first restored Terracotta Warriors and their restoration, then handle authentic Qin Dynasty relics in the Conservation Laboratory. Witness the Terracotta Army Gain insight into the staggering scale of the Terracotta Army, a monumental display of ancient China's remarkable artistic and military achievements. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1Xi'an Arrival Welcome to Xi'an! Your journey begins with a warm greeting from your knowledgeable tour guide at the train station or airport. Enjoy a seamless transfer to your hotel in a private car. During the ride, your guide will offer an insightful overview of Xi'an's fascinating past and present. Day 2Xi'an | Encounter the Terracotta Warriors Breakfast & Lunch Enjoy a relaxing one-hour private ride with your guide to the Terracotta Warriors and Horses Museum. Enter the Terracotta Warriors Museum via tram, and witness three captivating pits that display the concealed power of Emperor Qin Shi Huang's extensive military force. Your guide will provide insights into their fabrication and safeguarding, emphasizing the precise workmanship, lasting strength, and the former colorful brilliance of these historical sentinels. Get up close with the first restored Warriors, learn the restoration story, handle Qin Dynasty relics in the Conservation Laboratory. Explore the Huaqing Palace, a Tang Dynasty retreat famous for its hot springs and the love story of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei, and optionally, enjoy the popular 'Everlasting Regret' show. Day 3Xi'an - Yan'an | Discover the City Highlights Breakfast & Lunch Experience breathtaking panoramic views of Xi'an from the Ancient City Wall. This impressive defensive structure, boasting towering walls, ramparts, gates, and watchtowers, is a testament to China's well-preserved ancient military engineering. Wander through the vibrant Muslim Quarter, where the tantalizing aromas of street food mingle with the bustling sounds of local markets. Leisurely explore, savor delectable local snacks, and immerse yourself in the community's rich culture. Enjoy a comfortable train ride to Yan'an with your guide, followed by a relaxing hotel transfer. Day 4Yan'an | Admire Hukou Waterfall Breakfast & Lunch Relax and enjoy a private transfer to the magnificent Hukou Waterfall. Reach the majestic Hukou Waterfall on the Yellow River via a shuttle bus. This natural wonder features the river's powerful flow constricted through a narrow gorge, creating a thunderous cascade and far-reaching mist. Don the unique garments of Shaanxi farmers and document your experience with a memorable photograph at Hukou Waterfall. After the tour, a comfortable private transfer will be provided to your Yan'an hotel. Day 5Jingbian | Wave Valley Awaits Breakfast & Lunch Travel comfortably to Jingbian and discover the wonders of Jingbian Wave Valley. Experience the breathtaking sensation of walking across the Glass Bridge. Walk the trail through the red valley, where you can observe the vibrant red sandstone, its smooth, undulating surfaces sculpted by millennia of erosion. The tranquil pond at the stream's end offers a perfect view of the Red Cliff's reflection. Venture into the Sky-Piercing Gorge, a remarkable canyon celebrated for its impressive height and steep walls. This narrow passageway, suitable for single-file travel, provides excellent photo-taking. Day 6 Ganquan | Yucha Grand Canyon Adventure Breakfast & Lunch Take a leisurely ride to Ganquan and discover the spectacular Yucha Grand Canyon. Explore Longba Gully via shuttle bus, where deep passages and towering red rock walls create stunning light and shadow effects, perfect for photography and showcasing water's powerful land-shaping effects. Discover Huashu Gully, famous for its unique rock formations and the enchanting way sunlight filters through its narrow passages. This gully offers dramatic lighting and intricate patterns, ideal for capturing stunning images. Be escorted to Ganquan train station for your train ride to Xi'an, followed by a private transfer to your Xi'an hotel. Day 7Xi'an Departure Breakfast Make the most of your remaining free time to enjoy a final exploration of the city, before your guide's arrival for your airport or train station transfer.
