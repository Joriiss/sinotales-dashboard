Fengyangyi is a small village on part of the Yunnan-Tibet Tea Horse Road and it has gained popularity since it served as one of the filming locations for Meet Yourself in Dali, Yunnan. It still retains an authentic and natural ancient village ambiance, although it may have some commercialization. You could explore the historic Tea Horse Road and also revisit the warm and healing scenes from the series. Huangtang Food Art Museum It is actually a combination of a café, an inn, and a bookstore, featuring a strong artistic ambiance with plentiful nostalgic items. Many Chinese variety shows have been filmed there before. You could enjoy some afternoon tea there, order meals, take photos, or admire the various vintage items, immersing yourself in the atmosphere of China in the 1970s and 1980s. Fazhen Temple, located on the hill behind Fengyangyi, is one of the earliest Buddhist temples in Dali. You could witness the unique woodcut couplets as well as the old well that you could use a ladle to fetch water for drinking. If you prefer a tranquil atmosphere, Fazhen Temple, with its red walls and lush greenery, is ideal for visiting, and you could also sip tea and try your hand at drawing. Above Fazhen Temple is the Phoenix Eye, a teahouse situated on the mountaintop with a large viewing platform. But you need to spend some money to get in, such as ordering an afternoon tea set. You could enjoy the expansive view of Er Lake and Cang Mountain on the viewing platform, and the beautiful photo-friendly settings, such as swings, are pleasant to take selfies. How to Get to Fengyangyi Village Fengyangyi Village is near Dali Old Town, making it more convenient to reach. You could take a taxi or a Didi car. It is only a 20-minute drive from Dali Old Town. Fengyangyi has become popular again, primarily because of Meet Yourself. If you're a hardcore fan of the series or you have adequate time and want to find a place near Dali Old Town for strolling or taking photos, it is pleasant to visit Fengyangyi. However, for a more original and rustic feeling of an ancient Tea Horse Road village, Shaxi Ancient Town is recommended. If you are preparing to visit Fengyangyi Village, the following are some tips: • There are many uneven stone paths, so wearing comfortable shoes is recommended. • The series' popularity may create a commercial atmosphere in the village, so you could ignore some sales pitches as there might be many unexpected charges. • Some of the roads have a lot of horses passing through them and are not as clean as other roads, so be prepared to encounter horse manure and the smell if you want to experience horseback riding.
