Title: 6-Day Beijing and Shanghai Private Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/beijingtour/bj-5/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

If you want to understand the history of China over the last 600 years and how fast China is developing right now, this private 6 days Beijing and Shanghai tour is a great option. These 2 metropolises will let you see different sides of China. They facilitate discovery of China's history, culture, tradition and modern life for first-time visitors. Immerse Yourself in the Interesting Lives and History of the Royal Families Besides admiring the splendid palaces on the central axis of the Forbidden City, you will get off the main tourist route to explore spots related to some famous figures in the 2 dynasties: the most hardworking emperors, the longest-serving empress dowager, the most talented concubine and the last emperor. Walk into the 300-Year-Old Hutongs and Discover Traditional Beijing Lifestyle Enjoy a home-visit to a traditional hutong courtyard house and learn Chinese calligraphy. Ascend the Bell Tower and take a bird's-eye view of the hutong area. Explore a Top-Ranked Section of the Great Wall The Great Wall at Mutianyu is ranked top among all parts of the Great Wall on TripAdvisor.Walk leisurely through the watchtowers, enjoying a superb view of the Mutianyu Great wall snaking across the mountains. Discoverthe story of Shanghai's rise to the skies… Walk a route that seldom tourist will find by themselves to discover the hidden stories of the Bund. Cross the Huangpu River and travel from old Shanghai to Shanghai's Pudong New Area. Ascend Shanghai Tower and get a bird's-eye view of the entire city. Enjoy the Local Snacks and Local Life Experiences Wander through alleyways to get a glimpse of the locals' lives, sit down in a Michelin-starred restaurant for a break and enjoy some delicious Shanghai snacks. Admire the Charm of Old Shanghai in the Former French Concession The Former French Concession is the most charming and elegance area in Shanghai. Follow our local guide to wander in the gorgeous tree-lined avenues and listen to anecdotes about it. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Beijing Arrival Your private guide will be ready to meet and greet you at the arrival hall. A comfortable private car with an experienced driver will transfer you directly to your hotel.The rest of the day is your own to explore Beijing. DAY 2 Beijing | The Forbidden City and Hutong Discovery Tour After breakfast, visit Tian'anmen Square. Your guide will provide you with commentary about the history and architecture about the square. Then start to enjoy the main highlight of the day - the Forbidden City. Your guide will help you explore 12 carefully-selected places in the Forbidden City. In addition to giving a background introduction on architecture, history, and art of the city, your guide will explain to you stories of 8 famous figures in the 2 dynasties and help you to trace elements of their lives here. Ride to the old hutong area with your guide and walk through the 300-year-old hutongs. A visit to a local family, whose ancestors have lived in the hutongs for generations, offers a local perspective on history. DAY 3 Beijing| The Temple of Heaven and the Great Wall at Mutianyu In the morning, your guide and driver will take you to the Temple of Heaven – a place where emperors used to pray for a good harvest. The Park is full of life. Immerse yourself in the lives of local people – now and centuries ago – through joining in local elderly people's morning activities. In the afternoon, hike the Great Wall at Mutianyu. In order to save time, we will arrange round-trip cable car rides to ascend and descend the Wall, instead of spending about 40 minutes climbing steps. On the Wall, you will have free time to walk around, after your guide does some introductions. DAY 4 Beijing to Shanghai by High-Speed Train |The Summer Palace In the morning, visit the Summer Palace. It is where the emperors used to retreat from the summer heat. Your guide will show you the palace area and the lake area. The best way to do sightseeing at the Palace is by taking a dragon boat on the lake. On the way, you will have a chance to view all the beautiful palaces around the lake. After the tour, your guide will accompany you to the railway station to ride the high-speed train to Shanghai. DAY 5 Shanghai | Shanghai in Progress Tour Visit the Bund in-depth and learn about the history of Shanghai. Your guide will uncover stories hidden behind the old buildings. Catch a glimpse of locals' lives in an old alley and then sit down in a Michelin-starred restaurant for a break and enjoy some delicious Shanghai food for lunch. Take a ferry across the Huangpu River and be amazed by the skyscrapers in Shanghai's Pudong New Area, which tells another part of Shanghai's story. Ascend the tallest building in Shanghai - Shanghai Tower. In the more traditional part of the city, Yu Garden presents exquisite garden scenes. Listen to stories from the 1930s in the Former French Concession Precinct of China. DAY 6Depart from Shanghai Say goodbye to Shanghai. Your guide will escort you to the airport or train station and help you check in, before leaving. DAY 1 Beijing Arrival Your private guide will be ready to meet and greet you at the arrival hall.
