Chengdu, the capital of China's southwest Sichuan Province, is well-known for lovely giant pandas, and delicious Sichuan food like hotpot. Benefiting from Dujiangyan Irrigation Project which was constructed in 256 B.C., Sichuan Province is reputed as the Heavenly State, a place richly endowed with natural resources. Chengdu is a large but relaxed city, with many natural, cultural, and historical sights in and around it. Travelling with us to find Chengdu which has more than just pandas, also has an amazing food culture, over 3,000 years of history, and a unique local lifestyle!
