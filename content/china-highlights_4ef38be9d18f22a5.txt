It is under the jurisdiction of China Railway Guangzhou Bureau Group Co., Ltd. At present, it serves only normal-speed trains. Though with only about 20 trains passing it every day, Zhangjiajie Railway Station has trains that connect the city with major cities, such as Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Changsha, Nanning, Guiyang, Yichang, Zhanjiang and Zunyi. Zhangjiajie Railway Station is located in downtown of the city. It is about 5 kilometers (3 miles) from Zhangjiajie Airport, 40 kilometers (25 miles) from Wulingyuan Scenic Area and 68 kilometers (42 miles) from Zhangjiajie National Forestm Park. • Chinese: 张家界站 • Name on train tickets: 张家界 • Address: Guanliping, Yongding District, Zhangjiajie 张家界市永定区官黎坪 • Station size: 3 platforms, 7 tracks • Lines: Jiaozuo – Liuzhou Railway Lines and Trains from/to Zhangjiajie Railway Station (Updated on November 30th, 2019) Recommended Trains Destinations Departure Arrival Duration K268 Beijing 12:38 11:58 next day 23h 20min K1374 Shanghai South 18:58 15:13 next day 20h 15min T282 Xi'an 10:18 01:23 next day 15h 05min K9038 Guangzhou 16:45 06:12 next day 13h 27min K9066 Shenzhen 17:07 11:11 next day 18h 04min T8324 Changsha 08:15 12:40 4h 25min K2285 Guiyang 08:59 20:48 11h 49min K2285 Kunming 08:59 06:02 next day 21h 03min T281 Liuzhou 19:11 06:34 next day 11h 23min K968 Zhengzhou 13:36 07:25 next day 17h 49min K968 Wuchang 13:36 23:43 10h 07min K747 Nanning 17:10 08:31 next day 15h 21min Up-to-date train schedules from Zhangjiajie Railway Station. Transportation – from Zhangjiajie Railway Station Zhangjiajie Railway Station To Wulingyuan Scenic Area/Zhangjiajie National Forest Park There are shuttle buses from Zhangjiajie Bus Station, which is just beside Zhangjiajie Railway Station, to Wulingyuan Scenic Area and Zhangjiajie Railway Station with the interval of 10 minutes. It takes about 1 hour from the station to Wulingyuan, and 1.5 hours to the National Forest Park. To Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport It takes about 20 minutes to take a shuttle bus from Zhangjiajie Bus Station to Hehua International Airport. The bus station is just a 2-minute walk from Zhangjiajie Railway Station.
