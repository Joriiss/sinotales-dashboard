Title: Xinjiang Weather in May: First-Timer Travel Tips
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/xinjiang/weather-in-may.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

The weather in Xinjiang in May is warm and pleasant with an average daily temperature range of 12–24°C (54–75°F). There is little chance of rain and most of Xinjiang experiences less than 7 rainy days in May. May is a wise time for you to enjoy Xinjiang's remarkable landscapes and pleasant weather. You would see the wonderful combination of spring and summer. As the weather gets warmer, grasslands become greener and more flowery. More snow melts on the high mountains, and the natural world is full of life in May. Without the large crowds experienced in summer and autumn (July to October), you could visit the sites with comparatively fewer travelers and the best value for money (in general, a summer vacation trip to Xinjiang is 50% more expensive). Weather in Xinjiang's Major Cities in May Most tourist cities enjoy similar weather in May: warm with large temperature differences between day and night. However, Turpan is an exception, with highest daily temperatures reaching 34°C (93°F) on average. On sunny days, ultraviolet rays are strong, especially in desert areas such as Turpan and Kashgar. Be sure to protect yourself from the sun. Occasionally, May sees rain or even hail. Sayram Lake is also highly recommended. In early May, Sayram Lake is comprised of half blue ice and half blue water. The stunning ice waves would amaze you. In late May, the ice on the lake melts away completely and the whole blue lake wakes up, just in time to feel the "last tears from the Atlantic Ocean". Short sleeves and warm jackets are both needed because of the diurnal temperature difference. You would might need to wear a long coat in the early mornings, short sleeves or a dress during the day, and a warm jacket during the cool evenings. You would need to take a sweater or jacket even if you visit Xinjiang during its hottest month! Sunblock, a hat, and sunglasses are necessary. Remember to protect yourself from the strong ultraviolet rays. It's wise to prepare a veil or neck scarf in case you encounter wind-blown sand/dust in desert areas. Don't forget comfortable shoes. Be sure to wear lightweight walking shoes because most scenic spots are outdoors. Shoes that are water-resistant are recommended when you visit the grasslands. Dress tips for photos: Fairytale scenery and exotic charm could give you wonderful photos that would amaze your friends. Dress with solid colors (such as a long white dress) to contrast with the blue sky and lake when in nature, and clothes with an ethnic style are recommended when visiting Kashgar. In the desert, Western cowboy style could be a good choice. Is It Expensive to Visit Xinjiang in May? The travel cost in May is US$350–400 per person per day for a private tour with 4/5-star hotels based on a group of 2–5 travelers. We recommend that you spend at least 1 week in Xinjiang to fully experience it, so trip prices would start at 2,450–2,800 USD. During the Labor Day holiday (May 1st to 5th), costs are more expensive and there are more tourists. In order to increase the comfort level of your trip, it's recommended that you avoid this time.
