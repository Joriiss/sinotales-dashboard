Title: Guiyang Nightlife, Best Pubs and Bars in Guiyang City

The night in Guiyang is colorful and lively. It is said that the nightlife in Guiyang begins at 21:00. Cinemas, bowling rooms, bars, clubs, cafés and teahouses are all available in Guiyang. What's more, visitors in Guiyang also can watch the folk performances in Yangming Ancestral Temple (13 Luosishan Road, Yunyan District 云岩区螺丝山路13号). Shanxi Road (陕西路) is the most popular public place of entertainment among the young. It is the bar-street in Guiyang, gathering many pubs and night clubs. Besides, Hequn Road (合群路) is also a nice place to have a close encounter with the nightlife in Guiyang.
