Although Chengdu is hot in June and starts to enter the rainy season and peak tourist season this month, compared to July and August, it's still less crowded, not as hot, more affordable, and experiences fewer heavy showers. This makes June a good time for families and student groups to travel, avoiding the summer rush and hottest weather. What's the Weather Like in Chengdu in June? • Average daily highs: 30°C/82°F • Average daily lows: 22°C/61°F • Average precipitation: 443.2 mm/17.4 in • Sunny days: 5 • Rainy days: 9 • Cloudy days: 16 June is already summer in Chengdu, but it hasn't reached the peak heat yet. Daytime temperatures mostly range between 28–33°C (82–91°F). Based on past years, there can occasionally be a few days in mid or late June with highs of 35–39°C (95–102°F). But compared to July–September, such hot days are relatively rare. So, overall, the temperature feels quite acceptable. While it's comfortable in mornings and evenings, around 20–25°C (68–77°F). When it does get hot, you can consider heading to cooler spots around Chengdu, such as Jiuzhaigou, to escape the heat. In June, Chengdu also experiences about half a month of cloudy or overcast days, but rainfall isn't heavy (compared to 20+ rainy days in July and August). Most of the rain comes as short showers or night rain, so it won't have much impact on your travel. Though rivers in Leshan and Dujiangyan may occasionally turn yellow after rain. What to Wear in Chengdu in June Light summer clothes, such as T-shirts, shorts, and dresses. Sun protection, like a light jacket, sun hat, sunglasses, and sunscreen. Umbrella and waterproof shoes for occasional rain. Although there are more visitors than in the low season, it's not overly crowded. Flights and hotels are about one-third cheaper than in peak summer. For fewer crowds, you can travel before 10th, as numbers rise after some Chinese students finish their graduation exams. The Dragon Boat Festival usually falls in June, bringing a 3-day mini holiday. During this period, hotel and flight prices can double, and Chengdu and nearby attractions get crowded, so you might consider skipping this time if you don't like the crowds. 3 Smart Ways to Explore Chengdu This June 1. How to Visit the Pandas Wisely For the visiting time, we recommend visiting between 8–10 am when it's cooler. It's easy for you to see they're eating and playing, as pandas mostly stay outdoors at this time. While in the afternoon, it's often exceeds 26°C (79°F), and pandas will be less active. For panda base, June is the birthing season of pandas, so head to Chengdu Panda Research Base if you want to see the cubs. There are 3 nursery houses that allow you to see more lovely babies. If you prefer quieter and closer viewing, or visit with kids and the elderly, Panda Valley is a good choice. The area is flat and easy to walk in, the viewing distance is close, and you can even meet free-roaming red pandas. Or if you look for interactive and educational activities for your children, Dujiangyan Panda Base offers the panda volunteer program (for ages 10 and up.). You can enjoy cooler weather while taking in stunning scenery. In June, Jiuzhaigou's forests are lush, water levels are high, and the colorful lakes and waterfalls are at their most beautiful. If you want more water activities to cool off with kids, consider Huanglongxi. You can play in the water channels with locals or take a boat ride to enjoy the charm of the ancient town. For somewhere closer to Chengdu, Qingcheng Mountain is only a 1.5-hour drive away. You can walk peaceful forest trails, see waterfalls, and experience Taoist culture. It's also the hometown of Po from the Kung Fu Panda movies. You can also plan indoor cool activities, such as watching Sichuan Opera at Shufeng Yayun, learning to cook Sichuan cuisine at the Sichuan Cuisine Museum, or shopping at malls like MixC, Taikoo Li, or SKP. 3. Where to Experience the Dragon Boat Festival in Chengdu To experience some traditional customs, you can not miss Wenshu Monastery. There are a lot of Dragon Boat Festival activities to try, such as applying realgar, tying colorful strings, making zongzi (rice dumplings), crafting sachets, or enjoying a Zen tea session. Taste Zongzi and duck eggs during the Dragon Boat Festival Beyond Dragon Boat Festival events, Wenshu Monastery is also a great spot for photos, tea, and appreciating ancient architecture. And you can see the liveliest dragon boat races at Wujin Corridor Bridge. The competitions usually start in the morning, so it's best to arrive before 9 am to secure a good viewing spot.
