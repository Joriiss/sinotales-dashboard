November could be a smart time to visit Yunnan. You would encounter fewer tourist crowds while still enjoying good weather and nice scenery. Here are some November weather and travel tips. • Yunnan Weather in November • Top 5 Places to Visit • Crowds and Costs Yunnan Weather in November In November, the average temperature in Yunnan ranges from 8°C to 20°C (46– 68°F), with a significant difference between day and night. During the day, it's comfortable to wear sports outfits, T-shirts, light jeans, or casual clothes. In the evening, you would need a windbreaker, jacket, or sweater to keep warm. The weather in November in Yunnan is mostly sunny or partly cloudy, with little rainfall. In the northern regions, such as Lijiang, the average temperature is about 10°C (50°F), while further north in Shangri-La, it drops to about 2°C (36°F). If you're looking to enjoy snowy landscapes, Shangri-La experiences snowfall in November, making it an ideal time to visit. In central cities, such as Dali and Kunming, the average temperature is between 12°C and 13°C (54–55°F). The Meili Snow Mountains view from Feilai Temple November is the best time to visit Shangri-La to see the sun rise over the Meili Snow Mountains. With drier weather and more sunny days, the chances of witnessing this spectacular sight are higher. It is also one of the best times to enjoy the snow scenery. In November, Shangri-La enters early winter, and the Meili Snow Mountains and Haba Snow Mountain are covered in snow. In November, you would see Shangri-La's grasslands, forests, and lakes showcasing a unique blend of autumn and winter beauty, where golden fields contrast with snow-covered peaks, creating a stunning landscape. If you enjoy hiking, November is highly recommended as well. The clear skies and high visibility enhance the experience of viewing the majestic Meili Snow Mountains and the surrounding natural beauty. Jade Dragon Snow Mountain In November, Lijiang enjoys mild weather, with daytime temperatures reaching about 17°C (63°F). The sunny days make November ideal for outdoor activities, such as hiking in Tiger Leaping Gorge. During this time, Lijiang Old Town experiences pleasant weather as autumn transitions into winter. With fewer tourists, it's a perfect opportunity to stroll along the cobblestone streets and soak up the serene atmosphere. The summit of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain is already covered in snow by November. Whether you choose to hike or take the cable car, you would enjoy unique winter scenery and stunning glaciers. The fresh mountain air, clear skies, and high visibility make it perfect for capturing breathtaking photos. Feed the gulls in Dianchi Lake Kunming is known as 'Spring City' and, even in November, the weather remains cool/mild and comfortable, with temperatures ranging from 8–17°C (46–63°F). You could enjoy outdoor activities and sightseeing, making it an ideal destination for escaping the cold and relaxing. Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanic Garden Xishuangbanna is located in the tropics and its weather in November remains warm, making it an ideal destination for escaping the cold. With none of the summer heat and humidity, November is perfect for exploring Banna's tropical rainforest and botanical gardens. Xishuangbanna boasts the largest and finest botanical garden in China, where you could see strange flowers that change color and mysterious fruits, as well as learn how to obtain drinkable water in the jungle by cutting a banana tree.
