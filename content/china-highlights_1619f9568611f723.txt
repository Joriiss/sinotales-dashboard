Title: 7-Day Huangshan, Shanghai and Hangzhou Tour

An Odyssey of Nature, Culture, and Art Embark on an extraordinary journey through the art, architecture, culture, and natural wonders of Eastern China. Begin in Shanghai, a metropolis where futuristic skyscrapers and Art Deco masterpieces coexist, offering a visual feast for architecture enthusiasts. Then ventures into the breathtaking landscapes of Huangshan, a UNESCO World Heritage site celebrated for its dramatic granite peaks, ancient pine trees, and mystical cloud seas. Finally, escape to the idyllic Hangzhou, where you'll explore the serene West Lake and the tranquil tea village. This tour is an artistic and natural odyssey, weaving together urban innovation with the sublime tranquility of the Chinese countryside. Looking to explore more of China? Our expert travel advisors can help you create a customized itinerary to suit your needs. Suggested 7-Day Itinerary Day Highlights 1-2 Shanghai Art Deco Architecture City Walk, The Bund, Shanghai Tower, Yuyuan Garden 3-5 Huangshan West Sea Grand Canyon, Black Tiger Pine, Dream Flower Pine, Bishan Village 6 Hangzhou Boat on West Lake, Meijiawu Tea Village, Qinghefang Old Street 7 Shanghai Departure See Breathtaking Sunrise over the Majestic Yellow Mountains. The Yellow Mountains often have a "sea of clouds" below their peaks, creating a surreal and ethereal atmosphere. Explore Shanghai's Shimmering Past Where Intricate Geometric Patterns Tell Stories. László Hudec was a Hungarian-Slovak architect who became a prominent figure in Shanghai from 1918 to 1945. He is credited with designing over 100 buildings in Shanghai, with over 60 of them still standing today. Witness How Artists Creatively Repurposed Historic Buildings into Vibrant Cultural Spaces. Immerse yourself in the living canvas of a less-crowded village, where a quiet community has been lovingly reborn as an artists' utopia. Stay overnight to truly absorb the creative spirit and unique energy that pulses through its streets. Make a Herbal Aromatic Plaque and Connect with Ancient Chinese Tradition. The experience is both a craft and a sensory journey. You'll work with a fragrant, pliable herbal paste, pressing it into a mold you've chosen to create a unique piece. Harvest Fresh Dragon Well Tea Leaves in the Serene Tea Gardens. Savor the delicate flavors of Dragon Well tea, a renowned Chinese green tea known for its smooth taste and vibrant green color. Discover the Timeless Romance of West Lake as You Drift Past Willow-lined Shores on a Private Boat. During the boat trip, you will see several iconic sights and natural wonders. Enjoy the serene beauty of the lake, the lush greenery surrounding it, and charming traditional architecture along the shores. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Day 1 Shanghai Arrival Your private guide and driver will be waiting for you when you arrive at Shanghai Pudong International Airport. Sit in a comfortable and non-smoking car and enjoy a hassle-free journey to your hotel. Check in with the help of your guide. Day 2 Shanghai | Art Deco City Walk You will visit Yu Yuan as the first stop, a stunning example of classical Chinese garden architecture. Admire the intricate details of the Garden's pavilions, halls, and bridges. Then you will trace Shanghai's skyward journey. Ascend to the pinnacle of the iconic Shanghai Tower, and explore the Bund's architectural gems, remnants of Shanghai's golden age. Today's highlight is to experience Shanghai's rich history and architectural beauty with its iconic Art Deco masterpieces. Discover the captivating stories behind these historic buildings, including the works of legendary architect László Hudec. You'll learn to "read" the hidden details and design elements that make his buildings so unique. The journey concludes at a century-old newspaper site, where you can enjoy a special Old Shanghai-style coffee. This isn't just a coffee break—it's a chance to immerse yourself in the city's vibrant coffee culture and reflect on the past. Day 3 Shanghai to Huangshan | The Art of Herbal Fragrance Take the 3-hour high-speed train to Huangshan for a fast and scenic journey. The route offers stunning views of the countryside, making the trip feel like part of the adventure itself. After a delicious lunch at a local restaurant, you will head to a workshop to experience making herbal fragrance plaques, which are traditional Chinese crafts that believed to promote health and well-being. The process involved grinding herbs into a fine powder, mixing them into a paste, and then pressing the mixture into a mold. After a period of air-drying, the plaques were ready to be used or carried. After that, your guide will take you to Tunxi Old Street to glimpse into traditional Chinese culture and architecture. This street is a "living" museum referring to an ancient Chinese painting with well-preserved buildings, winding alleys, and traditional shops. And you will also find numerous restaurants and food stalls to sample local delicacies. Day 4 Huangshan | Meet The Mist-shrouded Peaks Your journey to Huangshan begins with a Yungu cable car to soar above the mist-shrouded peaks and immerse yourself in the mountain's legendary "Four Wonders": the bizarrely shaped pines, the oddly formed rocks, the breathtaking seas of clouds, and the soothing hot springs. Guided by a local expert, who spends over 200 days annually exploring every nook and cranny of the mountain, you'll gain a profound understanding of its unique beauty and rich history. Day 5 Huangshan | Modern Utopia If the weather permits, rise early to witness a magical sunrise over the legendary Yellow Mountain. Even when shrouded in mist, the mountain's ethereal beauty remains captivating. After taking the cable car down the mountains, we'll head to Bishan village. This isn't a typical historical village; it's a creative haven where old-world architecture meets modern artistic vision. You will encounter a couple that breathed new life into three abandoned buildings, turning a former pigsty, an old oil workshop, and a Ming Dynasty mansion into charming guesthouses. An acclaimed bookstore owner has opened a new branch inside a beautifully restored ancestral temple. Beyond these, you will also get a behind-the-scenes look at the stories and vision behind Bishan's rural Utopia plan. There's a lot to discover! Day 6 Huangshan to Hangzhou | Natural & Cultural Harmony A 2-hour high speed train takes you to Hangzhou in the morning. Your tour guide will be waiting to pick you up to have a delicious lunch at a local restaurant first, and then visit the West Lake. You will be taking a private boat on West Lake to enjoy a unique perspective on the city's most iconic landmark. From the water, you can see famous landmarks like Leifeng Pagoda and Su Causeway from a distance. Then you will get to the Former Residence of Hu Xueyan at Qinghefang Old Street. This is a magnificent and well-preserved mansion of one of China's richest and most influential merchants during the late Qing Dynasty. The living museum of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is very close. Except learning the history and principles of TCM here, you can see experienced pharmaceutical workers demonstrating the art of manual pill-making, slicing herbs, and compounding prescriptions using ancient tools. Day 7 Hangzhou to Shanghai | Departure If you have a late flight from Shanghai today, your guide will take you to the Meijiawu Tea Village, the original home of West Lake Dragon Well tea, China's most celebrated tea, to visit a local tea farmer's home. The tea farmer will greet you with a traditional bamboo basket and hat, and then guide you through the tea plantation. You'll learn to select and pick the best tea leaves, and then observe the stir-frying process. This technique both dehydrates the leaves and preserves their vibrant green color. Next, you'll learn the proper way to brew a perfect cup of tea. Afterward, we'll head to a restaurant tucked away in the tea plantation for a special lunch featuring local tea cuisine. Your high speed train will depart to Shanghai after lunch, taking only 45 minutes. Another tour guide will meet you at the exit of train station and escort you to the airport. Or you can choose to stay in Shanghai for one more night. Tailor-make the tour anytime!
