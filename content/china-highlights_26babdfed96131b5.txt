How to Spend Your 3 Weeks on the Silk Road The Silk Road is a long road — even if you only consider China. From east to west, travel routes of China's Silk Road could be divided into three parts: the eastern route, the middle route, and the western route. Three weeks would make it possible to explore China's Silk Road from east to west deeply. Normally, 2 weeks for a China Silk Road trip is the minimum for a overall view, while 3 weeks would make it possible to expand such a trip to some beyond-the-Silk-Road China destinations, or even to Central Asian countries that the ancient Silk Road passed through. Below are some outline suggestions for how to spend your 3 weeks on the eastern Silk Road.
