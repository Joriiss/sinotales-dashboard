Title: 6-Day Chongqing and Chengdu Tour with Wulong and Dazu

Vibrant Cities and Beyond In 2025, Chongqing and Chengdu have emerged as popular travel destinations for tourists from Southeast Asian countries. On this 6-day journey, immerse yourself in the breathtaking 8D urban spectacle of Chongqing, explore the majestic Wolong Karst landscapes and the rich cultural heritage of the Dazu Rock Carvings. In Chengdu, encounter the adorable pandas and authentic culinary delights. Our thoughtfully designed itinerary is relaxed and appealing, ensuring that everyone from the young to the old can enjoy this journey. Our itineraries are customizable to suit your preferences. Whether you want to explore other parts of Sichuan or embark on a Yangtze River cruise, we can tailor the perfect trip for you. Suggested Chongqing and Chengdu Itinerary Day Highlights 1-4 Chongqing Chongqing city Highlights, Wulong Karst, and Dazu Rock Carving 4-5 Chengdu Panda and Chengdu city highlights 6 Chengdu departure Marvel at the Famous 8D City of China Explore Chongqing, the 'Mountain City', where you'll find amazing structures like Hongya Cave and a train line that runs through a building! Embark on a Majestic Journey Through Wulong National Park's Karst Wonderland Marvel at the incredible limestone landscape and discover the famed three natural bridges, a spectacular sight that caught the eye of the Transformers 4 filmmakers. Meet Buddha's Gaze and Feel the Inner Peace Appreciate the breathtaking grottoes. Experience the art of the religion and discover the scenes of life in ancient China. Meet the lovely pandas in the Panda Breeding and Research Center It is best to go early in the morning to see the pandas feeding at around 9:30am, when they are most active during the day. Feel the Enthusiasm of Sichuan people and the Charm of Their Local Delicacies Savor the irresistible local snacks of Chongqing and Chengdu, a culinary adventure that's sure to tantalize your taste buds and leave you craving for more. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1: Chongqing Arrival Your personal guide and driver will greet you and pick you up at the airport or at the entrance of the train station. DAY 2: Chongqing | Massive and Enthralling Urban Vibe Begin your urban adventure in Chongqing with a stroll down the historic Xiahaoli Old Street. Immerse yourself in the area's authentic charm, where quaint alleys are brought to life by unique coffee shops and boutiques, offering picture-perfect photo opportunities. Next, take a cable car and glide across the Yangtze River to the Huguang Guild Hall. Here, delve into the rich history of the Yangtze River region and its economic evolution. Witness a light-rail train going through a building, a spectacle unique to this mountainous city. Continue your exploration at the Goose Neck Park, where you'll marvel at the time-honored Chinese gardens and the historical consulate sites. Conclude your day at Hongyadong, an unmissable hotspot in Chongqing. Indulge in retail therapy, relish the local snacks, and soak in the mesmerizing night view. DAY 3: Chongqing to Wulong | Amazing Karst Formation Drive to the Wulong region in the morning for about 3 hours. Your destination is the breathtaking Wulong National Park, a dazzling showcase of southern China's karst landscape. The park is home to three natural stone arch bridges and two colossal sinkholes, making it a geological marvel. Dedicate a few hours to leisurely exploring the valley floor, capturing the stunning vistas in your camera. After visiting, drive back to Chongqing. DAY 4: Chongqing to Chengdu | Dazu Rock Carvings Embark on a journey to the Dazu Rock Carvings, the only UNESCO World Heritage Site in Chongqing. Crafted between the 9th and 13th centuries, this site offers a fascinating glimpse into ancient spirituality and craftsmanship. Our professional guide will regale you with historical tales of the sculptures. After your visit, catch a bullet train to Chengdu. DAY 5: Chengdu | Panda and City Highlights Visiting the city's panda base can be a challenge as it is easily accessible and has various animals. We've designed a smart route to try our best to beat the crowds while matching the pandas' active period as much as possible for you. Explore the city in the afternoon. Visit Wenshu Monastery. Have a glimpse at the practicing monks and worshiping locals. End your day with a local snack hunt in Jinli Ancient Street. DAY 6: Chengdu Departure Enjoy a private transfer to the airport for your flight to the next destination or back home.
