The arrangement allows them to complete entry and exit formalities on the same floor, saving both time and hassle. • To enter Hong Kong, simply get off your train at B4 and then proceed to B2 for exit formalities for China Mainland and entry formalities for Hong Kong. • To exit Hong Kong, it's recommended to purchase your train tickets before arriving at West Kowloon Station. Once you arrive, head to B3 for exit formalities for Hong Kong and entry formalities for China Mainland, and finally proceed to B4 to board on your train. Transportation between Airports and High-Speed Railway Stations Hong Kong airport to West Kowloon station - The West Kowloon High-Speed Train Station can be reached easily by taking the Airport Express to Tsing Yi, then the Tung Chung Line to Nam Cheong, and finally changing to the Tuen Ma Line to Austin. From there, the West Kowloon Station is only 200 meters away. The cost of this subway trip is around USD 14. And it takes around one hour for this subway trip. Guangzhou Baiyun airport to Guangzhou East - Taking subway line three is a convenient way to travel from Baiyun Airport to Guangzhou East. Simply board the train at Airport South (Terminal 1) and disembark at Guangzhou East Railway Station. The journey covers 11 stops and takes approximately 40 minutes. The fare is quite affordable, costing only USD 0.90 (CNY 7). Guangzhou Baiyun airport to Guangzhou South - For the subway journey from Baiyun Airport to Guangzhou South, a single transfer is required. Begin by boarding subway line three at Airport South (Terminal 1) and alight at Jiahewanggang, where you'll transfer to line two. Finally, disembark at Guangzhou South. This route spans 27 stops and typically takes around 90 minutes. The fare for this convenient trip is USD 1.10 (CNY 9).
