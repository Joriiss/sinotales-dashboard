Day 2 Hiking on the Great Wall Hotel: Crowne Plaza Hotel Beijing Chaoyang U-Town (Superior Room) Breakfast & Lunch The Hike of a Lifetime—Get Close to the World Wonder Your professional guide will meet you at hotel lobby to start hike between Jinshanling and Simatai west. This section of the great wall covers some wilder parts, reinforced, partial restorations and full restorations, giving you the full flavor of the Great Wall. The hiking route takes from 3 to 4 hours and offers you spectacular views. It's no wonder its a favorite of photographers. Good to Know We choose our hiking guide very carefully. They are energetic and hiking on the Wall over 200 days a year! If hiking is not your thing, or three hours of hiking sounds a little long, you are also able to enjoy a few hours strolling on one of these sections, which is unlikely to affect the price of this tour. Jinshanling Section of the Great Wall Day 3 Beijing & Flight to Xi'an Hotel: Sofitel on Renmin Square Xian Breakfast & Lunch Your guide and driver are get ready to escort you visit highlights of Beijing. The Forbidden City is full of stories; you get to know Chinese royal family and experience life of the last emperor. The Tian'anmen Square is the heart of China, walk around and take a look at Chinese political center. Visiting to hutongs by taking on rickshaw is a pleasant experience. Your guide is very familiar with the host who has lived in the courtyard for generations. Have a cup of tea at the courtyard and have a nice chat with them. In the afternoon, not in hurry, your guide will confirm your flight to Xi'an and avoid traffic jam to see you off at airport. Your flight is not at late evening, your vacation, not tired. Good to Know To give authentic experience to customer, we choose local restaurant with good quality. You can order dishes with guide's recommendation no longer only sweet and sour pork, spicy diced chicken with peanuts at every meal. The Forbidden City Day 4 Xi'an - The Terra-cotta Warriors Hotel: Sofitel on Renmin Square Xian Breakfast & Lunch The Terracotta Warriors with local expert guide Enjoy breakfast and freshen up, take your time and when you're ready your guide and driver will be in the lobby ready to escort you to visit the iconic sight of Xi'an, the Terra-cotta Warriors. After lunch, we drive back to down town. Xi'an Ancient City Wall, one of the oldest and most complete city defenses. You can biking on the wall to enjoy sunset. The Terracotta Army Day 5 Xi'an & Flight to Guilin Hotel: Shangri La Hotel Guilin (Deluxe Room) Breakfast & Lunch It is your second day in Xi'an. The historic city becomes unveiled step by step. Shaanxi History Museum shows you the history of China. You will also know more about muslin religion by visiting to the Great Mosque. One of today's highlights is visiting to a local family and learning how to make dumplings. Friendly host is happy to communicate with guests and make dumpling banquet to show her hospitality. It is a wonderful personal touch. Then you will visit Xi'an's Big Wild Goose Pagoda, unusually this pagoda has survived since around 400 AD, as it is made of bricks. Good to Know The view over Xi'an from the pagodas is arguably the best in the city. The pagodas and gardens are lit up at night and offer a wonderful stroll. Learn to make Chinese dumplings Day 6 Yangshuo Hotel: Green Lotus Hotel Breakfast & Lunch Li River Cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo Today's activity is taking the Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo. The boat can hold about 140 passengers with two stories. We choose the upper storey with better view and less people. Four hours on board, just relax on the soft chair or go to deck to enjoy breath-taking view. Your guide will show you the highlights on the Li River at the right time and right place. Good to Know Only several minutes walk from Sheraton Guilin to a lake and the Li River. If you energetic, you are suggested to have jogging in the morning along the Binjiang Road near the river. Bamboo Rafting on Li River Day 7 Yangshuo Hotel: Green Lotus Hotel Breakfast & Lunch Cooking Class and Biking in Villages Cooking class offers an opportunity to learn how to cook local dishes. Usually English-speaking teacher will introduce you three dishes such as beer fish, the most famous dish in Yangshuo, spicy diced chicken with peanuts. The teacher will give everyone a recipe in English after the class. Biking along the Yulong River and visiting local farmer's house that has over 100 years give you a better understanding on Chinese rural life. Good to Know Teacher will guide you to a local market to experience Chinese people's daily life before the class. Fresh food is what Chinese people prefer, so live frogs, chickens even snakes are available in the market. Yangshuo Countryside Cycling Day 8 Yangshuo to Guilin & Flight to Xiamen Hotel: Pullman Xiamen Powerlong Breakfast Private Transfer to Guilin airport and to the hotel in Xiamen Say goodbye to the lovely Yangshuo. Driver and guide will escort you to the Guilin airport. The next station is also beautiful but different. You will meet a smiling face at the airport in Xiamen. Day 9 Gulangyu Hotel: Pullman Xiamen Powerlong Breakfast & Lunch Wondering on the Island to Enjoy Different Architecture On the island there are many colonial buildings built after the Opium War, including the Britain, the America, France, German, Spain, etc. Crag of Sunlight and Shuzhuang Garden are highlights. Shuzhuang Garden is connecting with the sea, you can enjoy the sea-view along the corridor above the sea. It is very relaxing to wander along the winding small paths and hunt for some interesting souvenirs at shops. The Gulangyu Island Day 10 Xiamen to Tulou Hotel: Pullman Xiamen Powerlong Breakfast & Lunch Tulou in Nanjing and Yongding—you never see them at somewhere else Today your guide and driver will escort you to Tulou in Nanjing and Yongding. Tulou means earth building that is Hakka people's traditional house. A four-store earth building can hold over 60 families with more than 400 rooms. The architecture reflects Chinese philosophy and culture.
