East China is the richest region in China. Its richness is not only reflected in its important position in the economy but also in its rich tourism resources and cultural connotation. You will find examples of both old and new China in East China, from peaceful water towns to the hustle and bustle of Shanghai, and from the exquisite Chinese-style gardens of the rich lords to the traditional lanes inhabited by friendly local families.
