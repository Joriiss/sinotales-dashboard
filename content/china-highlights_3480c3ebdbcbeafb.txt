When choosing where to stay in Beijing, we recommend basing yourself near the Forbidden City. This is the very heart of the city, with most major attractions located within the 2nd Ring Road. Staying here saves time and makes sightseeing, dining, and shopping effortless. For a first-time visit, it's the most convenient and hassle-free choice. Below we'll share 5 of the best areas to stay in Beijing and suggest some hotels for each. • 1. Wangfujing Area • 2. Qianmen Area • 3. Xidan Area • 4. Chongwenmen Area • 5. Hutongs Area 1. Wangfujing Area – Top Choice Wangfujing is definitely the most convenient choice for first-time travelers to Beijing. It is the city's most famous commercial area, located in the city center, with plenty of malls and restaurants. You could easily go shopping, dine, and experience the vibrant evening atmosphere. It's about a 15-minute walk to the Forbidden City and Tiananmen. Additionally, 5 subway lines pass through this area, providing easy access to other attractions. Hotels in Wangfujing are generally of a high standard and tend to be more expensive. The rooms are typically spacious and comfortable, which is great for families or guests with a lot of luggage. There are many dining options, both Chinese and Western, in and around the hotels, so finding a meal is never a worry. Regarding 4-star hotels, they tend to be a bit dated here. If you prefer the most central location, be prepared for that. If you want more modern facilities, we recommend the areas around Qianmen, Xidan, and Chongwenmen. Qianmen Area – Stay Central & Great Value If you want to stay central but prefer something more authentic than Wangfujing, Qianmen is a great choice. It's within walking distance of Tiananmen Square and the Forbidden City, making it ideal if you plan to explore the city's top landmarks. This area has a traditional Beijing feel, with hutongs, old shops, and local snacks around Dashilanr Street. You could easily spend an evening wandering through narrow alleys and watching daily life unfold. Hotels here are usually older and rooms smaller, but prices are more affordable than in Wangfujing. Streets are narrower, so taxis may not be able to stop directly at the hotel entrance. You'll need a short walk to reach the hotel after getting out. For easier access, we recommend staying near Zhushikou Subway Station, where roads are wider and cars can reach the hotel door. Xidan Area – Modern Shopping & Young Vibe Located west of Tiananmen Square, Xidan has a younger, more modern vibe. It's one of Beijing's main shopping areas, filled with big malls, fast fashion brands, and plenty of dining options popular among locals. Hotels are relatively newer, and the area is lively at night, so it's a good choice if you like a vibrant city atmosphere and want to shop or dine out at night without traveling far. Chongwenmen Area – Quiet Stay Close to Attractions Just east of Qianmen, Chongwenmen offers a peaceful stay while keeping you close to the Temple of Heaven and other major attractions. It's well connected by subway and less crowded than Wangfujing, Qianmen, or Xidan. This area is ideal for those who prefer a calm base after a full day of sightseeing. Hutongs Area – Courtyard Style If you're looking for a unique stay and want to experience "living in old Beijing," courtyard hotels in the hutong areas are the most atmospheric choice. We recommend the hutongs in Dongcheng District and the Jingshan area, near the Forbidden City. Staying in a courtyard offers tranquility and character, unlike standardized large hotels. You could enjoy tea in the courtyard or take a leisurely walk in hutongs. It's a truly unique experience. However, the roads in the hutongs are generally narrow, so cars might not be able to reach the entrance, requiring a bit of a walk. Some courtyard hotels are renovated old buildings, often with stairs, and room sizes and facilities might not match those of modern hotels. If you're traveling with a lot of luggage or with family, make sure to confirm the hotel's conditions in advance. Recommended Hotels: Jingshan Garden Hotel (4-Star): Closest to the Forbidden City. How to arrange accommodation when visiting Universal Studios Beijing? It takes about an hour by car from downtown Beijing to Universal Studios. For a one-day visit, you could stay in downtown Beijing. If you prefer using the subway, consider staying near Line 1 or Line 7 stations within the Second Ring Road (since you'll be walking a lot inside the park, it's best to rest as soon as you get off the subway). For a higher budget, Wangfujing on Line 1 is recommended; for a limited budget, consider staying near Zhushikou Station on Line 7. For a two-day visit, we recommend staying one night near Universal Studios. We recommend the Universal Studios Grand Hotel (5-star) inside the park, which offers the benefit of entering the park an hour early, saving queue time, and allowing you to rest at the hotel during the day. 4.
