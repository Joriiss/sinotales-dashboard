Train Stations of Shanghai - Hangzhou Bullet Trains Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station There are three Shanghai terminals and three Hangzhou terminals for these high-speed routes: Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station, Shanghai Railway Station, Shanghai South Railway Station, Hangzhou East Railway Station, Hangzhou South Railway Station and Hangzhou Railway Station. Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station (Chinese: 上海虹桥站) Located in Minhang District, west of downtown Shanghai, the station is only 500 meters west of Hongqiao Airport Terminal Two. Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station is one of the four major train stations in Shanghai, and an important transportation hub mainly serves high-speed trains to and from the the city. The metro platforms, bus stops and taxi stands are located on the underground floor of the station. Hangzhou Railway Station (Chinese: 杭州火车站) Hangzhou Railway Station (known to local residents as Chengzhan Station) is the oldest and main station in the city. It lies at the junction of Huancheng East Road and West Lake Avenue and is only 10 minutes' ride to West Lake. This strategic location ensures swift connectivity for passengers seeking a seamless transition between the railway station and the airport. FAQs on Shanghai - Hangzhou High-Speed Trains What is the distance of the high-speed railway between Shanghai and Hangzhou? The high-speed railway that connects Shanghai and Hangzhou, famously known as the Huhang High-Speed Railway, spans an impressive distance. According to the latest statistics provided by China Railway for the year 2023, the entire route is approximately 189 kilometers (117 miles) in length. This extensive railway system commences its journey from the bustling Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station and culminates at the well-connected Hangzhou East Railway Station, enabling seamless travel between these two bustling cities. How long does it take to travel from Shanghai to Hangzhou by high-speed train? Traveling from Shanghai to Hangzhou by high-speed train is a quick and convenient option, boasting an extensive network of 166 train pairs connecting these two cities. Impressively, half of these high-speed trains complete the journey in less than one hour, offering a swift and efficient travel experience. The fastest trains cover the distance in a remarkable 45 minutes, making the trip between Shanghai and Hangzhou or vice versa an absolute breeze. Even the slowest high-speed trains still provide an expedient journey, taking only one hour and 49 minutes to reach their destination. It is essential to note that the slower duration is not due to lower speed, but rather a result of making additional stops at various stations along the route. How to travel from Shanghai to Hangzhou? When it comes to traveling from Shanghai to Hangzhou, there are primarily two popular options: high-speed trains and long-distance buses. High-Speed Trains: Undoubtedly, high-speed trains are the most convenient and efficient mode of transportation between Shanghai and Hangzhou. With an average travel time of approximately one hour, they provide a swift and seamless journey. Shanghai and Hangzhou each have three high-speed railway stations located in different areas, allowing travelers to choose the station closest to their final destination. Long-Distance Buses: Another option for travel between Shanghai and Hangzhou is via long-distance buses. These buses operate regular services between the two cities, albeit with a longer travel time of around four hours. However, it's worth noting that long-distance bus fares are slightly more expensive, costing approximately 18 US Dollars for a one-way ticket. Considering the longer duration and higher cost compared to high-speed trains, fewer travelers opt for this mode of transportation.
