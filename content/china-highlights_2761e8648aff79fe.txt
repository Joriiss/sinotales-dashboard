Title: 10-Day Chinese Kung Fu Tour with Shaolin Temple
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/kung-fu-tour.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Come True Your Dream of Kung Fu Have you ever dreamed of having the same martial arts skills as Bruce Lee? Every child has a kung fu dream in their heart. To turn this dream into reality, your family will go to the birthplace of kung fu- Shaolin temple, and study genuine Chinese kung fu under the tutelage of a monk. Here is a suggested itinerary. If your family wants to add any activities or change anything, just feel free to let your travel adviser know. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and needs. Suggested 10-Day Family Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Beijing Great Wall, Forbidden City, and Learning Taichi 4-5 Luoyang Learning Shaolin Kung Fu, Longmen Grottoes, and White Horse Temple 6-7 Xian Terracotta Warriors, Xian food Tour, making traditional handicrafts 8-10 Shanghai Shanghai Highlights and Chinese Martial Arts Museum Practice Tai chi at the Temple of Heaven Tai Chi is widely embraced as a morning exercise routine by many individuals. In the largest park in Beijing - the Temple of Heaven- you can practice Tai Chi in the morning under the guidance of your teacher. Find your Master Shifu in the Shaolin Temple Let your kids find their Master Shifu just as Po did in this old temple that's hidden in the mountains. Then learn a set of kung fu moves from Master Shifu - a monk right there in the temple. Slid down from the Great Wall like a kung fu warrior Experience the thrill of ziplining across the beautiful landscape near the Great Wall. Discover the secrets of Chinese emperors at the Forbidden City The Forbidden City is the largest and best-preserved ancient imperial architectural complex in the world. It was home to 24 emperors Uncover a magnificent archaeological marvel at the Terracotta Warriors Marvel at the archaeological wonder of thousands of life-sized clay soldiers, chariots, and horses. Cooking dumplings at a local people's family You will be welcomed into local people's home and learn to make dumplings from them. This is a good way of learning some basic skills of dumpling making and experiencing local culture. Make Chinese traditional handicrafts Visit artisans and make your own traditional handicrafts at the Ancient Cultural Street of Shuyuanmen. Choose one among paper cutting, rubbing, weaving Chinese knot, and shadow play. Tasting Local Snacks at the Muslim Quarter All local snacks of Xi'an are gathered at the Muslim Quarter. As an experienced foodie, your guide will introduce these special snacks to you, such as Chinese hamburger, and Bread Soaked in Mutton Soup. Gaze upon the mesmerizing Shanghai city skyline Looking out from the Bund, the skylines on both sides of the Huangpu River resemble a magnificent painting, with towering skyscrapers piercing the sky and shimmering in the sunlight. Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful China family tour for you. Day 1Arrive in Beijing | Airport Transfer Your private guide will be ready to meet and greet you upon arrival. A comfortable private car (non-smoking) with an experienced driver will transfer you directly to your hotel. Your guide will help you check-in. Day 2Beijing | Forbidden City and Taichi Lesson Breakfast & Lunch In the largest park in Beijing - the Temple of Heaven, you will be able to practice Tai Chi in the morning under the guidance of your teacher. First, full illustration of Tai Chi by a Tai Chi master. Second, demonstration by Yang Style Tai Chi master. Third, tutor to learn Tai Chi Quan. Last, you will have the chance to compete doing Tai Chi after class. Enjoy a traditional Beijing lunch at a local Roast Duck restaurant. Exploring Tiananmen Square, the world's largest public square. And then visit the majestic Forbidden City, and discover the ancient Chinese imperial architecture. Your knowledgeable tour guide will explain historical stories related to the emperor and help you uncover the secrets of royal architecture. Day 3 Beijing | Great Wall and Hutong Family Visit Breakfast & Lunch After 1.5 hours' drive from downtown Beijing, you will reach the Mutianyu Great Wall. This is the most child-friendly section of Great Wall. A 2½-kilometer section was fully restored in 1986. Take the cable car up to save time and energy. At the Wall, you have about 2 hours of free time to walk around. Finally, slid down from the Great Wall through a zip-line like a kung fu warrior. Visit a local family living in a hutong courtyard for generations. Experience the hobby of the host, calligraphy or paper cutting and taste the yogurt loved by locals living in hutong. Day 4Beijing to Luoyang | Summer Palace and Bullet train Breakfast & Lunch Be amazed by the imperial garden at the Summer Palace. It was originally the imperial court's summer retreat, and still makes a nice place to escape the heat of summer. Get ready to race towards Luoyang on a high-speed train, which is a good way to experience the technological development of China. Day 5 Luoyang | Leaning Shaolin kung Fu Breakfast & Lunch Shaolin Temple is probably the most famous temple in China, not only because of its long history and its role in Chinese Buddhism but also because of its martial arts or Wushu. In addition to touring the temple, you'll also have the chance to enjoy a Kung Fu show performed by the monks in the courtyard. After the show, step into a quiet courtyard. Your "Master Shifu" might be standing under the eaves, waiting for you. Learn a set of kung fu moves from a monk right there in the temple. Feel the spirit of Chinese kung fu and the atmosphere of Zen. Let Chinese kung fu awaken the bravery and strength in your heart. Day 6Luoyang to Xian | Longmen Grottoes and White Horse Temple Breakfast & Lunch The Longmen Grottoes is known for its intricate Buddhist sculptures and statues carved into limestone cliffs along the Yi River. The grottoes date back to the Northern Wei Dynasty (316-534 AD) and contain over 100,000 statues and 2,800 inscriptions. The White Horse Temple is the first Buddhist temple in China. Built in the 1st century AD, it is a significant site in Chinese Buddhist history and is considered the cradle of Chinese Buddhism. The temple got its name from two white horses that were sent as a gift from the western regions to the Han emperor. Take a bullet train to Xian un the late afternoon. Day 7Xian | Terracotta Army and Local Snacks Breakfast & Lunch Visit the awe-inspiring Terracotta Army, an archaeological wonder found in the mausoleum of the First Emperor of China, Qin Shi Huang. Marvel at the thousands of meticulously crafted warriors and horses, each bearing unique details. The Ancient City Wall in Xi'an is a key landmark offering panoramic views of the city. Bike along the top of the wall to get a sense of the city's history and enjoy a unique perspective of Xi'an. All local snacks of Xi'an are gathered at the Muslim Quarter. You get the opportunity to try them, such as Chinese hamburger, Bread Soaked in Mutton Soup. Day 8 Xian to Shanghai | Cooking Dumplings and Making Traditional Handicrafts Breakfast & Lunch Explore the grandeur of the Big Wild Goose Pagoda, a major Buddhist site. The elegant brick pagoda stands in the grounds of the attractive Da Ci'en Temple. Visit a local people's home and learn to make dumplings from the host. Learn some basic skills of dumpling making and experiencing local culture. Enjoy the fruits of your labor at lunch. Walk on a cultural street factually restored in the style of ancient architecture. And make your traditional handicrafts in a workshop. Choose one among paper cutting, rubbing, weaving Chinese knots, and shadow play. Your personal driver and guide will escort you to the airport to catch your flight to Shanghai in the evening. Day 9Shanghai | Shanghai Highlights and Chinese Martial Arts Museum Breakfast Learn about the history and culture of Chinese kung fu at the Chinese Martial Arts Museum. Yuyuan Garden is typical Chinese classic architecture and style. The complex covers an area of 2 hectares, with rockeries, pavilions, ponds and corridors. Stroll along the historic Bund in Shanghai, a vibrant waterfront promenade that encapsulates the city's rich heritage and modern allure. Crossing the Huangpu River through the Bund Sightseeing Tunnel. Ascend to the observation deck of Shanghai Tower, and get unparalleled panoramic views of Shanghai's bustling metropolis. Day 10 Departure Breakfast After a buffet breakfast at the hotel, your guide and driver will make sure you get to the airport on time and without any stress.
