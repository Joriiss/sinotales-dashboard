Title: Most Beautiful Cities for Natural Landscape in China
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/travelguide/top-scenic-cities.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

China has many "cities" with natural scenery that will take your breath away, or at least make a feast for your eyes and a pleasant place to relax. Picking the top 10 scenic cities is not easy as China has a great depth of naturally beautiful places. Those listed below attempt to pick out the best of top tourist destinations with a variety of top scenery. 1. Guilin — Spectacular Karst Landscape • Location: Guilin Prefecture, Guangxi Province, South China • Scenic Features: Karst peaks, bamboo-lined rivers, rice terraces, caves Guilin's beauty is legendary and part of Chinese folklore. The Li River with its karst peaks is at its most enchanting between Guilin and Yangshuo. Longsheng with its spectacular mountain rice terraces is also in Guilin prefecture. Elephant Hill is the icon for Guilin scenery, uniting rock and river. Guilin's limestone also gives it a whole list of well-developed and remarkable tourist caves, like Reed Flute Cave. 2. Huangshan (the Yellow Mountains) — Precipitous Peaks • Location: Huangshan Prefecture, Anhui Province, Central East China • Scenic Features: Curious rocks, strange-shaped pines, the sea of clouds Huangshan Prefecture contains the Yellow Mountains, which are, once again, legendary in beauty. The austere beauty of these precipitous peaks, studded with twisted pines, and shrouded in cloud, makes this scenic area the crown of China's mountainous scenic areas. Xin'an River is a beautiful hiking route and Tachuan is known for its fall hues. Suggested itinerary: 3-Day Essence of Huangshan 3. Shigatse — Mount Everest • Location: Shigatse Prefecture, Tibet, West China • Scenic Features: Mount Everest, Tibetan Plateau landscapes Who can deny the daunting magnificence of Mount Everest? Many make their pilgrimage to Shigatse Prefecture in Tibet just to catch a glimpse of the world's tallest mountain. Even on days when cloud envelopes the peak, the journey to Everest Base Camp through the rugged Himalayas is incredible, and no mean feat. Suggested itinerary: 8-Day Lhasa and Everest Base Camp Tour 4. Jiuzhaigou — Colorful Lakes and Rivers • Location: Jiuzhaigou Prefecture, Sichuan Province, West Central China • Scenic Features: Colorful lakes and rivers, forested valleys Jiuzhaigou Prefecture is known for its beautiful valleys (Shuzheng, Rize, Jianyan, Changhai, and Zharu), with crystal clear or vividly-colored water and ancient forests. These high valleys are on the Natural World Heritage list, and are among China's finest mountainous areas that have been made accessible to tourists. Suggested itinerary: 4-Day Fairyland Tour to Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong 5. Zhangjiajie — Sandstone Pillars Zhangjiajie National Forest Park • Location: Zhangjiajie Prefecture, Hunan Province, South Central China • Scenic Features: A forest of well-vegetated huge sandstone pillars Zhangjiajie Prefecture is known for the awesome forest of towering sandstone pillars and deep canyons of Wulingyuan Scenic Area. Steep-sided Baofeng Lake's waterfalls, a wide variety of plants, along with Yellow Dragon Cavern, one of the largest limestone caves in China, are all in the scenic area. Suggested itinerary: 4-Day Essence of Zhangjiajie Tour 6. Yichang / Chongqing — Luxury Yangtze River Cruise • Location: Chongqing Municipality / Hubei Province, Central China • Scenic Features: Karst peaks, bamboo-lined rivers, rice terraces, caves One of the classic tours in China is a Yangtze cruise of the Three Gorges. The most popular cruise is downstream from Chongqing to Yichang. This once perilous stretch of canyon-bound mighty river has been calmed in recent years by the Three Gorges Dams, giving way to leisurely cruises of flooded canyons. The most scenic areas are the Lesser Three Gorges and Shennong Stream. Suggested itinerary: 6-Day Essence of Chongqing and Yangtze Cruise Tour 7. Lijiang — Relaxing Trip • Location: Lijiang Prefecture, Yunnan Province, South China • Scenic Features: Spectacular gorges, snow-capped mountains, Yangtze upper reaches After Yangshuo above, Lijiang is another backpackers' favorite, with its small-town feel and awesome mountain scenery. Tiger Leaping Gorge, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, and the First Bend of the Yangtze offer stunning places for hikers, photographers and all outdoor lovers to relax and take in the views. Suggested itinerary: 3-Day Essence of Ancient Lijiang Tour 8. Dunhuang — Silk Road • Location: Dunhuang Prefecture, Gansu Province, Northwest China • Scenic Features: Pristine desert sands with an oasis, desert rock formations The desert landscapes of the Silk Road are another facet of China's scenery, and Dunhuang is the classic Chinese desert destination. The pristine curved dunes of Echoing Sand Mountain have an oasis, Crescent Spring, and are only 15 minutes from the city center. Yardang National Park, with its wind-carved boulders and outcrops, offers some of China's most austere scenery. 9. Sanya — Beautiful Beach • Location: Sanya Prefecture, Hainan Island, South China • Scenic Features: Tropical beaches, island paradises For those who like the seaside, China also has some great tropical beaches. Sanya boasts some of the best of these with full tourist facilities. Yalong Bay has palm-lined sandy coves, and clear blue sea, and Wuzhizhou Island, a short distance off the coast, is an island paradise with coral reefs. Suggested itinerary: 5-Day Sanya Beach Holiday 10. Xishuangbanna — Rainforest Water Splashing Festival in Xishuangbanna • Location: Yunnan Province, South China • Scenic Features: Tropical rain forest, jungled valleys Xishuangbanna in deepest South Yunnan Province, bordering Laos and Myanmar, has most of Chinese mainland tropical rainforest. This area should be included in the top 10 scenic areas to capture China's full range of scenery. Key scenic areas include Daluo River and One Tree Forest, and Wild Elephant Valley. There are another about 20 cities that have high-class scenic appeal: Lhasa in Tibet, which without exception offer great Tibetan landscapes; Shangrila (Diqing) and Jiayuguan are known for their snowy mountains and glaciers, Haikou for its mangrove forest, volcano crater and beaches, Hong Kong has Victoria Peak, and many more mountains and bays besides, and who could deny that Suzhou and Hangzhou are scenic cities with their elegant imperial landscaping? Apart from these many other cities or prefectures might be advertised as scenic, but these are less-well known for their scenery, or their scenery is not outstanding enough to be a strong tourist draw.
