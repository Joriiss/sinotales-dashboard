Our Zhangjiajie maps cover Zhangjiajie's location in China, cities in Hunan Province, attraction locations in Zhangjiajie, and hotels in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park and downtown Zhangjiajie. Zhangjiajie's Location in China Map Zhangjiajie is in northwest Hunan Province. It is famous for its precarious peaks, limpid streams, dense forests, and large karst caves. It is about 2½ hours by air from Beijing and 2 hours from Shanghai. This map shows the locations of Hunan's main prefecture-level cities, including Zhangjiajie. Zhangjiajie Transportation Map This map shows you how to get to Zhangjiajie from the main cities in China, by air and by train. Learn more about How to Get to Zhangjiajie. Zhangjiajie Attractions Map This map shows the main attractions in Zhangjiajie (including Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Tianzi Mountain, Baofeng Lake, Yellow Dragon Cave, and Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon Glass Bridge), and the distance from the scenic areas to Zhangjiajie City.
