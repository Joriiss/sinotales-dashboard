Hangzhou is well known for its excellent silk, tea, and lotus root starch. Various shops, stores, supermarkets, and plazas crowd the commercial center. Recently, self-service supermarkets have appeared in the city. People can have a contemporary shopping experience, with developing technology. Popular Local Souvenirs Hangzhou silk has a long history of more than 4,000 years, featuring gentle texture, gorgeous color and great variety. There are 14 kinds of silk produced in Hangzhou, including satin, spin, crepe, damask silk and silk fabric. Silk products include landscape paintings, cushions, curtains and clothing. Places for Buying Longjing Tea • Hangzhou West Lake Longjing Tea Company Ltd Address: No.602, Qifeng Road, Hangzhou (杭州凤起路602号) • Hangzhou Shifeng Tea Company Ltd Address: No.228, Yanan Road, Hangzhou (杭州延安路228号) Xihu silk umbrellas feature bright colors and exquisite appearance, with the "bone" made of bamboo. They can be used not only as ordinary umbrellas for sunshade, but also as ornaments to decorate your house. You can buy one in Hefang Street in Shangcheng District Wangxingji Paper Fans 王星记扇子 The paper fans produced in Hangzhou have been famous in China since ancient times. The most famous three local products in Hangzhou are paper fans, silk and Longjing tea. Wangxingji fans are the best-known brand of fan in Hangzhou. The history of the factory dates back to 1875. At that time, the fans were regarded as appropriate tribute for the imperial household. Lan Yin Hua Bu (Blue Cloth with Design in White) 蓝印花布 This cloth is an ancient folk-printed fabric made by hand. The simple but elegant designs on the fabric mostly feature auspicious items, reflecting the unique folk culture in China. The dye is produced from the indigo plant. Top 3 Shopping Places in Hangzhou Hefang Ancient Street Hefang Street is one of the most famous historic streets in Hangzhou, reflecting social features of the Southern Song Dynasty (1127-1279). Taking a stroll along this street, you will be attracted by antique buildings and local crafts, such as silk parasols, brocades, renowned Zhang Xiaoquan scissors and Hangzhou fans. This road is regarded as the most prosperous in Hangzhou. It runs from north to south for about 3,000 meters and has become Hangzhou's commercial center. The road is home to the grand buildings of the Zhejiang People's Provincial Great Hall, Hangzhou Culture Center, Victory Theatre, International Mansion, Hangzhou Department Store, Hangzhou Grand Hotel, and Haifeng Western Restaurant. Wulin road is one of the most distinctive commercial streets in Hangzhou. It is known as female fashion street. There are about 1,000 shops along the road, most of which are selling women's wear in various styles and brands. There are many shops opened by local designers there. Popular New Shopping Styles in Hangzhou Self-Service Supermarkets The first self-service supermarket operated by Alibaba opened in Hangzhou in 2017. This new style of shopping attracts many people. You only need to scan your QR code on entering the supermarket. After shopping, you go to the exit. There is a "billing door". When you go through it, the accounting system will automatically recognize your goods and deduct the corresponding fees from your phone. There are now many such self-service supermarkets in Hangzhou. They have become a major feature of the city. This is a new experience and a good way to learn more about Hangzhou. Another new style of shopping is also organized by Alibaba: T-mall Online Supermarket. Snacks, drinks, appliances, kitchenware, and daily necessities are all available at T-mall Supermarket. Your order will normally be delivered in 3 days. If you are living in some nearby cities, such as Shanghai or Hangzhou, it can be delivered the next day! Alibaba has also launched fresh food supermarkets operating both online and offline. These are mostly located in residential areas. They provide fresh ingredients, semi-finished food and finished food. You can use an app to order online. It only takes half an hour to receive delivery. Alternatively, you can go to the offline supermarket. There they can cook for you with your ingredients.
