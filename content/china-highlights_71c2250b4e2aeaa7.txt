Shenzhen is one of China's wealthiest and most cosmopolitan cities, and has many Muslim restaurants in Luohu and Futian districts, and also in the Seaworld area, where tourists and travellers often go. Zhong Fa Yuan Muslim Restaurant (Chunfeng Road Branch) — Luohu This restaurant ranks high among Muslims and others for the quality Uigher-style meals. It is conveniently near the Luohu border crossing (2.5 kilometers away). People love the Uigher food for the healthy simple meat dishes, breads, and vegetable dishes. Ranked 78 of 3,913 on TripAdvisor (2016) • Chinese name: 中发源清真餐厅 (春风路店) Zhong Fa Yuan Muslim Canting • Recommended dishes: Roasted lamb chops (烤羊排), mutton soup (羊汤), boiled mutton (手抓羊肉), yogurt (酸奶), and barley pancakes (青稞饼). • Average price per person: 70 yuan • Address: No. 2012 Chunfeng Road, Shenzhen (罗湖区春风路2012号) • Tel: 0755-82229318 • Transportation: Take bus 14, 229, 336, 372, 381, 382, 387, or 395 and get off at Wenjing Middle School (文锦中学). Nadam Flavor Restaurant — Futian The Nadam Flavor Restaurant mainly specializes in Mongolian style Muslim dishes. It is a special experience to have the delicious Muslim dishes in the open air in the evening while watching the Mongolian performances. It is rated 580 out of 3,913 restaurants. • Chinese name: 那达慕蒙古风味酒楼 Na Da Mu Feng Wei Jiu Lou • Recommended dishes: Cold Chinese cabbage in sauce (凉拌大白菜), mutton soup (羊杂汤), roasted lamb chops (烤羊排), spicy kebabs (烤羊肉串), and mutton pie (羊肉馅饼). • Address: Floor 1, 18 Meilian Building, Sun Gang Xi Road, Futian District (near Hua Run Wan Jia) (福田区笋岗西路美莲大厦18栋1楼 (近华润万家)) • Average price per person: 60 yuan • Tel: 0755-83233081 Muslim Noodle House — Sea World Sea World's main landmark is this big restaurant/hotel boat. This Hui Chinese restaurant is in the Sea World area. It isn't a ritzy place, and the dishes are inexpensive. The consistent quality, large portions, and good tastes have made it popular. It is also among the least expensive restaurants for a meal around Sea World.
