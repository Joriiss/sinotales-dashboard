Customs and Immigrations at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station As a cross-border station, Hong Kong West Kowloon Station has immigration and customs facilities for passengers to go through. If you take a high-speed train from Hong Kong to Guilin, go through the customs at the Immigration/Customs Departure Hall on -3F at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station. Please ensure that you complete the 'Hong Kong Departure Card' and the 'China Arrival Card'. If you fail to obtain and complete these cards, you would be sent back to collect and fill-in the forms, and queue again. The queues can be long and this will cause some inconvenience with the possibility of you missing your train. All your luggage must also go through security checks and x-rayed. We advise that you arrive at the train station at least 2 hours before your departure time. If you take high-speed trains from Guilin to Hong Kong, you need to go through customs twice on arrival concourse on -2F. Please complete the Arrival/Departure Cards before you go to the officer. Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station is a major transportation hub located in the West Kowloon area of Hong Kong. It serves as the terminus for high-speed trains that connect Hong Kong with various cities in China's mainland, including Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beijing, and Shanghai. It is conveniently located approximately a 45-minute drive away from Hong Kong International Airport. With its convenient location near the Victoria Harbour waterfront, West Kowloon Railway Station provides a seamless connection to other forms of transportation. The station integrates with the Mass Transit Railway (MTR) system, allowing passengers to easily transfer to and from the urban areas of Hong Kong. How to Get to West Kowloon Railway Station There are several convenient ways to get to Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway Station: 1. MTR (Mass Transit Railway): The most common and convenient way is to take the MTR. West Kowloon Station is directly connected to the MTR West Rail Line and the Tuen Ma Line. Simply take the MTR to West Kowloon Station and follow the signages within the station to reach the High-Speed Rail terminus. 2. Airport Express: If you are arriving at Hong Kong International Airport, you can take the Airport Express train to Kowloon Station. From there, you can transfer to the free Airport Express Shuttle Bus, which will take you directly to West Kowloon Railway Station. 3. It is located in the south of the city, approximately 20 kilometers away from the city center and Huanghua International Airport. The station is a modern and well-equipped railway station that offers a variety of facilities and services to passengers. How to Get to Changsha South Railway Station There are several ways to get to Changsha South Railway Station. Here are a few options: 1. Subway: Changsha Metro Line 2 has a station right at Changsha South Railway Station. You can take Line 2 and alight directly at Changsha South Station. This is a convenient and efficient way to reach the railway station. 2. Taxi: Taxis are widely available in Changsha, and you can easily find one to take you to Changsha South Railway Station. Make sure to have the station's name written down in Chinese characters to show the taxi driver. Taxis in Changsha are generally metered, but it's advisable to confirm the fare with the driver before starting the journey. 3. Bus: There are several bus routes that serve Changsha South Railway Station. You can check the local bus schedules and routes to find one that stops near the station.
