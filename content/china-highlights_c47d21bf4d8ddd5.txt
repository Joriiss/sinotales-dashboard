It only takes around 10 minutes for this short subway trip. How to Go through Customs at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station? For travelers journeying between Shanghai and Hong Kong, customs formalities are streamlined at West Kowloon Station, regardless of direction. Shanghai to Hong Kong: Upon arrival at West Kowloon Station's B4 level, passengers will smoothly transition to B2 via elevator for China Mainland exit formalities, followed by entry formalities for Hong Kong.
