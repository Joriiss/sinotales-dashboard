Explore the Authentic Chengdu Life Many travelers visit Chengdu to see giant pandas but return for the food and its unique laid-back culture. This day trip invites you to explore Chengdu's local parks, folk theaters, and time-honored streets to understand better the city dwellers' lifestyle and their go-with-the-flow attitude toward life. Feel free to customize your private journey, we are more than thrilled to assist you. Learn Tea Etiquette in an Ancient Teahouse Relish the unique Gaiwan tea with the locals in the park, and immerse yourself in the genuine Chengdu way. Visit the Backstage of a Local Theater You have the opportunity to go backstage and understand the performance preparation process of the Sichuan Opera actors. Roam within the Age-old Alleys Explore the Wide and Narrow Alley, lauded as the epitome of old Chengdu, and immerse yourself in the city's culturally enriching street life. Your guide will adjust according to the actual circumstances of the day and your requirements. 9:00 am Chengdu People's Park Your private guide and driver will promptly meet you at your hotel, ready to embark on a day's leisure journey with you. Venture into the century-old Chengdu People's Park and begin your laid-back day. In the main square, locals participate in various activities like tai chi, kicking a shuttlecock, and square dancing. The daily ritual of stopping at a teahouse to meet friends and chat began in Chengdu at least 1,000 years ago. Take a seat and sip a cup of tea. Your guide will explain the etiquette of how to drink tea and the intentions you express when you put the lid, bowl, and tray in different places. 2:00 pm Backstage of a Local Theater Chengdu is the main home of Sichuan opera. Every day, there are many grassroots opera houses that we recommend. After lunch, follow your guide and have a chance to step backstage to see how the actors and actresses make up and chant with them. 3:00 pm Wide and Narrow Alleys Next, journey to the Wide and Narrow Alleys, the treasured remnants of Chengdu from the Qing Dynasty era. Here, you can delve into Chengdu's folk culture, explore ancient architecture, or shop for unique crafts and local snacks. Amidst traditional teahouses and modern cafes, experience a perfect blend of tradition and modernity. 5:00 pm Wave Goodbye At the end of the trip, we will escort you to your hotel.
