Day 6 Sunrise on the Yellow Mountain, Visit Hongcun Village Two highlights of today's journey are the Yellow Mountain's sunrise and local Hongcun Village. Driven to Hongcun Village to see ancient Anhui architectures, which are composed of old mansions, canals, and ancestral halls.
