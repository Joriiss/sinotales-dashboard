Title: 8-Day Shanghai, Hangzhou, Suzhou Family Tour includes Wuzhen

Source: China Highlights

Discover the Prosperous Jiangnan Jiangnan is the most prosperous region in China. It is famous for its traditional gardens, picturesque water towns, delicious cuisine, and important historical sites. During the tour, you will travel on the bullet train to visit three iconic cities, including Shanghai, Hangzhou, and Suzhou, and delight in the distinctive waterfront vistas of Wuzhen water town. Here is a suggested itinerary. If your family wants to add any activities or change anything, just feel free to let your travel adviser know. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and needs. Suggested 8-Day Family Tour Day Highlights 1-3 Shanghai Old and new Shanghai 4-5 Hangzhou West Lake and Tea culture 6 Wuzhen Classic Chinese water town 7-8 Suzhou Classic Chinese garden and rickshaw riding Rowing a boat leisurely on West lake As you glide across the tranquil waters, you can take in the scenic views of the lush green hills, traditional pagodas, and historic temples that line the shores of the lake. Picking tea leaves with local farmers in a tea garden You will first learn to choose appropriate tea leaves and pick them in the right way. After tea-picking, the tea farmer will show you the process of stir-frying tea. Then learn to make a cup of tea. Tasting lotus root powder at Chenghuang Ge Pavilion Try a bowl of lotus root powder in the pavilion and get a panoramic view of the West Lake. Stroll along the Ancient Streets of Wuzhen Explore the charming ancient streets of Wuzhen, lined with historic buildings, traditional shops, and local eateries. Ride a rickshaw along the bank of the ancient canals Rickshaw ride is a popular activity to experience the ancient culture and traditions of local people. You will see local life on the banks of the ancient canals on the rickshaw. Explore the classic Chinese gardens Suzhou gardens are known for their unique blend of natural landscapes, traditional Chinese architecture, and intricate designs that reflect ancient Chinese gardening principles. You will discover the Humble Administrator's Garden and the Master of Nets Garden this time. Ascend to the Shanghai Tower to enjoy panoramic views of the city skyline Enjoy panoramic views of the city skyline and the Huangpu River from the observation deck on the upper floors. Day 1Day 1: Arrive in Shanghai Upon your arrival in Shanghai, your private tour guide and driver will greet you with warm smiles. They will escort you to your hotel, allowing you to rest after your long flight. Day 2Shanghai | Discover the Highlights of Shanghai Breakfast & Lunch The first stop is Yuyuan Garden. Explore the traditional Chinese garden with beautiful pavilions, rockeries, and ponds. Don't miss the nearby old market for shopping and snacks. Walk along this bustling Nanjing Road to experience the vibrant atmosphere and find souvenirs or luxury goods. Have a stunning view of Shanghai's skyline from the top of Shanghai Tower. Then end your day with a stroll along the bund to enjoy the historic buildings, modern skyscrapers, and views of the Huangpu River. Day 3Shanghai | Free Day Breakfast Most of the families would like to have a free day to discover the city on their own. Visitors usually go to Disney, and Shanghai Zoo, as well as visit Xintiandi and various markets for shopping. Day 4Shanghai to Hangzhou | West Lake and Tea Garden Breakfast & Lunch Take a bullet train to Hangzhou this morning. Start your Hangzhou trip at West Lake, a picturesque lake surrounded by temples, gardens, and pagodas. Enjoy a relaxing boat cruise to view the scenic beauty. Head to the Meijiawu Tea Village to learn about and taste the famous Longjing tea. A local tea farmer guides you through selecting and picking tea leaves, demonstrates the stir-frying process to preserve the leaves' color, and teaches the art of brewing a perfect cup of tea. Day 5Hangzhou to Wuzhen | Traditional Architectures and Qinghefang Street Breakfast & Lunch Explore the Chenghuang Ge Pavilion and taste lotus root powder, a local delicacy. It is also a great place to get a panoramic view of the West Lake. Visit the Former Residence of Hu Xueyan, explore the historical residence and learn about the life of Hu Xueyan. Have lunch at Qinghefang Pedestrian Street. Stroll through the bustling street, shop for souvenirs, and soak in the vibrant atmosphere. Drive to Wuzhen in the late afternoon. Enjoy the wonderful night view of the water town. Day 6Wuzhen to Suzhou | Dong(East) Zha and Xi(West) Zha Breakfast & Lunch Wuzhen Water Town is separated into Dong Zha(East Area) and Xi Zha(West Area). Dong Zha is known for its ancient charm and well-preserved historical architecture. Xi Zha has been rebuilt and is famous for its serene waterways, old-fashioned stone houses, and peaceful ambiance. Your guide and driver will assist you in boarding the bullet train to Suzhou. Day 7Suzhou | Classical Chinese Garden Breakfast & Lunch Start your day by visiting the Humble Administrator's Garden. The garden is the best representative of Chinese classical gardens, constructed in the Ming dynasty, which focuses on a central pond with pavilions, terraces, chambers, and towers located nearby./p> Head to the Master of Nets Garden, a small but exquisite garden showcasing Suzhou's architectural and gardening finesse. Hop on the rickshaw to see local life on the banks of the ancient canals. Take a leisurely walk around the Pingjiang Road Historic District to soak in the lively atmosphere and traditional architecture. Day 9Suzhou to Shanghai | Departure Breakfast The drive from Suzhou to Shanghai Airport takes approximately 2 hours. We will ensure you are taken to the airport on time according to your departure schedule.
