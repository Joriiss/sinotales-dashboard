If you're visiting Shanghai but want to plan an excursion to Zhangjiajie, here is all the information you need to know including how to get from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie, how many days to stay, where to stay, and more. How to Travel from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie Shanghai is about 1,000 kilometers from Zhangjiajie, a city in SouthCentral China. The quickest way to get from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie is by airplane. By airplane(recommended): There are daily direct flights from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie, taking about 2 hours. Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport is only 4 kilometers (2 miles) from the city center. By train: The bullet train from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie takes about 9 hours. There is one departure each day, departing from Shanghai at 12:41 and arriving in Zhangjiajie at 21:55. See detailed information about how to get from Shanghai to Zhangjiajie by air and train. How Many Days Are Required In Zhangjiajie, you can stay for 2–4 days to visit the highlights there, such as Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Tianmen Mountain, and Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge. You can make the most of your time by taking an early morning flight to Zhangjiajie or an evening/nighttime flight to Shanghai. The Best Times to Visit Zhangjiajie Zhangjiajie in winter In terms of weather, the best times to visit Zhangjiajie are April–May and September–October. Winter is cold but you will be rewarded with no crowds and sometimes snowy scenery. Summer is the busiest season due to the summer vacation for students but it offers more chances to see the mountains shrouded in fog, which is when you will feel like you are walking in a fairyland.
