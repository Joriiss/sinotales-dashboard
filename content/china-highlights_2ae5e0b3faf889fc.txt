Title: 7-Day High Speed Railway Tour from Guangzhou to Guizhou

High-Speed Railway Tour from Guangzhou to Guizhou Both Guizhou and Guizhou are known for their natural beauty and unique cultural attractions. On this tour, you will see picturesque karst mountains, terrace fields, ethnic villages, and fantastic falls. Relax on a top-class guided Li River cruise, enjoying spectacular landscapes Cycle around the countryside of Yangshuo Walk on the terrace field and visit a Yao family Appreciate the most famous Dong architecture – the Chengyang Wind and Rain Bridge Marvel at the magnificent scenery of Huangguoshu Waterfall Suggested Itinerary Day 1: Arrival and Half-Day Guilin City Tour Take a high-speed train from Guangzhou South Station and arrive at Guilin North Station, where your guide will meet you. After half an hour's drive to downtown Guilin, start your half-day tour with Reed Flute Cave to see awesome stalactites and stalagmites. Then visit the city landmark of Guilin - Elephant trunk hill park. Day 2: Li River Cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo After half an hour's drive to Mopanshan Wharf, your guide will show you the wonders of the Li River on your four-hour Li River Cruise, a "must" for a Guilin tour. You can choose to stay on deck to breath the fresh air or take seat inside. The boat navigating down the river is just like watching a Chinese landscape scroll painting unroll. The boat arrives at Yangshuo at about 13:30. Enjoy free time in Yangshuo, then we will drive you back to Guilin. Day 3: Visit Ping'an village in Longsheng You will be driven about two hours to Longsheng. Then climb around 40 minutes to the viewpoints around Ping'an village to see the magnificent terraced fields from different angles. Get closer to local life to learn how they earn a living, and how they work in the fields with water buffalos. Stay at a Ping'an hotel in the middle of the rice terraces. Day 4: Drive to Sanjiang to see Chengyang Bridge In the morning you will head to Sanjiang, where Dong minority people live. After about 2½ hours you will be amongst the wooden buildings of the Dong. Wander through the villages and meet friendly people. Built in 1916, Chengyang 'Wind and Rain' Bridge is the best one among the several 'wind and rain' bridges in nearby Dong stockaded villages. It is the largest 'wind and rain' bridge in the world. In the late afternoon, you will be driven about 40 minutes from Chengyang to Sanjiang town. Stay at the best available hotel overnight. Day 5: Take the HSR to Guizhou and Visit Qianling Park Continue by bullet train to Guiyang. After two hours, you will arrive at Guizhou Province's capital city, Guiyang. Day 6: Enjoy Huangguoshu Waterfalls and Tianxing Qiao Scenic Area Journey north about two hours to see the Huangguoshu Waterfalls. It is one of the largest waterfall groups in China. There are 18 falls above ground and four underground falls. Tianxing Qiao Scenic Area features the perfect combination of stones, trees, and water. Tianxing Bridge Scenic Zone is like a natural miniascape (dish garden) that you can walk through. After the day trip, return to Guiyang.
