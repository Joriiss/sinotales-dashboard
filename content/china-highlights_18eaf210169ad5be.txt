After high-speed train service began on this route, the travel time between the two cities has been shortened from 3 hours to only 29 minutes. Trains run frequently from 06:00 to 23:00, which makes it very easy to fit your schedule. G-category high-speed trains run between Guangzhou South/Guangzhou North and Shenzhen North railway stations. C-category intercity high-speed trains run between Guangzhou/Guangzhou East and Shenzhen railway stations, which are closer to city centers. How to Choose Between Seat Classes on Chinese trains Public Transportation to Train Stations Guangzhou South Railway Station There are several train stations in large cities like Guangzhou and Shenzhen. Each one is far away from the other. Please make sure you go to the right station for your train. All the train stations in Guangzhou and Shenzhen are connected to subway network, thus easy to get to the other parts of the cities. Guangzhou South Railway Station ("广州南 Guangzhou Nan" on tickets) Guangzhou South Station is modern and airport-like and is one of the largest railway stations in China. All of the G category Guangzhou – Shenzhen trains depart from this station. It is also the terminus of Guangzhou – Shenzhen – Hong Kong Express Rail Link, Guangzhou – Beijing High-Speed Railway and Guangzhou – Shanghai High-Speed Railway. It also serves Guangzhou – Guilin bullet trains and Guangzhou – Guiyang bullet trains. Guangzhou South Railway Station is far from downtown but with convenient transportation: • By subway: Guangzhou Metro Line 2 • By bus: 3, K6, 20, 52, 61, 64, 75, 75B, 79, 99, 100, 102, 108 Guangzhou Railway Station ("广州 Guangzhou" on tickets) Conveniently located in downtown, Guangzhou Railway Station is also one of the busiest stations in China. Some of the C-category Guangzhou – Shenzhen trains depart from here. Overnight trains from Guangzhou to Beijing and Shanghai are also depart from this station. Guangzhou Railway Station can easily reach this station by subway, taxi or bus. • By subway: Guangzhou Metro Line 2 and Line 5 • By bus: B2, B2A, 3, 7, 8, B10, 11, 14, 15, 18, 21, 25, 30, 31, 41, 43, 51, 52, 55 Guangzhou East Railway Station Guangzhou East Railway Station ("广州东 Guangzhou Dong" on tickets) Guangzhou East Railway Station is the station for Guangzhou – Hong Kong normal-speed trains and some of the C-category Guangzhou – Shenzhen trains. • By subway: Guangzhou Metro Line 3 • By bus: 24, 28, 34, 41, 43, 45, 49, 62A, 302, 302A, 501, 508 See our detailed transportation guide for Guangzhou East Railway Station. Guangzhou North Railway Station ("广州北 Guangzhou Bei" on tickets) Remote from the city center, Guangzhou North Station serves some of the Guangzhou – Shenzhen C-category trains. This train station has been incorporated into renovation plan. It is expected to connect to Beijing – Guangzhou and Wuhan – Guangzhou railways in the future. • By subway: Guangzhou Metro Line 9 • By bus: 475, 105, 108, 111, 113, 185, 187, 198, 21, 274, 278, 291A, 291B, 519, 528, 538, 555, 556 Shenzhen North Railway Station Shenzhen North Railway Station ("深圳北 Shenzhen Bei" on tickets) The newly-built and airport-like Shenzhen North Station serves all the G category Shenzhen – Guangzhou trains. It is a bit far away from the city center, but can be easily reached by taxi, subway and bus. • By subway: Shenzhen Metro Line 4 (Longhua Line) and Line 5 (Huanzhong Line) • By bus: B666, B730, E7, E22, M217, M299, M341, M343, M352, M353, M360 See our transportation guide for Shenzhen North Railway Station. Futian Railway Station ("福田 Futian" on tickets) Futian Railway Station is located in the southern district of Shenzhen, a bit far from the city center. It was the largest underground railway station in China, before being surpassed by Hong Kong West Kowloon Railway. Some of the G trains depart from and arrive at Futian Station. Most of them also stop at Shenzhen North Station. • By subway: Shenzhen Metro Line 2, Line 3 and Line 11 • By bus: 327, M221, E28, E18, E613, M413, 134, 59 See our transportation guide for Futian Railway Station. Shenzhen Railway Station Shenzhen Railway Station ("深圳 Shenzhen" on tickets) Located in the city center, Shenzhen Railway Station is just beside Luohu Port, convenient for passengers who need to go to Hong Kong. Some of the C-category Shenzhen – Guangzhou bullet trains depart from here. • By subway: Shenzhen Metro Line 1 • By bus: 1, 17, 12, 101, 102, 25, 205, 215, 38, 302, 306, 337, 352, 387, 7, 82, 83, 97, K302 Guangzhou – Shenzhen Normal-Speed Tranins Over 20 pairs of normal-speed trains (K, T, Z trains) are in service between Guangzhou and Shenzhen, with the travel time of 1.5 to 3.5 hours. The ticket price of normal-speed train is cheaper than that of high-speed train. Hard seat only costs US$3 per ticket. Normal-speed trains from Guangzhou to Shenzhen is not recommended because high-speed trains are much faster, cleaner and more punctual and comfortable. • Shenzhen to Hong Kong Transport and Ports (Border Crossings) • China High-Speed Railway - 8 Main China High-Speed Routes, Maps , and Development
