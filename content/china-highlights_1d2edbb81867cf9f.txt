Beijing boasts a larger array of historical and cultural landmarks to discover, along with more chances to participate in experiential activities. Therefore, we suggest spending more time in Beijing. How Long to Spend in Beijing To experience Beijing as authentically as possible and maintain a comfortable travel pace, we recommend spending around 4 to 5 days in the city. Allocate 2 or 3 days to explore iconic landmarks such as the Forbidden City and the Great Wall. Dedicate an additional one or two days to discovering the hutongs, traditional residences of Beijing. You may also want to consider arranging some food tours during your explorations. For those interested in the culture of Beijing, we recommend attending a Peking Opera performance. You could visit their backstage and dress up in their costumes. How Long to Spend in Xi'an For a trip to Xi'an, we recommend a duration of 2 to 3 days. The Terracotta Warriors is undeniably the main attraction in Xi'an, and we recommend spending a day for a visit and trying to make a mini clay warrior yourself! For the remaining time, you could arrange visits to the Shaanxi History Museum and the Big Wild Goose Pagoda, go on a food tour around the Muslim Quarter, and take a leisurely stroll along the Ancient City Wall. A Classic Beijing and Xi'an Itinerary • Day 1: Arrive in Beijing and spend the rest of the day exploring at your leisure. • Day 2: Tour the Forbidden City, followed by a walk through the hutongs that provides an intimate glimpse into what old Beijing was like. • Day 3: Visit the Temple of Heaven, which used to be a royal sacrificial site, and then take a trip to the Great Wall. • Day 4: Head to the Summer Palace, which was once a royal garden, and then depart for Xi'an. • Day 5: Visit the Terracotta Warriors, along with taking a stroll along the Ancient City Wall. • Day 6: Explore the Shaanxi History Museum and the Big Wild Goose Pagoda to get an overview of Chinese history and culture. This itinerary covers the must-see attractions within a week's span while also allowing time for special experiences and personal exploration. The pace is well-balanced to ensure it's not too rushed. There's no need to worry about the long waiting time at the airport, and ticket changes in emergency situations are easier. Alternatively, there are direct flights available that can transport you between Beijing and Xi'an in 2 to 2½ hours. However, please note that airports are located quite far from the city center, taking around 1½ to 2 hours by car. Taking into account the additional travel and transportation time, the entire journey by air is roughly comparable to that of a high-speed train, or only slightly faster.
