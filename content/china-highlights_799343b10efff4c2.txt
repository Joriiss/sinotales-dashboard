The Warring States Period Map shows the land divisions by many regions during the Warring States Period. Click it to enlarge. From 475 BC to 221 When the Qinshihuang unified the country, is known as the Warrior Period. It is obvious so named for wars between the states continued, even intensified. This period is traditionally regarded as the beginning of the feudalism in china. Read more information about the Warring States Period and check out more about Chinese history.
