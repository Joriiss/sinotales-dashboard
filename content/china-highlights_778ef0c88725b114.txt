Title: Chongqing Weather in June, Chongqing Temperature in June

Weather: June may be the third hottest month following July and August. The average temperature ranges from 22 °C (72 °F) to 29 °C (84 °F) daily. You may feel hot in the morning and in the evening as there is minimal breeze. It is often cloudy or overcast, but when it is sunny, it may be scorching since the sunshine is strong and the humidity is high. There are 16 days of rain in the month for a total of 171 mm. Forecast: The weather is changeable, so stay abreast of the local weather forecast>> Air quality: Lightly to moderately polluted. Clothing: Shorts, T-shirts and rain gear. Things to Do in Chongqing in June In June, you can duck indoors into museums when it rains. Otherwise, you can enjoy the scenery and breezes on a Daning River cruise or a Yangtze cruise. Half-Day Daning River Cruise The Lesser Three Gorges: It is a good time to see the lush green gorges. Both the water and the landscape are green and contrasts with the muddy brown color of the Yangtze. Most tourists say that the scenery is better than at the big Three Gorges. Cool river breezes help you to stay cool. Ciqikou Old Town: This favorite tourist shopping area isn't for bargain hunting as much as for recreation. The area is known for old preserved architecture and many walking lanes. Try the local food such as Suan La Fen (Sour Hot Noodles). Stay Cool and Learn in the Three Gorges Museum The Three Gorges Museum is a large and modern museum where many tourists like to spend a few hours. It is a fascinating place to learn about the region of the Three Gorges, its history, and see the preserved antiques and artifacts from the submerged area. You can learn about the huge new dam too. Touring Chongqing in June June is officially "high season" for tourism, but tourism is relatively lower than in July and August when universities and schools are on summer vacation.
