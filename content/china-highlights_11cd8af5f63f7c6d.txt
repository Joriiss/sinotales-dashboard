Anji Bamboo Forest or Anji Bamboo Sea is located in Anji County, Zhejiang Province, about 1½ hours' driving from Hangzhou and about 4 hours' driving from Shanghai. Anji Bamboo forest is one of the largest natural bamboo forests in the world and is where the Academy-Award-winning film "Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon" was filmed. Anji is hailed as an eco-tourism destination. The Best Things to Do in Anji Enjoy a Light Hike in Anji's Bamboo Forest. Anji Bamboo Forest covers 600 square km (230 sq mi) and has been designated as an ecological preservation area. Walking through the bamboo forest is absolutely the best way to enjoy the fresh, green, and natural quietness. The sunshine casts leafy shadows over you through the bamboo, and the breeze whispers in bamboo tones around your ears. You can cross the historical Bamboo Sea Promenade, visit the King of the Bamboo Sea, view Wunü Lake, and finally climb Bamboo Tower, where you can have a panoramic view of the entire bamboo sea. It is about a 2-hour walk. Meet the Lovely Pandas in Anji Bamboo Expo Park. Anji Bamboo Expo Park is a man-made bamboo museum and a research center housing a variety of manmade bamboo items from all over China and Asia. There are 396 species of bamboo covered there! You can also meet four lovely pandas in the Panda Garden — bamboo is their favorite food. Pick Anji White Tea in Zhongnan Flora and Fauna Park. Zhongnan Flora and Fauna Park is a nice destination to get close to nature and animals. There is a big white tea (baicha) plantation in the park, where you can taste the tea and enjoy the tea picking experience. You can also take the well-designed sightseeing train ride to enjoy the European pastoral scenery in the park. Challenge Yourself in Skyland. Skyland is a paradise for outdoor activities. There you can experience cliff swings, grass skiing, glass walkways, rock climbing, and sky walking. There is also an alpine skiing resort in Skyland, which is one of the better winter skiing resorts around Shanghai and Hangzhou. Spring (March–May) is a good season to see the Bamboo Forest, and it is also a good time to taste fresh bamboo shoots (a local delicacy) in Anji. Summer (June–August) is a good time to play on the water. You should not miss the river rafting in Anji at Shenxi and Jingkongli. Anji Bamboo Forest is also a good destination to escape from the hot weather. Autumn (September–November) is a good time to climb mountains in Anji and enjoy the fall leaves' colors. Nearby Longwang Mountain and Lingdu Mountain are good options. Winter (December–February) is the season to enjoy snow activities at the ski resorts. You have many choices in Anji, such as Jingnan Tianchi Skiing Resort and Skyland Skiing Resort. After a skiing experience, you can find a hot spring in Anji to relax yourself in.
