It is one of the three main stations in the city of Xi'an. The other two are Xi'an Railway Station and Xi'an South Railway Station. Xi'an North is the terminus of the Zhengzhou – Xi'an, Xi'an – Baoji, Datong – Xi'an and Xi'an – Chengdu rail lines. Trains to Beijing, Chengdu, Zhengzhou, Shijiazhuang, Nanjing, Nanchang, Wuhan, Changsha, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen departs from there. • By subway: Line 2 • By bus: 108, 263, 264, 266, 329, 360, 362, 723, 727 Other high-speed trains operate at Xi'an North Railway Station: • Xi'an to Beijing bullet train • Xi'an to Pingyao bullet train Xi'an Railway Station (Chinese: 西安站) Xi'an Railway Station is located in the city center. It is the station with the longest history in Xi'an and the main train station for most of the ordinary trains in Xi'an. • By bus: 1, 2, airport shuttle bus line 2, 9, 13, 14, 25, 30, 40, 41, 42, 103, 105, 201, 231 • By subway: Line 2 Xi'an South Railway Station (Chinese: 西安南站) The newly-built Xi'an South Railway Station is along way from the downtown area. It serves most of the high-speed trains that depart from/arrive in Xi'an. Currently, there is no subway line to Xi'an South Railway Station. The most convenient way to get there is to take a taxi. • By bus: 423, 918, 920 Chengdu Railway Station (Chinese: 成都站) Chengdu Railway Station, also called Chengdu North Railway Station by local residents, is one of the main stations in Chengdu. It is in the north of the city, serving the Chengdu – Chongqing, Baoji – Chengdu, Chengdu – Kunming and Dazhou – Chengdu rail lines. • By subway: Line 1, Line 7 • By bus: 2, 9, 11, 16, 17, 27, 28, 36, 50, 52, 54, 55, 57, 65, 70, 73, 83, 86, 123, 298, 1131 Chengdu East Railway Station (成都东站) Chengdu East is also a main station in the city of Chengdu. Trains operating on this railway section have an average speed of 250 kilometers (156 miles) per hour. Road Distance: If you prefer road transportation, the distance between Xi'an and Chengdu is 830 kilometers (516 miles).
