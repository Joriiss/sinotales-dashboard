China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select • China Train • Int’l Train from China Chengdu Railway Station Chengdu Railway Station, also refers to Chengdu North Railway Station by locals, is one of the most important train stations in China. It is located near the city center, mainly serving normal-speed trains from Chengdu to Chongqing, Kunming, Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen. Chengdu South Railway Station Chengdu South Railway Station is one of the three main train stations serving the city of Chengdu. It servies Chengdu–Mianyang–Leshan intercity rail lines and some of normal-speed train to and from Xi'an. Chengdu South Railway Station is 6 kilometers (4 miles) from the city center, 13 kilometers (8 miles) from Chengdu Railway Station and 18 kilometers (11 miles) from Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport. Chengdu East Railway Station Chengdu South Railway Station is the youngest among the three major train stations. It is 10 kilometers (6 miles) from the city center, 13 kilometers (8 miles) from Chengdu Railway Station and 23 kilometers (14 miles) from Chengdu Shuangliu Airport. Chengdu East Railway Station mainly serves Chengdu–Chongqing intercity trains, Chengdu–Guiyang high-speed trains and Xi'an–Chengdu high-speed trains.
