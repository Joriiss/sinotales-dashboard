Title: 4-Day Club Med Yabuli Skiing Vacation with Airport Transfer
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/harbin/tours/club-med-yabuli-ski-vacation-airport-transfer.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Holiday at China's Largest Ski Resort Club Med Yabuli is the perfect destination for couples or families with kids who want a relaxing skiing holiday. Enjoy the ultimate in winter leisure, staying at the top of an ancestral mountain. This all-inclusive package includes lodging, dining, skiing, use of facilities, and the rich range of activities the resort offers. The first Club Med Village in China, Club Med Yabuli is well established and well prepared to offer a fun-packed, memorable holiday. Enjoy worry-free private airport transfers and easily book Club Med at a competitive price with us! Skiing and Snowboarding Freely with Professional Guidance Enjoy direct access to the thrill ski champion slopes with professional guidance from ski instructors trained by a French ski school. The daily free skiing/snowboarding lessons are also suitable for kids and children. Feel the Fun and Speed of Flying Trapeze Apart from the winter sports (skiing, snowboarding, Nordic walking), there are a wide range of indoor sports, including a flying trapeze (first in the mountains), bungee trampoline, table tennis, snooker, etc. Excellent Children's Care, Clubs, and Services Club Med Yabuli has three children's clubs catering to children from 2 to 17 years old. The kids' restaurant takes care of kids from 2 to 10 years old. Enjoy a Cheese Fondue with Your Family in a Chinese Ambience Club Med Yabuli offers gourmet full board, with various restaurants, all-day snacks, and an open bar with a selection of spirits, wines, beers, and other drinks. You can taste authentic Chinese flavors, but international cuisine is also available. Unwind in the Warmth with Amazing Mountain Views Admire the awesome mountain vistas while resting in a Canadian bath after a day of activities. Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Harbin Arrival | Private Transfer to Yabuli Upon your arrival arrive in at Harbin Taiping International Airport, our private driver will greet you with a warm welcome, and then transfer you to the Club Med resort (we also offer a tour guide if you ask). After check-in, begin your relaxing winter holiday. Parties are available every night at the resort, or you can enjoy some quiet time at the bar. In our all-inclusive package, you can enjoy: Childcare and children's club facilities Winter sports: ski pass and skiing/snowboarding lessons Indoor leisure activities: Table tennis, Dance lessons, Indoor pool, Lounge lounge and festive evening entertainments, Karaoke, Card games, Mah-Jong Bars and restaurants: Full board throughout the stay, including wine, beer, and or soft drinks with lunch and dinner DAY 2 Yabuli Club Med Skiing Vacation Your skiing vacation is all according to your arrangements. All meals are offered with generous buffets, or you can order à la cart. Attend skiing classes and polish your skiing skills on some of the best ski trails in China. Besides skiing, the resort offers various choices for your leisure time. There are indoor swimming pools, sauna rooms, jacuzzis, gyms, dancing classes, yoga rooms, and more. Try some totally new experiences such as the flying trapeze. In the evening, treat yourself to a soothing spa (at extra cost). DAY 3 Yabuli Club Med Skiing Vacation Continue to freely choose how you spend your time amongst the huge range of leisure options. You could have one more day of skiing/snowboarding or enjoy the indoor sports and activities. DAY 4 Yabuli Departure | Private Airport Transfer Check out of Club Med. Chat about your fun and memorable holiday as we transfer you to Harbin Taiping International Airport for your flight to your next destination.
