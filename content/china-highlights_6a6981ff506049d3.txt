The Pearl River's Embrace This 8-day adventure features a seamless journey of fun and excitement for families, connecting you with various theme parks, the highlights of Macau via the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge, and a unique perspective on the dynamic development of the Guangzhou-Shenzhen city clusters over the past decade. This itinerary is designed to be fully customizable to your needs and interests. Our team is available to provide you with personalised support. Suggested Greater Bay Area Tour Route Day Highlights 1-3 Hong Kong Hong Kong Disneyland, Macao Highlights, HK-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge 4-5 Shenzhen Window of the World, DJI Flagship Store, Driverless Taxi,Drone Delivery, Huaqiangbei Market 6-8 Guangzhou Chimelong Safari Park, Chen Clan Ancestral Hall, Canton Tower Greater Bay Area Tour Map Experience a world of adventure, from the magic of Disneyland to the global landmarks at Window of the World and the wildlife at Chimelong. Macau Discovery By Bridge Experience the fastest and most scenic route to Macau via the incredible Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge. Innovating in China's Silicon Valley Take a driverless taxi and get a drone delivery to experience how innovation is redefining our world. Witness China's future, where innovation takes center stage with the "Eye of Shenzhen" and the vibrant Canton skyline. Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests and schedule. Day 1 Hong Kong Arrival Welcome to Hong Kong! Your journey begins with a warm greeting from your knowledgeable tour guide at the airport or train station, followed by a seamless transfer to the hotel. Take some time to explore the city at your own pace and discover famous sights like the Avenue of Stars, Victoria Harbour, and Victoria Peak. Day 2 Hong Kong | Disneyland Awaits Breakfast At 9 a.m., your driver(without a guide) will be waiting in the hotel lobby to whisk you away to HK Disneyland. Show your QR Code to enter the park, and then the day is yours to explore. Hong Kong Disneyland offers a unique blend of classic Disney stories and local culture. Enjoy the freedom to visit classic attractions, meet beloved Disney characters, ride thrilling roller coasters, and witness spectacular parades and the nighttime fireworks show over Sleeping Beauty Castle. At 9 p.m., your driver will be waiting at the designated meeting point to provide a comfortable transfer back to your hotel. Day 3 Macau | Macau's Highlights by Bridge Breakfast Meet your Hong Kong driver(without a guide) in the hotel lobby at 7:45 a.m. for a comfortable ride to Macau via the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge. Your Macau tour will start after you meet your guide at the border. Wander through Macau's historic center, taking in the magnificent Ruins of St. Paul, the commanding views from Mount Fortress, the preserved Section of the Old Wall, and the traditional Na Tcha Temple. Discover the historic A-Ma Temple, a site of cultural significance, and then head to the Macau Tower (admission at your own expense) for an unforgettable view. Return to the Macau Border Crossing to meet your Hong Kong driver, who will provide a seamless transfer back to your hotel. Day 4 Shenzhen | High-Tech for Life Breakfast Enjoy a private transfer to the Shenzhen hotel(Driver only), where your Shenzhen tour guide will meet you for a seamless city exploration. A comfortable drive to Nashan awaits, during which you'll gain fascinating insights into the region from your guide. Step into the DJI Flagship Store to learn about the company's history and its role in the future of the 'low-altitude economy.' Enjoy a drone operation demonstration, where you'll witness a drone flying and discover how features like obstacle avoidance and automatic tracking make capturing stunning video effortless. Catch a PonyPilot Taxi at Shenzhen Bay Park Metro Station and head to Shenzhen Talent Park. It's a futuristic, driverless ride, offering the unique experience of having just a large screen in front of you instead of a driver. Explore Shenzhen Talent Park and enjoy stunning views of the city's modern skyline, including the iconic 'Spring Bamboo Shoot' building. Experience the latest technology by ordering your takeout for Drone Delivery. Witness the Eye of Shenzhen at Gangxia North Subway Station before taking the subway to the famous Huaqiangbei Electronics Market. You'll have time to explore its endless selection and join a fun challenge to "Find the most beautiful phone case for 2 bucks". Day 5 Shenzhen | The World in One Day Breakfast Meet your guide and driver for a one-day Shenzhen theme parks tour. Travel the globe at Window of the World, where you'll spend the day exploring over 130 miniature replicas of iconic landmarks, from the Eiffel Tower to the Pyramids of Giza. Following lunch at a local restaurant, our next stop is Splendid China Park, where you can take a whirlwind tour of the country by exploring miniature replicas of famous landmarks like the Great Wall and the Forbidden City. Explore the China Folk Culture Villages to experience the unique lifestyles, traditional homes, and lively performances of China's 56 ethnic groups. Day 6 Guangzhou | A Blend of New & Old Breakfast After a private transfer to the station, board a high-speed train to Guangzhou. Your tour guide will be waiting there and will transfer you to explore the city. Appreciate the traditional Guangdong-style architecture and decoration at Chen Clan Ancestral Hall. It is especially known for its display of delicate craftsmanship and folk art, including historical figures, carvings and cast iron. Ascend the Canton Tower to the 108th-floor Star Observation Deck. This highest indoor viewpoint offers unparalleled, breathtaking 360-degree vistas of Guangzhou. Relax and enjoy a seamless transfer to the Chimelong Hotel. Day 7 Guangzhou | Chimelong Safari Park Adventure Breakfast Experience a full day of wildlife and a night of spectacular entertainment at Chimelong at your own pace, all made stress-free with early access and express lanes. Experience a breathtaking ride on the cable car as you glide high above the park, enjoying a stunning panorama of the expansive African plains, where herds of zebras and giraffes roam, and the shimmering Swan Lake below, home to graceful flamingos. Hop aboard a safari train and journey past towering giraffes and majestic tigers, then explore the walking zones for an up-close encounter with giant pandas and adorable koalas. Prepare for a magical night at the Chimelong International Circus! This spectacular show features a fantastic lineup of international performers delivering a visual feast of gravity-defying acrobatics and dazzling stunts. Day 8Guangzhou Departure Breakfast Make the most of your remaining free time to enjoy the park before your guide's arrival for your airport or train station transfer.
