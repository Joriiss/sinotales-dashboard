It attracts people from around the world to join the activities such as picking tea, drinking tea, enjoying a tea ceremony, or just walking in the countryside. China International Qiantang River Tidal Bore Festival This Festival is held on the eighteenth day of the eighth Chinese lunar month, which usually comes during late September or early October. During the festival, tourists from all over the world will arrive to appreciate the great spectacle of the Qiantang River Bore, which is a quite unique natural phenomenon triggered by the pull of the moon. When the tides come in, a wave is produced that surges several meters high. West Lake International Fireworks Show Can you imagine the magnificence of setting off fireworks on the lakeside of the picturesque West Lake? The International Fireworks show, which is held during the middle of October, will provide you the chance to witness the glittering and colorful skyline.
