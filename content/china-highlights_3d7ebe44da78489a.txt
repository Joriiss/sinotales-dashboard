Guilin Classic Li River Day Tour with Yangshuo Countryside Exploration • Highlights: A 4-star Li River cruise, Yangshuo countryside exploration • Best for: First-timers, senior couples, families with children, solo travelers • Duration: 8–9 hours (4–5 hours on the cruise, 1½ hours for biking, 2 hours of transportation) Admire the Guilin Landscape Leisurely on a Li River Cruise To make the most of your day in Guilin, this classic route offers a series of features at a comfortable pace: karst hills, river landscapes, rural vibes, and relaxing activities close to nature. Typically, we suggest taking a 4-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo to fully enjoy the view leisurely, seeing the clear water flowing, and the mountain peaks slowly receding with bamboo groves on the banks. The 4-star cruise that we arranged usually departs from Zhujiang Wharf (20 miles southeast of downtown Guilin) at about 9 AM, and arrives in Yangshuo at about 2 PM. Then you could feel free to explore the countryside around Yangshuo by bike or electric scooter. A Hiking Day Trip to the Longji Rice Terraces • Highlights: Hike through the rice terraces, and visit a local's house • Best for: Active couples, solo travelers, shutterbugs • Duration: About 8 hours (including 2–3 hours for hiking and 4 hours of transportation) The Fall Colors of Longji Rice Terraces To experience the minority culture and admire the agricultural wonder built by smart ancient farmers, you should spend a day in Longji Rice Terraces. Hiking through the magnificent rice terraces, having lunch by visiting a local's house, and learning about the minority ethnic groups. This area could be roughly divided into three areas: Ping'an Village, Dazhai Village, and Ancient Zhuang Village. While you could hike from one village to another as you want, we recommend the route between Ping'an Village and Ancient Zhuang Village. It's a 2-hour hike with tranquil, scenic views, and is easy to complete, which is perfect for first-timers. However, Dazhai Village is more touristy and is usually swarmed with crowds. To ensure your day trip schedule and travel experience without wasting time in lines, it is preferable to choose the hiking route between Ping'an Village and Ancient Zhuang Village. Instead of taking a group tour, we suggest getting a private tour with an English-speaking tour guide. This day tour would maximize your traffic efficiency between Guilin City and Longsheng County, avoid crowds by time-smart arrangement, and you would get a comprehensive explanation from your professional tour guide. You don't need to hurry at all since all the attractions are within Guilin City. This tour is specially crafted for travelers who are looking for relaxation after a long journey, as a last stop and layover, and for travelers to view the Guilin scenery leisurely. Elephant Trunk Hill, Guilin Typically, we would show you around the Reed Flute Cave and Elephant Trunk Hill, as they are landmarks of Guilin and must-sees for a spectacular karst landscape. To discover the traditional Guilin residential lifestyle and culture, we recommend touring the Daxu Ancient Town. You would have an authentic experience strolling around this farmer's market that truly serves the locals for trading. The Night View of Guilin City Landscapes After your day with us, feel free to enjoy some nighttime activities to enrich your day in Guilin City. Take a stroll around the Two Rivers and Four Lakes, which is a park-like scenic area, and take a nighttime cruise here if you are interested. Zhengyang Pedestrian Street could be a paradise for foodies in downtown Guilin, where you could easily find Guilin street food and snacks that suit everyone.
