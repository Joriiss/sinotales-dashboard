Touch Chinese Elegance The soul of Chinese elegance lies in the profound reverence for beauty and art, deeply intertwined with the nation's culture and history. This route, perfect for your first visit to China, allows you to fully immerse in the rich experiences of elegant China. Explore ancient Beijing and Xi'an, home to iconic landmarks like the Forbidden City and the Terracotta Army. Unwind amidst the breathtaking karst landscapes of Guilin, taking in the serene beauty of their rivers, mountains, and Longji rice terraces. Experience the refined lifestyle of Suzhou and Hangzhou, strolling through picturesque gardens and along the tranquil waters of West Lake. Experience the best of China in just 14 days with our expertly curated private tour, ensuring a seamless and unforgettable experience. Moreover, this tour can be customized daily to suit your personal interests. Day 1 Shanghai Arrival Upon landing at Shanghai airport, you'll be greeted by your friendly tour guide in the arrivals hall. Your private car and driver will be waiting to transport you comfortably to your hotel, assisting with your luggage and check-in. Rest assured that your Shanghai journey will commence smoothly and stress-free. Day 2 Shanghai | A Unique Fashion of East and West Breakfast & Lunch Shanghai's colonial legacy combined with Chinese culture has created a unique fusion of East and West, and ancient architecture to modernistic buildings. You will visit the Yuyuan Garden, located in the center of Shanghai's old city! Stroll along the Bund, admiring the architectural treasures that echo Shanghai's golden era. Wander through a hidden alley, indulge in local flavors at a Michelin-starred restaurant, and experience the city's transformation from its historic neighborhoods to the futuristic skyline of Pudong. Explore the city's fascinating history through its towering skyscrapers, each a testament to its rapid growth and innovation. Venture offers a glimpse into authentic Shanghai life. Indulge in delicious local cuisine at a Michelin-starred restaurant. Day 3 Shanghai to Suzhou | Into Folk History Breakfast & Lunch Head to Suzhou by a private transfer. Enjoy a peaceful walk through the Master-of-Nets Garden, which is renowned for its exquisite landscaping, intricate architecture, and serene atmosphere. The overall design of the garden emphasizes the harmony between nature and architecture, creating a tranquil and inspiring space. We will drive you to a local museum, a treasure trove of traditional Chinese folk art that boasts an impressive exhibition space showcasing over 40,000 exquisite items across 60+ galleries. Over 95% of the museum's collection is displayed openly, inviting you to examine and appreciate these artifacts up close and personal. Day 4 Suzhou to Hangzhou | The Tranquil Charm Breakfast & Lunch Take a bullet train from Suzhou to Hangzhou within 1.5 hours. You will first discover the spiritual and cultural treasures of Lingyin temple, one of China's most renowned Buddhist sites. The nearby Feilai Feng (Flying Peak) grottoes are also worth a visit. Then we head to the West Lake to enjoy its serene beauty from a traditional rowing boat. Engage in conversation with your boatman and gain insights into the deep connection between Hangzhou locals and their beloved lake. Step back in time to the century-old Hu Qingyu Hall, a renowned Chinese pharmacy. Marvel at the vast array of medical herbs and ingredients meticulously displayed throughout the shop. Finally, unwind with a delightful Hangzhou-style afternoon tea, savoring the city's unique flavors and cultural heritage. Day 5 Hangzhou | Dragon Well Tea, and Silk Culture Breakfast & Lunch Drive to the place of origin of the best Dragon Well tea – Meijiawu Tea Village. A local tea specialist will accompany you to pick tea leaves in the right way. Then, he will show you the process of stir-frying tea. Afterward, you will learn the art of tea ceremony and the proper way to taste tea. China Silk Museum is another highlight of the day. Housing a vast collection of ancient silk artifacts, the museum showcases a wide range of silk clothing and accessories from different eras. An oil-paper umbrella painting experience concludes the day. You will be guided by a skilled artist who will teach you the techniques and history of oil-paper umbrella painting. Sketch your desired design and paint using traditional brushes, creating a one-of-a-kind souvenir. Day 6 Hangzhou to Guilin | Longji Rice Terraces Breakfast & Lunch An early flight from Hangzhou to Guilin will take you to another land of wonder. In the afternoon, you will visit the Reed Flute Cave, a natural wonder that is filled with a variety of stalactites. Many of the formations are adorned with colorful minerals, adding to their visual appeal. Day 7 Guilin | Longji Rice Terraces Breakfast & Lunch Your guide and driver are ready to escort you to the Longji Rice Terraces which are built into the hillsides and look like great chains or ribbons as they wind around the contours in tiered bands. The Chinese ethnic minorities, Zhuang and Yao people, have lived there in wooden houses for generations. You will have a chance to interact with them and have a glimpse of their lifestyle. Join a friendly minority family and learn how to prepare bamboo-tube-rice, a local delicacy in Longji. As you cook, savor the fresh bamboo aroma that infuses the rice. Afterward, explore the family's charming wooden home and gain insights into the lifestyle of ethnic people. You would return to Guilin in the late afternoon for accommodation. Day 8 Guilin to Yangshuo | Li River Cruise Breakfast & Lunch Taking a four-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo offers unforgettable views of towering limestone peaks, lush bamboo groves, and vibrant rice paddies. Keep your eyes peeled for hardworking farmers, water buffaloes, and fishermen navigating the river in traditional bamboo rafts. After arriving in Yangshuo, you will be led on a memorable countryside exploration by bicycle with your expert guide. Immerse yourself in the enchanting beauty of Yangshuo's countryside, characterized by its distinctive, sky-reaching hills. Day 9 Yangshuo to Xi'an | Step into the History Breakfast & Lunch You will be transferred to Guilin airport to catch the flight to Xi'an. Upon arrival, let's start with the Big Wild Goose Pagoda and the City Wall. The pagoda is a renowned Buddhist pagoda dating back to the Tang Dynasty in the 7th century. It houses a collection of Buddhist artifacts and sculptures, providing insights into the history and practices of Buddhism in China. Then explore Xi'an's iconic Ancient City Wall. Stroll along its historic ramparts, or for a more active experience, rent a bicycle and enjoy panoramic views of the city as you pedal around the wall. Day 10 Xi'an | The Terracotta Warriors Breakfast & Lunch Today, journey back in time to witness the awe-inspiring Terracotta Warriors, an ancient army buried 2,200 years ago. This iconic Xi'an attraction is a must-see. Your guide will share fascinating insights and captivating stories about this world wonder that you won't find online. Afterward, immerse yourself in the art of terracotta creation at a local workshop, where you can craft your own miniature warrior. Are you interested in wearing Hanfu? Hanfu is known for its intricate designs, vibrant colors, and flowing silhouettes. It has become a popular fashion trend in China. You will also have the opportunity to try on Hanfu and capture memorable pictures. Day 11 Xi'an to Beijing | The Summer Palace Breakfast & Lunch Embark on a scenic high-speed train journey from Xi'an to Beijing, covering over 1,000 km in approximately four hours. Witness the breathtaking landscapes and marvel at China's impressive railway development as you glide through the countryside. Upon arrival, your first visit is to the beautiful Summer Palace. Stroll through the luxurious gardens once frequented by the imperial family. Discover a variety of pavilions, long corridors, and serene landscapes that capture the essence of traditional Chinese architecture and design. Day 12 Beijing | The Great Wall Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Start your tour at the Temple of Heaven, a historic site where emperors once sought blessings for bountiful harvests. Today, this serene location has transformed into a popular spot for locals to engage in morning exercises. You'll often see locals practicing Tai Chi or doing sports here. Next, drive to the Mutianyu Great Wall, a less-crowded section located approximately 2.5 hours from downtown Beijing. Take a cable car to a convenient starting point, avoiding the steepest parts of the Wall. From there, hike along the historic ramparts, passing through watchtowers and enjoying breathtaking views of the surrounding hills and mountains. Day 13 Beijing | The Forbidden City Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Step back in time and explore the grandeur of the Forbidden City. While most visitors spend two hours following the central axis, our expert guide will take you on a more personalized journey, leading you through the imperial residences and revealing hidden gems that you would never discover on your own. Avoid the crowds and noise of other tour groups as your guide escorts you to off-the-beaten-path locations within the City. With us, you will be treated to a unique afternoon tea in an ancient imperial court. Indulge in fine coffee, sweet soups, and Chinese desserts. After experiencing the royal lifestyle, let's explore the everyday lives of the locals in Hutong. Step into the heart of old Beijing's hutong culture by visiting a local family. Engage in conversation with your host, gaining valuable insights into their daily life. A special dinner will be arranged tonight to end your tour on a culinary high. Indulge in authentic Beijing cuisine with a delectable Peking duck dinner at a renowned courtyard restaurant. Hope you like it! Day 14 Beijing Departure Enjoy a seamless transfer to the airport for your departing flight. Your guide will accompany you through the check-in process, ensuring a hassle-free departure experience. As you pass through security, bid farewell to your knowledgeable guide and embark on your journey home.
