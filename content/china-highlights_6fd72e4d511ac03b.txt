Step into Fujian's World Heritage Wonders This 5-day tour takes you on a journey through Fujian's most iconic treasures, highlighting three UNESCO World Heritage sites. It invites you to step off the beaten path to wander Gulangyu's quiet lanes, where colonial villas whisper stories of the past; enter Mulan's tulou home to share the warmth of Hakka family life; and finally climb Mount Wuyi's soaring peaks, follow ancient Taoist trails, and drift along the Nine Twists Stream beneath cliffs and emerald forests. Immerse yourself in Fujian's living heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and unforgettable moments. This itinerary is fully customizable — from the rhythm of your days to the experiences you choose. Our team ensures every detail fits your style, making the journey truly your own. Lose Yourself in Timeless Island Charm Step off the usual path to stroll through tranquil gardens, follow scenic trails, climb to stunning viewpoints, and let piano melodies on the sea breeze lead you through Gulangyu's living history. Discover Tulou Fortresses that Guard Centuries of Stories Explore the Nanjing Tulou Clusters — Tianluokeng and Hekeng — where round and square earthen fortresses stand tall. Step into a Hakka home, sip local tea, and hear family stories that reveal their living culture. Scale Wuyi's Peaks, Roam Its Valleys Climb rugged peaks, and wander through tranquil valleys, breathe in the crisp mountain air, and marvel at the vibrant landscapes carved by time. Let the scenery refresh your spirit. Glide on Bamboo through Emerald Waters Drift along the winding Nine Twists Stream on a bamboo raft, surrounded by emerald waters, lush hills, and rising red cliffs. Day 2 Xiamen and Gulangyu Tour You will spend a day exploring Xiamen — one of China's most leisurely cities — and Gulangyu Island. You will start with South Putuo Temple, famous for its Tang Dynasty origins, Buddhist relics, and views from Wulao Peak of Xiamen University and the port. Then you will enjoy a seafood lunch near Gulangyu Island, and visit Hulishan Fortress, built in 1891 with Ming-Qing architectural styles, home to the world's largest and smallest cannons, and historic military structures. You will take a 10-minute ferry to Gulangyu Island, stroll among its European-style buildings, browse boutiques for souvenirs, and visit Sunlight Rock Park and Shuzhuang Garden, using electric carts to explore comfortably. . Day 3 Tianluokeng Hekeng Tulou Clusters After breakfast in Xiamen, you will head to Tianluokeng Tulou in Nanjing County. Set in Shangban Village, this famous cluster of four round and one rectangular earthen buildings is among the most magnificent and best-preserved Hakka castles, and the village is honored as a Famous Historic Cultural Village of China. Next, you'll head to the Hekeng Tulou Cluster, known as Mulan's hometown in the movie Mulan. Here you can step into her earthen home, wander the upstairs rooms usually closed to visitors, sip local tea, and listen to family stories — a warm and authentic taste of Hakka life. Day 4 Climb Tianyou Peak for Majestic Mountain Views Our guide will pick you up from your hotel and transfer you to Xiamen Railway Station. A high-speed train takes about 3 hours to reach Wuyishan City, followed by a 20-minute drive to the Wuyi Mountain Scenic Area. The scenery of Wuyi Mountain is breathtaking. On your first day, you'll hike up Tianyou Peak for stunning views, and discover its rich Taoist heritage — Wuyi has long been a sacred Taoist site, with over 1,800 years of history. Day 5 Wuyishan Departure After breakfast, you will take a bamboo raft cruise along Jiuqu Stream, drifting past towering peaks, lush greenery, blue skies, and crystal-clear waters. The striking Danxia landform along the river are a true highlight of Mount Wuyi. As you glide downstream, you will also spot ancient stone inscriptions, left by poets and painters who once came here seeking inspiration. You will see the hanging coffins of Wuyi Mountains, dating back over 3,000 years — the oldest in China. Perched high on the cliffs, their placement remains a mystery, and they stand as a striking symbol of ancient Fujian culture. Song Ancient Street and Wuyi Palace are near the terminal of Jiuqu Stream. Your guide will confirm your return trip, escort you to the airport, help you with check-in, and ensure everything goes smoothly before saying goodbye.
