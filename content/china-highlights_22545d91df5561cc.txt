1. Ba Jixiang Sushi Chancha Restaurant The restaurant is embodied with a feeling of freshness, naturalness, cleanness and healthiness. Many dishes imitating meat and fish, which are of a striking resemblance, are cooked by highly skilled chefs. There are several hundred dishes which are made from vegetables, fruit, fungi and mushrooms. Many purely vegetarian desserts are also worthy of being recommended. In addition, under the simple but elegant environment there, top-grade tea is offered; the taste being pure, fragrant and pleasant. • Chinese name: 八吉祥素食禅茶馆 Ba Jixiang Sushi Chancha Guan • Kindly reminder: The "meat" and "fish" are all made from vegetarian ingredients. • Address: 160 Kaifusi Road, Kaifu District 开福区开福寺路160号 • Tel: 0731-82348463 • Opening hours: 10am – 11pm • Transportation: Take Bus 111, 143 (up direction),143 (down direction),149, 348, 368, 406 or 501 and get off at Yuequn Street (开福寺). 2. Auspicious Vegetarian Restaurant The dishes may be a bit expensive to many people, but they are pretty tasty and healthy. Much of the food imitates meat or fish, but actually it is made from bean products and so forth. Besides, an atmosphere of Buddhism fills the restaurant, making people feel quiet and peaceful.
