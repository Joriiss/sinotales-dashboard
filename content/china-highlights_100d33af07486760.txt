China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select China Train Ticket Booking China Train Int’l Train from China Please Select • China Train • Int’l Train from China There are both high-speed trains and normal-speed trains traveling between Beijing and Guilin. Two pairs of high-speed G trains operate at Beijing West and Guilin North railway Stations, with the journey duration of about 8-11 hours. They depart early in the morning and arrive at night. Second class seat is US$120 per ticket. Besides, there are five pairs of overnight trains run daily on this route. They operate at Beijing West and Guilin/Guilin North railway station, with the travel time of 19 to 28 hours. A soft sleeper ticket costs US$92. Transportation From Guilin North Railway Station Guilin North is a high-speed train station mainly serve Guangzhou-Guilin and Guiyang-Guilin high-speed trains. It is further away from the city center the Guilin Station. But passengers can get from Guilin North to downtown or major scenic spots by buses easily. Sometimes a flight ticket fare is even cheaper than a second class ticket of a high-speed train for the Beijing - Guilin journey. Taking an overnight train is the cheapest way of traveling between these two cities, and can also save you a night's hotel bill.
