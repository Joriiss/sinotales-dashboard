Title: Is November a Good Time to Visit Chengdu? (Travel Tips)

Weather: November is a bit chilly in Chengdu. The average daily high temperature is around 16 °C (61 °F), and the lowest temperature is about 10 °C (50 °F). It is generally cloudy or foggy at this time of year, but it rains very little (8 mm total). It won't feel dry because of the foggy climate. The sun rarely shines, so you may feel a bit cold. Snow is rare in downtown Chengdu, but you may see snowfall in nearby areas like Jiuzhaigou and Mount Emei. Clothing: long pants, a shirt or blouse, and a coat or a light jacket. If you plan to visit colder mountain areas, pack some sweaters and a warm, heavy jacket as well. Is November a Good Time to Visit Chengdu? Chengdu in November can be a bit damp and cold, so if you're from tropical countries like Singapore or Malaysia, it might take some time to get used to. But November isn't as cold as winter, so if you can handle a mild chill, this month is a good time to visit Chengdu. You Can Enjoy a Shoulder Season in Chengdu November is a shoulder season in Chengdu. Some visitors will choose to come during this time to avoid peak season crowds, so it's neither too busy nor too empty. When visiting popular spots like Kuanzhai Alley, Jinli Old Street, and Chengdu Panda Base, you may still feel a bit busy, but not crowded, and you don't need to queue. Most other attractions tend to have fewer visitors, so you can explore Chengdu at a relaxed pace. Travel costs in November are also more budget-friendly. You can expect hotel and airfare prices to be at normal or slightly reduced rates, as well as discounted off-season tickets. Jinli Old Street is much quieter in November You Can Admire the Most Beautiful Autumn Scenery In November, Chengdu is full of beautiful autumn colors, like chrysanthemums and yellow ginkgo, all around the city. You can enjoy the fall foliage in many must-visit or popular spots at the same time, so you don't have to go out of your way to see them elsewhere. November is also a season when you can catch both colorful autumn leaves and early snow. But you'll also enjoy some unique seasonal sights not usually seen in October. People's Park: Chrysanthemum Exhibition and Autumn Walks Chrysanthemum exhibition Every November, Chengdu's People's Park hosts a month-long Chrysanthemum Exhibition, where you can appreciate over 500 varieties of chrysanthemums and other flowers freely. There are also gradually turning yellow ginkgo trees and koelreuteria trees in the park to enjoy. You can take a leisurely walk or sip a tea like a local, while soaking in the peaceful autumn views. At this time, People's Park sees fewer tourists, with mostly locals around, so you can enjoy a lively yet peaceful atmosphere. Dujiangyan: Crystal-Clear Waters and Golden Ginkgo Dujiangyan with gingko trees The river of Dujiangyan Irrigation System at this time is clear blue-green, unlike the muddy yellow of the rainy season, and not yet in the low-water period, so you'll have a more pleasant viewing experience and photos, especially for a panoramic view of this grand program. The cool weather and few visitors also ensure you have a more comfortable walking experience. You can walk along the river to admire the golden gingko and red maples, or just sit down and sip tea at a riverside teahouse while enjoying the views. Jiuzhaigou and Bipenggou: Autumn Leaves and Early Snow Jiuzhaigou in early November In early November 1-5, it's still the golden time for Jiuzhaigou's autumn leaves. Although the crowds are heavy (over 30,000 visitors daily), you can catch the tail end of the most beautiful time of Jiuzhaigou and snap the most fabulous photos. From the 5th to 10th, some trees have started to wither, but you can still see some autumn colors, though the area remains busy. By mid to late November, many trees have lost their leaves, but the lakes are still brimming with water, so it won't affect you to admire the water views, and you may luckily meet the snowfall. Visitors also drop to 10,000+ daily, and you can enjoy off-season tickets at less than half the usual price, helping you save on your trip.
