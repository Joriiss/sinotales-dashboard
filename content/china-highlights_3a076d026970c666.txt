Title: Yan’an Attractions in Shaanxi Province, Must-See Attraction in Yan’an

Yan'an is not a popular tourist destination in China, but is an important place in contemporary Chinese history, with numerous sites that memorialize the Chinese revolution in the 1940s. The four attractions listed below are natural scenic spots in Yan'an. Baota Hill Overview Baota Hill (Pagoda Hill) gained its name from a 44-meter (144-feet) nine-story pagoda standing at its summit. The Pagoda is the symbol of Yan'an and you will see it as you arrive in Yan'an.
