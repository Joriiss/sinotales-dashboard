Title: 7-Day Shanghai, Suzhou, Hangzhou and Hengdian Cinematic Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/hangzhou/tours/cinematic-tour.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Discover China's Hollywood beyond the Screen Embark on an incredible 7-day cinematic tour through Eastern China! Start in Shanghai, where modern marvels meet historic charm. Explore the classical gardens and canals of Suzhou, then discover the breathtaking beauty of Hangzhou and its iconic West Lake. Conclude your journey at Hengdian World Studios, the world's largest film studio, for a taste of cinematic magic. This itinerary promises a captivating blend of history, culture, and stunning landscapes. Play Dress Up with Ancient Costumes and Take Loads of Instagrammable Photos You can rent a costume from the Han, Tang, or Qing dynasty to take amazing photos and imagine yourself in ancient China. Always Arrive on Time for the Must-see Shows across Different Studios. There are at least four shows in different studios on at different times every day. With the help of your guide, you will always be at the right place at the right time. Witness the Fascinating Interplay between Traditional Heritage and Modern Advancements. Experience Shanghai's captivating blend of old and new cultures and architectures in Yu Garden, City God Temple, and the Bund. Capture the Bund's Illuminated Skyline from the Unique Vantage Point of a Night Cruise. As the sun sets and the city lights come alive, you'll witness the dramatic contrast between the historic Bund on the west bank and the futuristic skyscrapers on the east bank. Step into a Painting and Breathing Water Wonderland with a Dozen Rivers and Countless Canals. These waterways are lined with traditional white-walled homes with black-tiled roofs, creating a timeless atmosphere. Savor Fragrant West Lake Dragon Well Tea amidst the Beautiful Village Scenery. Immerse yourself in the refreshing and tea-infused air athe tea village, and consider participating in tea picking directly. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Shanghai Arrival Welcome to China! Your tour guide will be at the airport, ready to whisk you away to your hotel. We can't wait for you to begin your Shanghai adventure! Tomorrow, we'll dive into an exciting exploration of both modern and classic China. DAY 2 Shanghai | City Essence Collection Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Yu Garden in Shanghai is a vast and beautifully designed classical Chinese garden, offering a tranquil escape from the bustling city. Throughout the garden, you'll find a harmonious blend of decorative halls, elaborate pavilions, glittering ponds, archways, and intricate carvings. Next to the Yu Garden, you will visit the City God Temple, which is a significant Taoist temple with a history of nearly 600 years. You can experience a strong religious atmosphere and the unique charm of Chinese traditional culture. Nanjing Road in Shanghai is a vibrant, iconic thoroughfare that pulses with the city's commercial energy and historical charm. Stretching approximately 5.5 kilometers from The Bund in the east to Jing'an Temple in the west, it's often hailed as "China's No. 1 Commercial Street." You will not miss the chance to take a picture with the latest landmark of Louis Vuitton, "The Louis" ship-shaped structure. This one-of-a-kind architecture blends retail, art, and gastronomy, serving as a connection to its spirit of travel and exploration. As Shanghai's most iconic spot, the Bund is perpetually bustling. However, with your guide, you'll easily navigate the crowds and never worry about getting lost. After dinner, let's take a night cruise to enjoy the unparalleled views of Shanghai's iconic skyline, including the Oriental Pearl TV Tower, the Shanghai World Financial Center, the Historic Bund, and the Yangpu Bridge. Your guide will tell you the stories behind those buildings. DAY 3 Suzhou | A Tapestry of Water and Stone Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Get ready to explore Suzhou today! We'll take a car, and in less than 1.5 hours, you'll be enjoying a unique city atmosphere that's quite a contrast to Shanghai. While smaller than the expansive Humble Administrator's Garden, the Master-of-Nets Garden offers a more intimate and delicate charm. This miniature masterpiece packs a world of beauty and tranquility into its modest space, perfectly capturing the essence of a classic Chinese landscape garden. Pingjiang Road is a vibrant, living testament to Suzhou's rich history. This 1,600-meter-long historic street, once a bustling thoroughfare in the Song Dynasty, remains a hub of activity today. Here you'll find lively shops, delicious local eateries, and picturesque canals. For a unique view of Suzhou's culture and history, consider a leisurely rickshaw ride along the waterway. With a history spanning over a thousand years, Tongli Water Town is a captivating network of canals and ancient architecture. Fifteen rivers divide the town into seven islands, all connected by 49 historic bridges. Visitors can explore hundreds of gardens, temples, cottages, and former residences of prominent figures, many dating back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. After dinner, you'll hop on a high-speed train to Hangzhou, a quick 50-minute journey. DAY 4 Hangzhou | Natural Serenity Breakfast & Lunch The classic viewpoints of the West Lake have inspired poets and artists for centuries. It offers divers perspectives of the lake's beauty throughout the seasons. A boat trip is a fantastic way to experience its tranquility and beauty from the water. Lingyin Temple is located northwest of West Lake, nestled amidst lush hills and grottoes, it offers a serene and historically rich experience. To experience authentic Longjing Tea culture, you will visit the Meijiawu Tea Plantation, one of the most important and largest production bases in Hangzhou. Taste freshly brewed Longjing Tea and learn about the unique "Four characteristics" of Longjing. The Hu Qingyu Hall Chinese Traditional Herbal Medicine Museum at Qinghefang Old Street is a fascinating and unique attraction that offers a deep dive into the world of traditional Chinese Medicine. It's housed in the beautifully preserved historic buildings of the famous Hu Qingyu Tang Pharmacy. After visit the museum, you will visit the Hefang Old Street for more cultural exploration. DAY 5 Hengdian | A Journey through Ancient Dynasties Breakfast & Lunch Drive for 2.5 hours from Hangzhou to Hengdian and start a journey through Chinese films. Modeled after the fabled Xianyang Palace of the Qin Dynasty, this grand complex has been the backdrop for numerous historical blockbusters like "Hero" and "The Mummy: Tomb of the Dragon Emperor." Qing Ming Shang He Tu (Riverside Scene at Pure Brightness) brings to life a famous historical painting, showcasing the bustling daily life and architecture of the Northern Song Dynasty. Each studio offers a wide array of spectacular shows, including imperial processions, martial arts performances, theatrical productions, special effects shows, and traditional Chinese Opera Performances. Follow your guide so you don't miss the shows. DAY 6 Hengdian | A Journey through Film Breakfast & Lunch Wander through incredibly detailed, full-scale replicas of ancient Chinese cities and palaces. Visit the Palace of Ming and Qing Dynasties, a replica of Beijing's Forbidden City. Many palace dramas are filmed here. The opportunity to dress up historical attire is irresistible. Choosing between the elegant flowing robes of the Han Dynasty, the vibrant and opulent styles of the Tang, or the intricate garments of the Qing, each costume promised a unique immersion. The Guangzhou Street & Hong Kong Street will transport you to the 19th and early 20th centuries with meticulously recreated streets, perfect for experiencing old Guangzhou and Hong Kong's retro charm. Beyond the static beauty, the experience is enriched by the feeling of being on a living film set, with the occasional sight of film crews and actors in period costumes further blurring the lines between past and present, making for a truly memorable and visually stunning visit. DAY 7 Hengdian to Shanghai | Departure Breakfast Enjoy a seamless 2.5-hour high-speed train ride from Hengdian straight to Shanghai. Upon arrival, your tour guide will take care of your transfer from the train station to the airport, providing full assistance to ensure a timely and stress-free boarding experience for your flight. Over seven days, this itinerary has presented a captivating blend of experiences: from the bustling modernity of Shanghai to the tranquil classical gardens of Suzhou, the picturesque lakes and mountains of Hangzhou, and the unique cinematic world of Hengdian.
