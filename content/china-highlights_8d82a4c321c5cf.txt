Chongqing is a vibrant city full of exciting attractions. Known as the "Mountain City," it offers stunning views with its unique hilly landscape. Located alongside the upper Yangtze River in Southwest China, this sprawling metropolis is the launching point for scenic relaxing cruise down the Yangtze River through Three Gorges. It's also home to the impressive Dazu Rock Carvings, a UNESCO World Heritage site. And Wulong Karst National Geopark boasts stunning limestone landforms and scenery. Don't forget to take the unique monorail that winds through this 8D magical city, offering fantastic views of the Hongya Cave, Chaotianmen bridge, Eling park and more.
