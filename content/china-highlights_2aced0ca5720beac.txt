Weather: March is cool, generally comfortable, and fairly dry. The wet weather starts in April. The average daily high temperature is 21 °C (70 °F), and the average low temperature is around 16 °C (61 °F). Most days are pleasantly sunny, and the nighttime temperatures are mild. Macau gets about 83 mm of rain in March, and it rains about 12 days each March. Clothing: Tourists should pack light jackets or sweaters, T-shirts, and an umbrella. Take advantage of the fine spring weather to go to outdoor attractions such as the ruins of St. Paul Cathedral and other historic sites. These sites require some walking outdoors to see them, and the cool spring weather is enjoyable. Visit Historic Center of Macau attractions: There are about 30 sites designated for historic preservation including Monte Fort, Senado Square, and other Portuguese sites. You can explore old streets, relax in the squares, visit the forts, stroll around in the parks and gardens, and appreciate the history and the contrast of Chinese and Iberian architecture. See the Ruins of St. Paul's Cathedral: What remains is just a facade that is 27 meters high and almost 400 years old that was built by Japanese craftsmen, but many tourists find it fascinating. The Macao Museum nearby is a fine place to learn a lot about the region's history and culture. March is in the peak travel season. A lot of tourists crowd the attractions.
