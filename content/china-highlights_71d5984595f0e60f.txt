Title: Hong Kong Weather in July (Temperature, Clothing and Travel Tips)

July is the midsummer period in Hong Kong. The most sunshine occurs during this month and it's generally very hot and humid with occasional showers and typhoons. July is the hottest month in Hong Kong. During the daytime, the temperature often exceeds 30°C (86°F). There are periods of sunny days lasting for 1 to 2 weeks or even more in July. The average low and high temperatures are 24 °C (75 °F) and 34 °C (93 °F). July is one of the wettest months in Hong Kong. There are about 17 rainy days and 376 mm (15 inches) of precipitation in Hong Kong in July. It rains a lot but it's concentrated in heavy showers and thunderstorms, especially in the mornings. What to Wear and Prepare in Hong Kong in July Summer clothes are recommended: Summer clothes, such as a T-shirt and skirt, are suitable for the hot weather. You can enjoy traveling lightly in July. Rainwear is helpful: As there are sunny and rainy days in July, an umbrella is helpful in both types of weather. A pair of sandals is useful on rainy days.
