Title: 6-Day Chengdu Educational Tour with the Leshan Buddha
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/chengdu-educational-tour.htm
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Explore the Secrets of Shu Culture Chengdu is a city with a rich history and profound cultural heritage. As well as exploring the area's ancient Shu culture, students will get up close to the adorable pandas and savor delicious Sichuan cuisine. Our exploration of culture goes beyond mere surface visits, but deeply immerses students of all ages into the local community to further understand its essence. For example, we engage with local residents in People's Park and learn about traditional opera from backstage staff at a theater. Observe Closely as a Giant Panda Quietly Enjoys His/Her Meal Unlike pandas in other zoos that are kept in cramped spaces, the pandas at Dujiangyan Panda Base live in spacious semi-open gardens. Visitors can observe these adorable creatures eating and playing more clearly and up-close. Learn the Wisdom of Ancient Water Conservancy Engineering Dujiangyan Irrigation System is a remarkable ancient engineering marvel that dates back to the 3rd century BCE. The system operates on the principle of gravity, channeling river water into irrigation canals without the need for pumps. Explore the Ancient Shu Civilization Gain a captivating insight into the mysterious and sophisticated ancient Shu civilization at Sanxingdui Museum. Join a course selected from bronze casting, ancient methods of jade crafting, pattern rubbing, or simulated archaeology. Cooking Sichuan Dishes at Sichuan Cuisine Museum A professional teacher will teach you to how to cook three typical Sichuan dishes, so that you will gain firsthand experience of Sichuan cuisine cutting skills, control of heat, cooking processes, and some secret tips to food presentation. Go Backstage at a Sichuan Opera You will not only appreciate the unique techniques of Sichuan Opera, such as face-changing (bianlian), fire-spitting, and acrobatics, but also go backstage to explore the costumes and face painting. Take a Cruise for a Perfect View of the Leshan Giant Buddha Sit onboard a Min River cruise for an amazing outlook on the Leshan Giant Buddha. You will see the full splendor of the giant Buddha from various angles. Observing the Giant Buddha from the right distance is even more fulfilling. Your preferences design the real journey. We're ready to create a wonderful school trip to Xi'an for you. Day 1: Arrive in Chengdu Welcome to China! Your guide, who is experienced in leading student groups, will meet you in the arrivals hall at the airport. Your group will be transferred to your hotel on our comfortable bus. Day 2: Chengdu | Pandas and Ancient Engineering Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Start your day early to maximize your visit. It takes about an hour to reach Dujiangyan Panda Base, which is less crowded than the Chengdu panda base and allows for closer observation of giant pandas in their natural habitats. Learn about panda reproduction, nurturing, genetics, and biodiversity conservation. Enjoy a local Sichuan meal at a nearby restaurant. Try dishes like Kung Pao chicken or mapo tofu. Afternoon: It's a short drive (about 15–20 minutes) from the Panda Base to the Irrigation System. The system dates back to 256 BC and is still in use today! Learn about its historical significance and engineering marvels. Day 3: Chengdu | Ancient Shu Civilization Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Explore the ancient Sanxingdui civilization, active approximately 3,000 years ago in the Bronze Age. The Sanxingdui museum exhibits a wide array of artifacts such as bronze masks, statues, gold pieces, and jade objects. Of particular interest are the strikingly-large bronze masks with intricate designs, showcasing distinct artistic styles not seen in other ancient Chinese societies. Afternoon: Enroll on a course at the Ancient Shu Civilization Exploration Base. Choose from four available courses including bronze casting, ancient jade crafting techniques, pattern rubbing, and simulated archaeology, tailored to your group's interests and daily availability. Day 4: Chengdu | Sichuan Cuisine and Art Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: Chengdu is a culinary paradise renowned for its spicy and flavorful Sichuan cuisine. Students will learn to cook Sichuan dishes at the Sichuan Cuisine Museum. First, they will visit the Classic Collection Hall to explore the history of Sichuan cuisine. Then, a professional guide will teach them to prepare three typical dishes. Finally, they can taste their creations and take home the recipes' "secrets". Afternoon: Relax with a cup of tea at Chengdu People's Park, the city's most popular destination. There, locals engage in traditional activities like tai chi, mahjong, dancing, and playing musical instruments. Evenning: Experience the local art from of Sichuan Opera. Students will not only watch an opera show, but also have the chance to try on opera makeup and costumes. Day 5: Leshan | Leshan Buddha Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Morning: You'll need about 2 hours to reach Leshan city. The Leshan Giant Buddha has been the largest stone-carved sitting Buddha in the world since 803 CE. It is fantastic to cruise by to get an overview of the Leshan Giant Buddha from a suitable perspective. From the water, you can see the full scale of the Buddha, along with the surrounding cliffs and lush landscapes. Afternoon: Then, climb up to Leshan Giant Buddha Park for an up-close view of the Buddha. Several platforms provide varying perspectives, while historical and cultural sites like Wuyou Temple and Lingyun Temple enhance the experience. Day 6: Chengdu | Departure Breakfast It's time to say goodbye to Chengdu! At the designated time, you'll be picked up from your hotel and transferred to the airport for your departure home.
