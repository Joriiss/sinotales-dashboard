Zhongwei City is located in the west of Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region. It is only 2 hours by plane from Beijing. Zhongwei was once said to have had no north gate, simply because there was nothing more to the north. Its advantageous and strategic location has endowed Zhongwei with its scenic features: vast deserts, green hills, the Yellow River, and oases. At the same time, tourists are able to enjoy various entertaining activities there, such as sand slides and camel rides. Thanks to the Yellow River, Zhongwei is blessed with rich agricultural land. The city is also well-known as "Jiangnan (the Yangtze River Delta) in the desert". • Highlights of Zhongwei • The Best Time to Visit Zhongwei • How to Plan a Tour to Zhongwei or Ningxia • Recommended Zhongwei Tours Have Fun in the Vast Desert — Highlights of Zhongwei People come to Zhongwei for one thing usually: to enjoy the desert. Zhongwei provides wonderful entertainments and unique accommodation in the desert. Shapotou Tourism Area — Best for Enjoying Desert Activities Shapotou Tourism Area Shapotou offers a lot of exciting activities including sand sledding, camel trekking, dune buggying, shuttle bus riding, speedboating, sheepskin rafting, glass bridge walking, and more. Bungee jumps and a zip line over the Yellow River are also available. There is a unique experience in Shapotou — planting wheatgrass squares. Wheatgrass squares are China's secret for turning desert into grassland. Thanks to these squares, China has transformed more than 6,000 hectares (23 square miles) of desert into grasslands and forests for the last 60 years. You could work with sand wardens in the desert to make wheatgrass squares and contribute to making our world a better, less desertified place. If you are looking for a desert trek in Shapotou, we can also arrange that for you with a professional guide. The route is about 6 km (3–4 miles) and takes approximately 2 hours. Recommended tour: 3-Day Zhongwei Desert Tour with Winery Experience Jinshahai Scenic Area ('Gold Sand Sea') — Best for Desert Accommodation Experience Desert tents in Jinshahai Scenic Area Jinshahai also offers a lot of exciting activities such as sand sledding, camel trekking, and dune buggying. ATVs (all-terrain vehicles) and go-karting are also available. Jinshahai has a special steam-train-themed hotel. The hotel was transformed from a steam train. It was the first-generation train traveling on the Bao-Lan Railway, China's first desert railway, in 1958. Each carriage is a room. In addition to carriage room, there are wonderful desert tents. In summer, living in the desert will give you an opportunity to see an amazingly starry sky. Besides those two desert locations, there is the Tengger Desert. It is more suitable for backpackers who seek adventure and a wild experience. Each year there is a desert rally there. Suji Yellow River — Experience the Most Beautiful Boutique Hotels in China On the edge of the Yellow River, opposite the Tengger Desert, there is a boutique hotel collection of five famous Chinese hotel brands. They keep the perfect balance of wilderness and luxury. There are art galleries, bookstores, cafes, teahouses, bakeries, restaurants, izakaya (Japan-style bars), swimming pools, hot springs, and more. You will have a very-well-catered-for vacation experience there. The Best Time to Visit Zhongwei April to October is the best time to visit Zhongwei. Zhongwei is in the middle of the temperate zone in an arid climate region and close to Tengger Desert to the northwest. It is often windy, and there is a lack of rain in the area. Zhongwei weather features a late spring, a short summer, and a long winter. Ningxia is famous for its wine production. Early September to early October is the season for picking grapes.
