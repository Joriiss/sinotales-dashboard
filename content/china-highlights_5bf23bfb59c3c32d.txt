Nanning, capital city of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, a provincial-level area in southern China, is renowned for its lush greenery and serves as the venue for the China-ASEAN Expo. For a first visit to Guangxi province, Guilin is a preferable destination over Nanning due to its world-famous attractions and picturesque landscapes. However, if you would like to see more of southern China's natural beauty and rural life, a trip around Nanning could be the perfect fit for you. • 1. Top Places to Visit around Nanning • 2. Top Places to Visit around Nanning 1) Detian Waterfall — the Largest Transnational Waterfall in Asia Experience the awe-inspiring Detian Waterfall, also known as Ban Gioc – Detian Falls, situated on the China-Vietnam border. Explore the waterfall up close by taking a bamboo raft to its base, ascend an overlooking mountain on a 'Magic Carpet' for a panoramic view from the cliff's glass walkway, and enjoy a thrilling descent on its marble slide. Take bamboo rafts for Detian Waterfall viewing 2) Mingshi Scenic Area — Classic Karst Landscape Just an hour's drive from Detian Waterfall lies Mingshi Garden, which is known as "Little Guilin" for its striking karst formations and serene rural scenery. It is recommended to take a bamboo raft down the river and enjoy the enchanting scenery flanking both sides. Spend at least a night there to connect with nature, calmed by the gentle chirping of crickets and surrounded by mountains and streams. 3) Tongling Large Barranca — Deep Blind Valley Tongling Large Barranca is a natural wonder blending tropical rainforests, a waterfall with a drop of up to 188 meters (617 feet), and hidden canyon caves. Take a leisurely hike with a private guide through this oxygen-rich haven, inhaling the fresh air and enjoying the breathtaking gorge landscapes. Tongling Large Barranca 4) Baimo Cave — the Secret of Longevity Baimo Cave is famous for its stunning natural rock formations, unique limestone caves, and beautiful subterranean rivers. What's more, its vast chambers, giant sinkholes, and meandering passages attract tourists and cave explorers from around the world. Don't miss the opportunity to sip the spring water, which is drunk by most of the centenarians in Bama and is said to be able to cure all diseases. 5) Dashiwei Tiankeng Group National Geopark — the Largest Sinkhole Complex in the World Discover the magnificence of Dashiwei Tiankeng Group National Geopark, home to awe-inspiring limestone formations, particularly the colossal tiankengs (sinkholes) that dominate the landscape. These tiankengs are enormous and deep natural sinkholes formed by the collapse of underground caverns, making them a popular destination for geology enthusiasts, adventurers, and nature lovers. Dashiwei Tiankeng Group 2. How to Travel To and Get Around Nanning Travel to Nanning, capital of Guangxi province, offers convenient options for visitors. How Long to Spend on a Round Trip from Nanning If you only want a short trip, we suggest spending at least 3 days on a trip to Detian Waterfall, Tongling Large Barranca, and Mingshi Scenic Area. Typically, we would suggest staying for 2 nights in Mingshi Scenic Area for its relaxing ambiance. However, if you are not so interested in Mingshi Scenic Area, you could skip the half-day there, just go to Detian Waterfall and Tongling's gorge, and then return to Nanning, saving one night's hotel fee and making this a potential weekend trip. To appreciate all the main highlights around Nanning on a more comprehensive journey, you would need 5 days. You would see more awe-inspiring natural wonders at a leisurely pace, including Baimo Cave and Dashiwei Tiankeng Group National Geopark. Karst scenery in Mingshi Scenic Area 4. Best Times to Visit Nanning and Its Surrounding Areas The best times to visit Nanning and its surrounding areas are during its spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) seasons. These periods offer pleasant weather with sunny days, which are perfect for outdoor activities and sightseeing. Although summer (June to August) is the flood season for Detian Waterfall and the scenery is magnificent, we still suggest avoiding this period due to the humid and hot weather. The lack of shade in the Detian Waterfall Scenic Area means long wait times in the sun during the peak summer season. Tongling Large Barranca is hot and very humid in summer due to its blind valley terrain, and even a light hike would become very strenuous. Winter (December to February) is mild and cool for sightseeing, but it is also the dry season, so Detian Waterfall would be less impressive. However, the low season offers advantages, such as fewer crowds and better prices, making it ideal for planning a budget trip. Admire Detian Waterfall on sunny days 6. What to Eat in Nanning and Its Surrounding Areas Nanning cuisine, which is also called Gui cuisine, is famous for its natural and pollution-free ingredients with original flavors. Laoyoufen, which means 'old friend rice noodles' in Chinese, consists of rice noodles topped with a flavorful sauce made from pork, pickled bamboo shoots, peanuts, and various seasonings, and is known for its savory and slightly sour taste. Another local delicacy in the area is snail and duck feet stew. This dish combines the unique textures and flavors of snails and duck feet in a rich broth made with herbs and spices. It is a favorite among locals and visitors for its exotic taste. If you find you don't like certain local cuisine, ask your tour guide for advice, and we could always find something that suits you as there are many less-exotic options. 7.
