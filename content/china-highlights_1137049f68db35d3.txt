Discover the Water Town's Charm Customize your exploration of Shanghai and Suzhou with a private tour. Wander in Shanghai's charming neighborhood with a rich history and a unique blend of French and Chinese influences. Explore a masterpiece of classical Chinese garden design, renowned for its exquisite landscaping and harmonious blend of architecture and nature. With a private tour, you'll have the opportunity to experience these hidden gems and more. Let us handle the details. By booking with us, you'll enjoy a personalized tour experience tailored to your interests and preferences. Our expert guides will ensure a seamless and unforgettable journey. Witness the Exceptional Spatial Arrangement of Chinese Garden Design. The Lingering Garden is divided into four distinct sections, each with its own unique character and charm. It showcases the essence of classical Chinese landscape aesthetics. Relax on a Hand-Rowing Boat in Tongli Water Town. Embark on a boat journey through Tongli's enchanting waterways, a captivating blend of history and everyday life. Ascend the Slopes of Tiger Hill. Explore the ancient temples, gardens, and rock formations that dot the Tiger Hill, and immerse yourself in the rich history and natural beauty of Suzhou. Unveil the Secret Culture of Night Garden in Suzhou. Enjoy a variety of traditional Chinese performances and classical music. Experience the serenity of the Garden at night. Experience the Refined Allure of the Bund. Wander along the iconic Bund, exploring its historic architecture and hidden gems. Your knowledgeable guide will share fascinating stories of the 1920s. Day 2 Shanghai | City's Highlights Breakfast & Lunch After breakfast at hotel, have the In-Depth Bund Culture Discovery tour and trace back to the golden age of the Bund in the 1920s, experience the luxury and elegance of the Bund. If you are a thrill-seeker, don't miss the highest landmark of Shanghai - the Shanghai Tower. Take the fastest elevator in the world, and have a bird's-eye view of the entire city of Shanghai. Walk in the Former French Concession and learn about the colonial past of Shanghai, appreciating the elegant residences. The area is home to numerous other historic buildings, charming cafes, and cultural attractions. It's a great place to explore and experience the unique atmosphere of old Shanghai. Day 3 Shanghai to Suzhou | Suzhou's Unique Charm Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner After the breakfast at hotel, enjoy a comfortable private transfer from Shanghai to Suzhou. The journey takes about 1½ hours. Your first site of Suzhou will be the Lingering Garden, it will satisfy all your imagination of ancient China. In the afternoon you will visit Tiger Hill, which is famous for its bonsai garden. There you will see various of bonsai in different styles, and will have a chance to learn how it made. After dinner, enjoy a night tour of Nets Master Garden. Explore such a lovely garden at night is totally different from during the day. Follow the step of the staff, and you will find amazing live traditional Chinese culture performance hidden in the pavilions. Day 4 Tongli | Back to Shanghai Breakfast & Lunch Today we'll drive 30 minutes to Tongli water town, often praised as the Venice of China. Take a small boat along the canal to see the old town. It's a great place for ancient architecture and traditional culture. Have lunch in a local Tongli restaurant.
