Lijiang's combination of high altitude (2,400 meters / 7,874 feet) and low latitude results in a climate that is characterized by relatively cooler summers and milder winters. The temperature swing is greater on a daily basis than it is on a season-to-season basis, meaning that layered clothing — at least the use of warm outer clothing in the mornings and evenings — is recommended. But like all highland areas that are bordered by mountains, the weather can change several times a day. In fact, there is an apt saying in the north of Yunnan Province that captures this highland weather phenomenon: "in the north of Yunnan Province, you can experience all four seasons in a single day!" • Best Times to Visit Lijiang • Lijiang Temperature by Month • Lijiang in Spring • Lijiang in Summer • Lijiang in Autumn • Lijiang in Winter • Weahther Tips Best Times to Visit Lijiang The best times to visit Lijiang are from late March to May and mid-October to November — you will see a sunny, flowery, and enchanting Lijiang. Its mild temperatures, dryness, sun, and tempting scenery will brighten the cold winter. Lijiang's rainy season begins in June and ends in September, with the heaviest and most recurrent rainfall periods (some locals would swear that they can set their clocks by it) occurring during July and August, meaning that this is hardly the ideal time of year to visit the Lijiang area. There are often heavy rains, which may cause landslides. It is usually cloudy and maybe too foggy to see the snow-capped mountains. It is also more expensive to visit Lijiang in summer. June and September have moderate rainfall and July and August high rainfall. The rainfall keeps daily temperatures mild and closer together at around 14–23°C (57–73°F). Wear summer clothes, such as shirts, shorts, and skirts, but take a light coat or sweater for early morning and evening… and an umbrella. If you visit the mountainous areas, you need to prepare warmer clothes and waterproofs. Sunlight is very strong in summer, wear sun protection such as hats, sunglasses and sun block, and drink lots of water. Lijiang's high altitude makes it mild in summer. Breathing the fresh air and doing exciting/interesting activities in the mountainous area are recommended: • Take a cable car up to the holy Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. • Go bar-hopping in Lijiang Ancient Town • Discover the mysterious Naxi culture by visiting a shaman's house. Feel free to contact us. Autumn — Dry and Clear Autumn is from October to November. It is the most beautiful season, and also has comfortable weather. Dry and clear, with fall-colored trees and late blossoms, rainfall is rare by mid-October. October averages 9–20°C (48–68°F) with low rainfall. November is much drier still, averaging 4–17°C (39–63°F). Temperatures vary greatly between day and night; usually it feels chilly during the night. Bring long-sleeve shirts, light sweaters, and a coat. In October you can see farmers harvesting in the fields. You should try a hike in the golden season. • Go hiking at Tiger Leaping Gorge to check out the power of the water and the towering mountains. • Climb the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain to see the snow scenery. • Enjoy the stunning meadows and lakes in Blue Moon Valley and Spruce Meadow. • Look into the former residence of a Naxi chieftain — Mufu Mansion. Lijiang's rainy season spans from June to September, so if you're planning a summer trip, rain is likely. If you're concerned about the impact of the rainy season, the following tips may help you make a decision: If your schedule is flexible and you prefer good weather, it's best to avoid this period. However, if this is your only available travel time (e.g., during your child's summer holiday), we recommend going, but with adjusted expectations. Outdoor activities may be limited, and cloudy or overcast skies could dull the scenery. Even so, Lijiang's summer remains cool and pleasant, with rain typically being brief. You'll still experience sunny moments during the day.. To ensure an enjoyable trip, here are some tips: • Bring essential gear: Pack a lightweight raincoat or umbrella to ensure sudden rain doesn't disrupt your plans. • Plan alternative activities: Consider indoor options like visiting museums, art galleries, or experiencing traditional crafts as backup plans. • Use weather forecasts: Monitor the weather closely before and during your trip, and try to schedule outdoor activities on days expected to be sunny. • Enjoy the beauty of the rain: The rainy season offers unique photo opportunities—rainbows, wet cobblestone streets, and fresh air can all make for stunning shots. In summary, while the rainy season may pose some challenges, with proper preparation, you can still fully enjoy the unique charm of Lijiang.
