Walk in Fairy Tales of Autumn Located on the border of Sichuan Province, Yunnan Province and the Tibetan Autonomous Region, Daocheng and Yading enjoy the reputation of the last pure lands on this planet. September and October are the best times for a Daocheng and Yading tour. Let's take a dreamlike journey through snowy mountains, glacier lakes, meadows and Tibetan villages. Make a Pilgrimage to the Three Sacred Mountains Discover one of the 24 holy lands of Buddhism. Let the sun on the plateau, snow on the sacred mountain, and the pure blue sky refresh your heart. Run to the Gemstone-Colored Lakes Uncover the 'treasures' of the snow mountains. Be fascinated by the colors of the glacier lakes, which look like the jewels on the necklace of a Goddess. Immerse Yourself in the Culture of Tibetan Buddhism White pagodas, prayer flags and monasteries will tell you the stories of this sacred land of Tibetan Buddhism. Feel the respect and love the Tibetan people have for nature and their homeland. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary.
