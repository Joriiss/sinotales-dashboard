June marks the beginning of summer in Guilin. The weather becomes warmer with average daytime temperatures between 28-32°C. The heat is often accompanied by high humidity, making it feel quite hot and muggy. However, frequent rain showers provide temporary relief. The rainfall typically cools the air by 5-8°C. Nighttime temperatures usually stay around 25°C, with less mugginess compared to the day. June is also the rainy season in Guilin, receiving the highest concentration of rainfall throughout the year. Showers or thunderstorms occur almost every other day, with 5-6 days experiencing heavy rain or storms. Most downpours, however, last less than an hour. Occasionally, the Li River may swell due to heavy rainfall, but these high-water events are short-lived. The floodwaters generally recede within 1-2 days. Will the Rain in June Affect My Trip? Is It Still Worth Visiting? The rainy season does bring some inconveniences. Outdoor activities like hiking, cycling, and trekking may be less suitable due to weather conditions. However, you can still enjoy Guilin's stunning landscapes by taking river cruises or exploring caves. Occasional heavy rain may cause rising water levels or temporary transportation disruptions, though such situations are relatively rare. Based on my over twenty years of living in Guilin, I've only experienced this three or four times. June also has its advantages: • The rain enhances the natural beauty of the mountains and rivers, making them appear even more lush and vibrant. The mist-shrouded karst landscapes create dreamy scenes perfect for great photos. • Additionally, early June coincides with the Dragon Boat Festival. It offers you the unique opportunity to witness exciting dragon boat races on the Li River. You could immerse yourself in rich local traditions - an experience not available in other months. • Compared to May and the busy summer travel season, June sees fewer tourists, allowing for a more relaxed and peaceful visit with better travel value. 5-Day Guilin Family TourEndless Fun in the Countryside 5-Day Guilin Family TourEndless Fun in the Countryside 5-Day Guilin Family TourEndless Fun in the Countryside Recommended Activities in June Given the frequent rain in June, you can flexibly adjust your itinerary to include activities less affected by weather, such as taking a Li River cruise, exploring caves, or experiencing local rural life. Here are some recommended options: 1. Enjoy Coffee Overlooking Elephant Trunk Hill Overlooking Elephant Trunk Hill As the iconic symbol of Guilin, Elephant Trunk Hill is a must-see for most visitors. On rainy June days, there's no need to brave the weather to see it. Instead, head to the rooftop terrace café at The Jolie Vue Boutique Hotel. Here, you can relax under a canopy with a hot coffee and enjoy a misty view of the Li River and Elephant Trunk Hill. The hotel also serves desserts and light snacks, making it an ideal spot to wait out the rain in comfort. 2. Explore the Karst Wonder: Reed Flute Cave With frequent rainfall in June, mountain paths and undeveloped areas can become slippery. We recommend avoiding semi-developed caves like Swallow Cave and opting instead for well-maintained attractions such as the Reed Flute Cave. With paved walkways, professional lighting, and guided tours, you can comfortably explore this "Palace of Natural Art" regardless of the weather. Inside the cave, you'll escape the humid summer heat and enter a cool world with a constant temperature of 20°C. Marvel at stunning stalactites and stalagmites formed over millions of years, enhanced by colorful lights and engaging stories from your guide. Read more about Reed Flute Cave. 3. Take a Li River Cruise During the rainy month of June, taking an air-conditioned cruise on the Li River is the most comfortable way to enjoy the scenery. It is unaffected by the weather. Although the water may not be clear in June, the rain-washed vegetation is especially lush, and the air is fresh. On rainy days, you can enjoy the views through the cruise ship's large windows or step onto the deck with an umbrella. For the best experience, we recommend a four-star luxury cruise for its spacious cabins, comfortable seating, and superior amenities. 4. Watch Lively Dragon Boat Races In early June, Guilin comes alive with the Dragon Boat Festival (June 19th, 2026, or June 9th, 2027). The Li River echoes with drumbeats and cheers as traditional dragon boat races take place. Join the enthusiastic crowds along the banks and immerse yourself in this vibrant cultural experience. The best viewing spots are along the Li River in downtown Guilin, especially near Elephant Trunk Hill and downstream of Jiefang Bridge. Races are also held on the Yulong River in Yangshuo and the Li River near Xingping Ancient Town. For a more authentic experience, visit local villages to witness traditional customs. After the races, be sure to try seasonal treats like zongzi (sticky rice dumplings), qingtuan (green rice balls), and mung bean cakes. 4. Experience Zongzi Making and Oil Tea in Village For a hands-on cultural experience, head to the Xiatang Village in Yangshuo. With arrangements by our guide, you can join local residents to learn how to wrap glutinous rice, mung beans, and cured meat or egg yolk in fresh bamboo leaves to make Guilin's distinctive pyramid-shaped zongzi. As they steam, the air fills with a delightful aroma - a quintessential part of celebrating the festival in China. You can also learn to make traditional oil tea by pounding tea leaves, ginger, and garlic in a special pot. This flavorful and energizing tea is not only a culinary delight but also a wonderful way to connect with Guilin's food culture. What to Wear in Guilin in June When traveling to Guilin in June, prioritize clothing that is cool, breathable, rain-resistant, and sun-protective. Opt for quick-dry T-shirts or lightweight breathable shirts, paired with a sun-protective jacket. For your lower body, choose quick-dry long pants or casual shorts. Be sure to bring non-slip and durable sandals or water shoes - ideal for sudden rain and water activities. Given the frequent rain and strong sunlight in June, rain gear (a compact raincoat + umbrella) and sun protection (sunscreen, sunglasses, sun hat) are essential. It's also wise to carry a light jacket to stay warm in air-conditioned spaces or when temperatures drop after rainfall.
