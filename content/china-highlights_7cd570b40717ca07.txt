Title: 5-Day Chengdu, Leshan and Mount. Emei Tour

Culture and Nature of Chengdu Dive into the heart of culture and nature with this 5-day journey, designed specifically for those eager to explore the must-see attractions of Chengdu and its surroundings. It's noteworthy that Chengdu is among the cities covered by the 10-day visa-free transit policy, offering travel convenience. In this tour, you will encounter adorable pandas, marvel at the majestic statue of the Leshan Giant Buddha, and immerse yourself in the rich Buddhist culture and the breathtaking natural scenery of Mt. Emei. Beyond these key attractions, you can also immerse yourself in local life by visiting a local park and ancient street. China Highlights will create a custom itinerary with you, ensuring a unique and unforgettable experience. Meet the Lovely Pandas in the Panda Breeding and Research Center It is best to go early in the morning to see the pandas feeding at around 9:30am, when they are most active during the day. Be Like a Chengduer in a Local Park Take a seat and sip a cup of tea in a local teahouse, see or join in with line dancing, and see some vibrant scenes that may only happen in Chengdu. Witness the Largest Buddha Statue in the World in Leshan Besides climbing to get a Lilliputian view of the Giant Buddha, you will also go on a cruise to take a look at the huge inscription. Take a Relaxing Walking Trip to Enjoy the Natural Beauty of Mt. Emei Take a walking trip from the Monastery of Ten Thousand Years to the Monkey Eco-Area to enjoy the natural beauty of the sub-tropical forest and waterfall. Travelers Love China Highlights Travelers Love China Highlights Here is a suggested itinerary. We are happy to customize a tour according to your interests. DAY 1 Chengdu Arrival | Airport Transfer Your private driver and guide will be ready for you when you arrive in Chengdu. Enjoy a trouble-free transfer to your hotel. The rest of the day is your own to explore Chengdu. DAY 2 Chengdu | The Panda and Chengdu City Highlights Tour Visit the Panda Breeding and Research Center, about 10 kilometers (six miles) north of central Chengdu. It is best to go early in the morning to see the pandas feeding when they are most active during the day. Lunch is at a local Chinese restaurant. Go to Chengdu People's Park to get a deep breath of fresh air. You can join the local people to experience the bustle and chatter of a local teahouse in this area. You will also see some vibrant scenes that may only happen in Chengdu: desperate parents are posting messages in a marriage market looking for a partner for their unmarried offspring; a local man is enjoying a traditional teahouse ear massage with a metal rod in his ear; Chengduers are playing mahjong everywhere. DAY 3 Chengdu to Leshan | The Giant Buddha Tour You will be driven to Leshan City this morning, which takes about two hours. After a short break and lunch, you will begin your journey to the Giant Buddha. Besides climbing to get a Lilliputian view of the Giant Buddha, you will also go on a cruise to take a look at the huge inscription. After the tour, you will be escorted to a hotel near Mount Emei, which is about 31 kilometers (19 miles) from Leshan City. DAY 4 Mt. Emei to Chengdu | The Mount Emei Tour You will spend a full day visiting Mount Emei, which is another UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the four famous Buddhist mountains in China. Visit the Monastery of Ten Thousand Years, the largest and oldest monastery on Mount Emei. Take a relaxing walking trip from the Monastery of Ten Thousand Years to the Monkey Eco-Area to enjoy the natural beauty of the sub-tropical forest and waterfall. The whole journey takes about 3 to 3½ hours. Your guide and driver will escort you back to the hotel in Chengdu after the tour. DAY 5 Chengdu Departure | Airport Transfer Your private guide and driver will wait for you in your hotel lobby after breakfast. They will accompany you to the station or airport and help you check-in for your next destination.
