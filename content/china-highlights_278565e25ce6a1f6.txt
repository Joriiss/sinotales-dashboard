Title: 10-Day Yangtze River Cruise, Zhangjiajie, and Shanghai Tour
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/tour/cht-yz-01/
Source: China Highlights
================================================================================

Explore the Surreal Landscape This 10-day tour offers a unique opportunity to experience the best of both nature and culture in China. From the surreal mountains of Zhangjiajie, the historic sites along the Yangtze River, and the classic water town in Shanghai, you'll be captivated by the beauty and diversity of this ancient land. This tour is ideal for retired couples or families seeking a comfortable and hassle-free experience. Relax and unwind on our private tour with a dedicated guide and vehicle, ensuring minimal travel stress. Of course, you can personalize your itinerary with our expert guidance and recommendations. Suggested 10-Day Itinerary Day Highlights 1-2 Chongqing Three Gorges Museum, Chiqikou Old Street, and Hongyadong 3-5 Yangtze River Yangtze River Cruise, Three Gorges Dams, Fengdu Ghost City 6-7 Zhangjiajie The Hallelujah Mountain, Tianzi Mountain, the Glass Bridge, the Bailong Elevator 8-10 Shanghai The Bund, Shanghai Tower, and Yu Garden Cruise through Nature's Gallery Embark on a scenic cruise through the Three Gorges, where you can relax and enjoy the breathtaking views of lush green hills, terraced rice fields, and towering cliffs that create a picturesque landscape. Glimpse into Traditional Chinese Life Exploring charming ancient towns nestled along the Yangtze riverbanks offers a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in traditional Chinese culture and experience daily life firsthand. Marvel at the Towering Pillars and Lush Forests Zhangjiajie is a world where towering, otherworldly rock formations pierce the sky, shrouded in mist and surrounded by lush greenery. Hike through ancient forests, and experience the magic of a truly unique natural landscape. Experience the Unique Mountain City Witness the unique blend of ancient and modern in Chongqing, a city nestled among mountains and shrouded in mist. Delight Your Taste Buds with Amazing Chinese Food Challenge yourself with spicy but delicious Sichuan hotpot. And try Xiaolongbao, a type of steamed dumpling with delicate skin and a flavorful filling. Day 1Chongqing Arrival Your personal tour guide will greet you at the airport arrival hall. The driver will help you with the luggage and transfer you directly to the hotel via a comfortable private vehicle. Day 2Chongqing | City's Highlights Breakfast & Lunch Get prepared for your cruise by learning about the history of the Yangtze River and the Three Gorges Dam. The presentations and exhibits at Three Gorges Museum cover many aspects of the Yangtze River, including the animals living alongside the Yangtze, the regional population in ancient times, and ancient artifacts discovered near the Yangtze. Walk around the Goose Neck Park where you will see the local people playing Mahjohng and Chinese chess. Find the trail of the history on the building of the former Embassies and the old mansion of Chiang kai-shek. Then witness a light-rail train going through a residential building. At night, walk around Hongyadong which is a typical style of the residential houses but now it is a busy place for shopping and tasting the local snack. Day 3Chongqing to Yangtze | Board the Cruise Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Embark on a journey to Ciqikou Ancient Town. Beyond its lively main thoroughfare, delve into serene backstreets to fully embrace the allure of this thousand-year-old settlement, originally established during the Song Dynasty. In autumn, golden ginkgo leaves contrast with Baolun Temple's crimson walls. Discover a Qing Dynasty school and an ancient dock, enriching the town's historical aura. Visit Huguang Guild Hall on the bank of Yangtze River. Learn the history of the Yangtze River region and its economic development. The boarding time for the Yangtze River Cruise is 9 pm. Your guide and driver will ensure you a smooth transfer to the wharf. Day 4Yangtze River Cruise | Mystical Ghost City Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner On the Century Oasis Cruise, you will spend 3 nights along the Yangtze River, combining stunning natural scenery, and rich cultural heritage. The first day you will arrive at the Fengdu Ghost City, which is said to be the capital of hell according to Chinese mythology. The city features towering "Hell Gates", "Ghost Street", and the "Ten Courts of Hades", each representing a different aspect of the afterlife. Day 5Yangtze River Cruise | Majestic Three Gorges Breakfast & Lunch & Dinner Witness the breathtaking beauty of the Three Gorges, including Qutang Gorge, Wu Gorge, and Xiling Gorge. Embark on a scenic journey to Baidi City, where you'll be captivated by the grandeur of Kuimen. After exploring this ancient city, navigate through the dramatic Qutang Gorge and Wu Gorge. In the afternoon, enjoy a relaxing cruise on Shennv Creek, taking in the breathtaking beauty of its natural surroundings. Day 6Yichang to Zhangjiajie | Private Transfer to Zhangjiajie Breakfast & Lunch Today marks the end of our Yangtze River Cruise. Before disembarking, you will be guided to the Three Gorges Project Museum, learning about the engineering challenges and triumphs behind the dam's construction. Upon arriving in Yichang, you'll embark on the next leg of your journey to Zhangjiajie. Enjoy a private transfer directly to your hotel in Zhangjiajie. A driver and a guide will accompany you to ensure a smooth 4-hour journey. Day 7Zhangjiajie | National Forest Park Breakfast & Lunch Zhangjiajie National Forest Park's distinctive pillar-like formations are the product of centuries of erosion. The Bailong Sightseeing Elevator, the world's fastest sightseeing elevator, provides a stunning view of the Sandstone Peak Forest. Standing at 335 meters tall, it can transport 3,000 people per hour in its four transparent elevators. Tianzishan's towering sandstone pillars and lush greenery served as inspiration for the floating mountains in the movie Avatar. The summit of Tianzishan provides breathtaking panoramic views of the entire Zhangjiajie National Forest Park. Day 8Zhangjiajie to Shanghai | The Glass Bridge and Tianmen Mountain Breakfast & Lunch Visit Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon with your guide and driver. You won't miss to chance to walk across the most iconic attraction, the Glass Bridge, to have a thrilling experience. Then head to Zhangjiajie city for the Tianmen Mountain, often considered the "soul" of Zhangjiajie, is the region's highest peak. A 30-minute cable car ride from the city center takes you to the mountaintop, offering the longest cable car journey in the world. The Tianmen Mountain Glass Walkway, a daring attraction, is one of the world's highest observation platforms. It perches 1,420 meters above the ground, offering panoramic views of the mountains and a thrilling experience. Fly to shanghai at night. Day 9 Shanghai | Traditional Garden and Fantastic Skyline Breakfast & Lunch Discover Yu Garden, a tranquil classical oasis tucked among skyscrapers. Hear fascinating tales of its former owner—and the tale of how its legendary dragon wall nearly brought him misfortune. Stroll the Bund, tracing Shanghai's transformation from trading hub to global metropolis. Your guide will lead you through historic buildings, where you can handle antique relics while learning their storied past. Wander narrow alleyways to peek into Shanghai's everyday life. Then, follow your guide to a celebrated local eatery for Shanghai's signature snack: xiaolongbao (soup dumplings). Master the correct technique—or risk a scalded tongue from the boiling broth. Cross the Huangpu River like a local, jostling aboard a public ferry amid bustling crowds as you transition from historic Shanghai to the futuristic Pudong skyline. Day 10Shanghai Departure Breakfast Say goodbye to Shanghai. Transfer to the airport for your flight back home.
