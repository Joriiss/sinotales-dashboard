Weather: July gets the furnace smelter temperatures Chongqing is known for. It is the second hottest month of the year after August. When the sun shines with the high humidity of 76%, it is hot. The average temperatures range from a high of 33 °C (91 °F) to 25 °C (77 °F) at night. There are 12 days of rain in the month for a total of 175 mm. It is usually partly cloudy or cloudy, and there is some smog. Forecast: The weather is changeable, so stay abreast of the local weather forecast>> Air quality: Lightly polluted Clothing: Shorts and T-shirts and rain gear. Things to Do in Chongqing in July In July, you can duck indoors to museums or malls if it is hot or use the long summer days to explore. It is also good month for a river cruise on the Daning and Yangtze. The Lesser Three Gorges: It is a good time to see the lush green gorges. Both the water and the landscape are green contrasting with the muddy brown color of the Yangtze. Most tourists say that the scenery is better than at the big Three Gorges. Cool river breezes help you to stay cool. Ciqikou Old Town: You can walk the many lanes, shop, and see the old architecture. This isn't an area for bargain hunting as much as for recreation. It was the former cottage of General Stillwell who helped defend China in the war. Touring Chongqing in July The summer vacations for schools and universities start in early or middle July.
