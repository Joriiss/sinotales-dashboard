Day 1Arrive in Hong Kong| Airport Transfer Welcome to China! Your personal guide and driver will meet your family at the airport, and transfer you to the hotel in a non-smoking private van, making sure your holiday gets off to a smooth start. Day 2Hong Kong | Free Time Breakfast Hong Kong is easy for your family to explore on your own. It is a safe and well-developed city with efficient public transportation. And English is available in Hong Kong. It is a family-friendly destination. From Disneyland and Ocean Park to interactive museums, nature parks, and family-friendly dining options, there are plenty of things for families to enjoy together in Hong Kong. Day 3Hong Kong to Guilin | High-speed Train and Guilin Food Tour Breakfast Be ready to your first high-speed train experience. Going through security and customs will take a longer time. Please arrive at the train station at least 1 hour early. Your guide tour guide will meet you at the arrival hall, and help you check-in at the hotel. Day 4 Guilin to Yangshuo| Li River Cruise Breakfast & Lunch Take a morning cruise along the Li River from Guilin to Yangshuo. Enjoy breathtaking limestone karst landscapes, lush greenery, and picturesque countryside during the cruse. Spend your afternoon at the West Street. West Street is a vibrant and bustling pedestrian street, known for its lively atmosphere, diverse dining options, shops, and entertainment venues. Day 5Yangshuo to Xian| Countryside Life Experience Breakfast & Lunch You will go out of the area where the most tourists are wandering and see the untouched rural China, to experience the daily lifestyle of the local farmers. Visit a traditional house, take a local ferry and motorbike taxi, and wander through the vibrant farmer market. Release your artistic talent at 'Chinese Painted Fan Town'. You will have the chance to visit a workshop and learn from a local artist how to paint a traditional landscape on a paper fan. You will have your own handmade fan to keep as a souvenir. Day 6Xian | The Terracotta Warriors and Muslim Quarter Breakfast & Lunch Visit the Terracotta Warriors, be immersed in the awe-inspiring presence of the meticulously crafted clay soldiers, horses, and chariots that stand as a testament to ancient China's rich history and cultural heritage. Enjoy a leisurely walk on Xian's ancient City Wall, taking in panoramic city views, and admiring its centuries-old historical significance. Explore Xian's lively Muslim Quarter for a sensory journey through bustling markets, aromatic spices, colorful streets, and a variety of local delicacies and cultural finds. Day 7Xian to Beijing | Big Wild Goose Pagoda, Cooking Xian Noodles Breakfast & Lunch Explore the Big Wild Goose Pagoda to witness intricate carvings, climb spiral stairs for panoramic views, and uncover its rich historical and cultural significance. Enjoy the cooking experience with your family! Xian noodles are known for their unique flavor and texture. You will be welcomed into local people's home and learn to cook the most amazing traditional Xi'an noodles from them. Experience the speed of China by taking a high-speed train from Xi'an to Beijing, which can reach speeds of up to 350 km/h. Day 8Beijing | Forbidden City and Hutong Tour for Kids Breakfast & Lunch The vast Tian'anmen Square is probably the most famous square in the world, and the surrounding buildings, such as Chairman Mao's Mausoleum, the National Museum, and the People's Congress, emphasize its iconic appearance. History comes to life in the Forbidden City. Your knowledgeable guide will show you an interesting story about the emperors' lives and the impressive architecture. To keep the kids active, we specially choose some sites that interest kids more and play an interesting game. At lunchtime, you will taste the popular Beijing roasted Duck. The classic hutong area is a charming place to spend an afternoon. Discover the traditional lifestyle and let your kids be like Chinese children living in a hutong as they experience how they study and play, and taste the snacks they love. Day 10Beijing | Boat ride in Summer Palace and Lovely Panda Breakfast & Lunch The kids will not need to wait any longer to see the pandas; Beijing Zoo has many wild and rare animals from China, and among them are some adorable pandas. Have lunch in the first "smart restaurant" in China to experience the high level of technology. You'll be driven to Beijing's CBD after lunch. See the new skyline of Beijing and take pictures of the famous modern architectural structures. The Forbidden City was the winter abode of the emperors, but the Summer Palace was where they escaped the heat and enjoyed outdoor living in the vast gardens and pavilions. A short boat ride across the lake takes you to the main buildings, and you can climb the tower and see the entire park. Day 11Beijing | Departure Breakfast Your private car, driver, and guide will be ready and waiting at your hotel lobby to get you to the airport on time. Sit back and relax, as they help you check-in and escort you to the correct security gate. We will take the stress out of your travel and set you up for a nice journey home.
