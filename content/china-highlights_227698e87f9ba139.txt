Title: Guilin Weather in December - Cold and Quiet Season
URL: https://www.chinahighlights.com/guilin/weather/december.htm
Source: China Highlights
Date: 2025-08-28T00:00:00
================================================================================

December marks the start of winter in Guilin. It brings chilly and damp weather (typical of a humid-cold climate). Daytime highs generally range between 10°C and 18°C, with limited sunshine. Overnight and early morning temperatures often drop further, reaching lows of around 2–3°C. In mountainous areas like the Longji Terraces, temperatures can fall below freezing, as low as -2°C. Rainfall in Guilin during December is infrequent, with only 6–8 rainy days per month, mostly light drizzles. However, the high humidity on rainy days can make it feel much colder. Snow is extremely rare. Light sleet or brief mixed precipitation may occur on just 1–2 days per year. In over 20 years of living here, I've only experienced snow 2 or 3 times, and it never lasted long. Additionally, Guilin tends to be windy in winter. It can make outdoor activities feel even colder. Be sure to wear wind-resistant and warm layers to stay comfortable. How to Better Explore Guilin's Landscape in Winter With cold weather in December, water-based activities like swimming and bamboo rafting are no longer suitable. However, other outdoor experiences such as cave exploration, and hiking are still workable. Especially, during the midday, the temperatures often rise above 20°C in the sunshine. On colder days, you can choose more comfortable ways to enjoy the scenery, such as taking a cruise on the Li River or driving through the countryside along the Yulong River. Additionally, airfares and hotel rates hit their annual low in December. As long as you pack adequate warm clothing, you can fully enjoy a peaceful and serene winter journey. Below are some recommended activities. 1. Take a Li River Cruise Taking a Li River cruise in December is undoubtedly one of the most comfortable ways to experience Guilin's winter scenery. The enclosed, air-conditioned cabins allow you to enjoy views of the karst peaks and riverside landscapes in warmth. Unlike open bamboo rafts exposed to the wind, you can freely move between the cozy cabin and the deck for photos without enduring the cold. For the best experience, we recommend opting for a four-star cruise ship. It offers more spacious cabins, comfortable seating, and better amenities. The classic route from Guilin to Yangshuo (approx. 4 hours) covers the most scenic section of the Li River. Recommended Guilin tour with a Li River Cruise. 2. Drive Along the Scenic Yulong River and Countryside Afternoon tea by Yulong River Bamboo rafting is the classic way to experience the Yulong River. But in December we recommend a comfortable car tour along its banks. This allows you to admire the karst landscapes from a warm vehicle without enduring the chilly wind and damp weather. You can stop at scenic viewpoints to take photos and explore. The route passes through ancient villages like Jiuxian Village and Xiatang Village. Here you can observe local daily life in winter. With a guide, you can also participate in village activities such as making traditional oil tea or local rice cakes and taste authentic specialties. On sunny days, enjoy afternoon tea in the riverside gardens of countryside resorts like Yangshuo Mountain Retreat or Yangshuo Moondance Hotel while taking in the serene views. 3. Visit Wujiu Beach for Red Leaves and Li River Views Located 25 km from downtown Guilin (about an hour's drive), Wujiu Beach on the Li River is an ideal spot for red leaves. In December, the leaves of the tallow trees (often mistaken for maple leaves) turn brilliant red, reaching their peak color. The combination of the river, dramatic peaks, and vibrant foliage creates a stunning scene. The best viewing period is usually early to mid-December. After that, the leaves fall, so check ahead to confirm if they are still visible. Besides leaf viewing, you can also enjoy a picnic or afternoon tea by the river. A sunny afternoon is highly recommended for a relaxing experience with warm sunshine, good food, and gorgeous views. 4. Soak in Hot Springs and See Mirror Terraces in Longsheng Longsheng Hot Springs During the chilly days in December, nothing is more relaxing than soaking in a hot spring. The most famous hot spring spot is at Longsheng Hot Springs Resort, about 2 hours' drive from Guilin. These natural geothermal springs are rich in minerals and surrounded by mountains, offering a soothing escape. After the hot springs soaking, you could head to the Longji Terraces, about 60km away from the Hot Springs Reaort. In Ping'an Village, farmers water the terraces during the off-season, creating breathtaking "mirror fields". The fields reflect the sky, clouds, and mountain outlines. For the best views and photo opportunities, hike to the Nine Dragons and Five Tigers and Seven Stars Around the Moon viewing platforms in Ping'an. Read more aout Longji Terraces. 5. Admire the Rime Ice Scene in High Mountain Areas Rime Ice Scene in High Mountain Areas In mid- to late December, if a cold snap brings temperatures below 0°C with rain or moisture, you might witness a rare natural wonder in Guilin's high-altitude areas: rime ice. Trees and grasses become encased in sparkling ice crystals, glistening in the sunlight like a fairy-tale ice kingdom. The best viewing spots are in high-elevation scenic areas around Guilin, such as Ziyuan's Aimengjie Scenic Area, Bajiao Village Scenic Area, and Quanzhou's Tianhu Lake Scenic Area. These are about 100 km from Guilin and roughly a 2-hour drive away. Since rime formation is unpredictable, be sure to check local weather websites or travel forums before your trip to confirm conditions. What to Wear in Guilin in December Dressing for Guilin in December should prioritize warmth and wind protection. We recommend a layered approach. Start with a base layer of thermal underwear and a wool sweater to retain warmth. Add a mid-layer such as a fleece or light down jacket to trap heat. Top it off with a windproof and waterproof outer jacket. For your lower body, opt for thick pants or thermal leggings. Warm, slip-resistant shoes are essential, as surfaces can be cold and potentially slippery in winter. Accessories are also key: a warm hat, gloves, and a scarf are necessary to protect against the cold wind. Given the significant temperature differences between indoors and outdoors, choosing easy-to-adjust layered clothing is far more practical and flexible than relying solely on a single heavy coat.
