Shanghai, a vibrant city situated on the Yangtze River Delta, has a fascinating past as a gateway to China. It is not only the largest economic and trade hub in the country but also a fantastic cultural center. You can still catch glimpses of Shanghai's colonial history in the charming Bund area, which adds to its cosmopolitan charm.
