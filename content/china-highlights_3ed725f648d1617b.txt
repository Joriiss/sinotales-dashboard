Title: 1-Day Private Tour to Dazu Rock Carvings from Chongqing

Highlights Itinerary Price FAQ Embark on an extraordinary day trip to explore the hidden depths of the Dazu Rock Carvings, Chongqing's only UNESCO World Heritage Site. Led by an experienced tour guide, discover this awe-inspiring wonder, one of the eight greatest caves in the world. These breathtaking carvings bring to life the tales of Confucianism, Buddhism, and Taoism, alongside vivid depictions of everyday life, offering a unique glimpse into a richly diverse cultural tapestry. Tour Highlights Skip-the-line access to Dazu Rock Carvings to ensure a relaxing day trip. Knowledgeable tour guide with insightful commentary in Dazu Rock Carvings. Two available options with flexible pace, allowing you to spend more time on the parts you like. You can WhatsApp me at +86-18807734970 in case you need to reach us. 9:00 - 11:00am Transfer from Chongqing to Dazu Rock Carvings Your guide will pick you up at your hotel lobby at 9 AM in Chongqing downtown and drive to Baodingshan Rock Carvings Tourist Center about 2 hours. Dazu Grottoes 11:00 - 12:30pm Visit Dazu Baodingshan Rock Carvings The Dazu Rock Carvings are concentrated in five main areas, with Baodingshan and Beishan cavings are the most impressive. Firstly we'll visit the Baodingshan area. The Dazu Baodingshan Rock Carvings are located northeast of Dazu District. Although it's only 500 meters long, it's densely packed with tens of thousands of exquisitely crafted stone sculptures. Many of these carvings are painted, showcasing the skill of the ancient craftsmen. They used a stunning array of colors in blues, greens, reds, and even gold leaf. Dazu Rock Carving The most famous Reclining Buddha, one is the world's largest half-reclining Buddha statue. It symbolizes the state of Nirvana in Buddhism and symbolizes the nirvana and transcendence of Sakyamuni Buddha. Thousand-Armed Avalokiteshvara is a massive statue with 830 arms, each holding a Buddhist ritual object, weapon, or farming tool. Wheel of Samsara describes cycle of birth, death, and rebirth, driven by desire and ignorance. A demonic figure turns the wheel, propelling beings through the six realms of existence. Thousand-Armed Avalokiteshvara 12:30 - 3:00pm Visit Dazu Rock Carvings Museum We'll take a break for lunch. The lunch fee is not included. Continue to visit the Dazu Rock Carvings Museum, a grottoes-themed museum covering an area of ​​6,500 square meters. It is the largest professional grotto temple museum in China. It primarily houses relics from the outdoor grotto temples in the Dazu Rock Carvings scenic area, along with nearly 500 artifacts from the collection. The centerpiece of the museum is a Southern Song Dynasty statue of Shakyamuni Buddha, a national first-class cultural relic. 3:00 - 4:00pm Visit Beishan Rock Carvings (Optional) The Beishan Rock Carvings, situated 1.5 kilometers north of Longgang Town in Dazu, occupy a 500-meter area divided into north and south sections. This expansive site showcases over 5,000 meticulously crafted sculptures, like Avalokiteshvara with Rosary, Marici, the Warrior Goddess, the Largest Water-Moon Avalokiteshvara which is most well-preserved statue of Avalokiteshvara meditating on the reflection of the moon in water and Wheel of Dharma Sutra Cave, etc. 4:00 - 6:00pm Transfer Back to Chongqing The tour will ends around 4pm, then we will transfer back and arrive in Chongqing around 6pm.
