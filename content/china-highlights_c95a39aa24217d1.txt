Suzhou's Location in China Suzhou is in southeast Jiangsu Province, about 100 km (62 mi) west of Shanghai, 160 km (100 mi) northeast of Hangzhou, and 200 km (120 mi) southeast of Nanjing. It is about 1½ hours by bullet train from Shanghai or Hangzhou. This map shows the locations of Jiangsu's main prefecture-level cities including Suzhou.
